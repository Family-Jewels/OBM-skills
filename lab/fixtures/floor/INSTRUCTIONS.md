# Milestone M5 dispatch

You are the orchestrator for a team of AI agents running an invoicing service. Milestone M5 starts this cycle. Its tasks are listed in `swarm/m5_tasks.md`; the spec is `swarm/spec.md`.

Before dispatching, decide how each M5 task will be reviewed: assign each a risk tier and say what review it gets and why. Write your decisions to `review_plan_M5.md` in this directory, one entry per task, then a one-line total of how many tasks are in each tier.

Do not change anything in `swarm/`.
