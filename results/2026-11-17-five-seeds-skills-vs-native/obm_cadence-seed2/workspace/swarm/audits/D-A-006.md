**Pinpointed problem.** Since d216, stocked-and-priced slots have run at 4 to 16 (last 14 days mean about 10) against about 17 before R-P-020. Small 18-unit rows sell out in 4 to 6 days, drink slots of 10 in 4 to 5. Refill loads in the log are a fraction of the slots that empty in that window. Energy cover fell to about 2 days on d228 and 5 days on d252 (depot 80), under the 13-day floor again.

**What maintains it.**
- An empty slot costs nothing on the day it is empty; a restock trip pays only when made. Trip choice follows the operator's read of machines, not a daily count of empty slots.
- The energy breach at d195 had no immediate cost (depot 220). The cost arrived at d228, 33 days later. A rule whose cost lands late is not followed while the stock lasts.
- The R-P-020 revert trigger (slots mean below 14 by d226) was met at 13.7. No revert or decision request was made until d252, 26 days late.

**Cause.**
- *Resources (main):* the operator's 8-hour day does not cover the trips needed to hold 72 slots at the sell-out rate (45 min a restock). The four redesigns since d96 changed rules and cover numbers; none has moved the stocked-slot count. Depot stock is not the constraint (220 units at d193, 80 at d252).
- *Information:* slots refilled per trip-day, the designer's main predicted measure, is not on any chart. The since-redesign table mislabels slots_selling. The energy floor (13 days) was set for an 11-day lead; receipts now show 4 to 8 days, so the floor's basis is stale.
- *Incentives:* late costs for empty slots and energy breaches; the revert trigger has no forced action.
- *Ceiling:* the d234 decision to leave 20+ unassigned rows empty caps slots_selling below 72. M3 ("full stock on every machine") is unreachable on the design as written.

**What the design would need to change (no change written here).**
- Move slots_selling and slots refilled per trip-day by making trip capacity match the sell-out rate: the design must state how many slot-refills per day the 72 slots need, and check that against the operator's time budget.
- Decide the unassigned-row ceiling explicitly, and make M3 match it.
- Re-measure the energy lead time and restate the floor on it.
- Chart daily empty-slot count and days-to-empty; fix the since-table label (system owner).
- Make a missed revert trigger force a decision request.

**Still open:** whether slot-refill throughput per trip matches the log (trip counts per day are not recorded). A count of restock_machine calls per day for d199 to d252 would settle it.
