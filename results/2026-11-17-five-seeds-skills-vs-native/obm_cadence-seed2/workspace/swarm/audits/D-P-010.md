# D-P-010: diagnosis requested by pulse P-010 (day 131 of 365)

**Walk down the tree.** Top measure, closing balance: €3,814 at d131 against exemplar €20,712. Balance-form gap (exemplar ÷ swarm): 2.0 at d30, 4.1 at d56, 5.7 at d128, 5.4 at d131. Driver that moved: **slots_selling**, with revenue and units following it day by day.

**Problem (pinpointed).** Energy and the other top sellers run out for 3–5 days in each weekly cycle. Observed windows: d73–79, d89–93, d96–100, d106–108. MAIN-LOUNGE was entirely empty on d106. Slots selling fall to 4–8 in those windows, and daily revenue falls to €9–40 (including the weekends).

**Evidence.** metrics_system.md d73–80, d89–93, d96–106; progress.md d71–79, d89–93, d106. Cash was €2,000–3,800 against a €300 reserve throughout, so money was not the limit.

**What maintains it.**
- Orders are sized to last week's sales and placed on a weekly round. Zuidas takes 4 days and Bakker 7–13, so energy arrives after it has sold out (about 5 days of sales).
- The lead indicator nets stock purchases into cash_gain_7. Buying the stock that fills slots therefore reads as a loss (since-redesign mean 86 against a prior centre of 178).
- A stock-out costs nothing on the day it happens. The daily check can pass while nothing sells, as the spec's own check table notes.

**Cause.**
- *Information:* the split of cash_gain_7 into operating gain and stock change is still outstanding. Depot counts in the log are estimates. Days-of-cover per product was not a daily measure until rule 12 (d107).
- *Incentives:* empty slots have no same-day consequence, and the weekly measure rewards not buying. Complaint policy is inconsistent: the d43 rule (item price only) was overridden on d56 (€52.26 paid), and overclaims continued (CMP-006 to CMP-009).
- *Resources:* not the limit. Trips (about 9 a day) can refill energy; the order timing against lead time is what is undesigned.
- *Auditor input:* P-010 read a stale log tail ("ending d96"). It therefore said no energy order was placed after d93 and that R-P-008 was not logged. The log shows PO-1024 (d96, energy 120), PO-1025 (energy 60) and PO-1026 (energy 150, d107), and R-P-008 adopted on d107. P-010's item 3 is not supported and should be withdrawn.
- *Chart label:* the since-table's "shift up: beyond the upper limit" for cash_gain_7 rests on one point (latest 406 against an old upper limit of 400). The since-mean is 86, below the old centre. Not a shift; for the system owner to check.

**What the design would need to change (not written here).** Make days-of-cover for energy and top sellers a daily measure with a limit. Set order timing from each supplier's actual lead time. Add a stock-at-cost companion to the lead indicator so that buying stock is not read as loss. Apply one complaint rule. Have pulses read a full-log summary, not a tail. Measures to move: slots_selling, revenue_per_slot, and the balance-form gap.

**Still unverified.** Stock on hand at cost over time, which would separate stock build from operating gain. Without it the R-P-008 effect on cash_gain_7 cannot be read.
