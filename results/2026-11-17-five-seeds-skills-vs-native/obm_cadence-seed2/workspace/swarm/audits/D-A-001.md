## Cause

- **Information:** `rules/operator.md` has no storage-check rule. cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as cost, with no stock-on-hand measure. Spec open questions (elasticity, reserve, marketing payback, supplier terms) are still open. The price test has no baseline.
- **Resources:** Bakker lead time (7–9 days) against a weekly order rhythm. Units per slot by location is not recorded, though the spec requires it.
- **Incentives:** the lead measure rewards buying less. Empty slots and failed trips carry no visible cost.

## What the design would need to change

- Separate stock purchases from cost (working capital) in cash_gain_7, and add stock cover or empty-slot days as a lead measure.
- Size orders to lead time, not last week's sales (measure: slots_selling, revenue_last_7).
- Add the storage check to the operator brief.
- Fix the PIP definition to one form and close the open questions.
