## Cause addressed

- Information / timing: rule 12 (v3) fires at "under 4 days' cover", but the energy lead time is 4 days (Zuidas). An order placed at that point lands after the stock has sold out. The fault is the trigger, not the supplier or the money.
- Not changed here, and why: the lead-time question for energy (which supplier serves it) stays open in the spec until receipts confirm it; the rule uses the longer-safe margin meanwhile. The cash_gain_7 split and the stock-at-cost companion are a system-owner request (R-A-001, D-P-008, D-P-010). The designer cannot edit the chart.

## Change (one driver, one rule)

- `swarm/spec.md`: version 3 -> 4. Stock cover is now a daily read of days-to-empty for energy and top sellers, with a floor of supplier lead time plus 2 days (6 days on the 4-day Zuidas lead), cap about 10 days. The "Each day" check now includes days-to-empty; a reading below the floor is a same-day signal.
- `swarm/rules/operator.md`: version 3 -> 4. Rule 12 changed from a Monday/under-4-days trigger to a daily check at 09:00: order at or below 6 days' cover, sized to lead time plus 7 days of sales, within the weekly cap and the reserve guard. Trips go to those slots first. Measured lead time from receipts replaces 4 days if it differs.
- Old versions kept: `swarm/history/spec.v3.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v3.md`.
- Nothing in the reserve, the weekly cap, the done criteria or the core loop (restock, price, sell) is removed or gated.
