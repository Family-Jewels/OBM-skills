# Redesign R-A-001: day 40 of 365

Trigger: D-A-001 (day 38): cash_gain_7 below its centre for 8 periods; cause in the environment, not effort.

## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The top measure (closing balance) and the chain to it (revenue, units, slots_selling, price) are the right drivers. Revenue is recovering and every sales measure is inside its limits, so the swarm is not selling the wrong things. The problem is the lead measure and the buying rhythm.

## 2. Is the design causing the problem?

Yes, in two ways:

- **Measure:** cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as cost. Buying stock lowers the lead indicator, so the operator is rewarded for buying less, and empty slots (the real loss) never show on it. This is the cause of the buying rhythm the diagnosis describes.
- **Brief:** operator.md sizes orders to last week's sales, with no lead-time rule and no storage check. The day-15 lesson sat in the log, not the brief, and failed trips repeated on day 20.

Not a cause: the exemplar, the top measure, the reserve, the done criteria. None of them is changed.

## 3. Change made (smallest set that addresses the cause)

1. **spec.md v1 → v2 (tree only):**
   - cash_gain_7 is the operating gain (revenue − cost of units sold − fees). Stock on hand at cost is shown beside it as working capital, not netted off.
   - Added **stock cover (days)** as a driver of slots_selling, target up to about 10 days (lead time 7–9 days).
   - PIP defined in one form: exemplar balance ÷ swarm balance.
2. **rules/operator.md v1 → v2:**
   - Rule 4: order to cover lead time plus one week of sales, within the weekly cap (not last week's sales alone).
   - Rule 11 (new): check storage and the slot list before a restock trip.

Not changed: the reserve, the weekly cap (85% of free cash after reserve), the 25% decision threshold, the exemplar, the done criteria, milestones, cadence, budgets.

Old versions: `swarm/history/spec.v1.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`.

Limit on this change: the system writes cash_gain_7 on the charts, and still counts purchases as cost. The operator cannot change that. The system owner should split working capital from the chart (request filed here, not done by the designer).

## 4. Prediction

- **Measure:** slots_selling (chart, centre 8.3, latest 6).
- **Direction:** up. Empty-slot days fall.
- **By when:** above its centre (8.3) for 3 consecutive periods by **day 54**.
- **Secondary:** revenue_last_7 (latest 307.0) keeps rising; no restock trip fails for want of stock after day 41.
- **Not the test:** cash_gain_7 will likely stay low while stock is bought. That is the expected cost of stocking, not a failure, so do not judge this change on it.

## 5. Cost and revert

- **Cost:** more cash tied up in stock for about 9–10 days of cover. The reserve guard still binds, so cash may run tighter for a week.
- **Revert to v1 if:** (a) slots_selling is not above 8.3 by day 54; or (b) cash falls below the reserve on any day because of stock purchases; or (c) units per slot falls while slots_selling rises (sales not following stock). On revert, copy the current file to `history/` as v2 first, then restore v1.
