# R-P-006: plan revision in response to D-P-006

**Date:** day 40
**Change:** swarm/plan.md revised to version 3. Previous version (revision 2) saved as swarm/history/plan-v2.md.

**Why:** D-P-006 found a level problem, not instability: sales bursty and falling (selling slots 15 to 8), thin unit economics (about EUR 2 per unit against a EUR 12/day spot fee), about EUR 1,540 idle above the reserve, and the day-11 inventory audit never recorded.

**What changed:**
- Added a gate: no new stock purchase until the "inventory-audit" note exists. The audit now covers units bought, loaded and sold, stock in machines vs storage, stocked vs total slots, and margin per product.
- Restock from storage before any purchase, and spend restock touches on slots with the highest expected sales per touch.
- Capped idle cash: buy only what loaded slots can sell within 7 days.
- Reprice only products with confirmed positive margin. Charts are not a trigger for action.
- Reviews moved to day 45 (stop purchases if selling slots do not rise) and day 50 (economics review).

**Not changed:** the EUR 200 reserve, the 45-minute restock and about 8 touches per day, the refund rule, and the spot-fee and bankruptcy rules.

**Open:**
- The cause (restock throughput and stock held in slots) is still unverified. The audit must confirm or reject it.
- Selling-slot count conflicts: D-P-006 says 8, while charts.md shows 14 as the latest slots_selling. The audit must settle which is correct.
- Unit margin is unknown, and the purchase total (about EUR 1,089) may include unsold stock.
