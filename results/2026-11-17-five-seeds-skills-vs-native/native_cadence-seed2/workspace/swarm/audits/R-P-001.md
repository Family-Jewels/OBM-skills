# R-P-001: plan revision in response to D-P-001

**Date:** day 11
**Change:** swarm/plan.md revised to version 2. Previous version saved as swarm/history/plan-v1.md.

**Why:** D-P-001 found that the plan was stale (it still asked for approval before day 0), that sales were very low (10 units, 4 slots selling by day 8), and that about EUR 1,000 of cash sat idle above the EUR 200 reserve. The likely cause, not verified, is that stock bought before day 2 was not loaded until about day 6.

**What changed:**
- Removed the day-0 approval and first-day steps. The swarm is already trading.
- Added an inventory audit (machine and storage) as the first action, before any new purchase.
- Added a target to load every empty slot by day 12, and at least 12 selling slots by day 14.
- Set a rule to deploy idle cash above the EUR 200 reserve, only on stock that loaded slots can sell within 7 days.
- Added a day-16 review with a stop rule: if fewer than 8 slots are selling, return to the stock audit.
- Stated that charts are not acted on inside their limits.

**Not changed:** the reserve (EUR 200), the restock and swap time budget, the refund rule, and the supplier and marketing rules.

**Open:** the cause is unverified. The inventory audit (due day 11 to 12) must confirm or reject it.
