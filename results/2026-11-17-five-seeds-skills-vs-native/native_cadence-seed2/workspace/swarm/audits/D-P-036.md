## What is going wrong

1. **Records have stopped.** The plan rev 8 gates were due on day 252 (reconciliation), day 260 (load-test check) and day 265 (freeze decision). None exists in swarm/audits/, and the latest audit is P-036. The purchasing freeze, the load test and the 3.84 units-per-slot figure cannot be verified.

2. **Revenue has fallen.** 7-day revenue was 930 at day 258, 864 at day 263, 444 at day 270, and 586 at day 276. cash_gain_7 has been below its centre (388) since day 268: 225 at day 276.

3. **Units per selling slot is not rising within the window.** Weekly figures: 4.27 (days 257-263), 3.28 (days 264-270), 3.94 (days 270-276). The 3.84 headline averages over a fall. Slots selling fell from about 13 a day to about 10, so the denominator is shrinking.

4. **Cash leaves at roughly the rate of sales.** Cash rise is revenue less the EUR 12 fee less about 0.8-1.0 per unit sold, in every window checked: days 213-242 about 0.90 per unit (gap 740 over 822 units, approximate); days 264-270 about 0.81; days 269-276 about 0.98. If the freeze held, the stock sold was already paid for, and cash should rise by about revenue less fee. The pattern fits ongoing purchases or payments on earlier orders. The records cannot say which.

5. **The exemplar gap is narrowing, not widening.** Plan and charts state PIP 6.8 (day 251) and 6.1 (day 276). Recomputed: 41,485 / 7,419 = 5.59 at day 251, and 45,812 / 8,724 = 5.25 at day 276. The 6.1 in charts.md is wrong.

6. **Dates disagree.** charts.md says R-A-004 was adopted on day 254; plan rev 8 and R-A-004 are dated day 251.

## Why

- The plan's gates depend on records that are not being written, so the freeze, the load test and the purchase rules are still untested assumptions, as D-A-004 found. A missed review is supposed to be recorded on its day, and none was.
- Cash is the headline measure, but it cannot be reconciled, so decisions rest on gross revenue and an unknown cost line.
- Most likely (a hypothesis, not proven): purchasing has not stopped, and restocked stock is not reaching selling slots (storage is not counted). Cash spent at the sales rate, plus falling stocked slots, fits that. It is consistent with the load rule not being followed.
