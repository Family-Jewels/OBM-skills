# D-P-011: diagnosis of P-011 (day 66)

**Finding:** Cash 2,876.51 (+1,376.51 on the 1,500 start). Exemplar 9,459.87, PIP 5.8 (charts.md). Revenue is not weakening on the system's own measures. Volume is flat-to-rising, and the gap is a volume problem, not a margin problem.

**Checks against the records:**
1. **"Weakening" is an endpoint reading.** Revenue per day: 58 (days 7-20), 62 (21-35), 70 (36-50), 91 (53-66). Rolling 7-day revenue since day 40 ranges 380-777, mean about 573; the latest 494 is inside that range. charts.md flags revenue above its upper limit since the day-46 redesign (shift up), which P-011 does not mention.
2. **The "8 vs 14 selling slots" conflict is not a conflict.** D-P-006 quoted day 35 (8); the plan quoted day 40 (14). metrics_system.md matches both. Plan item 4 rests on a false conflict.
3. **The day-45 test compares single days** (day 40 vs day 45) on a bursty series. It cannot show a restock effect. Use 14-day means.
4. **Unit economics look positive.** Days 51-66: revenue 1,492; cash gain 783; spot fees about 192; implied stock purchases about 517 for 709 units, roughly EUR 0.73 per unit against EUR 2.10 revenue. This assumes stock bought is stock sold, so it is approximate. The "thin unit economics" premise in D-P-006 and the plan is not supported.
5. **Cash is not the constraint.** About 2,676 sits above the EUR 200 reserve.

**What is going wrong (most likely, unverified):** Volume is capped by stocked selling slots. Selling slots swing from 5 to 18 (days 61-64 at 5-9), and revenue follows them: revenue per selling slot is steady at about 8-12 when slots are stocked. The likely limit is restock throughput (about 8 touches a day at 45 minutes each). A purchase gate does not explain it, because cash is not binding.

**Not known:**
- Whether "inventory-audit", "daily-log" and the day-50 review exist. This auditor sees only the workspace, and P-010 and P-011 did not find them. Confirm in the notes store.
- Total slots per machine, so the restock backlog cannot be sized.
- Day 37 is missing from metrics_system.md.

**For the plan revision:** judge on 14-day means of selling slots and units; record total slots in the audit; rank restock touches by expected revenue per touch (about EUR 2 revenue per unit); keep the audit gate.
