# R-A-006: plan revision v6 to v7, day 216 of 365

Responds to: D-A-006 (day 206 diagnosis). Old plan: swarm/history/plan-v6.md. New plan: swarm/plan.md (v7).

## What changed
- Order gate is closed by default. Orders need today's 72-slot census, a passed restock-landing check, a reconciled previous-day cash bridge, and purchases per unit at or below 1.0, all written to the daily-log note first (step 5).
- Steady refill targets only slots the census confirms as priced and selling in the last 7 days (step 6).
- New delivery trace (step 6a): untraced stock blocks orders after 2 days.
- Missing records block all orders that day and the next, not just new ones (step 10).
- New signal rules: any zero-sales day, or slots_selling at or below 7 for two days, freezes orders until 12 priced-with-stock selling slots show in a census. Two unreconciled cash days freeze orders and trigger naming of outflows for days 175-216.
- Unexplained cash freeze lasts until the bridge reconciles (risk controls).

## Why
D-A-006 found purchasing continued while the census, records and gates were not operated, so stock was not reaching selling slots and outflow was unnamed. v7 makes the evidence a precondition for buying.

## Not changed
Prices, marketing rules, late-game plan and the business itself. Diagnosis is not confirmed: the census and cash bridge for days 175-216 are still needed to settle D-A-006.
