But the spec names in-stock fraction and lead time as "Yes, first", and only lead time is tracked, and that by the operator from receipts. In-stock fraction and 7-day COGS are still not measured 86 days in. The request went to the owner as D-002 on day 48; the queue has no answer. The operator has steered on a manual empty-slot proxy, with 31 of 48 slots empty on day 49 and 25 of 42 on day 56. The proxy's denominator changed from 48 to 42 without a note (log lines 424, 446, 448).

**F2. The seasonal control has been below 1 since day 70 (outcome).**
revenue_last_7 ÷ revenue_same_7_a_month_earlier is below 1 on all 16 recorded days from day 70 to day 86 (day 82: 0.73; day 86: 0.87). Over the 27 recorded days in 57–86 the raw revenue sum is 3,312 against 3,790 over the 28 recorded days in 27–56 (about 0.91 a day against 0.95 a day in raw terms, not season-adjusted). C4 is read at day 120. On the present trend it is at risk. This is outside the five charted measures, so no charted signal fires. It is a decline the pulses did not report.

**F3. Recurring stock-out loop; A-001's action 1 did not close it (operations).**
Empty machines: days 34–36 (A-001), days 46–49 (31 of 48 slots empty on day 49), days 56–58 (25–26 of 42), days 77–79 (all six machines empty; the loads of days 72–75 sold through in 4–5 days, log lines 465–466). Each restock is about 60 units per SKU spread over six slots, about 10 per slot. The cover figures in the log are correct on paper, but the loads do not fill the machines and sell out in days. The fix on day 48 (cover to next delivery plus 12 days) is recorded and was applied, but the cycle returned. The chart's slots_selling values over days 77–81 (9, 5, 5, 2, 8) are five points below centre, one short of the 8-point run rule, so this is not a chart signal.

**F6. PIP is on a basis the spec does not define, and its exemplar source is still unanswered (system / chart).**
- The chart defines PIP as exemplar gain ÷ actual gain (charts.md line 25). The spec defines it as exemplar cash ÷ swarm cash (spec.md line 48). On day 86 the chart prints 4.1; the spec basis gives 12,922.02 ÷ 4,259.83 = 3.03. The chart's arithmetic is correct on its own basis, so P-010 and P-011 were wrong to call the printed figure an error. The basis is the problem.
- The chart's exemplar values do not follow from the spec. The spec gives day 30 = 3,228 and day 365 = 61,219, which imply about 0.88% a day. That path gives about 5,280 on day 86, not 12,922. The spec says no interim path is given (open question 4), so the chart value may be correct, but its source is not in the records. A-001 F6 asked for it on day 36; nothing in the records answers it.
- The trigger "PIP above 2" holds on the spec basis (3.03) and on the chart basis (4.1). It would not hold on the spec's own constant-rate path (about 1.2). This is why the exemplar source matters.
