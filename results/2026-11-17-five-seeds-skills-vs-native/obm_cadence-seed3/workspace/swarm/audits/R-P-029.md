## 2. Cause, and whether the design caused it

Cause (D-P-029): supply is binding. The depot runs out before restock (protein day 195, crisps day 199), and orders are placed only after a SKU is exhausted, with realised lead time 3 to 4 days. The rule in `rules/operator.md` v4 already says to cover the longest realised lead time, but nothing makes the cover figure a recorded input, so the rule is not applied and no check sees it. The rotation cap in v4 limits trip allocation; it does not touch depot supply.

Design causes: the cover rule is written as advice with no recorded input (a design fault); depot stock by SKU and the in-stock fraction are not measured (information, designer cannot build it, owner request D-007 is still open). Not the cause: direction, price, the goal, the exemplar.

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)

Change 1, `rules/operator.md` v4 -> v5: the cover rule becomes an ordered procedure. Before each order the operator reads depot and slot stock, takes units per day from the 7-day sales report, computes cover days, takes lead time from the last five deliveries, and orders to cover of lead time + 1 day. Reorder when cover reaches lead time + 1, not at zero. One log line per order carries all the inputs.

Change 2, `spec.md` v5 -> v6, additions only:
- Driver tree: row `cover_days (per SKU)`, pushed on now, marked as needing instrumentation for depot stock.
- Check C8: each order line states its inputs and its cover after; checked weekly by the checker against receipts. It is a guard on the driver, not an outcome.
- Exemplar basis: interim days are read from the stated 0.88%/day interpolation between the two sourced points. The chart's exemplar series is not reproduced by that basis (day 214: chart 35,079.77 against about 16,240), so its figure is not quoted as the exemplar until the measurement owner documents it. PIP at day 222 on this basis is about 1.46, not 3.4.
- Open questions 9 and 10: system requests for depot stock by SKU, lead time per supplier and stock-out slot-days; the pulse quotes the chart's status line as written.

Not changed, and why:
- The goal, the aims, the reserve (€300), the done-means outcome checks C1 and C4: none is the cause, and lowering or moving any of them is out of scope.
- The 16-slot rotation cap and the rotation read from v4: they address trip allocation, which D-P-025 found real. Keep them.
- The refund rule C6: an incentive cause, but a secondary one; still deferred.
- The chart's exemplar series and status labels: system-owned; the change is in the spec, the chart is the measurement owner's to fix.
