## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)

Change: `rules/operator.md` v6 -> v7. Old copy: `history/operator.v6.md`. Addition: the **leak hold** bullet. A SKU whose last recorded unexplained loss at a slot is above zero is loaded only to the units sold since the last read, not to full, and the freed units go to the next slot in the rotation with zero loss. The hold lifts after two consecutive zero-loss visits. Each hold is logged as `LEAK HOLD <slot> <SKU>` with the figures. The hold yields to the guards (M1, the 2-day location rule). A missing found count is a C9 breach, not a hold.
