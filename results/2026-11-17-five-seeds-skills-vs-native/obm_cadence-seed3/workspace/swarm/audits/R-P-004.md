# Redesign R-P-004 (day 29)

Trigger: D-P-004 (day 26): depot ran dry while orders were in transit; slots_selling fell to ~8 against centre 12.35 over days 21–26; revenue_last_7 fell 875 → 492.

**Direction check.** The drivers are right: slots_selling and in-stock fraction are what move revenue and units, and the tree adds up to final cash. The tree is incomplete in one place: lead time is a real input to how much stock a machine needs, and the tree had no measure for it. The design is causing the problem: the operator brief has no lead-time rule, so orders are sized on a quoted 2-day lead time that realised at 2–11 days, and each stock-out is found only after it happens.

**Chart note.** At day 29 the slots_selling latest is 15, above centre 12.35, so the dip may already be recovering. The change is kept because a recurrence is what the diagnosis found, and the charts can only show it if the cause is removed.

**Change (smallest that addresses the cause).**
1. `rules/operator.md`: version 1 → 2. New rule: order to cover the longest realised lead time for that supplier; write the cover figure in the log; if the reserve cannot fund it, order what it allows and note the gap. The order is not skipped.
2. `spec.md` driver tree: version 1 → 2. Added `lead_time_realised (per supplier)`, sourced from dated delivery receipts. Open question 5 added to track it.
3. Old versions kept: `swarm/history/spec.v1.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`.

**Not changed, and why.**
- Refund rule C6 (full amount on complaints) is the incentive cause in D-P-004. It is deliberately left for the next redesign: the log shows no complaint after day 23, so refunds_7 is no longer rising, and one change at a time keeps this one measurable.
- The in-stock fraction and COGS_7 instrumentation is a system request, not a design edit. It stays open (spec open question 1). Until it exists, in-stock is not charted.
- Goal, reserve (€300), exemplar and aims are unchanged. The core selling loop is not gated by any new step: the rule limits order size, it does not stop ordering.

**PIP definition.** The spec's definition (exemplar cash ÷ swarm cash, same day) is the one used. The D-P-004 figure of 17.0 was a gain ÷ gain basis and is not used. Day-29 gap on the spec's basis: 3,170.4 ÷ 1,816.75 = 1.75. The chart's 5.3 is a gain ÷ gain ratio ((3,170.4 − 1,500) ÷ (1,816.75 − 1,500) = 5.3), a different basis. The system should chart one basis only, the spec's; this is a measurement request, not a design change.

**Prediction.** slots_selling should shift up: its average over days 36–45 is at or above its centre of 12.35 (v1 limits, unchanged), and revenue returns to at least its centre of 93.77 by day 45. Confidence: moderate for slots_selling, low for revenue (it also depends on demand and season).

**Cost.** Larger, earlier orders tie up more cash in stock (cash drawdown bounded by the €300 reserve rule) and may leave more slots to clear under C7 (21-day rule). Expected to be small against the lost sales.

**Revert if.** Any machine is empty for 2 or more days on stock loaded after day 29 (log); cash falls below €300 on any day; or slots_selling sits below centre for 8 periods in a row by day 45 with the new rule followed (log shows cover figures).

**Next redesign.** Not before day 49. Candidate: C6 refund rule (item price first, unless evidence supports more), with shelf-life added to records (D-P-004). Measure: refunds_7.
