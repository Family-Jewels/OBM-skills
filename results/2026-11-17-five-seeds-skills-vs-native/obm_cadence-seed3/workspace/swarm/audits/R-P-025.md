Change (one, smallest, in the rule, not the tree): `rules/operator.md` v3 -> v4.

2. Cap the active restock rotation at about 16 slots, the number the day's trips can keep full. No slot is added while the rotation is full. Slots leave the rotation only when they sell out or reach the 21-day rule, never cleared early.
