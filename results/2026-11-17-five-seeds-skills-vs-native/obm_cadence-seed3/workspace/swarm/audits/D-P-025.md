- The operator cut machine reads to one per restock day on day 47 ("tool spend is the binding budget"). Trips go out without a current view of which slots are empty, so trips go to full slots and missing SKUs.
- Each empty visit costs a 45-minute trip and returns nothing. No measure in the tree prices that cost. The stock-out is visible only in operator counts, which the tree does not chart.
- Relief is brief: a large order (€500–€760, days 100–126) lands, the machines refill, and they empty again in about four days. Cash rises in the meantime, which keeps the top measure looking routine.

- **Resources (primary):** restock capacity is about 8–10 loads a day against about 42 assigned slots. Loads hold about 10 units. Trip planning runs on a read cut for cost, and depot supply by SKU is unrecorded.
- **Incentives (secondary):** no consequence in the tree for empty selling slots, and empty trips cost nothing visible. Complaint refunds paid at the full claim (C6, deferred since day 109) drain cash but do not explain the slot count.
- **Not the cause:** direction (cash is up and the tree is sound); the operator's honesty (its counts are labelled and match the log); the price (revenue_per_slot is up).
