# Audit A-001 (day 36, full audit, system-invoked on a wrong-direction signal)

Trigger: `charts.md` signal, slots_selling beyond its lower limit (latest 0, limit 0.17). Scope read: charts, metrics_system, spec v2, index, log (days 1–35), rules/operator.md v2, R-P-004, D-P-004, P-005, P-006, D-001 queue, and the swarm file tree.

## Verdict on the outcome

Cash is €1,643 on day 36 (from €1,500). Spec-basis gap to exemplar is 2.60 (4,266.64 ÷ 1,643.25). Two measures are beyond their lower limits since R-P-004 (adopted day 33): slots_selling (0) and cash_gain_7 (−117.7). Revenue and units are at 0 but inside their limits. Capacity is not in use: 0 of 72 slots selling on day 36, after roughly 24 non-empty slots on day 26 and 60+ in the day 30 stock peak. The cash signal is partly a timing effect of the cover order (see F4). The slot signal is a real stock-out.

## Findings

**F1. Wrong direction, pinpointed (direction).** slots_selling went 11 → 7 → 2 → 0 over days 33–36, below its centre of 12.35 on four points in a row. The log shows the cause: stock loaded on day 31 (PO-1014) ran out by day 34, and the cover orders due days 35 and 37 had not arrived by day 36. The four post-redesign points average 5.0 against a centre of 12.35.

**F2. R-P-004's own revert test is close to met.** Its revert clauses are: (a) a machine empty 2+ days on stock loaded after day 29; (b) cash below €300 (not met); (c) slots_selling below centre for 8 periods in a row by day 45 with the rule followed. By the log's own words (day 34 "nearly empty", day 35 "storage empty", day 36 slots_selling 0), (a) appears met. The log has no per-machine empty-day count, so this is unconfirmed. Clause (c) is at 4 of 8 now. The prediction for days 36–45 is not yet due, so no predicted shift is a finding yet; the first day of the window is 0.

**F3. The cover rule was not applied as written (orchestrator).** R-P-004 allows less cover only if the €300 reserve cannot fund it. Cash on day 33 was €2,336. The crisps order was 100 units, about 8 days, against the rule's "next delivery plus longest lead" (about 15 days, about 180 units at the 12/day the operator logged). The log's reason was the 120-unit quote price, which the rule does not allow as a reason.

**F4. The lead-time input is one day short, and the outcome measure books stock as a loss (design).**
- The index's realised lead times use the due date, not arrival. Arrivals are logged the day after due every time. Order to arrival by the log's own dates: PO-1010 placed day 17, in depot day 29 = **12 days** (index: 11); PO-1009 placed day 14, in depot day 26 = **12 days** (index: 11); PO-1001 placed day 1, in depot day 12 = **11 days** (index: 10). The spec defines lead time as order to arrival. The cover rule therefore under-covers by a day.
- cash_gain_7 is ledger cash, and COGS_7 is booked at order time. The day-33 cover orders (€753.85) therefore read as a loss in the week they were bought. The spec's chart cannot separate stock investment from lost money. The signal is partly built into the measure.
- cash_gain_7 does not reconcile on the face of the records: ledger cash day 36 − day 29 = 1,643.25 − 1,816.75 = −173.5, against the chart's −117.7. Card-settlement timing (open receipts €71.60 on day 26) may explain it. The system has not stated its formula.

**F5. The tree's first levers are not charted.** The spec says to push first on slots_selling, in-stock fraction and lead time. Only slots_selling is charted. In-stock fraction, COGS_7 and lead time have no system measure. The operator has steered by a manual empty-slot count since day 9. The instrumentation request (spec open question 1) appears in pulses P-001, P-005 and P-006 and in the log, with no system response in 36 days.

**F6. PIP on the wrong basis, and an exemplar value with no stated source.** The chart's gap (19.3 on day 36) uses gain ÷ gain. The spec's basis is cash ÷ cash (2.60). R-P-004 asked for one basis; the day-36 chart still shows gain. The chart's exemplar values also do not follow the spec. The spec gives day 30 = 3,228 and day 365 = 61,219, and says no interim path is given. The records then carry day 26 ≈ 2,997.6, day 31 = 3,401 (P-006), and day 36 = 4,266.64. Those imply about 5%/day, against the spec's stated 0.88%/day. The source of these values must be shown. The operator must not look for it.

**F7. Redesign effect on the goal and aims.** R-P-004 did not change the goal, reserve, exemplar use or aims. Good. Its added measure is charted only as a log table.

**F8. Earlier findings, acted on or not.**
- D-P-004 items: lead-time receipts (done, but see F4); in-stock and COGS instrumentation (not done); reconcile slots_selling with the log count (explained in the log, day 22 note); shelf-life records (not done); C6 refund rule (deferred to day 49 by design).
- R-P-004's chart basis request (F6): not done.

The leak is in C6 ("full amount"), a spec rule, and is deferred to day 49. Its cost is small against the stock-out, but it is a known leak with no decision date before day 49.

- Spec Parked list: "leaving a location unstocked for more than 2 days" needs a decision request. Days 34–36 look like that, and `queue/decisions.md` has none. Whether it was a choice or a stock-out is the operator's to state.

**F10. Checks that passed.** I cross-checked the logged opening cash for days 6–9, 14, 16, 17, 22, 23, 26, 29, 30, 31, 33–35 and sales for days 5, 8, 14, 29, 31, against `metrics_system.md`. All match, allowing for the one-day label offset. The R-P-004 "since" means (revenue 32.9, units 16.25, revenue_per_slot 4.24, slots 5.0) reproduce from the metrics. No passed-work misreport found. Probes not run: no probe register exists and seeded errors would touch the live business. Spend: no spend records in the swarm files, so cost per result is unverified.

## Actions for the orchestrator (keep restocking throughout; none of these pauses core work)

1. **Fix the lead-time input before the next order.** Recompute realised lead times from arrival dates (zuidas 12, bakker 12). Size cover to the next delivery plus 12 days, and write the figure in the log. Do not trade cover for unit price unless the rule is changed first. At each close, log the empty slots per machine, so revert clause (a) can be answered.
2. **Put the instrumentation request into `queue/decisions.md` as a decision for the owner.** Default: operator keeps the per-machine empty-slot count, labelled as a proxy and not as in-stock fraction. Do not repeat the request in pulses.
3. **Stop quoting gain-basis PIP.** Quote the spec's cash ratio (2.60 on day 36) and ask the system in the same decision entry for the source of the day-31 and day-36 exemplar values and the cash_gain_7 formula.
