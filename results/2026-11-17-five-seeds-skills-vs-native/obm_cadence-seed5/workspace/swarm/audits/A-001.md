## Findings

**F1. Wrong-direction signal, and a predicted shift that did not appear (direction).**
The only chart signal is cash_gain_7 at -30.5, beyond the lower limit. It follows an improvement run (above centre from day 21 to day 35, 15 points). P-007 described that run as "8 in a row above centre since day 8", which is inaccurate; the rule was met. The pivots from P-006 and P-007 predicted slots_selling and revenue_per_slot would rise. slots_selling fell from 13 to 2 in the same period. The prediction did not appear.

**F2. The top row of the driver tree does not add up.**
`spec.md` line 11 defines the final bank balance as "1,500 + sum of cash_gain_7 over the run". cash_gain_7 is a 7-day window. Summing it every day counts each day's cash seven times. The spec's own done check (line 49) uses daily cash gains. The row needs correcting.

**F3. cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as losses (direction and measure).**
The tree gives cash_gain_7 = revenue_last_7 − goods cost (7d) − fee − marketing, with no split between goods bought and goods sold. Purchases on days 35 and 36 were PO-1013 (80.80) and PO-1014 (147.10), which I recomputed from the unit costs, total 227.90. Revenue_last_7 (285.6) minus those purchases minus the 84 fee gives about −26, close to the chart's −30.5. Most of the day-40 signal is restock cash, not lost sales. The real problem is the empty machines (F4), which the chart does not show. The designer should define cost of goods sold separately from purchases to inventory.

**F4. The cause of the decline is missing from the tree: stock cover and mix (direction).**
Energy was at zero everywhere from day 35 to day 39 ("energy depot empty", log day 35; "Machines thin", day 36). Energy and crisps were the top sellers (log days 25, 30). PO-1011 and PO-1012 (days 29–30) contained no energy and no crisps. Energy and crisps were only ordered again on days 26 and 35. The operator's cover rule (3–4 days, adopted day 37) appears in no spec measure. Stock-out slot-days are a driver marked "yes" in the spec, but no chart carries them, and no measure of days of cover exists. The zuidas lead time is 3 days.

**F5. Chart PIP and spec PIP disagree; the exemplar series is unsourced.**
The chart's exemplar values are 4,093.54 on day 35 and 4,959.07 on day 40. The spec's pace line gives about 3,370 and 3,520 for those days. The spec says the exemplar is used only as a pace line. P-006 asked the designer to reconcile this on day 30. The designer's note was received (log, day 28). No R- file exists, and the chart still uses gains at day 40. This request is not acted on.

## Diagnosis

Pinpointed problem for the diagnostician: the network ran out of its top sellers (energy, crisps, cola) on days 32 to 39. Stock cover was under 3 days, against a 3-day lead time, and the order mix did not follow sell-through. The weekly order budget and the 45-minute fill cost both limit how fast the network can be restocked. The chart signal (cash_gain_7) also counts restock cash as a loss, so it overstates the decline. The designer's inputs are F2, F3, F5 and F7.
