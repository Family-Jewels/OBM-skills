## What maintains it

- **Stock bought fell in step with sales.** Orders since day 177 that I could read are EUR 85–165 each (PO-1040 paid 152.90; PO-1043 about 84). Orders PO-1028, PO-1030 and PO-1033 (days 123–147) were EUR 390–580. PO-1039, PO-1040, PO-1042 and PO-1043 together are about 600 units over the 14 days to day 196, about 43 a day, roughly equal to sales. So the network is not buying much less than it sells; it holds too little on hand, because orders arrive in small lots and each slot is loaded to about half its cap.
- **Cash does not limit orders.** Cash is about 7,300, about 40 times the EUR 168 reserve. The day-193 order was cut to about 84 EUR anyway, under the EUR 75 minimum and the one-order-per-week budget. The records show the sizing cap is the budget and cover rule, not cash.
- **The top row penalises buying.** cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as cost (spec row 12; A-004 F9). An order shows as a loss for 7 days in the measure the chart reads as wrong direction. The operator's log says so for days 160–167 (A-004 F2).
- **The cover floor is not firing.** Orders on day 193 were placed with depot cover at zero. Rule 11 (8-day floor, machine and in-transit stock) is on disk, so either the daily check is not run or it does not reach the order decision.
- **Fill routing leaves capacity idle.** Rule 2 (v5, units lost per hour empty) skipped trips when the depot held stock too thin to be worth a trip (day 164: "no fill today", with choco 26 and crisps 20 in depot and 14+ slots empty). Fills of 10 units into 18-cap slots run out within a day or two.
- **Records disagree on which rule is in force.** `index.md` says rule 2 went back to v4 on day 186 (decisions.md agrees). `rules/operator.md` on disk still reads version 5 with the v5 rule 2, and spec.md is v5. The operator may be running a different rule from the one the swarm is held to.

## Cause

- **Information:** the top row mis-states the goal (1,500 + sum of 7-day differences, spec line 11; A-001 F2, still unfixed) and counts stock purchases as losses. Stock-out slot-days are not charted (requested R-002, day 134). The rule in force is ambiguous (above).
- **Resources:** the depot cannot keep the machines full. Orders are small, one network order per week in practice against a per-location budget (D-005/D-007 unresolved), lead 3–5 days, and fill trips are 4–8 a day for 30 slots. Time used is not measured (the time log has no entries after day 5).
- **Incentives:** buying lowers the top measure at once and its benefit arrives 3–5 days later, so the signal favours small or late orders. Fills are ranked by value per trip but not rewarded for loading to cap. Nothing rewards a full slot.
- **Knowledge / model tier:** not the cause. The operator names the stock-out and the fill limit in its own log, and the counts are consistent with the ledger.
