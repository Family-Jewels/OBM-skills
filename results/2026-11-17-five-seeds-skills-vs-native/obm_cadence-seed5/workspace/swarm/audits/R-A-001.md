# Redesign R-A-001 (day 66, from diagnosis D-A-001)

Designer, REDESIGN mode. Read: `audits/D-A-001.md`, `charts.md`, `spec.md` v1, `queue/decisions.md` (D-005, D-006, D-007). First redesign; the 20-day spacing rule is met.

## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The top measure (final balance) and the drivers (slots_selling, revenue, units) are the right ones. Revenue is being lost to empty slots, which the tree already names as the highest-leverage driver. The tree itself is sound; the problem is the rule that feeds it.

## 2. Cause and change

**Cause (design, not effort):** operator rule 4 buys at the cheapest unit cost on the offer calendar, with no measure of stock cover against the 3–5 day lead time. Orders on days 42, 46, 52 and 59 arrived after stock had already run out (stock-outs on days 45–46, 49–50, 53–57, 61). The operator wrote the cover lesson four times; no rule carried it.

**Change (one, driver: stock-out slot-days / slots_selling):**
- `spec.md` v2 Budgets: the one-order-per-location-per-week budget stays. Each order is sized to about 11 days of forecast sales at that location (7 days to the next order plus lead time). Cover is the constraint; cheapest unit cost chooses among offers that meet it. This also resolves D-005 and D-007: the reading is per location.
- `spec.md` v2 Cadence: the order row records days of cover at order time and at expected arrival.
- `rules/operator.md` v2, rule 4: the same change, as the operator's rule.

Previous versions: `history/spec.v1.md`, `history/operator.v1.md`.

Not changed here (one redesign at a time): the cash_gain_7 COGS split, the exemplar and PIP definition, reputation and complaint payment, and fill capacity. These go to R-A-002 if the prediction holds or fails.

## 3. Prediction (charts test it)

By **day 80**:
- stock-out slot-days fall to zero on most days in days 67–80 (from 7 of 22 days with fewer than 4 slots selling in days 40–61);
- slots_selling rises above its centre (7.15 on the chart) and its latest 7-day average is above 10;
- revenue_last_7 is above 472 (the level a month earlier).

If stock-out slot-days stay above 1 per week on days 67–80, the prediction fails and the change is not the cause.
