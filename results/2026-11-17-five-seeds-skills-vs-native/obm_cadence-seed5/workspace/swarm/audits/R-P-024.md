# Redesign R-P-024 (day 206)

Trigger: diagnosis D-P-024 (day 198) and pulse P-024: revenue, revenue_per_slot and units below their post-R-P-021 limits since day 177; slots_selling flat; units per selling slot 5.1 → 3.6.

## Is the direction right?
Partly. Selling more through the slots already stocked is right, and the goal is unchanged: bank balance on day 365, with the same exemplar pace line and the same reserve. But the push on fill trips (spec v5, R-P-021) aimed at a symptom. D-P-024 shows the binding limit is stock on hand: fills load about 10 of 18 units, and the depot is empty for the products that sell. The top row also penalises the purchases that create stock, because cash_gain_7 counts goods bought as cost for seven days. So the tree pushed the operator to hold less stock. That is a design fault, not a lack of effort.

## Change (one redesign; spec v5 → v6, operator rule file v5 → v6)
1. **Push driver:** days of cover per location (band 8–11) and stock-out slot-days now lead; fill throughput second, with depot units logged beside the empty count.
2. **Top-row fault:** the final-balance row now reads 1,500 + sum of daily cash change (cash_gain_7 is a rolling 7-day gain and summing it overcounts; A-001 F2). Net worth gain (cash change + stock at cost) is added as the measure for ordering decisions. Final cash is still the outcome and is still the only check on the goal.
3. **Unsold inventory:** no longer "lower is better"; bounded by cover band and the 25%-of-cash cap (M2).
4. **Order sizing:** no per-order EUR budget applies; size comes from cover and the reserve (rule 5). This settles the cause of the day-193 cut.
5. **Charts:** net worth gain, days of cover (lowest product per location), stock-out slot-days and units per selling slot are charted from day 207. v5 limits are kept for comparison.
6. **Rule 2:** the operator's on-disk text (v5) is replaced by v4 wording, which the index and decisions.md say is in force since day 186, plus a fill-depth clause (load toward cap while depot stock allows) and a 09:00 log of depot units beside the empty count. Previous texts are in `history/spec.v5.md` and `history/operator.v5.md`.

Not changed: rules 4, 5 and 11 (cover, reserve, floor); the goal, exemplar, reserve and marketing cap.

## Prediction (the charts should show it by day 226, twenty periods after the change)
