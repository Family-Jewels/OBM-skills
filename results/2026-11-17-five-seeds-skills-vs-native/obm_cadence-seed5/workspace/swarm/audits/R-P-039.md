# Redesign R-P-039 (day 314)

**Trigger:** D-P-039 (day 310): units per selling slot down about 20% since day 287 (about 4.5 against about 5.6); slots_selling flat; the depot holds no sellable stock on fill mornings. Late deliveries land the day after due, and the cover count treats in-transit units as stock until they arrive.

**Direction:** right. Stock on hand is still the binding limit, and slots_selling (via units per selling slot) is still the highest-leverage driver. No change to the goal, the exemplar, the reserve (EUR 168), the unpaid-fee limit, the push order, or the 8-day floor.

**Cause addressed:** information. The cover measure counts a late order as cover after its due date has passed, so the 09:00 check does not fire while the slots run dry. The design, not the operator's effort, produces this.

## Change (smallest)

1. **Spec v8 → v9** (`swarm/spec.md`; previous at `swarm/history/spec.v8.md`): in-transit units count toward days of cover only while their due date is after today. An in-transit order due today or earlier and not yet in the depot at 09:00 counts as zero. Changed in the driver-tree row for days of cover and in Budgets (cover line).
2. **Operator brief v8 → v9** (`swarm/rules/operator.md`; previous at `swarm/history/operator.v8.md`): rule 11 carries the same definition, so the operator reads cover the way the spec does.

Not changed, and why:
- The 8-day floor and the 11-day order-up-to level: changing them now would confound the test. Revisit on the next pulse with arrival data.
- Fill throughput (6–9 trips a day): a resource limit, not the cause here; the cover fix comes first, and fill routing is read after it.
- Charting stock-out slot-days and units per selling slot with their own limits: a useful instrument, but a separate change; requested again below.
- Cash_gain_7 and the top row: the tree already says read net worth gain for orders. No change to the outcome measure.

## Prediction

- **Mechanism (tree: priced slots empty before the morning reload):** falls to **10 of 30 or fewer on every 09:00 count from day 318 to day 330**. Direction: down.
- **Mechanism (tree: days of cover, per location):** every late order (due date passed, not in depot) shows cover below 8 at 09:00 on that day and a same-day order logged (rule 11). Count of such days with no order logged: zero.
