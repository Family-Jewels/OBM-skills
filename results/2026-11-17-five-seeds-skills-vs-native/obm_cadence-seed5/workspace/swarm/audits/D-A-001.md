## Pinpointed problem

The network runs out of its sellers in a repeating cycle: stock-outs on days 45–46, 49–50, 53–57 and 61, each about 3–5 days long. Revenue falls to 2–10 units a day while cash sits unused.

## What maintains it

- Orders are triggered by the offer calendar: weekly on the validity date (spec line 97). Orders on days 42, 46, 52 and 59 arrive 3–5 days later, after stock is already gone (day 52: "machines almost fully empty"; stock-out days 53–56).
- Rule 4 (cheapest unit cost above reserve) buys discount lines in bulk. No rule or measure asks for days of cover.
- Cash_gain_7 counts purchases as losses, so the week of restocking looks like decline; the lost sales of the empty week are not charted.
- The operator recorded the cover lesson on days 32, 36, 52 and 56. It was never built into a rule, check or measure, so it did not change behaviour.

## Cause

- **Information:** the spec's cadence guarantees a gap (weekly order, 3–5 day lead). No days-of-cover measure exists, and the "stock-out slot-days" driver marked "yes" has no chart. The exemplar series on the charts (8,594 at day 61) does not match the spec pace line (≈4,236), and PIP is defined two ways (gains vs cash).
- **Resources:** fill capacity is about 6–8 fills a day (log day 51) against about 22 slots needing a fill every ~3 days. The time log stops at day 5, so "time used" cannot be checked.
- **Incentives:** the operator's twice-weekly order rule (day 37) conflicts with the spec's one order per location per week (spec line 88). A-001 F6 asked for this to be parked; no entry exists. Reputation fell to 0.84 by day 60 and is not in the tree. On day 60 four unverified complaints (46.62) were paid in full to protect it, which rewards claims.
- **Knowledge / model tier:** not the main cause. The operator states the right lesson repeatedly.

## Records

No pulses after P-007 (day 35) and no full audit at day 50 exist in the workspace. Whether they were not invoked or not written cannot be told from the records; the system's invocation log would settle it.

## What the design would need to change (not written here)

- Add days-of-cover, or stock-out slot-days, to the tree and charts, with its own limits.
- Make the order trigger stock cover against lead time, not the offer calendar; resolve the cadence against the one-order budget.
- Separate cost of goods sold from purchases in cash_gain_7.
- Settle one exemplar series and one PIP definition.
- Add reputation as a driver, with the complaint rule.
- Match fill capacity to the number of slots; restore the time log.
