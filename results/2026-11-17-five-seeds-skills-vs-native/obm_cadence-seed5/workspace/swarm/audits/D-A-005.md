## Pinpointed problem

Machines and depot run to zero for the products that sell, between orders. Orders are placed on the offer's last validity day, at zero cover, and each order is small. Cover at order: crisps and dropjes 0 (day 224); gummy and cola 0 (day 231); choco and crisps 0 (day 236); cola 0 and energy about 0.2 days (day 239). Empty priced slots at 09:00 run 17 to 26 of 30. Each order is about 190 units, about 4 to 5 days of network sales (about 43 a day), against the 11-day target (about 470 units).

## What maintains it

- **Timing is calendar-driven.** The weekly `check_offers` on the validity date triggers the order. The cover floor (8 days, spec Budgets, rule 11) fired only on day 239. Daily cover was logged on 4 of 26 days.
- **Size is not set by cover.** The 11-day sizing and the one-order-per-week reading are still open (D-005, D-007, A-004 F7). The records give no reason for each order's size. Nothing shows a per-location cap or an offer-quantity limit.
- **What shows up in the measure.** Stock bought is a cash cost for 7 days in cash_gain_7, which the operator still reads. The day-242 spike is the delivery day, so stock on hand is what drives the sales the measure rewards. The cost of an empty slot shows up later, as lost sales.
- **The chart that would show it is missing.** Days of cover, stock-out slot-days and units per selling slot were promised from day 207 (spec cadence; R-P-024 item 5). None is on `charts.md`. The operator has no chart on the binding limit.

## Cause

- **Information:** the order-sizing rule (11 days) and the one-order-per-week reading are unresolved. The cover floor is not tied to the order decision. The stock measures the tree names as push-now are not charted.
- **Resources:** lead time is 3 to 5 days, and one delivery ran 11 days (PO-1047). The 8-day floor does not cover that. Time used is not measured (time log: days 1 to 5 only).
- **Incentives:** the weekly offer date and the cash reserve (never close: about 8,000 against 168) reward an order placed on time, not an order placed on cover. Nothing rewards a full slot.
- **Not the cause:** cash (8,000 against a reserve of 168); the 25% stock cap (about 400 of stock against a cap near 2,000, an estimate); the model tier.

## Evidence that would settle it

- The order rationale for PO-1047 to PO-1053: the quantity chosen, the cap it hit, and the cover computed.
- The `check_offers` quantity limits at the two order sizes the spec asks for.
- Daily cover readings for days 218 to 242 (the cover check ran on few days).

## What the design would need to change (not written here)

- Trigger orders by days of cover at that day, not by the offer date. Measure: days of cover at order, and stock-out slot-days.
- Settle the order size: 11 days of cover, per location or per network, with the reason written each time.
- Chart days of cover, stock-out slot-days and units per selling slot with limits, as promised from day 207.
- Put the stock term inside the units row of the tree.
- Name the owner and date for the R-P-024 revert ruling. Its revert line is met.
