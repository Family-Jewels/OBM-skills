# Redesign R-A-005 (day 246)

**Trigger:** D-A-005 (day 242, from full audit A-005): units per selling slot fell from 5.1 to 3.4 since day 217; empty priced slots at 09:00 ran 17–26 of 30; orders were placed on the offer validity date at zero cover, and each order was about 4–5 days of sales.

**Change:** spec v6 → v7 (`swarm/spec.md`; previous at `swarm/history/spec.v6.md`). Operator brief v6 → v7 (`swarm/rules/operator.md`; previous at `swarm/history/operator.v6.md`). No change to goal, exemplar, reserve (EUR 168), unpaid-fee limit, marketing cap or the push order in the tree.

## Direction

The direction is right. Stock on hand is the binding limit (D-P-024), and slots_selling is the highest-leverage driver. The tree's push order holds. The revert line in R-P-024 (units per selling slot below 4.0) was met, which is a signal the implementation failed, not the push order. R-P-024 is not reverted. The owner of that ruling is this designer; the ruling is: keep the stock push, change the order rule.

## Cause addressed

Information and incentive, per D-A-005: the order trigger was the offer date, the size was never set by cover (the 11-day sizing was open), and the cover check ran on 4 of 26 days. The cause is in the rules, not in the operator's effort.

## Changes

1. **Trigger:** orders fire on days of cover below 8 at the daily 09:00 check, for every location. The weekly offer date no longer triggers orders; it is for price comparison (spec Budgets; operator rules 4, 11).
2. **Size:** quantity = (11 − days of cover) × forecast daily sales, rounded up to the offer's unit size. If the offer caps it, order the cap and log it. This settles the sizing that was open since v1 (spec Open questions now carries the cap question, answered by the order log).
3. **Visibility:** cover per location is logged at 09:00 each day (operator rule 1), beside the empty-slot count.
4. **Tree:** the units row carries the stock term; the days-of-cover row names the order trigger.

Not changed, and why: the 8-day floor stays. PO-1047 (11 days) shows the floor does not cover every lead time; that is logged as known exposure in the spec, to be decided on the next pulse with lead-time data, not guessed now.

## Prediction

- **Primary:** units per selling slot (units ÷ slots_selling, 7-period average) rises from 3.4 to **at least 4.5 by day 262**. Direction: up.
- **Secondary:** empty priced slots at 09:00 fall from 17–26 of 30 to **10 of 30 or fewer by day 262**. Direction: down.
- **Mechanism check:** every order from day 247 shows cover below 8 at order time, the quantity computed, and cover at expected arrival of 8 or more.

The day-226 prediction in R-P-024 (4.5 by day 226) was missed; this one is set 16 days later so the order cycle can complete at least two full turns.
