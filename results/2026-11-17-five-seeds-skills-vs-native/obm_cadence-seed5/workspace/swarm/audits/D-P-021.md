## Pinpointed problem

Priced slots stay empty for much of each stock cycle, and this has continued through the cover fix. Since day 144 the network still runs 14–20 of 30 priced slots at zero before the morning reload.

## What maintains it

- **Fills are the binding limit.** The operator logs 6–9 fill trips a day (45 min each) against 22–30 active slots. Snack slots (18-unit cap) sell out in about 2 days. Each empty slot costs its sales until the next trip.
- **Stock in the depot does not reach the machines.** Day 116: depot held nuts 53, choco 32, gummy 48, energy 51; snack machines were at zero within 1–2 days of refill. (Day 131–134 depot was 0–13 per product, so timing was also the cause then; the post-144 depot levels are not in the records I read, which is a gap.)
- **Ordering is rewarded for waiting.** Cover-floor orders fired late (days 117, 123, 127) after gaps had opened; purchases lower cash_gain_7 at once, so the signal punishes the earlier order.
- **Demand shared by duplicate lines** (operator note, day 16) means more trips are needed per unit sold.

## Cause

- **Fill throughput (primary now):** trips per day cannot cover the slots. R-A-003 fixed order timing (its cause, per D-A-003), and the timing fix did not move slots_selling. The co-cause D-A-003 parked (fill throughput) is what remains. Stock exists; trips do not.
- **Information:** the spec's "push now" driver for stock-outs has no chart or system column. The operator's zero counts are the only evidence. The daily time log exists only as minutes per action, not in the system records.
- **Incentives:** full fill sequence is not rewarded per trip beyond per-slot revenue; the cover measure still counts depot stock first (R-A-003 changed this, outcome not yet visible).
- **Knowledge / model tier:** not the cause. The operator names the throughput limit in its own log.

## Evidence that would settle it

Daily, 14 days: trips made, slots filled per trip, zero-stock slots at 09:00 and at close, depot units per product. If zero counts stay at 14+ with depot stock above 20 units for most products, the cause is throughput. If depot is empty for most products when slots are empty, it is order timing and R-A-003 was incomplete.

## What the design would need to change (not written here)

- Chart stock-out slot-days with its own limits in `metrics_system.md`, so the operator's count is not the only evidence.
- Decide whether the fill plan or the slot count changes: match trips to slots, or cut slots that sell slowest (driver: slots_selling).
- Apply or revert R-A-003 explicitly: its revert rule is met.
- Split cash_gain_7 into purchases, cost of goods sold and receipts in transit; settle one PIP.
