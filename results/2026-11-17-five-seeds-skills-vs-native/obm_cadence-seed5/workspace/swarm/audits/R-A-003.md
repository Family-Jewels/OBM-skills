# Redesign R-A-003 (day 141, from diagnosis D-A-003)

## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is 4,628 on day 141 against a pace line of about 8,400; net growth since day 111 is about 1.3% a day against the spec's 0.88%; the PIP is 6.7 against the exemplar. The top measure is moving the right way. The drivers chosen (slots_selling first, then cash_gain_7 and revenue) are the ones that move it. The tree is right. The problem is a design gap inside the top driver, not a wrong direction.

## 2. What is causing it
The cover trigger (rule 11, spec v3 Budgets) fires at 6 days of depot-only stock. Lead time is 3–5 days and orders are weekly, so the trigger fires after the gap has opened (D-A-003: cover 0.2 days for choco and 0 for energy and crisps when PO-1029/1030 were placed). Machine and in-transit stock are not counted, and the rule does not say per location or network.

## 3. The change (smallest that addresses the cause)
- Cover is defined per location: (depot + machine stock of that product at that location + in transit) ÷ forecast daily sales there.
- The cover floor is 8 days, not 6, so an order placed at the floor arrives with at least 3 days of stock left.
- Rule 11 and the Budgets, Cadence rows are updated to match. Weekly ordering, the reserve, and the marketing cap are unchanged.

Not changed in this redesign, on purpose: fill throughput (the co-cause, which needs the daily time log first), the cash_gain_7 split (the chart is the system's to change), and the exemplar/PIP series (A-003 F2). These are parked below.

## 4. Prediction
- **slots_selling** (chart, better: up) should shift up: at least 8 points in a row above its centre, or a point above the upper limit, by **day 161**. Its mean since day 141 should move from about 13 toward the own best of 16.9.
- **Stock-out slot-days** (not yet charted; requested below) should fall over the same window.
- **revenue_per_slot** should not move much. A shift there would mean the fix is only adding units, not selling them.

## 5. Cost and revert
- Cost: more orders, so more cash sits in stock. cash_gain_7 will dip in order weeks; those dips are purchases, not losses, and must not be read as failure. The reserve (EUR 168) still applies on every order.
- Revert to spec v3 and rule v3 (in `history/`) if: slots_selling has not shifted up by day 161, or any day's cash falls below EUR 168 because of an order placed under the new floor.
