## What changed

2. Phase 1: both ledgers are also kept as files (swarm/ledgers/slot-ledger.md, swarm/ledgers/cash-ledger.md). Missing day records are marked "missing", not inferred.
3. Phase 3: restock is sized on 14-day average units per slot, not the peak week. Weekly stock spend is capped at average weekly revenue over 14 days, unless the cash buffer is met and the spend is explained.
4. Phase 3: each 7-day review lists zero-sale slots and each one is repriced, swapped, or recorded as deliberately empty. No new slots until slots_selling shows a signal.
5. Review loop: restock and spend decisions use the cash-ledger and 14-day averages only.

## Why
