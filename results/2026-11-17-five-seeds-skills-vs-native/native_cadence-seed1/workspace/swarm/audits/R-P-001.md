# Revision R-P-001 (day 11 of 365)

Responds to: D-P-001 (day 9). Plan changed from v1 to v2.
Old version: swarm/history/plan-v1.md. Current: swarm/plan.md.

## What changed
1. Principle 3: stocking and pricing are one action. A slot counts as stocked only when get_machine_inventory shows a product and a price.
2. Phase 1: added a hard gate. All six machines are checked, and every slot is either priced or listed as deliberately empty, before the first day ends.
3. Phase 1: added two notes, "slot-ledger" (product, price, dates) and "cash-ledger" (every order, fee, restock and refund), so cash changes can be explained.
4. Phase 2: a daily inventory check on all six machines is mandatory until every stocked slot has sold in a week.
5. Review loop: act on charts only on a signal. Movement inside the limits is not a reason to change prices or stock.

## Why
D-P-001 found that slots were not stocked and priced until about day 8, so the day 1 to 2 stock purchase (EUR 124.1) produced no sales for a week. The gate and ledgers make that visible the day it happens.

## Not changed
Benchmark targets, spending limits, marketing rules and the supplier approach are unchanged. D-P-001 did not give evidence against them.

## Open
D-P-001 still needs the day 1 to 9 inventory, price and delivery history to confirm the cause. This revision does not depend on that confirmation.
