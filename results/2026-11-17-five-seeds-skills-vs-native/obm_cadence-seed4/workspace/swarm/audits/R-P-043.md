## 1. Is the direction right?
Mostly yes. Cash is the top measure, and the drivers it runs through (fills, revenue per visit, sold-out slots, storage per sold-out slot, lead time) are the ones that move it. The tree needs no change. What is wrong is the design of two things the drivers depend on:
- **The cover rule is short.** Check 7 and rules 9 and 13 use quoted lead time plus one (5 + 1 = 6 days). Every order since PO-1037 has landed 6 days after ordering, one day later than that (A-006). Each order therefore runs out a day before the next lands. This is an information cause: the rule states a figure the records do not support.
- **The stock-out that costs most is not on any chart or check.** Sold-out selling slot-days are a tree row marked "yes, now", but the system does not chart them, and no check counts them. A zero-storage stock-out is free on every check. This is an incentive and measurement cause.

Not the designer's to decide, and not changed here:
- **The 8-hour day and clock stops (D-010).** Whether the day is a business rule is the human's answer. The designer does not extend or shorten it. The diagnosis's suggestion to name the day a designer budget decision is declined: the spec already says the designer does not extend it, and the goal and any budget the human owns stay with the human.
- **The cash_gain_7 chart does not reconcile with the ledger** (chart 317 to 370; ledger 600). That is a system measurement fault. It is requested below, and no judgement on the top chart is drawn from it here.

## 2. Change (one design change: the cover rule uses the observed lead time)
- **spec.md v8 → v9** (old: `swarm/history/spec.v8.md`):
  - Driver tree, lead-time row: effective lead time = the observed figure per supplier, not quoted + 1. Push on: yes, now.
  - Done means 7: units ordered ≥ (observed effective lead time + 1) × sell-through. At present 6 + 1 = 7 days, not 6.
  - Checks table, order row: same change.
  - Open questions: one line naming the sold-out gap and this record.
- **rules/operator.md v7 → v8** (old: `swarm/history/operator.v7.md`):
  - Rule 9: effective lead time is the observed days from order to landing per supplier (6 days now).
  - Rule 13: cover is effective lead time + 1 (7 days now).
- **Not changed, on purpose:** the goal, the exemplar, the pace table, checks 1 to 6, the reserve, the weekly cap, the 8-hour day, the fill rules, the revenue-per-visit driver, the cadence, the milestones, the decision rights.

## 3. Why this change
It addresses the supply cause in D-P-043 section 4 at its source: the rule states a lead time the observed landings contradict, so every order is short by one day. Check 7 already exists and already reads the right ground truth (runs.jsonl). Only its figure is wrong. It is the smallest change that corrects the criterion without setting a new limit or touching the clock. It stops no core work: reads and price changes still come first, and orders still pass the weekly cap and the reserve. It does not lower the goal or any aim.

The change does not address the clock-stop stock-outs (sold-out with storage stock left open). Those are the second half of the diagnosis and depend on D-010. They are not fixed here.
