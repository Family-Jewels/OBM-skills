## 2. Pinpoint

The swarm makes 6–8 fills a day, and its reads take about 25 minutes per location. Storage holds the stock while slots sit empty: on day 205, 9 selling slots were at zero while storage held cola 120, crisps 70, oat 65, sparkling 60. Overstock and stock-outs coexist on the same day. Supply quantity is not the constraint. Visit capacity is.

Units per day (~57) match fills per day × units per fill, and cash_gain_7 (~61/day) is in the same range as margin minus the €12/day fee. The revenue ceiling is set by how many machine visits fit in the day.

## 3. What maintains it

- The 09:30 read pivot has been missed on days 211, 220 and 225. The operator's own log says three reads take about 75 minutes. The auditor set the pivot without checking that time. Nothing charges for a late read, so it repeats.
- Every restock is a visible, immediate cost on cash_gain_7. The return arrives 5 days later and then sells through. The charted cash signal therefore pushes against buying stock, and the sold-out measures reward filling slots.
- Revenue-trend failure (check 4) is not charted, so no signal reaches the operator or the auditor.

## 4. Cause (environment first, not performance)

- **Information.** (a) The spec gives fill time (45 min) and swap time (30 min) but no read time, so the 25-minute read is the operator's own estimate. (b) Whether the 8-hour day is a tool limit or a self-set budget is still open. The log shows the day rolling at 16:40 on check_offers, but the day-117 to 125 test allowed a sixth fill. (c) Check 4 says "seasonality-adjusted", but the system field is raw. The operator notes August is weak; that is unverified. (d) Charts omit checks 3 and 4, the pace line, margin, COGS and sold-out slot-days. Requested since P-015. (e) The system ground-truth table has no row for days 185, 186, 194, 200, 201, 206, 208, 210 or 213, though the log has cash for 200, 201, 206, 208, 210 and 213. A 7-day difference over a gap may be wrong.
- **Resources.** One operator does reads, fills, orders and complaints in 8 hours. Visits cap units. Slot size and mix are not a driver; small rows sell out within a day (R-P-024 section 8).
- **Incentives.** The pass conditions reward filled slots, not revenue per visit. The charted cash signal reads restock as loss. The 09:30 pivot has no consequence.
- **Pattern.** Two supply-side redesigns (R-P-020 cover, R-P-024 fills) each predicted a fall in sold-out slot-days. That measure was never charted, and A-004 records the prediction as unverified. The swarm has not tested whether the lever moves the top measure.

## 5. What the design would need to change (not written here)

- Settle whether the 8-hour day is enforced by the tool, and put a read time in the spec.
- Chart check 3, check 4 (with a real seasonal baseline), the pace line, sold-out slot-days and storage per sold-out slot, and fix the PIP arithmetic. Current PIP: 4.5 at day 222 and 4.45 at day 225 (the pulse said 5.4 and the chart said 5.2; both misstate their own figures). Treat as indicative.
- Make the push depend on revenue per visit (slot size, mix, price), since adding slots does not add demand. Measure it.
- Remove the 09:30 read pivot unless the time model allows it.
- Set per-line cover against the visit route, not per line alone.
