# Redesign R-P-024 (day 175 of 365)

Trigger: Diagnosis D-P-024 (day 171), from pulse P-024. Charts at day 175: cash 5,811 against the exemplar line at 28,329 (PIP 6.2, indicative: the exemplar series is interpolated). Pace for day 180 is about 12,000; the swarm is well behind.

## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is the right top measure, and storage per sold-out slot, sold-out slot-days and lead time are the right supply drivers. The tree is right. What is wrong is the design's call on fills.

- Spec v5 set fills to "watch only", citing the day-125 6th-fill test. That test showed the tools allowed a sixth fill, and at that time storage bound, so the cap did not bind. The same answer does not hold after day 157: five fills a day, every day, with sold-out slots left at zero and stock waiting in storage (cola 41, crisps 50 on day 167).
- The 5-trip cap is a budget the operator set (audits/A-003.md, audits/D-A-002.md), not a tool limit. It now binds, and nothing in the tree charges for it.
- R-P-020's revert clause is met for the cover rule: sold-out slot-days not down, slots mean 9.08 since R-P-020 (below its centre of 10.2), storage holds stock. The cover rule is not the cause, and the stock it buys is already on hand, so I keep it and do not chase lead time (the records show stock on hand, and orders landing within a day of due). Lead time is not the binding step.

## 2. Change (one design change: fills are pushed on again, with no flat cap)
- **spec.md v5 → v6** (old: `swarm/history/spec.v5.md`):
  - Fills per day: "watch only" → **yes, now**. Stop reason logged for any day that stops with a sold-out slot holding storage stock.
  - Time budget: the flat 5-trip cap is retired. Reads and price changes come first; fills continue while a sold-out slot with storage stock is open, within the 8-hour day.
  - Open questions: the 8-hour day's status (business rule or self-set) is logged as unanswered; the cash_gain_7 IMPROVEMENT is logged as unexplained.
- **rules/operator.md v4 → v5** (old: `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`):
  - Rule 4: "within 5 trips a day" → "without a flat trip cap". Priority order unchanged.
  - Rule 8: rewritten as above.
- **Not changed, on purpose:** the goal, the aims and pace table, checks 1 to 7, the cover rule (Done means 7), the weekly cap, the reserve, the cadence, the milestones, the decision rights.

## 3. Why this change
It addresses the primary cause in D-P-024: the design told the operator not to act on the supply driver, and the cap that binds is the operator's own. It stops no core work: reads and price changes still come first, and the day's fills only grow when stock is waiting. It adds no spend: the stock is already bought and held. Overstock is still bounded by checks 2 to 4 and the weekly cap.

## 4. Prediction (testable on the charts)
- **Fills per day** on days with a sold-out slot holding storage stock: above 5 on at least 3 of the first 5 such days after day 175, and the stop reason is logged for the rest. Checked at the day-180 pulse.
- **Sold-out selling slot-days** (operator-computed until charted) fall by day 185 to at most half the days 157–170 rate. Checked at the day-185 pulse.
- **slots_selling** 14-day mean back above its R-P-020 centre of **10.2** by **day 185**, and stays there through the day-190 pulse. Chart: moves up from 9.08 (the since-R-P-020 mean).
- **Storage per sold-out slot** falls toward zero on sold-out days, because stock is reaching the shelf.

## 5. Cost
- Operator time: up to 2 more fills a day at 45 min each, only on days with a sold-out slot holding stock. Reads and price changes still run first. No new spend. The stock sold is already bought.
- Check 2 (cash above reserve) is unaffected by the change.

- Any unpaid day, any skipped read or price change, or cash below reserve from restocking: stop the change and restore `swarm/history/spec.v5.md` and `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`, and record why.
