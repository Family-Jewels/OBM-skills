Trigger: Diagnosis D-P-034 (day 225), from pulse P-034 ("Diagnosis needed: yes"). Charts at day 231: cash 8,601 against the exemplar's 38,023 (PIP 5.1, indicative: the exemplar series is interpolated). Pace for day 231 is about 18,800 at the day-222 checkpoint; the swarm is far behind.

## 1. Is the direction right?
Partly. Cash is the right top measure, and the tree's cash, check-3 and check-4 measures are right. The supply-side push is not. Two redesigns (R-P-020 cover, R-P-024 fills) pushed on slots and fills. Slots rose 9.4 to 12.4 (+32%); revenue rose 108 to 115 (+6%); revenue per slot fell 11.5 to 9.1 (−21%). Adding slots adds capacity, not customers, and the swarm's visits per day are the limit (D-P-034 section 2). The tree still pushes on slot count and fills, but not on what a visit earns.

## 2. Change (one design change: push on revenue per visit, not slot count)
- **spec.md v6 → v7** (old: `swarm/history/spec.v6.md`):
  - Driver tree: new row **Revenue per visit** (revenue ÷ fills per day), "yes, now". Judged on this, not slot count.
  - slots_selling: "move the push to the drivers below" → "not pushed on"; new slots only where the sales report shows a stock-out with demand.
  - Time: each location read is about 25 min (the operator's estimate, to be verified by logging read times). This is the missing input the diagnosis names; it is not a new limit.
- **rules/operator.md v5 → v6** (old: `swarm/history/operator.v5.md`):
  - Rule 4(c): new slots only on a demonstrated stock-out with demand; do not add slots to raise the count.
  - Rule 8: log the day's revenue per visit and each location's read time.
- **Not changed, on purpose:** the goal, the exemplar and pace table, checks 1 to 7, the reserve, the weekly cap, the cadence, the milestones, the decision rights, and fills (already pushed on by v6).

## 3. Why this change
It addresses the cause in D-P-034 section 4: the tree judges a visit by slot count, and the swarm has no measure of what a visit earns. The change makes visits the thing pushed on, and makes the operator log the two numbers needed to test it. It stops no core work: reads and price changes still come first, and it adds no slot spend. It does not lower the goal or any aim.
