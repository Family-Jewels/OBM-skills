## 3. Why this change
It addresses the primary cause in D-P-020 (no supply driver acts on the stock-out) and the resource cause (5-day effective lead time against a 3–4 day cover rule). It adds a criterion, which the diagnosis asked for. It stops no core work: the operator still fills, prices and orders; the only new requirement is to hold cover when ordering, and overstock is still bounded by checks 2 to 4 and the weekly cap.
