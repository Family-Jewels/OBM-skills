# Diagnosis D-P-004 (day 24 of 365)

**Trigger:** P-004 (day 20), `Diagnosis needed: yes`. At day 24 the chart now signals: cash_gain_7 is WORSE (8 in a row below centre; latest −108.29, lower limit −147.24). P-004's "no signals" was true at day 20 only.

**Top measure walked:** final cash ← weekly cash_gain_7 (signal) ← revenue (in limits, 331.6 last 7 days) minus stock bought (315.2 ordered d17–d23) minus fees (84). The driver that moved is **cost of stock bought against sales that reach the machines**. Revenue is not the problem; slots are.

## Problem (pinpointed)
Only 1–8 of 72 slots sold from day 10 to day 25 (mean about 4 from day 16). Stock is bought (3 orders, €315 in 7 days) but about €110 sat in storage at day 24 (crisps 30, energy 20, oat 20) while sold-out slots stayed empty. Restock trips were the limit: five fills a day at about 10 units each, and four failed restock attempts (days 17–22).

## Evidence
- `metrics_system.md`: slots_selling 9 (d10) → 1 (d21–22) → 6 (d25). revenue_last_7 331.6 at d24.
- `progress.md` d17, d21, d23, d24: sold-out slots, storage holding stock, failed trips, "PO-1007 NOT yet in storage".
- Margin is positive on every line (d18, d24 log). Below-cost sales are ruled out.
- Cash bridge d1–d24: revenue €863, fee €288, refunds €15, stock €790 paid at order. This is within about €30 of the ledger; the residual is not in the records.

## What maintains it
1. **Censored demand.** P-004 pivot 3 sizes orders from sell-through, but a sold-out slot shows no sell-through beyond its stock. Small orders therefore cause the next stock-out.
2. **Cash falls, buying stops.** After P-003 and P-004 the operator held new-slot buys (d18, d24). Cash_gain_7 counts stock paid at order as a loss, so buying looks like failure. Holding back shrinks the selling slots, which lowers revenue and cash further.
3. **Delivery lag** (2–4 days, Zuidas; Bakker up to 18) leaves slots empty between cycles. Order timing ignores lead time.

## Cause
- **Resources (primary):** 5 fills a day cannot keep 72 slots stocked. The spec sets "every selling slot restocked" (T-0003) with no fill plan sized to the trip budget.
- **Information:** the spec and pivot 3 read demand from sales that stock-outs cap. Lead time is not in the ordering rule. Margin, COGS and marketing are not charted; the operator's cash line differs from the system ledger by exactly the order paid on that day (d17, d23: €100.80).
- **Incentives:** cash_gain_7 punishes the stock purchase that the slots_selling push asks for. The check and the push conflict, so the operator is pulled both ways.
- **Environment, not performance:** supplier delays and Bakker's 18-day delivery.

## What the design would need to change (not written here)
- Size slots and orders to the fill budget and to uncensored demand (stock-out days counted), and set order timing from supplier lead time.
- Reconcile cash_gain_7 with stock bought, or chart cash plus stock at cost alongside it, so buying is not scored as loss.
- Chart margin, COGS per day and slots selling per trip; reconcile the operator's cash line with the ledger.
- Check the day-30 checkpoint: it needs +1,990 in 6 days from 1,238, and the 5,470/12,000 interpolations are the designer's own.

**Would settle the open points:** system-charted stock on hand by slot and sold-out days per slot, and per-supplier delivery dates.
