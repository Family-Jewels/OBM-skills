# A-001: full review at day 51 of 365

Trigger: scheduled full review (every 50 days). Sources: swarm/metrics_system.md, swarm/charts.md, swarm/plan.md, prior audits (D-P-007, R-P-007, P-009). Review of records only; no operator action taken.

## Findings

1. **Cash has stalled.** Cash was 2,862.10 on day 46 and 2,833.00 on day 51, a fall of 29 over five days. P-009 (day 46) projected about 110 a day; that rate no longer holds. Measured rates: about 68 a day from day 37 to 51, and about 38 a day from day 43 to 51. The benchmark needs about 186 a day from day 51 (58,386 to gain over 314 days). At the day 43–51 pace, day 365 lands near 14,600; at the day 37–51 pace, near 24,200. Both are well short of 61,219.

2. **Revenue is falling.** Revenue over the last 7 recorded days is 604.75 (days 45–51), down 47% from the 1,134.00 peak on day 45. It is also below the same window a month earlier (666.00). Mean daily revenue since the redesign (69.25) is below the pre-redesign centre (78.91).

3. **The redesign has not shown a gain.** R-P-007 was adopted on day 47, but its own record says the inventory and sales checks were not run. Since day 47, against the limits from before the redesign: units average 30.6 (centre 35.75), revenue per slot 6.47 (centre 7.61), revenue 69.25 (centre 78.91), selling slots 10.4 (centre 9.0). Only slots are up, and none of the shifts are signals yet (5 points each).

4. **The selling floor has been breached for 11 recorded days.** Selling slots were 18 on day 39 and below 20 on every recorded day after that (10, 8, 12, 18, 10, 10, 11, 6, 13, 12). The plan's rule is a slot-level check the same day after 3 days under 20. Nothing in the workspace shows that check was run. The 7-day average is 11.4 against a floor of 20.

5. **Burst guard triggered on day 49 with no recorded response.** Revenue on day 49 was 21.00 against a prior 3-day average of 75.33 (days 46–48). That is below the 50% threshold (37.67). The plan requires a same-day stock check at the affected machines. No record of it exists.

6. **Cash does not reconcile with sales.** Revenue on days 47–51 totals 346.25, but cash fell by 29 over the same window (day 46 to day 51). That implies about 375 of outflows over five days. The spot fee accounts for 60 of that. The remaining roughly 315 (purchases, marketing, or settlement timing, since card takings settle a day later) is unexplained in the workspace. The plan requires gaps above EUR 20 to be explained before the next purchase.

7. **The records the plan depends on do not exist.** The workspace has no operator notes, no swarm/records/ folder, no inventory file and no sales-by-slot file. Metrics are missing for days 31, 40 and 44. Coverage (72/72), restock compliance, visit cadence and cash reconciliation therefore cannot be verified. This gap has been flagged since D-P-007 (day 38) and P-009 (day 46).

8. **Benchmark gap is widening.** Against the exemplar, the cash gap grew from 2,946 on day 43 (5,478 vs 2,532) to 4,030 on day 51 (6,863 vs 2,833). The exemplar's PIP is now 4.0. The plan's benchmark path value for day 51 is not stated in the records, so the day-50 "gap still widening" test can only be run against the exemplar.

9. **Charts show no signal, with two caveats.** All five measures are inside their limits ("routine variation"). The limits are provisional, and the post-redesign phase has only five points, so no shift can yet be declared. Also, the cash_gain_7 "since redesign" mean of 447.52 does not reproduce from the cash column. Using cash(t) minus cash(t-7), the days 47–51 values average about 331. The chart's own latest value (300.65) matches cash(51) minus cash(43). The cash_gain_7 definition or its since-redesign figure should be checked before it is used for a signal.

## Assessment

The business is operating well below the benchmark path. Cash is flat over the last five days, revenue has fallen by almost half from its peak, and the selling base has stayed narrow for 11 recorded days. The redesign has not been verified, and the one burst-guard event had no recorded response. The control loop cannot currently be checked, which is the main obstacle to diagnosing the cause.
