# D-P-025: diagnosis of P-025 (review at day 143; state read at day 147)

Sources: swarm/metrics_system.md (daily table), swarm/charts.md, swarm/plan.md (v5), P-024, D-A-002, R-A-002. This session has no vending tools, no sales, inventory or purchase records, and no shell, so all figures are hand-checked from the daily table. Nothing here is confirmed against operator records.

## What is going wrong

1. **The business is small and has not moved.** Daily revenue averages 97.2 since day 125 and 88.6 before it. Selling slots (single-day count) average 9.9 since day 125 and 9.6 before it. The highest daily count ever logged is 20 (days 38, 72, 100). Nothing in the record shows that R-A-002 or any earlier change shifted the process.

2. **The pace gap is growing, not closing.** Cash is 7,061.42 at day 147. Gain since day 125 is 41.4/day, against revenue of 97.2/day, which implies about 56/day of outflow (12/day is the spot fee). At 41.4/day, day 365 lands near 16,100. The benchmark now needs (61,219 − 7,061) / 218 = **248/day**. Plan v5 and P-025 still use 220/day, which is the day-113 figure.

3. **The control loop is measuring the wrong thing.** The plan's targets (40, 30) and triggers (below 20 for 2 days) use a rolling 7-day distinct selling-slot count. The system logs a single-day count. The 40 target is double the highest daily value ever recorded. The day-120 test compared the daily count (peak 13) with a threshold of 20, so it was not run on the defined measure. The "below 20" trigger has been true on nearly every day, so it fires on routine variation.

4. **Recent movement is routine variation, with one edge point.** The day-100 peak (7-day revenue 1,022.75) was a spike. The 510.25 that followed is inside the limits. The cash_gain_7 "shift down" compares post-125 values with a pre-125 centre (439) that the day 90–101 peak likely inflates. The latest point (421.89 at day 147) is above the upper limit (410.78), an improvement signal from a single point.

5. **The "missed visits" cause is untested.** It fits the slot drop, but no visit, stock or price record exists to confirm it.

## Why it is happening

- The plan reacts to noise: A-002 and D-A-002 compared a peak with a trough, and R-A-002 changed the plan on that basis.
- The metric definitions do not match the targets or the test.
- The gates cannot pass. The cash gate needs purchase and marketing records that do not exist. If that gate blocks purchases, restocking stops and selling slots fall further.
- The unexplained outflow (about 252 for days 131–138, P-024) is about 36/day. That is the same order as the non-fee outflow (about 44/day) and most likely purchases. It is not shown to be a leak.

## Implication for the revision (plan not changed here)

At current yield (about 8.65 per selling slot per day), 220–250/day needs roughly 25–29 selling slots daily, against about 10 now. Breadth is the lever. Revision should: restate the slot measure to match the logged daily count (or log the 7-day count); set interim targets from the observed range (about 10 now, maximum 20) rather than 40; re-baseline the benchmark pace to 248/day; judge changes against current-phase limits; and record purchases and marketing so the cash gate can actually be met.
