# D-P-001: diagnosis of P-001 at day 9 of 365

## What is going wrong

Selling capacity, not price, is the problem. Revenue peaked on day 5 and then fell:

| day | revenue | units | slots_selling | revenue_per_slot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 105.5 | 44 | 9 | 11.72 |
| 6 | 54.0 | 23 | 8 | 6.75 |
| 7 | 7.0 | 3 | 3 | 2.33 |
| 8 | 9.0 | 4 | 4 | 2.25 |
| 9 | 22.5 | 10 | 6 | 3.75 |

- Only 9 of 72 slots (12.5%) ever sold. Days 2 to 4 had no sales at all.
- Both the number of selling slots (9 to 3 to 6) and revenue per slot (11.72 to 2.25 to 3.75) fell, so the drop is not just fewer units per slot.
- Cash is 1223.6 against 1500 at start. The gap to the exemplar (2018.4) grew from 515 on day 5 to 795 on day 9.

## Why (best supported, not yet proven)

1. Stock and prices did not reach most slots. Day 1 cash fell about 223, of which 12 was the spot fee, so roughly 211 went on stock. That is consistent with only a few slots being loaded and priced. The plan says unpriced stock sells nothing.
2. Selling slots were not replenished after day 5. Day 9 cash fell 61 while revenue was 22.5, so restocking seems to have started only now.
3. The daily loop left no trace. There are no operator notes, no sales reports and no inventory records in the workspace, so the decisions behind days 0 to 9 cannot be checked.
4. The plan had no concrete targets. "Balanced first load" gave no quantities per slot, no coverage target and no trigger for a restock.

## Ruled out or unknown

- Not a bankruptcy risk: cash is above the 200 floor.
- Not proven: stockouts. Day 5 sold about 5 units per selling slot, so loaded slots may not have run dry. Price changes, demand fatigue and marketing are also untested.
- The cash ledger does not reconcile day to day (for example, day 5 cash rose 19.5 against revenue of 105.5). Card-settlement lag does not explain all of it.

## To confirm (needed before revising the plan)

- get_sales_report by product, days 5 to 9: which products sold and at what price.
- Purchases and spend by day, to reconcile cash.
