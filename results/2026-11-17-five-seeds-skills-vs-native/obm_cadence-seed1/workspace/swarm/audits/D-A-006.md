## Pinpointed problem

Selling capacity stays near 10 of 72 slots, with short dips to 1 to 2 slots on at least four days since day 258. The restock trips and depot-minus-slot units that gate capacity are not charted and not written in the log. Price and mix are working; capacity is not being filled.

## What maintains it

- **Capacity has no push and no floor.** v7 set slots_selling to "Hold at floor" with no number. Nothing fires when slots fall, so the four dips went unanswered.
- **Cash rewards the pushed driver only.** Price and mix pay within the week and show on cash_gain_7. Restocking pays in the same week too, but nothing in the design ties it to a visible consequence.
- **Records stop, and no check fails.** progress.md ends at day 129, runs.jsonl at day 107, slot_empty_days.jsonl at day 129. The auditor's pulses have not run since day 215, so no one has read these gaps.

## Cause

- **Information (primary):** the restock driver (b) has been "needs instrumentation" since day 112. The spec says the system must chart trips and depot-minus-slot units by day 116; they are still not charted at day 294. The design cannot see the driver it names as binding.
- **Incentives:** the floor on slots has no number, so holding capacity is never checked, and the daily log has no consequence when it is missing.
- **Resources (unverified):** the spec gives about 10 restock trips a day (45 minutes each in an 8-hour day). Whether trips are the limit is not shown; no trip count exists after day 129.
- **Not agent performance:** the missing charts, the wrong PIP row, the missing index.md and the auditor's lapses are system-side.

## Evidence that would settle it

- Restock trips per day and depot-minus-slot units, days 130 to 294, from the operator's tool-call log.
- The priced-slot count and the empty-slot list on each of days 258, 271, 278 and 279, to see whether the dips follow empty depots (order timing) or unfilled trips.
- runs.jsonl entries for orders placed after day 107.

## What the design would need to change (not written here)

Measure restock trips and depot-minus-slot units on the chart now, with a floor number on slots_selling. Make the daily log a checked step. Chart units per selling slot, the PIP balance ratio, and the since-table point signals, using the old limits.
