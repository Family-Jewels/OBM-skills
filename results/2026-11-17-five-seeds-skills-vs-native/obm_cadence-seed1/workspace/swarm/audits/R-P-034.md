Trigger: Diagnosis D-P-034 (day 341): slots_selling below its centre (15.0) at every close since day 326, the 8-closes-below-10 floor missed on days 316 to 337, restock trips down to about 4 a day (days 329 to 340), and no progress.md entry for days 329 to 340 except a backfill.

## Is the direction right?
Yes for the goal and the top measure. Cash is rising (14,467 at day 354), and price and mix moved units per selling slot up from about 3.7 (since day 198) to 4.7 (since R-A-006). The tree is right: revenue is slots_selling × revenue_per_slot, and both nodes are charted.
The drivers are not right for the next 13 days. Capacity (b) is now the binding driver, but the spec holds it "at floor" (v7). Price and mix are being pushed while the slots that would sell are empty. So the push is on the wrong driver for the current constraint.
The exemplar gap is not closing (PIP 4.10 at day 354 by the spec's definition: 59,314.82 ÷ 14,467.29). The charts row reads 4.5, which is wrong by its own numbers. The absolute gap, not the ratio, is the thing to track.

## Is the design causing it?
Yes, in two ways.
- Information: trips and depot-minus-slot units have not been charted since day 116. The binding driver has been visible only through a daily line that lapsed on day 328.
- Incentive: a zero-trip day or sub-floor close had no consequence the same week, and "depot limit" was accepted with no per-product count, although the logged depot-minus-slot units were about 240 (day 330) and about 300 (day 332).
- Not shown: whether the day's calls ran short. The tool-call log settles it (see below). The brief already puts trips first, so the cause is not the order of steps.

## Change (smallest that addresses the cause; nothing stops the core loop)
1. Spec, driver tree: restock trips (b) moves from "Hold at floor" to "Yes, first (v9)". The priority line now reads (b) first against the floor of 10, with (c) and (a) continuing at one change a day.
2. Brief v8, skipped restock: a day with fewer trips than the day allows, or a close below 10, records the per-product depot count at open for each product with empty slots. "Depot limit" alone does not stand.
3. Brief v8, missing record: a missed day is written "GAP: day N" with only the ledger and sales-report figures. No backfill from memory.
4. Unchanged: goal, aims, exemplar, the floor of 10 (a check, not a ceiling), reserve EUR 150, done criteria, milestones, budgets, cadence, price-and-mix push.
Not changed on purpose: the floor (lowering it would be lowering an aim); the trip count (spec says about 10, brief about 8; the logged count will settle it, as R-A-006 noted).
