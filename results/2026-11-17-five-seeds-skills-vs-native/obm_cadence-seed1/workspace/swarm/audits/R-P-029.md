Trigger: Diagnosis D-P-029 (day 225): revenue down about 20% over 30 days; units per selling slot down about 30%; slots_selling not the moving measure; restock trips and depot-minus-slot unrecorded since day 129.
Change: spec v6 -> v7 (swarm/spec.md; old copy swarm/history/spec.v6.md). Brief operator v5 -> v6 (rules/operator.md; old copy swarm/history/operator.v5.md).

## Is the direction right?
Yes. The tree decomposes correctly: revenue = slots_selling × revenue_per_slot, and revenue_per_slot = units per selling slot × price. The top measure (balance) is still rising (about +30 a day), so it does not show the loss; the revenue measures do. The tree was missing one node, units per selling slot, which is where the loss sits. Goal, aims and exemplar stay as they are.

## Is the design causing it?
Partly. The records after day 129 cannot be read, so the cause (product mix, price, or both) is untested. Push (b), restock throughput, has been pushed in v3, v4 and v6 and has not moved units per slot. Slots_selling has held near its count (since-mean 9.55 against 10.65), so restock is not what fell.

## Change (one, smallest that tests the candidate cause)
1. Tree: add "units per selling slot" (units ÷ slots_selling, from charted measures; no new source). Push first on it, along with price (c) and product per slot (a).
2. Hold (b) and slots_selling at floor: the daily restock stays first, as the core loop.
3. Brief v6, step 4: one price or swap change per day, on the product furthest below its own 14-day level; each change written the same day with old and new value.

Core work is not stopped: restock, order-on-cover, the reserve and the refund rule are unchanged.
