Requested by: system, on D-P-021 (day 143). Spec v4 → v5. Operator brief v3 → v4.

## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is not the constraint (6,978 at day 154 against a 150 reserve). The top measure is the final balance, and revenue = slots_selling × revenue_per_slot is the chain that moves it. slots_selling is the right first driver (capacity in use). The tree is right. ## 2. Is the design causing the problem?
Yes. Information, resources and incentives, as D-P-021 found:
- Order timing was tied to the weekly offer day (operator brief v3, step 3; spec Cadence "check_offers and any new order: Weekly"). Zuidas takes 3–5 days, so depots ran out between deliveries (PO-1042: ordered day 115, landed day 120; PO-1044: ordered day 124, landed day 128). Slots sat empty: slots_selling mean 9.55 since R-A-002 against 11.15 before.
- One active supplier. Kanaal was a dearer backup, not named in the plan.
- Stock-out share and trip charts were due day 116 and are still absent, so the empty-slot cost was invisible on the day it happened.
- R-A-002's revert clause is met (trips rose to 7–10 a day; slots_selling did not). Trip count was not the constraint.

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)
- Spec v5: driver (d) now means order on depot cover, not the calendar. Trigger: a product's cover (depot units ÷ units sold per day, last 7 days) below 6 days, on any day. Kanaal named as backup for a line Zuidas cannot cover in time. Cadence rows updated. Stock-out count logged by the operator until the system charts it.
- Operator brief v4: step 3 rewritten (order on cover; sized to 10 days' cover; Zuidas default, Kanaal backup, premium logged). Step 6 logs stock-out count and cover per product.
- Not changed: the goal, the exemplar, the reserve (150), the done criteria, the milestones, the decision rights, the roles, the budgets, the restock priority (b).
