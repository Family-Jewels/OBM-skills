## Changes

1. **Restock trigger** (operating loop, step 4): check selling-slot fill every 2 to 3 days, and buy enough depth to cover the 7-day offer cycle, so machines do not sit near empty for the rest of the week.
2. **Idle cash** (money and fees): keep a buffer of about €470 (€300 plus 2 weeks of fees). Cash above it goes into stock for slots that sell.
3. **Expansion gate**: measure units per slot on 7-day totals. Expand after 2 consecutive weeks at or above the prior week, replacing the 5-day rule a weekly cycle never meets.
4. **Demand drop response**: judge drops on weekly totals (more than 30% below the prior 7 days). A short dip after a restock is noted, not treated as a drop.
5. **Progress measure** (review cadence): judge on balance plus stock value, not cash change alone. Purchases are logged as inventory.
