2. **Cash deployment** (operating loop step 5; "Money and fees"): a quantified threshold. Deploy when balance is more than €300 above the €470 buffer; no day ends with more than €770 idle while a selling slot is not full or lacks depot cover.

4. **Drop trigger** ("Demand drop response"): the 30% window-to-window rule is removed. The full check runs on a control-chart signal in charts.md, or when empty slot-days rise for two weeks running.
