## What P-001 got right and what is stale

- Days 2 to 5: revenue, units and slots selling were zero, and cash fell only by the €12 daily fee. That is confirmed.
- P-001 says zeros on every recorded day. That is out of date. Sales started on day 6 (€119.50, 49 units, 8 slots selling). Its step 3 ("hold orders until a day shows non-zero revenue") is already met.

## What is going wrong

1. **Slow start.** About €234.60 went out by day 2 for stock. The first sale came on day 6, four days later. Stock was probably not in the slots until about day 5 (delivery or loading lag). This is a delay, not a permanent failure, but it is unverified.
2. **Volume fell on day 7.** Units dropped from 49 to 21 (-57%) and revenue from €119.50 to €51.00. Revenue per slot fell from €14.94 to €4.64.
3. **Price is not the cause.** Revenue per unit is about €2.44 on day 6 and €2.43 on day 7.
4. **Slots grew faster than demand.** Slots selling rose from 8 to 11 while units fell. The plan says to expand only when sales support it. Whether the three extra slots were new placements is not visible.

## Why (most likely, not confirmed)

Stock loaded for the opening sales was probably used up on day 6, and the three new slots were filled without enough demand behind them. Each cause predicts a different inventory reading: empty slots with stock still in the depot points to restocking lag; full slots with falling sales points to demand or placement.
