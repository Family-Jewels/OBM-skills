**Going wrong**

1. **Selling capacity is collapsing, and A-004 missed the latest days.** Selling slots: 25 on day 200, 13 on day 201, 15 on days 202 and 203. Revenue_last_7 fell from 1,216 (day 200) to 1,018 (day 203). Revenue per slot is 4 to 6 on days 201 to 203.
2. **Large cash outflows coincide with slot drops.** Day 114: cash −444, slots 15 to 3. Day 137 to 138: cash −557, slots 12 to 6. Day 201: cash −310, slots 25 to 13. The pattern points to stock paid for but not reaching the machines (depot-first rule, delayed transfers), or to stock-outs between batches. It is not proven: the cash drop on days 41, 58 and 72 had no slot fall.
3. **Idle cash is blocked by the plan's own rules.** Excess above the €470 buffer is about €9,200. The idle-cash sink needs three day-ends above €770, all slots full, and no slot at or below half that week. With slots emptying, that condition is almost never met, so the sink cannot fire.
4. **The charts measure the wrong thing.** cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as losses. The A-004 signal (169.7 vs lower 234.25) was day 194. On day 203 cash_gain_7 is 448.3, inside its limits, and the chart says "no shift yet", not "mean shifted down" as A-004 stated. The charts cannot see a slow revenue slide.
5. **Restock throughput may be the binding limit (arithmetic, unverified).** About 20 selling slots, each refilled every 2 to 4 days, need 5 to 10 refills a day. The plan allows about 6 machine trips a day, at 45 min per restock. Whether one trip refills several slots is not recorded.

**Why (unconfirmed)**
- Restocking throughput and the depot-first rule leave slots empty after purchases.
- Slot removals, if any, are unlogged, so empty slots and removed slots cannot be told apart.
