Sources: charts, metrics_system, plan, P-018, P-019, D-P-013, R-P-013. No game tools were available here, so notes, inventory and the purchase log were not read. Causes are inferred from ground-truth measures and cash changes.

**Going wrong**
1. Idle cash. Cash is €5,272 against a €470 buffer, so about €4,800 is idle. Cash rose €1,145 over days 110–133, against a plan that says to deploy the excess daily.
2. The stock-out cycle continues after R-P-013. Restock days show a cash fall of €260–450 net. Sales spike, then collapse within 2–4 days: day 115 had 136 units on 22 slots and day 117 had 10 units on 4 slots; day 123 had 95 units and day 127 had 10.

4. Plan flaw: the depot cap ("one week of forecast sales") and the 7-day floor use sales that stock-outs suppress. Purchases stay small while cash sits idle.

**Likely cause (unconfirmed):** the sizing rules are anchored to stock-out-depressed sales, so batches sell out quickly while cash is left idle. Units are up only 17% since R-P-013.
