# D-P-007: diagnosis at day 40 of 365

Source: swarm/metrics_system.md (days 2 to 40), swarm/charts.md, swarm/plan.md, swarm/audits/P-007.md. This session has no game tools, and the workspace holds no operator notes, inventory reads or sales reports. Causes below are inferred from cash and sales arithmetic. They are unconfirmed.

## Summary

The business is flat and slow, not falling. Revenue runs in a 7-day restock cycle: one batch per week sells out in 2 to 4 days, and selling slots then fall to 0 to 3. The plan's own gates keep this cycle in place. Cash of about €1,970 is mostly idle.

## What is going wrong

1. **Not a decline.** Revenue by fixed calendar week: days 6 to 12 €440; 13 to 19 €381; 20 to 26 €540; 27 to 33 €327; 34 to 40 €487. Average per day: €52 in days 6 to 15, €66 in days 16 to 40. P-007's "down 53%" compares the day-29 rolling peak (€605, which includes the day-23 restock) with the day-35 rolling trough (€287). Rolling 7-day sums swing with where the restock falls.

2. **Weekly restock cycle.** Cash fell further than revenue less the €12 fee on days 9 (about €284), 16 (about €95), 23 (about €126), 30 (about €294) and 37 (about €244). These are 7 days apart. Each batch sells out in 2 to 4 days. Selling slots were 0 to 3 on days 12, 13, 19, 20, 32, 33 and 34 (7 of 35 days). The day-30 outflow is most likely a stock purchase, not an unexplained loss. A €12 fee cannot explain it.

3. **The chart signal is a purchase-timing artifact.** cash_gain_7 counts stock purchases as losses. The day-35 breach (-53.80) is the day-30 purchase, not a loss in sales.

4. **Idle capital.** Purchases run at about €200 to €290 per week. The plan's buffer (about €300 plus two weeks of fees) is about €470. Roughly €1,500 is not deployed.

5. **The plan's controls keep the cycle in place.** The demand-drop protocol fired on 14 of 34 days since day 7 (unit drops over 30%). Each firing asks for reads, and none are on file, so no cause is recorded. The expansion gate needs 5 days of stable units per slot, which a 7-day cycle never provides. Price is not the cause: revenue per unit is €2.4 to €2.6 throughout.

6. **Chart centres not reproduced.** The revenue centre for the phase since day 16 is 60.61 in charts.md, but the mean of those same 25 days in metrics_system.md is 66.3. I could not reproduce the centre, so the limits are unverified.

## Why (most likely)

Restocks follow a weekly cadence, which matches the 7-day offer cycle the plan cites, rather than the plan's half-empty trigger. A batch sells out in 2 to 4 days, so the machine runs near empty for the rest of the week. Growth levers (slots, duplicates, price) stay shut, so the business stays flat while the exemplar compounds.

## Predictions and checks

- **Days 41 to 43:** unless a restock lands mid-cycle, revenue will be under €30 a day with 3 or fewer selling slots. If so, the cycle is confirmed.
- **Day 40 inventory reads:** empty slots with depot stock means restock timing (operator-side). Full slots with low sales means demand or placement.
- **Day-30 outflow of about €294:** match it to an order invoice (`list_emails`, `get_sales_report`). If it is a stock order, it is inventory, not a loss.

## Not changed

swarm/plan.md is unchanged, as requested. Points for the revision: a restock trigger checked every 2 to 3 days by slot level; deploying cash above the buffer into stock; demand-drop checks on weekly totals; and a stock-inclusive measure in place of cash_gain_7.
