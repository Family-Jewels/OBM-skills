Previous plan: swarm/history/plan-v3.md. Current plan: swarm/plan.md (version 4).

## Changes

1. **Restock trigger and size** (operating loop step 4; new "Restock rules"): restock any selling slot at or below half full at the morning read, and fill it to capacity, with a 7-day floor. Previously the rule was to buy depth for the 7-day cycle without a fill-based trigger or size.
2. **Cash deployment** (operating loop step 5; "Money and fees"): balance above the €470 buffer is deployed into fills each day, before idle cash can build.
3. **Expansion gate**: measure units per in-stock slot-day and block expansion while any selling slot is empty for more than 1 day a week.
4. **Demand drop response**: count empty slot-days, so sold-out batches are not read as falling demand.
5. **Review and notes**: log batch size and fill before and after each restock; review compares them against the rules.

## Not changed

- Prices, marketing rules, fee and refund rules, supplier sourcing, and the benchmark targets.

## Basis and limits

- The charts stay inside limits, so no special cause was identified; this change addresses the cycle design, not a signal.
- Revision made from the workspace files only; no game tools were read in this step.
