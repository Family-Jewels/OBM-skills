Plan not changed. Sources: swarm/metrics_system.md, swarm/charts.md, swarm/plan.md (v9), swarm/audits/. Estimates are marked. Bash was unavailable, so counts were made by reading the tables.

**What is going wrong**

1. **Sales are lower and unstable.** Revenue over 7 days was about €1,250–1,440 on days 250–260 and €640–1,100 from day 277 on. Selling slots fell to 4 on days 300 and 301. Of 26 recorded mornings from day 277 to 303 (day 295 has no row), 14 were below the 15-slot failure line. The day-300 target of 20 slots was missed.
2. **Real gain has slowed.** Cash rose about €56 a day from day 275 to 303 (estimate). cash_gain_7 is in the since-redesign table below its lower limit (mean 413.6 against a limit of 351.8; pre-redesign centre 645.7). The plan blames stock purchases for this, but cash is rising with few purchases, so that explanation does not fit.
3. **Cash is idle by design.** Excess is about €16,400 against a €770 ceiling. The depot cap absorbs about €1,000. The only other outlet is the sink, at one slot per 3 days, and no sink placement or slot growth is visible in the folder.
4. **The benchmark is out of reach.** About €715 a day is needed from day 303 (estimate), against about €56 a day now.

**Why (partly confirmed)**

- Slot count and revenue per slot both fell since redesign R-P-041: slots about 11% lower (15.0 against 16.85), revenue per slot about 15% lower (8.1 against 9.54). Both matter.
- The cause of slot losses is still unknown. Notes, purchase log, slot-change log and stock-at-cost record have been requested since P-041 and are not in this workspace. Restock failure, transfer lag and removal cannot be tested. The v9 test depends on them.
- The plan ties stock to demand (depot cap) and gates expansion on in-stock sales. Together these keep cash idle while sales are weak.

**For the plan revision to settle**

- Whether the logs exist anywhere. If they do not, the operator must write them daily, or the plan must not rely on them.
- Whether the sink rate (one per 3 days) is enough to convert idle cash.
- Whether the day-365 benchmark is a usable target. If not, set a reachable one.
