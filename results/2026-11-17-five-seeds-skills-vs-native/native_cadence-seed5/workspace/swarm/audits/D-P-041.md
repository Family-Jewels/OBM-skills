# D-P-041: diagnosis at day 270 (follow-up to P-041, day 266)

Plan not changed. Figures are from swarm/metrics_system.md and swarm/charts.md. Estimates are marked.

**What is going wrong**

1. **Selling slots stepped down and did not recover quickly.** Slots fell 29 to 17 in one day (day 241 to 242) and 25 to 9 over days 255 to 258. Since then they have stayed mostly below 20. P-041 read this as "below 15 on three mornings (263 to 265)". The metrics show below 15 on seven mornings (days 257, 258, 259, 260, 263, 264, 265). The plan's "below 15 on two consecutive mornings" full-check trigger was met several times. The 1-to-2-day rebound pattern in D-P-035 does not hold for this drop.

2. **Cash is not being deployed.** Cash rose from €13,169 (day 243, plan v8 adopted) to €15,122 (day 270), +€1,953 in 27 days. Excess over the €470 buffer is about €14,650 against the €770 ceiling. Cash rises on the days slots fall (for example days 241 to 242 and 257 to 258), which fits no restock purchases on those days. Transfer lag and no purchasing both fit that; the missing logs cannot separate them.

3. **Even full deployment would absorb little.** The two-week depot cap is about 2 × 434 units (last 7 days) = about 870 units. At last week's revenue of €2.11 per unit, cost is roughly €1,000 to €1,150 if cost is about half to 55% of price (estimate; cost prices are not recorded). That is under 10% of the excess. Cash is idle because the caps are sized to demand, and demand is about €130 a day.

4. **The pace cannot reach the benchmark.** Net gain is about €50 a day after fees and cost of goods. The benchmark needs about €466 a day for 95 days. This is a structural gap, not a dip.

5. **The charts now flag WORSE.** cash_gain_7 is €347.2 against a lower limit of €351.8. This may partly reflect stock purchases counted as losses, which the plan notes as a blind spot. Stock value is not recorded, so this cannot be checked.

**Why (unconfirmed)**

Slot losses are the likely binding constraint. Revenue per slot rose from about €7 to €9.3 while slots fell from about 23 to 17, so the slots that remain sell well. Restock failure, transfer lag, or slot removal could each cause the step-downs. The operator's notes, purchase log and slot-change log do not exist, so none of these can be tested. The idle cash follows from the caps and gate, not from a missing trigger.

**For the plan revision to settle**

- Log every slot placement and removal, and every restock, from now on. The step-downs cannot be attributed without them.
- Measure cost price and stock value at cost, so the depot cap and the cash_gain_7 flag can be read correctly.
- Decide how idle cash is used when the depot cap is far below excess (for example, more placements under the sink, not larger depots).
