# R-P-035: plan revision at day 243 (responds to D-P-035)

Previous plan: swarm/history/plan-v7.md. Current plan: swarm/plan.md (version 8).

**Changes**

1. Step 5 deploys excess above €300 into every selling product under its depot cap, without requiring a clear queue or full slots. The day note records excess and depot stock value at cost before and after.
2. Depot cap raised from one week to two weeks of demand for all selling products while excess is above €300.
3. Restock trigger raised from half full to two-thirds full, plus a day of restock lag in the order timing. Up to 8 trips a day while slots are triggered or excess is above €300.
4. Idle-cash sink no longer requires a clear restock queue or full slots. It requires excess above €770 for three day-ends after deployment.
5. A cash_gain_7 IMPROVEMENT signal is treated as idle cash, not progress. Pre-redesign limits are checked for slots_selling.
6. Any slot drop of 3 or more not explained by the transfer-or-purchase test triggers the full check, and rebounds within 2 days are logged as restock failures.

**Reason**

D-P-035: restock lag causes slot dips, which keep the queue unclear and block the sink and expansion gate. The one-week depot cap then leaves about €12,000 of cash idle.

**Not changed**

Pricing, marketing, supplier rules and the expansion gate's two-week test. The cause chain is not yet confirmed, so the transfer-or-purchase test and stock-value record stay in place to test it.

**Check at next review**

Excess above €770 should fall. Depot stock at cost should rise toward the two-week cap. Slots at or below two-thirds should be refilled within a day.
