## Walk down the tree

1. **Top measure, final cash.** Cash 3,799.51, celeration x1.19 (provisional). The chart's floor (13,285) and aim (61,219) are built on the benchmark's day-365 value, not the spec's day-91 goal (~5,500), as A-002 item 2 already found. Against the spec goal, cash needs about x1.11 per point from here (25 days, about 3.6 points) and is running at x1.19. **The top measure is not off course against the right goal.** The "below floor" reading is an artefact.
2. **Revenue = slots_selling x revenue_per_slot.** Revenue celeration /1.15 (provisional, wrong way). slots_selling x1.10 (improving). revenue_per_slot /1.00 (flat). The revenue decline comes from **units_per_slot_day**.
3. **Driver that moved: units per selling slot.** Units /1.23 against slots x1.10 means units per slot fall about 20% per point while slot count rises.

## Pinpointed problem

Slots keep being added (20 selling, up from 10 at day 42 in A-002) but each slot sells fewer units. Duplicate-product slots share one demand pool (spec, driver tree), so added duplicates add capacity, not customers.

## What maintains it

- **Milestones count slots.** M2 (36), M3 (60) and M4 (66) are set on slots_selling. Filling duplicate slots pays on the milestones; units per slot is not a milestone. The shortcut pays at once; the mix fix (E-002) pays late.
- **E-002 ran a different change from the spec.** It refilled empty duplicate slots instead of swapping to distinct products, and started without its prerequisite (duplicate-sharing confirmed by day 14, never recorded). Its result is judged on product-level units only (spec open question 6), so the mix problem is not isolated.
- **Experiment results do not change behaviour.** E-001, E-002, E-004 and E-005 all read "ended: keep or revert" with no recorded decision.

## Cause, environment first

- **Information:** the floor and aim lines use the wrong target (61,219). Slot count disagrees with the stocked count (spec open question 7, unresolved). The shift labels do not match the stated rule (A-002 item 2). No series exists for operator minutes or stock-out slot-days.
- **Resources:** 53 of 72 slots idle (26% in use). Capital 3,679.51 of 3,858 idle (5% in use). Idle cash beside empty slots is the signal the spec names, and it has been running since day 16 (A-002). Whether restock throughput is the constraint cannot be settled: operator minutes are not recorded.
- **Incentives:** the only check on spending is the EUR 120 reserve, so unspent cash is never penalised. Nothing rewards units per slot. Decisions D-001 to D-004 have no status.

## What the design needs to change

- Measure **units per selling slot** on the chart as a driver with its own ceiling and limits, and make the mix (distinct products per demand pool) an explicit rule with a check.
- Set milestones on units and revenue, not slot count alone.
- Put the restock constraint on the chart: operator minutes per trip, and slots filled per trip.
- Fix the floor and aim to the spec's day-91 goal.

## Evidence that would settle it

Operator minutes per restock trip; stock-out slot-days by day; a product-level sales split by slot for the duplicate pools; a recorded keep or revert for each experiment.
