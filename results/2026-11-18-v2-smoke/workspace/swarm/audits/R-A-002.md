## Direction

**Right on the goal, wrong on one driver.** Cash is 4,174 at day 70 against the spec's day-91 goal of about EUR 5,500. It needs about EUR 1,326 in 21 days, about EUR 442 per point. The last three points gained about 490 to 510 each (cash_gain_7), so the goal is reachable at the current gain. The risk is the revenue decline: revenue /1.22 and units /1.25 per point, while slots_selling rises. Units per selling slot is the driver that moved the wrong way, and the tree points at it correctly.

**The floor and aim lines on the charts are not a valid judge.** They are built on 61,219 (the benchmark's day-365 value). Against the spec's goal the "below floor" reading is an artefact. The aim is the human's and is not changed here.

## Cause (design side)

1. The operator fills duplicate-product slots, and the spec's milestones (M2 to M4) count slots. The brief's rule 4 says to move duplicates to distinct products, but it does not say not to restock them. E-002 refilled them instead. So added stock buys capacity, not customers.
2. The spec and the operator brief disagree on when purchases stop: the spec's done-check says day 71, rule 7 says day 84.
3. Restock throughput (operator minutes) and stock age are not on the charts, so the constraint cannot be measured.
4. Experiment results and parked decisions have no recorded outcome, so nothing changes behaviour.

## Change (smallest that addresses it; no new experiments)

1. **Operator brief v1 → v2** (`swarm/rules/operator.md`; v1 at `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`):
   - Rule 4: do not restock a duplicate slot; swap to a distinct product only if expected units before day 91 cover its unit cost.
   - Rule 7: purchase test from day 71 (aligned with the spec's done-check).
   - New rule 11: no experiment from day 71; each ended experiment gets a keep or revert by day 71; each open parked decision gets a status at each close.
   - Daily line adds: slots_selling from machine inventory, operator minutes used, and parked-decision status.
2. **Spec v2 → v3** (`swarm/spec.md`; v2 at `swarm/history/spec.v2.md`):
   - Push-now column: slots_selling no longer pushed; units_per_slot_day pushed by the duplicate rule.
   - Experiments: none new. Reason: a 21-day experiment from day 70 ends on day 91, the day the goal is read, so it can inform no decision.
   - Open questions 8 to 11 added (see below).

Goal, aim, done-checks, milestones and experiment table are unchanged.
