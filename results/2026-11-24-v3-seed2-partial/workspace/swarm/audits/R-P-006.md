## 1. Direction
Right. Cash is the top measure, worker minutes are the constraint, and slots_selling and depot cover are the drivers that move with them. The tree adds up to the ledger. The aims (minutes median at least 450, slots at least 22, depot cover 1.0) are the current standing aims and are not changed here.

## 2. Is the design the cause?
Partly. Three things in the environment are missing:
- **Information:** no progress line after day 149 (47 working days), no dispatch record on disk, depot cover not charted. The pulse and the diagnostician cannot tell whether the operator ran.
- **Incentive:** the rules and the charts have no visible cost for idle minutes, since cash clears every guardrail.
- **Unsettled:** whether the operator ran and stopped early, or was not dispatched. The design cannot settle this from the rules alone. Rules 14 and 15 are not changed until it is.

Not the cause: the rules themselves. Rule 14 (supplier first) and rule 15 (refill until 480) already say what to do. Changing them now would be a second change on an unsettled fact.

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the information gap)
- **spec.md v3 -> v4** (copy in history/spec.v3.md):
  - Check added: every working day has a progress line, and every dispatched day has a dispatch record; the pulse counts lines per working day, target 100%.
  - Cadence: the orchestrator writes `log/dispatch.jsonl` each working day (day, time, what was dispatched, what came back). The operator's line in progress.md is a named record.
  - Open question 10 added: whether the operator ran on days 150-209, settled by the clock and the dispatch record.
- **rules/operator.md:** no change. Rule 16 already requires the line.
- **aims.md:** no change (aims v10 stand; see section 6).

## 4. Prediction (testable by the charts)
- **Progress lines** per working day rise from 0 of the last 60 working days to 100% from day 210, read by the pulse at point 31 (day 217).

- Revert: if progress lines are still missing at point 31 (day 217), or minutes are below 180 at day 224, revert the spec check and diagnose the dispatch path (not the rules).
