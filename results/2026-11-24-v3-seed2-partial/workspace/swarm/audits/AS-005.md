## Why the redirect cannot be met as the work is written
1. **Stock lead time.** Stock lands 4-10 days after order. A slot emptied now can only be refilled from stock already in the depot, so 20 selling and 24 stocked depend on depot cover ordered a lead time ahead. Point 19 closes on day 133; the earliest stock ordered on day 130 lands on day 134 (zuidas). The stock aims are therefore judged at point 20 (days 134-140).
2. **Order of the day conflicts.** Brief v5 rule 14 says refills take the first 270 minutes and supplier work comes after. Aims v7 and S-005 say supplier work is the first claim (225 min). A day that refills first leaves little time for ordering, which is the constraint. The brief is the operator's instruction, so this is the likely cause of the depot falling behind, but I have not read progress.md to confirm it.
3. **Depot cover is not measured on charts.** Spec open question 9 is still open. Without it, the constraint is judged only from the ledger, and the system cannot chart it.
4. **Worker-time records disagree.** S-005 quotes a median of 270 minutes used. The charts' worker-time row shows a 7-day median of 5 minutes, latest 430, 3 days unused. Both cannot be right for the same window. The 420-minute aim is set on the clock reading in charts; the auditor should check which figure is correct.
