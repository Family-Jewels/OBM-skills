## 2. Is the design causing it?
Partly. The operator rules (v3) already say to order the missing stock first, and the sequence is right. Nothing in the design shows whether supplier work ran: depot cover was not a measure, and idle minutes were visible only in a pulse. A daily check that reads only cash lets a falling stock driver hide behind a good top number (D-P-004, cause 3). That is the environment fault the design can fix.

The cause of the supplier step not running is not yet confirmed. The daily tool-call log for days 71-77, progress.md, supplier receipts and check_offers records were not in the review set (D-P-004, "evidence not seen"). This change does not depend on that answer; the new log line in section 3 will show it.
