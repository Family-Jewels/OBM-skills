# Redesign R-A-001 (day 118, point 17; responds to D-A-001, day 112; designer)

Trigger: D-A-001 (diagnosis, day 112). Read: D-A-001, charts.md (day 119 close), aims.md (v7), spec.md (v2), rules/operator.md (v4), AS-004, R-P-004. Nothing else changed.

## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is above its floor and is not the problem. Slots selling is short (latest 5, median 7 at day end, aim 24 stocked), and it is short because stock does not reach the machines. Idle capital is 5,516 (96% idle) and worker time is 210 minutes idle a day. The aims (20 selling, 24 stocked, depot cover 1.0) stand. The tree is right: depot cover feeds stocked, which feeds slots selling, which feeds revenue. Only the depot cover link was unmeasured on the charts.

## 2. Is the design causing it?
Yes, in two places:
- **The ordering trigger is reactive.** Operator v4 orders a product when its slot is already empty or short. Supplier lead times are 4 to 10 days, so the depot can never get ahead of the slots. The fault is in the rule, not the worker's effort.
- **The stock driver is not charted.** Depot cover is in spec v2 but not on charts.md, so the top measure (above floor) hid the short driver, as D-P-004 and D-A-001 found.

Not confirmed: whether supplier work ran on days 71-112 and where the 340 minutes a day went. progress.md has no entries for those days. The change below does not depend on that answer; progress.md will show it if the log is written.

## 3. The change (smallest, two files)
- **spec.md v2 → v3** (old copy: `swarm/history/spec.v2.md`): depot cover gets an order trigger, "order when cover would not last the supplier's lead time plus one point"; a daily check that no product's cover runs out before its lead time has passed; open question 9 asks the system to chart depot cover.
- **rules/operator.md v4 → v5** (old copy: `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`): the supplier rule moves from "when the slot is empty" to "when depot stock would run out before lead time plus one point"; ordering ahead is the aim, and idle cash is there to buy the lead time. The cash guardrail is unchanged.

Not changed: the goal, aims (aims.md v7), guardrails, the time split, the charts (system-written), the log format.
