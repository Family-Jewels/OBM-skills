# Redesign R-P-001 (day 17, point 2 of 52; after D-P-001)

Trigger: D-P-001 (day 12): slots selling short, 9 of 72 at day 12; cash below the exemplar; worker time and capital idle while stock does not reach the machines.

## 1. Is the direction right?
Partly. The top measure (cash) and the driver tree are right: slots selling and stock in the machines move revenue. The aims are right. The stated constraint is not: aims.md says worker time, but the clock shows 47% used (median 225 of 480 min), and the stocked count is 5 of 72 at day end. The binding limit is stock getting into the machines. The aims stay as they are; only how the work is sequenced changes.

## 2. Cause in the design
The daily sequence in aims.md puts refill first and ordering last, with "order only what cash allows". Nothing ensures stock is in the depot before a refill trip, so refill minutes produce no stocked slots. The operator brief has no rule for depot stock or for lost trips, and no daily record of the calls that would show it. This is a gap in the environment (information and sequence), not a lack of effort.

## 3. Change made (smallest that addresses it)
- `rules/operator.md` v1 -> v2 (copy in `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`): read depot stock first; order first when the depot cannot cover the next day's refills (five slots' worth, about 50 to 90 units); refill from the depot; a trip with no stock is skipped and recorded; progress.md records depot units and the result of each restock, order and offer call.
- `aims.md` daily sequence: supply step before refill (copy of v1 in `swarm/history/aims.v1.md`). Aim values and the time split are unchanged. The supply step uses the 60 min supplier share already planned.
- Constraint restated in aims.md as provisional, for the Strategist to confirm.
- `spec.md` unchanged (v1). The goal, the aims and the checks are not changed.
