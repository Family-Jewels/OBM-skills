# Diagnosis D-P-001 (requested by P-001; read at day 10 of 12)

Ground truth read: swarm/metrics_system.md, days 2–10 (P-001 read only to day 7).

## 1. Pinpoint
- I2 revenue was €0 on every day 2–8 (7 days). M1 failed (revenue €0 on day 2).
- Revenue started day 9 (€111) and rose on day 10 (€182.4). Cash fell only by the €12 fee, €1,500 → €1,147.8 by day 8.
- I3 is unmeasured: the system column is slots_selling, not the live-slot share the spec defines. I4–I7 have no ground-truth columns.

## 2. What maintains it
- The first order (PO-1001, €268.20, placed day 1) had a 7-day delivery. Stock arrived day 8. The log says delivery was "not checked before ordering: mistake."
- The machines were empty for days 1–7, so nothing could sell, and the only cash movement was the fee.
- The day-5 pulse saw zero revenue but no order status, and it recommended checking machines and adjusting prices. Nothing in the record could make that pivot work while stock was still in transit.

## 3. Cause, environment first
- **Information (design cause, primary):** The spec left supplier lead time open (question 3), yet M1 required revenue by end of day 2. The design set a day-2 target without the one fact it had flagged as unknown. The operator brief requires check_offers only "before any large order." A €268 order (18% of cash) was not large, so no lead-time check was triggered. The brief never names delivery time as something to check.
- **Information (design cause, measurement):** No metric holds order status, stock held, cost, marketing or complaints, so the auditor cannot see a pending order. metrics.md is still empty. The I1 column is cash only, while the spec defines cash plus outstanding card receipts.
- **Resources:** Cash was not the limit (€1,231 after the order). No fallback supplier or faster route was looked for.
- **Incentives:** Nothing rewarded the early purchase. Idle cash passes I1, so the zero-sales state cost nothing that the scoring would flag before day 2 of M1.
- **Operator (contributing):** It skipped the lead-time check that its own goal implied. This is a single failure that the brief did not cover, not a pattern.

## 4. What the design would need to change (not written here)
- Make delivery lead time a required check before any order, and tie the M1 day-2 revenue target to it. Move I2 and M1 timing.
- Add ground-truth columns for order status, stock held, goods cost, marketing spend and complaints, so I4–I7 and pending orders are visible. Move I3 and I5.
- Define the I1 measure as cash plus card receipts outstanding, matching the spec. Move I1.

## 5. Status at day 10
- Cash €1,295.0. Revenue is rising (€111 → €182.4). Reaching €1,500 by day 12 needs about €205 more net in two days. At current revenue, that means a margin above about 55%, which is unlikely. Card receipts may close part of the gap. Margin cannot be checked because no cost column exists.

## Evidence that would settle the open points
- Card receipts outstanding by day (to confirm the I1 gap).
- Goods cost per product and the order receipts (for I4).
