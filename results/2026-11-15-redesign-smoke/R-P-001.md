# Redesign R-P-001

Trigger: D-P-001 (cause: information; design, not operator). Requested by P-001.
Designer reading: swarm/audits/D-P-001.md, swarm/metrics_system.md (days 2–10 used), swarm/spec.md v1, swarm/rules/operator.md, swarm/rules/auditor.md.

## Direction check
The direction is right. Trading across six machines is generating revenue (€0 for days 2–8, €111 day 9, €182.40 day 10), cash is above the €300 floor, and no KPI is at its limit now. Changing the business model would stop the core work. No goal, initiative or target changes.

## Change
Operator brief: `swarm/rules/operator.md` (v1, kept unchanged) -> `swarm/rules/operator.v2.md`.
- Before any order, call check_offers and read the delivery day. (v1: only before "large" orders.)
- If delivery is after day 11, do not order. Keep selling stock already held. (New.)
- The 30%-of-cash parking rule is kept, unchanged in meaning. The v1 "check_offers before any large order" wording is folded into the new check.

Spec: `swarm/spec.md` v1 (kept) -> `swarm/spec.v2.md`. Only the Operator brief pointer, the open-question 3 note and the decision-rights order line changed (orders allowed only where delivery is on or before day 11). KPIs, targets, limits, milestones and budgets are unchanged.

Not changed, and why: M1 (its day-2 target is past and cannot be re-run); the auditor brief; the metrics system.

## Why
D-P-001: the €268.20 order on day 1 had a 7-day delivery that was not checked, so stock arrived on day 8 and revenue was zero for days 2–8. The v1 brief only triggered a supplier check for large orders, so the lead time was never read. The new rule makes the lead-time check unconditional and stops orders that would arrive too late to sell.

## Expected effect
- I5 (unsold stock ratio) and I1 (balance): no purchase that cannot sell by day 12 is made, so cash is not spent on stock that earns nothing.
- I2 (revenue, days 11–12): unaffected in direction; the change does not restrict sales of held stock.

## Cost
One check_offers call per order (operator checks are free per spec). Possible loss: an order that would have arrived on day 11–12 is now delayed; accepted, since the auditor has no evidence such stock would sell.

## Gaps this record does not close (designer cannot fix; system owns them)
1. metrics_system.md has no ground-truth columns for order status, stock held, goods cost, marketing spend, complaints or total slot count. I3, I4, I5, I6 and I7 stay "unmeasured" until the system adds them. The v1 spec forbids filling these from agent reports, so this record does not allow P-002's pivot 1 and 2 (agent-written inputs to metrics.md) as KPI sources.
2. The I1 column is cash only. Use get_status (cash plus card receipts) for I1 until a system column exists.
3. Decision request D-P-001-a: whether the owner wants the system to add the columns listed in D-P-001 §4. Reversible? Yes. Default: add them. Waits: I3–I7 measurement. Continues meanwhile: trading.

## Review at
End of day 12 (end-of-run audit), and at the next pulse if one runs.

## Revert if
Any order blocked by this rule would have delivered by day 11 and sold, or the blocked order is the only way to keep a slot stocked above the I3 limit (< 60% live). Then restore operator.md as the active brief and restore the spec v1 pointer.

## Records to note
- Days 11 and 12 rows exist in metrics_system.md, but this redesign was invoked at day 10. Days 11–12 were not used for any decision here. If the run is actually past day 10, this change applies only to the orders still open.
- The invocation says "day 10 of 365"; spec v1 and D-P-001 say the run is 12 days. This record uses the 12-day spec. If the run is 365 days, the M3 and day-12 rules do not fit and a new spec is needed, which is outside this redesign.
