# D-012: capacity-split-drift (day 87 of 91)

**Walk-down.** cash_gain_7 is beyond its upper limit, so the top measure is not the fault. Its drivers: unit_cost_paid_30d rose from 0.93 to 1.09, and slots_selling is flat within limits.

**Pinpoint.** Sourcing (check_offers, order_goods) runs about once every 3 to 4 days (order_goods: 25 calls in 87 days, none in point 12) against 40% planned. Restock is 80% of logged minutes, but logged minutes are about 34% of the 480-minute day (Resources, median 165). On the 480 base, restock is about 27%, close to its 28% plan. The drift is sourcing and idle time: 315 minutes a day unused, 89% of capital unused.

**Maintained by.** Restock is the only same-period payoff, and a day closes on it (operator Done line). Rules 12 and 17 restock with no stock condition and outrank aims.md. No control has been in force since C-008 (day 77), so nothing caps or checks the split.

**Cause.**
- Information: Pursuit divides by logged minutes, not the 480-minute base the spec and aims use, so the chart overstates restock. Quote margins are unrecorded, so sourcing has no visible outcome.
- Resources: not binding. Time and cash sit idle; the empty depot is the constraint, and only sourcing refills it.
- Incentives: restock pays now and closes the day; sourcing pays late. A rule outranks the aim.
- Worker: follows the immediate payoff. Its own log is empty, so its reasoning cannot be checked.

**What would change.** Controls: a checkable floor, at least one order_goods per point while cash is at or above reserve (Facts already counts it); no restock cap, per D-011. Designer: Rules 12 and 17 and the Done line require check_offers and order_goods before a day closes on restock. Measures to move: order count, unit_cost_paid_30d, slots_selling.
