## Changes
1. **swarm/rules/operator.md, version 2 -> 3** (prior copy: swarm/history/operator.v2.md).
   - Rule 12 (restock): restock needs a same-period get_storage_inventory read, and only products that read shows in stock. This is the in-stock rule from aims.md and matches the control already in force.
   - M-001's rule 13 is reverted as written and replaced: where a machine has empty slots and the depot shows no stock for that product, check_offers with at least 2 quotes, then order_goods the lowest quote within reserve, before the next restock call.
   - Done line: no longer rewards restock alone. A day counts as done only with restocks limited to products the depot read showed in stock, and the planned shares met.
   - Capacity split: referenced, not copied. swarm/instructions.md already carries the split (its Capacity split section, assembled from aims.md each period), so the worker already reads it. Copying the numbers into the brief would create a second copy that goes stale at each redirect (the redirect-to-worker lag was 11 days in AS-003).
2. **swarm/spec.md, version 2 -> 3** (prior copy: swarm/history/spec.v2.md).
   - Driver tree, top row: horizon target EUR 5,500 at day 91 for the floor and aim lines. This is the spec's own day-91 estimate (log-linear between the exemplar's day-30 EUR 3,228 and day-365 EUR 61,219, which gives about 5,530; low confidence). The goal (maximise the day-91 balance) is unchanged, and the figure is not a cap on the result.
   - Open question 8: REQ-005 closed. The clock stays a separate row; which binds stays open with the human (REQ-008).
3. Not changed: swarm/controls.md (risk branch), swarm/aims.md, swarm/instructions.md (system-assembled), the goal.
