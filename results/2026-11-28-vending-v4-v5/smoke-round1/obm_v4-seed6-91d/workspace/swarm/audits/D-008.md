# Diagnosis D-008 (day 63 of 91)

Fault: capacity-split-drift (from P-008; REQ-006, asked 12 times). Last point: restocking 65% vs 40% plan, sourcing 15% vs 25%, pricing 2% vs 10%, buffer 0% (charts Pursuit; P-008's 73% was an earlier window).

Driver path: cash celeration x1.06 vs floor x1.45. Slots_selling (/1.29) and revenue (/1.18) moved; slots stocked 7 of 72 while capital is 6% in use (charts Resources).

What maintains it: restock_machine is the only action that fills slots in the same period, so it pays at once. Sourcing (19 order_goods in 63 days) and pricing pay late or unseen, so they are dropped. The drift survives the control: the depot-read gate in force since day 56 cut restocking only from 73% to 65%, because it limits when a restock may happen, not how much time it takes. The instructions say it "does not cap restocking or set a share of time."

Cause:
- Information: the 40% split lives only in aims.md as a plan; nothing enforces it. Stock quantity per product is not in ground truth (REQ-010, REQ-013), so restock size cannot be checked.
- Incentives: the restock-first rule in the operator brief (REQ-009) and its done-condition (REQ-012) reward restocking; no in-day signal rewards sourcing or pricing.
- Resources: median worker time used is 195 of 480 min, so time is not short for sourcing or pricing; they are crowded out by ordering, not scarcity. Resources and Facts shares disagree (REQ-008 covers this).
- Worker: the worker follows the brief's order. Its log is absent (REQ-002), so its reasoning cannot be checked.

Change needed: a control that caps restock_machine at 40% of a point's minutes (enforced, not only planned), and a brief change so sourcing precedes restock (designer).
