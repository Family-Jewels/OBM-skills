# Diagnosis D-004 (day 39 of 91)

Fault: unit-cost-signal-worse (P-005). unit_cost_paid_30d is 1.039, beyond the upper limit.

**Pinpoint.** Unit cost rose from 0.82 (days 2-8) to 0.93 (day 17) and 1.01-1.04 (days 31-39). The same lines cost more over time: chocolate bar 0.69 (day 1), 1.01 (day 16), 1.26 (day 27). Crisps were bought at 1.97 against a 2.30-2.50 price.

**Maintained by:**
- Information: no record shows unit cost by supplier at order time. Lead times vary (PO-1010 took 10 days), so D-003's "Zuidas 3 days" is not fixed.
- Incentives: no aim names unit cost. Stock that fills slots pays at once; a dearer buy never shows.
- Resources: not short. check_offers costs 25 minutes; 425 of 480 minutes sit idle (median); check_offers ran 7 times in 39 days.

**Cause:** environment (information, incentives). The worker had the tool and the time, and nothing showed it cost. D-003 stands.

**Would change:** a unit-cost aim, and a lowest-offer check before each order. Move: unit_cost_paid_30d.

**Limit:** prices paid are recorded, offers open at each order are not, so supplier choice and price drift are not yet separated.
