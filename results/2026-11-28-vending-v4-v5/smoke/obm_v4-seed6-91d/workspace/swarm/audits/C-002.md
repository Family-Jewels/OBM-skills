- Before calling wait_for_next_day, while worker minutes remain before 17:00: check get_machine_inventory; if a slot is empty and the depot holds stock, restock those slots until the minutes run out or no such slot is left. No waiting with minutes unused while both hold. Review at point 5.

## Why

D-002 located the waste in the gap between idle worker minutes and empty slots with stock in the depot. The spec already states the rule ("do not end the day with minutes unused while a slot is empty and stock is in the depot"), but nothing checks it, so it is not held. The control is the smallest thing that closes that gap: a check before the day-end wait, not a ban on any action and not an aim.

Charts support the risk: slots_selling sits at 9 of 72 at day 27, the depot is the constraint on fills, and the Resources row shows median 70 idle minutes a day. Cash is 1905.62 against the reserve of 120, so the reserve guardrail is not at risk from this control, and the control spends only minutes that would otherwise go unused.

## What I did not add

- No control on the worker's reasoning or behaviour: D-002 says the worker cause is not established; logs carry no reasoning and the depot contents on days 18-19 are unrecorded.
- No control on ordering or depot cover. D-002 assigns the depot cover rule (REQ-009) and the fill-minutes aim and Pursuit table (REQ-007) to the designer. Those are method and aim work, not controls.
- No change to the worker-time row. D-002 says it does not match the simulator clock (REQ-006, designer). The control reads the clock directly, so it does not depend on that row being fixed.
