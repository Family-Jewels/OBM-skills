Fault: unit-cost-signal-worse (P-003). Latest measured day: 27. unit_cost_paid_30d is 0.939, beyond the upper limit 0.91.

By my arithmetic from quoted prices (not a measure), Zuidas is about 0.19 a unit cheaper on the last-7-day sales mix.

**Maintained by:**

- Resources: price comparison is check_offers only (25 min; 6 calls in 27 days).

**Cause:** environment (information, incentives). The worker chose the fastest-looking supplier with the information it had.

**Would change:** an order check against the lowest current offer among suppliers no slower than the one chosen (controls); a unit-cost aim with lead times shown (designer).
