# C-001: control change (day 18 of 91)

Trigger: REQ-005 from D-001 (split-off-plan-sales-unread).

Change: controls.md, none -> v1. No controls.md existed before (instructions.md reads "(no controls yet)"), so there was no old version to copy to swarm/history/.

Control added: before each wait_for_next_day, call get_sales_report that day if it has not been called since the morning. This is a check before the close, not a block: it stops no selling, restocking or pricing, and it costs one 25-minute call against a median of 240 idle minutes a day (charts.md Resources and Facts).

Why: D-001 pinpointed the skip. Closes proceed with no precondition, so the skipped read costs nothing on the day, and the read pays off only the next day through pricing. A check at close is the smallest control that makes the read a condition of the close. The aim-side fault (Source and buy and Notes and day close shares against plan) is the designer's to fix through REQ-004, not a control.

Cost: about 25 minutes a day of worker time, inside idle capacity. No effect on the top measure's floor or aim.

Risk check against the charts: cash (1207.2) and revenue are above their limits after M-001, so the risk is not yet visible on the top measure. revenue_per_slot is routine variation. The control is kept because it is cheap and the diagnosis shows a live fault; it goes at point 5 if the reads have not followed.

Caveat: charts.md counts get_sales_report calls (11) and wait_for_next_day calls (16) but does not align them by day, so today I cannot tell what share of closes had a same-day read. The control's review at point 5 needs that measure; see Requests.

Review point: 5 (days 29 to 35). At review: keep if closes without a read still occur and revenue_per_slot is still at risk; loosen or remove if reads are on every close and the risk has closed, or if the control has cost more than it guards.

Revert if: the read is made on every close and the worker's capacity split moves toward a shortfall on any driver the Pursuit table charts.

Prediction: revenue_per_slot up within 3 points
