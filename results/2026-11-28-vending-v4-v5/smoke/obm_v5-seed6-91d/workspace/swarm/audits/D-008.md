**Worker.** check_offers runs before orders (18 calls, 20 order_goods, pairing not measured). No record shows whether the lowest quote was taken. No worker fault is established.

**What would change.** Controls: consider a sourcing share fixed before restock, not only a check. Evidence that settles price versus mix: per-order price paid against the lowest quote that day, and per-product unit cost.
