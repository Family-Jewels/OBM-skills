## Change

1. **Amended C-002** (the restock cover check), one line in controls.md:
   - Cover now counts an open order only if it arrives within the supplier's quoted lead time. An open order that lands after the depot runs out is not cover, so it no longer blocks a new order (REQ-029).
   - Added: for a product the depot holds none of, do not restock that product that day. Restock the others (REQ-031, restock share).
   - Restocking itself is not stopped, and no aim or split is set. Review point moved from 6 to 7.
2. **C-001 and C-003 unchanged.** C-001's review point is 7 and C-003's is 7; neither is due at point 6.

## Why

- **Restock into an empty depot (D-006).** The control's rule for restock was that it runs even when the depot is empty, so each fill on those days produced nothing. The latest charts still show restock as the largest time share (Facts: restock_machine 74% of time spent, against the 70% plan; Pursuit gap +4 points). D-006 places the cause in the restock path, not in the split. Skipping an empty product costs nothing sellable, because there is no stock to fill with, and it leaves the time for Source and buy. This is a limit on one restock, not a capacity split.
- **Cover ignores transit (REQ-029).** Under the old text, "no order for it is open" blocked a new order even when the open order arrived after the stock-out. The amended rule counts arrival day, so a late order does not count as cover. The within-lead-time rule also stops a new order each day while one is already in the pipeline.
- **Unit cost (REQ-031, second half).** `unit_cost_paid_30d` is 1.011 at the latest point, inside its limit of 1.02 (1.021 on day 41 and 1.026 on days 32-37 were above it). The quote check before each order (C-003) is already in place and not due. D-006 itself says no per-order price is measured, so no control can be tied to the buys that raised cost. That fact is with the human under REQ-020. I have not added a control for it.
- **C-002 review, due at point 6.** The risk is still live: slots_selling fell from 15 to 9 over days 32-34 (metrics_system.md) and rose again to 17 by day 36 and 15 at day 42. The check costs one order_goods (about 25 minutes) on days cover is short, and it is inside idle time (median 120 minutes a day, Resources table). Kept and amended, review at point 7.
- **Revert condition (C-002, kept).** Revert if slots_selling stays flat or falls while Source and buy's share stays under plan for 2 points, or if the empty-depot skip leaves a product unrestocked for more than a few days with no open order.
