# D-004: restock-share-over-plan (day 24 of 91; ground truth to day 24)

**Fault (pinpointed):** restock-share-over-plan. Pursuit, last point: restock_machine takes 70% of time spent against 45% planned; Source and buy 8% against 15%. P-004's 76% and 2% are an earlier point, not a finding.

**Walk down:** cash is beyond its upper limit and improving, so the top measure is not the fault. Slots_selling is flat at 12 to 15 against the 60-by-day-30 aim: a shortfall for the Strategist, not diagnosed here. The fault is worker time going into restock while the sourcing that feeds it runs short.

**Evidence:** 85 restock calls; 11 slots stocked at day end of 72 available; 61 slots and 1,401 capital idle. Median worker time used is 400 of 480 minutes, so 80 minutes a day go unused. Order_goods: 12 calls. Stock-outs on D13 to D14 followed the D10 to D15 restock run. Supplier lead times are 5 and 15 days (progress.md D1, D6, D16).

**What maintains it:** a restock is the only action that moves the aimed number (slots) the same day, and a visible stock-out is fixed at once. Sourcing pays off only after 5 to 15 days. Nothing shows the worker its own actual share: Pursuit is system-only, and the worker's log says it "will balance" with no mechanism. The overrun has no cost in any control.

**Cause:**
- Information: the split is a plan share with no actual share shown to the worker and no link to supply.
- Resources: capacity does not force the overrun. 80 minutes a day are unused and idle capital could fund buying, so the split is a choice.
- Incentives: immediate reward for restock, delayed reward for sourcing, no check on the overrun.

**What would need to change:** a control (received automatically by the controls owner): a check that sourcing runs before a day's restocks when stock on hand is short, and a daily check against the split. Designer: show the worker its actual share in instructions.md and pair each restock visit with a sourcing step.

No new facts beyond the charts and metrics_system.md.
