# D-001: split-off-plan-sales-unread (day 11 of 91)

**Walk.** Top measure (cash 990.8 vs exemplar 2133.6) shows no signal; cash guardrail ok. The fault sits in the capacity split: "Price and read" 21% vs 15% planned; "Notes and day close" 0% vs 10%.

**Pinpoint.** Across 10 wait_for_next_day calls (day closes), get_sales_report was called 5 times. Two notes written in total. Restock takes 66% of time spent against a 45% plan, while the median day uses 250 of 480 minutes.

**Maintained by.** A close proceeds at once with no precondition, so skipping the read costs nothing. The read pays off only the next day (pricing, revenue_per_slot). A restock shows at once against the slot aim (12 vs 36 by day 10). P-001 found the skip, but no control yet blocks it.

**Cause, environment first.**
- Incentives (primary): the wrong path passes at once; the right path pays a day late.
- Resources: not the limit. A read costs 25 min against 230 idle minutes a day; 823 capital idle.
- Information: read-before-close is one line in the Notes, not a close step with a defined output. The operator brief sets no per-close requirement.
- Worker: follows the path that pays; secondary.

**Would change (for controls).** A close that requires a same-day sales read, or a check at close. Measure: share of closes with a read (target every close), and the "Notes and day close" share.

**Caveat.** P-001 (day 7) saw no reads; Facts now show 5. Charts carry no per-day timing, so I cannot tell whether reads rose after P-001 or simply with time. Per-day timestamps from the step log would settle it.
