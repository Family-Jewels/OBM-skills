## What I changed

1. **Added one control** (controls.md, fourth line): restock_machine and swap_item together may use no more than the restock share in aims.md's capacity split, 40% of the 480 minutes (192 minutes now). Once the day's restock minutes reach that share, no further restock that day. Sourcing, pricing and the day close continue.
   - It bounds restock time (REQ-015, and the bound option in D-009). It is a limit, not an aim: it refers to the share the improvement branch sets, so the number moves with aims.md and the controls owner does not set it.
   - It does not stop the core work. Restock is capped at about four calls a day, not closed. Sourcing has the idle minutes to run.
   - Review at point 9.
2. **Kept C-001, C-002 and C-003 unchanged.** None is due (all review at point 9).

## Why this and not the other options

- **A sourcing step before each restock** (the other option in D-009) would add about 25 minutes to each restock visit, roughly the idle time, and would not stop a restock that runs anyway. The bound acts on the overrun directly and leaves sourcing to the aims.
- **Tightening the buy rule for unit cost (REQ-021).** The cause is still open between the price paid and product mix. C-003 already requires a check_offers before each order_goods and a buy at the lowest quote that meets the cover rule. Ground truth does not record the price paid per order, or the lowest quote that day, so I cannot tell whether the day-55 buy was above the lowest quote. A stricter rule now would be a control for an unshown risk, and it could delay orders the cover rule needs. Deferred (see Dispositions).
- **A stricter empty-depot skip for restock (D-009's "thin depot" point).** The depot's units per product are not in ground truth (REQ-030, open to human). A control keyed to depot units cannot be checked by the system yet. The time bound does not need depot data.

## Cost

- No new call is required on any day. The control only stops restock calls once the share is reached. Those minutes go to sourcing or sit idle; median idle time is 120 minutes a day (Resources).
- The control costs nothing in cash. The one cost is the restock calls it withholds on days the depot holds stock for empty slots. Watch for that (see Revert).
