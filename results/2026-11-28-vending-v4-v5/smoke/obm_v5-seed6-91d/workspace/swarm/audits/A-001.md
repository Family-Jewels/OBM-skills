2. **The top measure's floor and aim lines run to the wrong target.** charts.md (top measure) sets the floor at x1.45 a point and the aim at x2.09 a point, both to 61,219 by the horizon. spec.md (line 30) gives 61,219 as the day-365 exemplar, and line 116 puts "the day-365 figure as a target" out of scope. Against 61,219 the floor is unreachable for any plan, so a breach would send the Strategist after a wrong line. Also, charts.md "Since redesign" labels the slots_selling mean (14.57) as "beyond the upper limit"; that limit is 17.86, so the label reads the single latest point, not the mean. Fix: the designer names the day-91 ceiling for the top measure (key `top-target-day91`); the human confirms the floor-line target and the label fix (key `floor-line-target`). Fix the floor and aim lines before the Strategist's next strategy.

3. **FAULT: the worker's capacity runs over its split, and the Strategist's redirect never reached the worker.** The Pursuit table (charts.md) shows restock at 70% of time spent against a 45% plan and sourcing at 5% against 15%. The gap was 25 points at day 7 (P-001), 31 at day 21 (P-004), and is 25 at day 23. P-004's corrections (day 21) were not acted on: restock is still 70%. The split in force is still the day-0 one: aims.md reads "As of day 0 (after the initial design, spec v1)", no AS-001 aim update exists in swarm/audits/, and charts.md says "Strategy to aims reaching the worker: none yet." So S-001's 70% split was never adopted, the 45% plan is the one measured, and the worker follows neither. The organizer's record O-001 says "the aim update followed" (see finding 4). Fix: the designer writes aims.md from S-001 now, or declines it with a reason, and states the Pursuit plan basis (REQ-007). The designer also answers the open request to pair each restock with a sourcing step (D-004, key `split-sourcing-share`). Fault key below.

4. **O-001's claim is not shown by the ground truth, and its diagnosis points at the wrong link.** O-001 (day 23) says "P-004 caught it on day 21 and the aim update followed", and attributes the slow loop to strategy latency, then set strategy_every from 3 to 2 (escalations.md, day 25). The ground truth shows no aim update after S-001 (see finding 3). The slow link is the designer's aim update, which a faster Strategist cannot fix. Also, charts.md shows REQ-003 as in_progress although S-001 disposed it as done; the system's status lags the disposition. Fix: the human reviews the configuration change against the ground truth (key `organizer-diagnosis-latency`); the organizer's next review tests its prediction ("Strategy to aims reaching the worker" shows a value) and records a miss as a miss.

5. **The Strategist's latest split pulls against the diagnosis of the constraint.** S-001 (day 21) names restock conversion as the constraint and sets restock at 70% and sourcing at 12%. D-002 (day 18) found slots emptied when the depot ran out and sourcing at 3% against 15%, and asked for orders before restocks. D-004 (day 24) found 61 of 72 slots empty and 1,401 capital idle while restock runs, with supplier lead times of 5 and 15 days. Restock is the only same-day payoff, and it is also the path that empties a thin depot. slots_selling did rise from 13 (day 21) to 18 (day 23), which S-001 may cite; the Strategist should show that it still holds once the depot is the limit. The Strategist should reconcile the constraint in its next strategy and say, against the outcome, what the M1 and M2 misses show.

6. **Over-control whose cost premise has gone stale: C-001.** C-001 (day 18) justified the close-check (a get_sales_report before each wait_for_next_day) as "inside idle capacity: a median of 240 idle minutes a day". charts.md now shows a median of 405 minutes used, 75 idle, and worker time at 84%, the binding resource. The check costs about 25 minutes a day, roughly half a restock fill, and it competes with the restock path that is the constraint. Its prediction (revenue_per_slot up within 3 points) is not yet judged, and revenue_per_slot is routine variation. The control is cheap per call, but its justification is no longer true, and its review point (days 29 to 35) comes after the time constraint has been binding for a week. Fix: the controls owner re-costs C-001 now on current idle time and keeps, loosens or removes it. Cost of the whole review layer so far: $0.11 (charts.md, System health), which is not the problem.
