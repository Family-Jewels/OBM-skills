## Claims and verification

1. **Claim: "D62 (Sat): opening 4190.21"** (cash at the open of day 62)
   - Evidence: swarm/metrics_system.md, day 62 row, cash = 4190.21. Exact match.
   - Cross-check: the day-61 row gives cash 4001.61, which matches the worker's D61 "Cash 4001.61 at open" line; the day-63 row gives 4243.81.
   - Verdict: **verified**.

2. **Claim: "Sales read done (102 units D61)"** (units sold on day 61, read on the morning of day 62)
   - Evidence: swarm/metrics_system.md has 102 units on the **day-62** row (revenue 224.0) and 90 units on the **day-61** row (revenue 190.0). So the figure 102 exists in ground truth, but its day label does not line up with the row labelled 61.
   - The table does not state which day its units and revenue columns date to. Two cash comparisons point in different directions: open-61 to open-62 cash change is 188.6 against revenue 190.0 on row 61, and open-62 to open-63 is 53.6 against revenue 53.8 on row 63. Both fit a same-day reading with small costs, which would put 102 units on D62, not D61. A one-day-lag reading also fits if purchases took about 35 to 48 in those intervals, and the log does not record purchases for those intervals. The evidence cannot settle the day label.
   - Verdict: **unverified** as to the day. The figure 102 is in ground truth, and no mismatch in the figure itself is shown. The day label needs the system to define the columns (see Requests).

3. **Claim: "reputation 1.00"** (reputation at the open of day 62)
   - Evidence: no ground-truth measure in swarm/metrics_system.md or the charts Facts table records reputation. Open request REQ-032 (K-003, "reputation-unmeasured") records the same gap.
   - Verdict: **unverified**.

Not sampled: the fills (five priced slots), PO-1027 (about EUR 99, D64 to 66), and the PO-1025 and PO-1026 arrivals. The system counts order_goods and set_price only as totals across the run, and records no per-order or per-day arrival, so none of these can be checked from ground truth.
