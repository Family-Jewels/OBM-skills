Trigger: D-P-002 (day 18). Cause found there: stock arrives in small lots on 3–4 day cycles, and the rule "do not re-order until the quantity has arrived" makes buying serial. Slots empty while worker time (420 min/day idle) and capital (€1,378 idle) sit unused. No measure or aim covers stock received, so the plan could not reach 24 slots even with the price and cash rules met.

**1. Direction.** Right. Slots_selling is the top driver under revenue, and the 1a1 aim (24) is the right one. The tree was missing the link that feeds it: stock in. The goal and the aims are unchanged.

**2. Change (smallest that addresses the cause).**
- Spec v2 (v1 at `swarm/history/spec.v1.md`): new driver 1a1b "stock received per point", under 1a1, pushed first. Ground truth: supplier receipts and the order log. Guardrails: unit cost < price, cash ≥ €120 after each payment, no stock held beyond lead time plus one cycle. Open questions 7 and 8 added: supplier maximum order quantity (not yet recorded) and the stocked-vs-selling reconciliation (charts day 18: 5 stocked, 14 selling). New check row for stock-in.
- Operator brief v2 (v1 at `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`): the reorder-wait rule is replaced. Order only the uncovered quantity (target cover minus on hand minus in transit), within the cash headroom above €120 and the supplier's maximum. The no-double-pay benefit is kept, because in-transit units count toward cover.
- Not changed: aims, goal, reserve, guardrails, milestones, cadence, worker-time split.

**3. Prediction.** Stock received per point rises above its current level within the next point (days 22–28). slots_selling moves above its centre (7.76) and reaches 24 by day 28 (the end of point 4; this is the stretch aim from S-001). Revenue follows slots_selling. Cash stays ≥ €120 every day.

**4. Revert if** cash falls below €120 on any day, any unpaid day occurs, or stock held beyond cover for 2 points with slots_selling still flat. Then restore `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`.

**5. Cost.** Capital tied up in stock up to the headroom above the reserve (about €1,000 today). Some order-call minutes, which the 10% buying share covers.
