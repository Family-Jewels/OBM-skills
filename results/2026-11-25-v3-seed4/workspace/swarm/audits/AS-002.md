**Charts vs S-002.** Three numbers in S-002 do not match the system:
- S-002 says median 295 of 480 minutes used. 295 is the day-50 latest reading. The 7-day median is 120 (25%). Worker time is idler than S-002 states. The conclusion (time is not the constraint) holds and is stronger.
- S-002's aims for days 43-49 lapsed on day 49. Slots selling were 5 on day 49 and 7 on day 50 against a target of 16. Cash was 2,331 on day 49 against the 2,681 aim (floor 1,985): floor met, aim missed.
- Refills per point are not charted, so the 40-refill trigger cannot be read directly. The 7-day median of 120 minutes a day bounds refills at about 18 a point (at 45 minutes each), well under 40.

**Feasibility of the redirect as the work is done now:**
- Time: 9 more slots at 45 minutes is about 405 minutes over 7 days, about 58 a day. Within the refill share. Not the limit.
- Cash: about 2,120 above the reserve. Not the limit.
- Stock: the limit. Orders have been €60-90 lots on a 3-4 day lead time. The depot ran empty on days 34, 36-37 and 41, and PO-1013 slipped. The supplier maximum order quantity is still unrecorded (spec open question 7; P-006 item 5), and stock per point (1a1b) is a spec driver with no chart.
- The cover rule in the operator brief already allows ahead-of-need orders. The route has not delivered stock at the pace the aims need.

**Decision.** The redirect cannot be met by the split alone, which is S-002's own condition. The aims are written as S-002 directed (carried forward from day 49 to day 56) and the split is kept. Meeting them needs the stock-in route to change: order sizing ahead of need across the three locations, the maximum quantity recorded, and stock received charted. That changes how the work is done, so it goes to a redesign (queued behind any fault diagnosis, spaced ≥ 3 points from R-P-002). Spec and rules are not changed here.

**What would make this "no":** if the supplier's maximum quantity is large enough to cover 16 slots' lead time plus one cycle in one or two orders, the operator can reach the aims by ordering bigger within the existing rule. The redesign then reduces to instrumentation and a sizing check. The maximum quantity is the first thing to record.
