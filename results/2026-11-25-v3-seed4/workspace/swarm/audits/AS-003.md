**Feasibility of S-003's aims as the work is done now:**
- Time: not the limit. Refills at 45 min need about 26 a point at a 35% share (1,176 min). Buying at 35% (1,176 min) covers well over 20 order and check calls a point at the 5–10 minutes the progress log shows.
- Cash: not the limit. Cover for 14 slots at about 50 units a slot, at about €1 a unit, is about €700 of stock on hand plus in transit, against about 2,850 above the reserve.
- Stock: the limit, and the route cannot deliver it on time:
  - Depot about €8 on day 67; about €390 of goods arrived days 59–66; PO-1029 (€162, day 70), PO-1028 (€95, day 69) and PO-1027 (€194) are the stock in transit.
  - Lead time follows order size. Orders of about €90 arrived in 2–4 days; PO-1008 (30 cola, bakker) took 10 days; PO-1027 (€194, zuidas) takes 12 days. R-P-007 assumed fixed per-supplier lead times (zuidas 3–5 days). The ledger contradicts that.
  - Spec open question 7 (maximum order quantity) is now answered for sizing: no cap up to 60 units a line. So the maximum is not the limit. Lead time is.
  - Noordzee refuses orders under €90, so small orders have a floor too.

**Decision.** The aims are met only if stock lands in time, and the current route does not deliver it: a larger order takes longer to arrive, and the cover formula in spec v3 and the operator brief v3 assumes it does not. Bigger orders within the existing rule therefore cannot reach the aims, which is the condition AS-002 set for "no". The aims are written as S-003 directed (carried to point 11, not lowered). The worker split is kept as S-003 set it, converted to minutes. The fix belongs in the spec and the operator brief, so this is a design change. It is queued, and spaced at least 3 points from R-P-007 (day 67), so the earliest is point 13 (day 85), or sooner if a diagnosis lands first. Spec and rules are not changed here.
