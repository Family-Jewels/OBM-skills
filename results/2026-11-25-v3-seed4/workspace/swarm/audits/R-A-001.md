# R-A-001: redesign, day 224 (point 32), after diagnosis D-A-001 and aim update AS-010

**Read:** D-A-001, AS-010, S-010, charts.md (day 224), aims.md (AS-010), spec v4, rules/operator.md (brief v6).

## 1. Is the direction right?
Partly. The aims point at the right driver: stock in is the constraint, and stock in is short (7-day median 8 stocked at day end, aim 20). Worker time is spare (median used 0 on the clock at day 224; 215 idle minutes a day at day 217). Two things in the design were wrong:
- **The cover target was summed over selling slots only** (spec 1a1b; brief v6). With 61 of 72 slots empty, the target barely grows, so nothing pulls stock into empty slots. This is the cause that fits the data.
- **The design named worker time as the constraint** (spec 1e and Resources; brief rule 12 and "Why"). That sends the operator to skip refills for minutes while minutes sit idle.

The minute count is not the lever. The worker-time aim is also judged on a 7-day median that hides idle days.

## 2. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)
1. **Spec v5** (`spec.md`; v4 in `history/spec.v4.md`): cover target per planned slot, summed over every slot assigned to the product, selling or empty. Stock in named as the constraint; worker time as spare capacity. Open questions 9 to 11 added (stock charts, clock against log, aims stale at the time of writing).
2. **Brief v7** (`rules/operator.md`; v6 in `history/operator.v6.md`): the same cover target; rule 12 no longer skips a refill that keeps a slot selling while minutes are unallocated.
3. **Aims basis** (`aims.md`; AS-010 in `history/aims.AS-010.md`): worker time aimed on the daily total, not the 7-day median. Values unchanged.

Not changed: the goal, the €61,219 line, the €120 reserve, the aim values, the cadence and the rest of the brief.

## 3. Prediction (testable on the charts)
- **Which measure:** stock in (slots stocked at day end, 7-day median), then slots_selling.
- **Which way:** up.
- **By when:** slots stocked 7-day median at least 14 by day 238 (point 34); at least 20 by day 245 (point 35). slots_selling 7-day median at least 14 by day 245.
- **Also expected:** the stock-in share of minutes is used (idle minutes fall), and cash stays above 120.

## 4. Cost and revert
- **Cost:** more cash goes into stock. Headroom above the €120 reserve is about 6,300 at day 224, so the first large orders are within reach. Unsold stock ties up cash.
- **Revert:** if by day 245 the stocked median is still below 14, or any guardrail is breached (cash below 120, a sale below unit cost), restore `history/operator.v6.md` and `history/spec.v4.md` and open a diagnosis.
- **Not fixed here:** the worker clock against the log (D-P-012). The charts show a 7-day median of 0 minutes used; D-A-001 read 265. The 360-minute aim is not judged until this closes. The stock-in chart and depot charts (spec open question 9) are the system's to add; the operator reports them until then.
