# R-P-014: redesign, day 285 (point 41), after diagnosis D-P-014 (day 266) and Strategist S-013 (day 273)

## Is the direction right?
Yes. Stock in is the constraint: slots stocked is 1–3 against an aim of 20, cash is about 60 times the €120 reserve, and minutes did not move stock in (AS-010 to AS-013). Worker time is spare. The tree's order (stock in → slots selling → revenue → cash) is right. The top measure is not pushed directly, and the aims are not lowered.

## Is the design causing it?
Probably yes, in one place. Brief v7 sizes each order to a cover target: units per slot per day × (lead time + 7 days), summed over the slots assigned to the product. Stocked slots are what the sum is over, so the target shrinks as stock falls. Orders of €90–190 every 2–5 days then leave the depot empty, and refills stop at depot level (D-P-014 §4). v7 fixed the selling-only sum but kept the same base. The order log, which would confirm the base, is not on disk (D-P-014 §7). So this is the hypothesis, not a proven cause.

## Change (smallest that tests it)
1. **Spec v5 → v6** (`swarm/spec.md`, previous `history/spec.v5.md`): the cover target is summed over the planned slot count per product (the slot plan in `aims.md`), not the slots stocked now. Open questions 12–14 added: slot plan, the unresolved R-A-001 revert clause, and record conflicts.
2. **Brief v7 → v8** (`swarm/rules/operator.md`, previous `history/operator.v7.md`): the same change in the operator's ordering rule; never sum over stocked slots alone; write the planned count per product on each order day.
3. **Aims** (`aims.md`, AS-013, written day 283): values unchanged. A slot plan of at least 20 is added. The aims are not lowered.

No change to the guardrails, the reserve, the goal or the exemplar. No change to worker time or the clock (D-P-012 stays open; the clock is not designed around).

**Revert clause (R-A-001, day 245):** triggered and not executed. v6 had the same selling-only base, which v7 corrected, so restoring v6 would bring back a known fault. This record overrides that clause and says so. Revert instead on a cash guardrail breach.
