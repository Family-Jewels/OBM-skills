# R-P-008: redesign, day 96 (point 14). Spec v4, operator brief v4, aims for points 14-15

**Trigger:** D-P-008 (day 84): slots_selling 4 of 72 latest against centre 8.1; idle worker time and capital; no stock charts; aims lapsed.

**Direction: right.** The top measure (cash) runs through slots selling, and slots selling runs through stock in. The tree is correct. The fault is in the design of the stock-in route, not in the aims.

**Cause in the design (checked against spec v3, operator brief v3 and aims.md):**
1. A per-visit cap of €90–100 sits in `aims.md` as a "spec v3" guardrail, but spec v3 does not contain it. It came from a log pattern (progress log, day 61) and was carried forward as if it were a goal rule. With a cover target of about 50 units a slot across 14 slots (about €700 of stock), the cap forces many small orders, each waiting on its supplier's lead time.
2. The cover formula assumes fixed per-supplier lead times. The ledger shows kanaal and noordzee at 4–5 days recently, even at €125–135 (PO-1033, PO-1035, PO-1037), zuidas at 12 days for €194 (PO-1027), bakker at about 10 days. Lead time by order size is only partly supported; the zuidas and bakker cases fit it, the kanaal cases do not.
3. Worker time sat idle (median 0–65 of 480 min) while the split read as a plan, so unused minutes had no route to refills.
4. Information: no charts for stock on hand, in transit or deliveries; check_offers not recorded.

**Not verified:** `log/work.jsonl` (days 78–84) is not in the workspace, so the calls cannot be checked against the diagnosis's "would settle" test. The system should supply it.

**Change (smallest that reaches the cause):**
1. Remove the €90–100 cap. At kanaal and noordzee, order the full uncovered quantity on each visit, within cash headroom and the stated maximum (none seen up to 60 units a line). Zuidas orders stay near €120 until its lead time at size is recorded.
2. Use lead times measured from the progress log per supplier, not fixed figures.
3. The worker-time split becomes a floor per driver. Minutes one driver leaves unused go to refills of slots with stock in hand.
4. Daily depot line in the progress log (units on hand and in transit per supplier, deliveries, check_offers results) until the system charts it.

**Not changed:** goal, exemplar, €120 reserve, cover formula, milestones, checks, cash guardrails, decision rights.
