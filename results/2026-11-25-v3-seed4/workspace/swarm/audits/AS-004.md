**Feasibility of S-004's aims as the work is done now:**
- Stock in: the limit, and the current route cannot meet it. Orders of about €90 arrive in 3-5 days; larger orders have taken 7 to 12 days (PO-1027, PO-1031, PO-1008). The depot is short and the stock in transit is small: PO-1037 (kanaal, about €126, due about day 102) and PO-1036 (noordzee, due day 99). Cover for 14 slots at about 50 units a slot needs far more than the orders now in place can deliver in the point.
- Time: not the limit on the stock-in side. Stock in at 50% (240 min a day) is ample for order and offer checks.
- Cash: not the limit. The cover for 14 slots is well within the cash above the reserve.
- Slots and revenue: follow stock. Refills at 25% (120 min a day, about 2-3 refills) are enough for the stock that arrives.

**Decision.** S-004's aims are met only if stock reaches cover in time, and the route does not do that: the lead time follows order size, and the cover formula in spec v3 and the operator brief v3 assume it does not. Bigger orders within the current rule do not reach the aims. The aims are written as S-004 directed (carried to point 14, not lowered). The worker split is S-004's, converted to minutes. The fix belongs in the spec and the operator brief, so this is a design change.
