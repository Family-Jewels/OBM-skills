# Fidelity check F-E-001-2: E-001 price step on AI-HOUSE:SNACK

Invoked: day 15 of 91. Experiment Start as written in E-001.md: day 7. Evidence: system ground-truth snapshots only. Pre-Start snapshot: swarm/snapshots/00006.md (day 6). At-Start snapshot: 00007.md (day 7). Latest: 00015.md (day 15). Intermediate: 00010 (day 10), 00011 (day 11), 00012 (day 12), 00013 (day 13), 00014 (day 14). The orchestrator's `Applied` and `Re-check` lines in E-001.md, and any orchestrator notes, were not used as evidence. Prior check F-E-001-1 (day 10) was read for context only.

## Criteria from E-001.md

- Change: raise the top-selling product in AI-HOUSE:SNACK by 10% from its current price (stroopwafel-2pk, 1.50 → 1.65), one product only, no other change to the machine (no restock of other products, no swaps).
- Slice: AI-HOUSE:SNACK. Control: the same machine's prior week; the other five machines unchanged.
- Other running experiments must not touch the slice.
- Clock: Start day 7, End day 28.

## What I compared

1. **Price on the slice, latest vs pre-Start.** AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 (stroopwafel-2pk): day 6 empty (no product); day 7 price 1.50; day 10 1.50; day 11 1.50; day 12 **1.65**; day 13 1.65; day 14 1.65; day 15 1.65. Expected after the Change: 1.65 (1.50 × 1.10). Observed in ground truth: 1.65, which matches. Every other AI-HOUSE:SNACK slot (A2 to D3) is empty on every snapshot from day 6 to day 15.
2. **Timing of the Change.** The E-001 Start is day 7. Ground truth shows the price still at 1.50 on days 7, 10 and 11 and at 1.65 from day 12. So the Change was not in place at the designed Start; it went in between the day-11 and day-12 snapshots. (The orchestrator's line says day 11; the day-11 snapshot shows 1.50, so the change was made after that snapshot was taken, on or after day 11.)
3. **Units outside the slice, day 7 vs day 15.** The set of occupied slots is identical on both days, and no price changed:
   - AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A1 energy-drink-500ml: 2.5 → 2.5
   - AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A1 sparkling-water-500ml: 1.5 → 1.5
   - AI-LOUNGE:SNACK:A1 choco-bar-100g: 2.0 → 2.0
   - MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A1 sparkling-water-500ml: 1.5 → 1.5
   - MAIN-LOUNGE:SNACK:A1 dropjes-100g: 1.8 → 1.8
   Quantities on these slots fell or rose as sales and restocks happened, and no slot gained or lost a product. No price change at any location outside AI-HOUSE:SNACK.
4. **No other change to the machine.** AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A1 went from 8 (day 7) to 0 (day 15) with the same product and price, consistent with sales, not a swap or restock into the slot. No AI-HOUSE slot other than SNACK:A1 changed product.
5. **Other running experiments on the slice.** swarm/experiments/ holds E-001 and E-002 only. E-002 drives MAIN-LOUNGE machine units (Start day 14) and does not reference AI-HOUSE:SNACK. No overlap.
6. **Storage.** The storage table is not per machine, so it cannot show which machine a top-up went to. Stroopwafel-2pk storage moved 2 → 32 between day 14 and day 15, and the slot itself remained at quantity 0 on day 15 (not yet filled). Choco-bar and dropjes storage also rose 2 → 32 on day 15, and energy-drink storage rose 10 → 20. None of these products is in the AI-HOUSE:SNACK slice. Whether any of these top-ups went to AI-HOUSE is not visible in the snapshots; I did not treat it as a mismatch.

## Mismatch

- **The Change was late against the designed Start.** E-001 says Start day 7, and the Change was made on day 11 or 12. The experiment did not run as designed: the price was 1.50 during the days the design says the treatment was running (days 7 to 11). The clock was moved by the orchestrator to day 11, with an end on day 32 (its `Clock` note). That clock change is not in the experiment file as the designer set it, and I did not verify or accept it. The experiment's own pre-condition list does not cover timing.
- The Change content itself (product, +10%, slice, no other slot) is correct in ground truth. The mismatch is the timing, not the content.

## Verdict

Criterion 1 (change on the slice): met from day 12 on, not at the designed Start of day 7.
Criterion 2 (no change outside the slice): met.
Criterion 3 (no other running experiment on the slice): met.

Because the Change was not in place at the designed Start, and the experiment's clock has been moved in the orchestrator's notes without a designer decision, the experiment was not run as designed and cannot be judged on the current file.

## What would settle it

- The designer (not the orchestrator) decides whether E-001's clock restarts at the change date (day 12 on the ground-truth snapshot, or day 11 if the orchestrator's time is accepted) or keeps day 7. Write that into E-001.md as a designer decision.
- If the clock restarts, re-invoke this check at the new Start. The price, slice and outside-slice checks above would then be run against the snapshot taken before that Start.
- The day-7 to day-11 window (price at 1.50) should be recorded in the experiment's log as pre-change time, so the control baseline is not contaminated.

Fidelity: fail
