# Fidelity check F-E-001-3: E-001 price step on AI-HOUSE:SNACK

Experiment E-001 as written in `swarm/experiments/E-001.md`: Change = raise the top-selling product in AI-HOUSE:SNACK by 10%, hold 21 days, "no other change to this machine (no restock of other products, no swaps)". Slice = AI-HOUSE:SNACK. Pre-conditions: price change matches (one product, +10%); no other slot in AI-HOUSE:SNACK changed; M1 baseline week in the log.

Timing is not a criterion for this check, per the request. The clock is not assessed here.

Evidence: system ground-truth snapshots only.
- Latest: `swarm/snapshots/00021.md` (day 21).
- Pre-change: `swarm/snapshots/00010.md` (day 10). The price change first appears in `00012.md` (day 12); `00011.md` (day 11) still shows 1.50.
- Intermediate snapshots 00011 to 00020 were read for when AI-HOUSE slots changed.
- Also read: `swarm/experiments/E-002.md` (to check for overlap), and the prior audits F-E-001-1 and F-E-001-2 for context only.

The orchestrator's `Applied` and `Re-check` lines in E-001.md were not used as evidence. Where they conflict with the snapshots, the snapshots were used.

## What I compared

1. **Price change on the slice, latest vs pre-change.** AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 stroopwafel-2pk: day 10 at 1.50, day 21 at 1.65. Expected 1.50 × 1.10 = 1.65. Observed 1.65 on 00021 and on every snapshot from 00012 to 00021. **Matches.**
2. **Product identity.** The product on AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 is stroopwafel-2pk in both the pre-change and latest snapshots, so the same product was repriced. I could not verify that it is the top-selling product: the snapshots carry no sales ledger, so this claim is unverified rather than mismatched.
3. **No other change to AI-HOUSE:SNACK (slice-internal).** Compare day 10 with day 21:
   - AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A2 was empty on day 10 and holds **nuts-mix-75g at 2.0** from day 17 onward (00017 shows 14 units, 00018 shows 10, 00019 shows 6, 00020 and 00021 show 2).
   - AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A3 to D3 are empty on every snapshot, day 2 to day 21.
   - **Mismatch:** a product that was not on this machine before the experiment was placed into AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A2 between day 16 and day 17. This is the "no restock of other products, no swaps" clause, and the pre-condition "no other slot in AI-HOUSE:SNACK changed". Both are unmet.
4. **No other change to the machine (AI-HOUSE, outside SNACK).** AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A2 was empty on day 10 and holds **cola-500ml at 2.0** from day 17 (00017 shows 4 units, 00018 shows 0, then 0 through 00021). This is also a new product placed in the same machine, outside the slice's snack section, and is part of the same "no other change to this machine" clause. **Mismatch.**
5. **Units outside the slice, day 10 vs day 21.** Every unit that held a product on day 10 kept the same price on day 21:
   - AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A1 sparkling-water-500ml 1.5 → 1.5
   - AI-LOUNGE:SNACK:A1 choco-bar-100g 2.0 → 2.0
   - MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A1 sparkling-water-500ml 1.5 → 1.5
   - MAIN-LOUNGE:SNACK:A1 dropjes-100g 1.8 → 1.8
   - AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A1 energy-drink-500ml 2.5 → 2.5

   No 10% step appears on any unit outside AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1. **Met.**
   - Observation, not a verdict criterion: between day 16 and day 17 the two LOUNGE machines also gained new products (AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A2 cola, A3 oat latte, SNACK:C1 crisps; MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A2 energy drink, A3 oat latte, SNACK:A2 choco, SNACK:C1 crisps). The experiment's Control assumes "other five machines unchanged", so this assortment change weakens the control, though it is not the price change. The designer should note it.
6. **Other running experiments touching the slice.** `swarm/experiments/` holds E-001 and E-002 only. E-002 drives a print campaign and machine units at MAIN-LOUNGE (Start day 14) and does not reference AI-HOUSE. **No overlap.**

## Mismatches

- **Assortment change on the slice (criterion 3).** AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A2 gained nuts-mix-75g on day 17, and AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A2 gained cola-500ml on day 17. E-001 allows no other change to this machine (no restock of other products, no swaps). The Change as written was not made on the slice only.
- **The orchestrator's `Applied` line states "No other change to AI-HOUSE:SNACK; no swaps."** The day-17 snapshot contradicts that. I did not use the line as evidence either way; I report only that it does not match ground truth.

## Verdict

- Criterion 1 (price change on the slice, as written): **met** (+10%, 1.50 → 1.65, on AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1, held through day 21).
- Criterion 2 (change on no unit outside the slice): **met** for prices. Assortment changes on the other machines are noted above and are not part of this criterion.
- Criterion 3 (no other change to the slice; no other running experiment touches it): **unmet** for the no-other-change clause (nuts-mix on SNACK:A2 and cola on DRINKS:A2 on day 17). **Met** for the other-experiment check (E-002 does not touch AI-HOUSE).

The experiment was not run as written, so it cannot be judged on its current form.

## What would settle it

- Find out who placed nuts-mix-75g and cola-500ml into AI-HOUSE on day 16 to 17, and why. The ledger or the tool log for those days will show it.
- Either remove those two products from the machine, or have the designer amend E-001.md to allow restocking or new products on AI-HOUSE for the duration. Only the designer can make that amendment; it cannot be made by the orchestrator's note. If it is amended, re-invoke this check on the amended file.
- Confirm from the sales ledger that stroopwafel-2pk was the top-selling product before the change (criterion 2 above is currently unverified).
- The designer should decide how the day-17 assortment changes on AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE affect the control baseline.
