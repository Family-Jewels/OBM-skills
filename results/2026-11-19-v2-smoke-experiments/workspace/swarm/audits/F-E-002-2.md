- Campaign existence, channel, location, day: met. Snapshot 00021 (ground truth) has a marketing campaign record: `{"channel": "prints", "day": 14, "location": "MAIN-LOUNGE", "message": "Fresh snacks and drinks on every floor. Find your favourite at the MAIN-LOUNGE machines, stocked for the working week."}`. Snapshots 00013 and 00014 have no campaign. Snapshot 00023 and 00024 show the same single record.
- Exactly one campaign: met. The campaign table in 00021 has one row. No other campaign appears in any snapshot checked.
- Copy: matches the `Applied` line's copy text. Verified against the snapshot record, not against the note.
- Cost: NOT verified. The snapshot has no cost field. The only source for EUR 8.00 is the orchestrator's `Applied` line and the progress note. No run record or ledger entry for day 14 exists in runs.jsonl.
- "Within the get_marketing_report limit" (limit prints 8, weekly 2): NOT verified. No get_marketing_report output is recorded in ground truth. The limit value appears only in the orchestrator's notes.
- Traffic multiplier 1.10: not recorded in ground truth. Not a Change criterion, but it is part of the claimed effect.

Result: campaign made as written, met. Cost and limit compliance, unmet (no ground-truth source).
