# Fidelity check F-E-003-1: E-003 (fill within 24 hours at AI-HOUSE), day 36

Checker: obm-swarm-checker, experiment fidelity mode. Evidence: ground-truth snapshots only (swarm/snapshots/). The orchestrator's `Applied` line in E-003.md, its progress note and the operator-written runs.jsonl were read but not used as evidence.

## What I compared

- Slice: AI-HOUSE:DRINKS and AI-HOUSE:SNACK (both AI-HOUSE machines). Control: AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE.
- Start: day 33 per the file. The day-33 and day-34 snapshots do not exist in swarm/snapshots/ (the files go 00032, 00035, 00036). The latest snapshot at invocation is day 36 (00036). The latest snapshot before Start is day 32 (00032). I compared 00032 with 00036, and 00035 with 00036 for the recent window.
- Snapshots 00037 to 00043 also exist in the directory, although this check is invoked at day 36. I did not use them. They should be explained by whoever writes the snapshots.
- Change as written: "every unit of depot stock with unit margin > 0 is loaded into an empty AI-HOUSE slot within 24 hours of landing."

## Findings

**Criterion 1: change on every unit of the slice. Unmet (not verifiable from ground truth).**

- The rule is visibly operating on both AI-HOUSE machines. Between day 32 and day 36, depot energy-drink fell 30 to 20 and AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A1 rose 0 to 7. Depot oat-latte fell 10 to 0 and AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A3 rose to 9. Depot chocolate fell 26 to 8 and AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A3 rose 0 to 17. Stock moved from depot into the slice.
- Depot stock with empty AI-HOUSE slots, not loaded:
  - Sparkling water: 40 (day 32), 30 (day 35), 30 (day 36). It sat in depot across the day-35 and day-36 snapshots. AI-HOUSE:DRINKS has many empty slots (B1 to D3). Unit margin is not in ground truth. The only margin statement is the orchestrator's O-001 note ("set at 1.50"), which is a price, not a cost.
  - Tortilla chips: 10 on days 32, 35 and 36, never loaded into AI-HOUSE. Margin unknown.
  - Nuts-mix: 2 on days 32, 35 and 36, while AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A2 was empty on all three. Margin unknown.
- Stroopwafel: depot 0 on day 35, 60 on day 36, and AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 empty on day 36. The stock landed inside the last day, so it is within the 24-hour window. Not a violation on this evidence. Its margin is 0.85 (E-001 Applied: price 1.65 against cost 0.65), so the rule does apply to it.
- Whether these units landed more than 24 hours before the day-36 snapshot, and whether their margin is above zero, cannot be read from snapshots. Snapshots carry no landing times and no cost.

**Criterion 2: change on no unit outside the slice. Met on visible evidence; the day 33 to 34 gap cannot be closed.**

- Depot outflows between day 35 and day 36 match the AI-HOUSE fills (energy, oat-latte, chocolate). Remaining AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE changes are sales-sized moves (for example AI-LOUNGE:SNACK:A1 chocolate 14 to 13).
- Between day 32 and day 35 (no snapshots for days 33 and 34), depot dropjes fell 26 to 0 and depot stroopwafel fell 16 to 0. Only 10 dropjes are visible in AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE, and 16 dropjes plus 16 stroopwafel are not in any slot on day 35. They may have been loaded and sold out on day 33 or 34, into AI-HOUSE or elsewhere. The snapshots cannot say which.

**Criterion 3: no other running experiment touches the slice. Met.**

- swarm/experiments/ holds E-001, E-002 and E-003 only.
- E-001 (AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 price) has its clock from day 11 for 21 days, ending day 32, before the day-33 Start. The day-36 snapshot shows stroopwafel back at 1.50, consistent with its day-26 revert. It is not running at the check.
- E-002 touches MAIN-LOUNGE only and ends day 35.
- O-001 proposes an E-001 re-run on AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 but says to run it only if E-003 does not overlap. No experiment file for it exists, so it is not running.

## Mismatch

The file's Change is not shown on every unit of the slice from ground truth. Depot stock of sparkling water (30 units, held across two snapshots), tortilla chips (10, held across three) and nuts-mix (2, held across three, next to an empty AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A2) sits without AI-HOUSE loads. Whether that breaks the rule depends on unit margin and landing time, and neither is in ground truth. The report that the rule is in effect for the whole window is therefore not confirmed. The mismatch is between the file's Change and the snapshots, not a judgment about the orchestrator's intent.

## What would settle it

1. A ground-truth record of unit cost and unit margin per product, for each product in depot, on days 32 to 36 (for example a ledger entry or cost field in the snapshot).
2. A ground-truth record of landing times for each delivery, and of each load into an AI-HOUSE slot, so that the 24-hour rule can be checked per unit.
3. The day-33 and day-34 snapshots, to see where the 16 dropjes and 16 stroopwafel went.
4. An explanation of why snapshots 00037 to 00043 exist at a day-36 check.

## Feedback

- Fix for criterion 1: load sparkling water, tortilla chips and nuts-mix into empty AI-HOUSE slots where their margin is above zero, and record the margin in ground truth. Then re-invoke the check.
- Fix for criterion 2: the system should write snapshots every day, including 33 and 34, so depot outflows can be traced.
- Done well: the energy-drink, oat-latte and chocolate stock moved from depot into the AI-HOUSE slots, and the AI-HOUSE:SNACK:A1 stroopwafel stock landed within the window, showing the rule is working on the slice.

Result: criterion 1 unmet, criterion 2 met on visible evidence with a gap, criterion 3 met. Partial is fail. The clock does not start.

Fidelity: fail
