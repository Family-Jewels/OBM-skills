# Redesign R-P-005 (day 121 of 365, point 18)

Trigger: D-P-005 (day 112): slots selling below its lower limit since R-P-004; worker time idle; the depot empties between weekly orders, so refills fail (days 31, 79, 100: about 315 min lost). Read: D-P-005, charts.md (point 17), aims.md (day 115), spec.md (v4), rules/operator.md (v3), S-005, R-P-004.

## Is the direction right?

Yes for the top measure and its drivers. Cash is the top measure; slots selling and revenue per slot are the drivers that move it. The tree stands. The problem is upstream of the drivers: refills cannot load stock the depot does not hold.

## What maintains it

- The order cover is too short for the 7-14 day lead time plus the weekly offer cycle, so the depot empties between orders.
- Nothing stops a refill from starting when the depot is empty, so each failed refill costs 45 minutes and returns nothing.
- Failed-refill minutes were not measured, so the loss was invisible on the charts.
- The operator worked from a stale aims copy (P-010).

## Change (smallest that fixes the cause)

1. **spec.md, version 4 → 5** (old copy: swarm/history/spec.v4.md):
   - D3 guardrail: order cover is now the supplier lead time plus the 7-day offer cycle, up to the D1 aim (was lead time plus one sell-through).
   - New driver D4a, failed-refill minutes, target zero, under D4. Needs instrumentation (not yet in metrics_system.md); until then the system's clock reading is the source and the auditor checks it.
   - New check row: D4a, target zero, not passable by trying.
   - Operator daily cadence: read aims.md at the first action of each point; refills only where the depot holds the product.
   - Open question 7 now marks the chart-vs-log minute conflict (chart zero, log about 410 min on day 108) as open: the auditor settles it before D4 and D4a are judged.
2. **rules/operator.md, version 3 → 4** (old copy: swarm/history/rules-operator.v3.md):
   - Supplier rule: cover is L plus the offer cycle.
   - Refill rule: a refill whose product is not in the depot is not started; order it or skip and log the slot.
   - New rule: read swarm/aims.md when the point changes.
3. **aims.md:** not changed by the designer at this redesign. The day-115 version stands (see audits/AS-005.md, which corrects an overwrite of an AS-005 record made in error). No aims are in force for point 18; the Strategist must restate them (S-006, due day 126).

Not changed: D2 price and mix; the margin-per-slot method (open question 9); the aims; the goal.
