Trigger: D-P-002 (day 35 diagnosis, "redesign due"). Charts read: swarm/charts.md (day 42 point 6 and day-44 cash). Aims read: swarm/aims.md (point 4 aims still in force; not changed here).

## Is the direction right?

Yes. Cash is above its floor line (1,864.59 vs 1,476.35 at point 6) and celeration is x1.14 against a required x1.08. The top measure is moving the right way, so the drivers stay: D1 slots_selling (aim 15 selling) and D4 worker minutes (408 used). The tree is not changed.

The fault is in the environment, not the aims or the worker. The order rule caps stock at lead time plus one sell-through. That is safe for cash but leaves the depot empty when a refill block runs (D31: depot held 10 units, refill failed). Stocked slots stay near 9 of 72, and the worker's idle minutes follow from that.

## Change (smallest that fixes the cause)

Resources and incentives:

1. **Order sizing (spec D3 guardrail; rules/operator.md rule on supplier lead time).** Orders now cover each selling slot up to the current D1 aim, through lead time plus one sell-through, within the EUR 120 reserve. The phrase "and no more than that" is removed; it was the cap. The 15-selling aim is kept, not lowered.
2. **Depot check before each refill block (rules/operator.md, new rule).** The operator checks the depot holds each planned product before the block and logs refill failures for lack of depot stock. Depot stock is now a named resource in spec.md.

Not changed here (recorded as open questions 8 and 9 in spec.md): slot count reconciliation (11 selling vs 5 stocked, AS-001), and the margin-per-slot method (price finding, unit-cost comparison). Each is a separate redesign, spaced by the three-point rule.

Aims: aims.md is not changed in this record. Its point-4 figures are stale; the Strategist restates them at the next redirect, and the designer then writes the aim update (AS-002).
