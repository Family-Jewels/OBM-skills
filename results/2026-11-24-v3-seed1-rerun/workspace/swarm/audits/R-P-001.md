Trigger: diagnosis D-P-001 (day 10). Charts read: swarm/charts.md (day 15). Aims read: swarm/aims.md (point 0, unchanged). Spec read: version 1.

## 1. Is the direction right?

Mostly yes, on the measures. D1 (slots selling) and D4 (worker time, idle) are the right drivers, and the top measure is cash. The aims are correct and are not changed.

The diagnosis's mechanism is partly out of date, and the design should not act on it as written:
- The first refill did not wait on the supplier step. PO-1001 was placed on day 1, delivered on day 7 (arrived day 8), and all six machines were stocked and priced on day 8. The aim "all six machines with a margin-positive slot by end of M1" was met inside point 1.
- The board still shows T-0002 to T-0005 as open with 0 attempts. The board was not updated, so it does not show real status. The diagnostician read stale state.
- Orders were not gated by the weekly offer date: PO-1002 (day 9), PO-1003 (day 10) and PO-1004 (day 12) were placed off-cycle.
- log/progress.md now exists and records the events.

The real constraint is supply, not time. On day 12 every selling slot was empty. Worker time is idle (median 130 of 480 minutes) and capital is idle (1,195 of 1,239). The orders were small (EUR 75–117 each) against lead times of 2 to 14 days. The spec's D3 guardrail, "no stock beyond about two sell-through cycles", can be shorter than the lead time, so it forces stock-outs.

## 2. Change made (smallest that addresses the cause)

- spec.md: version 1 to 2. D3 guardrail changed from "no stock beyond about two sell-through cycles" to "order enough to cover the supplier lead time (7 and 14 days seen) plus one sell-through cycle, and no more than that". Open question 4 updated with the observed lead times.
- rules/operator.md: one rule added. "If a supplier's delivery takes L days, then order enough to keep each selling slot stocked through L days plus one sell-through cycle, within the reserve, because slots emptied while stock is in transit leave worker time and capital idle."
- Not changed: aims.md, the goal, the cash reserve (EUR 120), the minute budget, milestones, checks, cadence. The board is not changed; the record gap is noted below.

Old versions copied to swarm/history/: spec.v1.md and rules-operator.v1.md.
