## Adapter gap (not fixed here; the spec is not changed)

"Slots selling" (11) is greater than "slots stocked at day end" (5). A selling slot must be stocked, so the two cannot both be right for the same moment. Either they are taken at different times of day or one measure counts something else. The D1 aim cannot be judged until the system reconciles them. Recorded for the next redesign (spec Open questions).

## Can the redirect be met as the work is done?

- **D1 (15 selling) and D4 (408 used): yes.** About 163 minutes a day are spare after the 72-minute slack, and the spec already has the levers (refill, price, order). No design change needed.
- **D2 (revenue per slot): yes to hold.** Price and mix are already drivers in the tree (D2a, D2b).
- **M0 cash and the day-30 reference (EUR 3,228): no.** From 1,402 on day 26 the reference needs about +1,826 in 4 days (about 456 a day). Revenue is about 114 a day latest and gross margin is far below the ~176 a day the reference implies. More refills will not close it; that is what S-001 says.
- **Why this needs a design change, not only a different effort:** the spec's price driver (D2a) asks for "the price that maximises margin per day, read from observed response", but experiments are out of scope and no other method is given. The operator cannot find the margin-maximising price, so the margin lever S-001 names has no working method in the design. Unit cost (D3) has the same gap: there is no rule for comparing offers across suppliers beyond "at or below quoted offer".

## Decision

Design change needed: yes. The redesign of margin per slot (price-finding method, mix and unit-cost rule) is for the designer's next redesign record (R-NNN), not made here. Spacing: R-P-001 was adopted on day 20, so under the 3-point spacing rule the earliest slot is about day 41, unless a diagnosis takes it first.
