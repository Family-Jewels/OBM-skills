## 1. Is the direction right?

No, not entirely. The swarm has pushed worker minutes (D4) and refill count (D1) since R-P-006. Refills that find no depot stock cost 45 minutes and sell nothing, so the minute aims have missed for three strategies. The tree had no measure for the link upstream of D1: how much stock the depot holds when a refill is made. The diagnosis traced the shortfall there: orders cover a few days of sales, while the lead time is 7 to 14 days. Capital sits idle (about 6,670 of 6,900 on the chart), so the room is there. Direction changes: push order cover first, then refills that load stock. Minutes stay a reserve check and a constraint only once the depot holds product.
