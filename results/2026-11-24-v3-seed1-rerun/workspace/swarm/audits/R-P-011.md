## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The tree puts order cover (D3a) upstream of stocked slots (D1), and D1 upstream of revenue and cash. The evidence supports that: capital is 97% idle while 68 of 72 slots are empty, the depot is empty at refill time, and worker minutes do not bind while refills fail. R-P-010 pushed the right driver. The direction is not the fault.

What has failed is that the direction cannot be seen or verified. Three things hold it back: the order step has no record after day 218 (so no one can tell whether it happens), D3a and D4a are not charted, and the clock conflicts with the refill log (spec question 7). The swarm has been asked to push on D3a for three redesigns and has no reading of it.

## 2. Is the tree right?

Yes, with one fix. The order step sits last in the day's list (cadence v7: refills, then swaps, then orders). A day's minutes can run out before the order is reached, and an order skipped on an offer day leaves the depot empty for another week. An order placed first costs no refill today, because a delivery takes 7 to 14 days.
