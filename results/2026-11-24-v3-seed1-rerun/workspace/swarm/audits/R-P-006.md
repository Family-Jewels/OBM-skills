# Redesign R-P-006 (day 174 of 365, point 25)

Trigger: D-P-006 (day 163, point 23): slots selling falling (/1.14, wrong way); cash below the floor line; refills fail on an empty depot after R-P-005; the minute measure reads zero while the log shows 365 to 410 minutes a day (days 118 to 135); the log has no entries after day 135; aims.md still holds point-24 aims, which expired on day 168.

Read: D-P-006, charts.md (point 25), aims.md (point 24), spec.md (v5), rules/operator.md (v4), R-P-005.

## Is the direction right?

Yes for the top measure and its drivers. Cash is the top measure; slots selling, revenue per slot and worker minutes move it, and the tree still adds up. The refill is the right place to push, because refill minutes limit stocking and stocking limits slots selling.

The aim itself does not fit the capacity as work is done now. At 45 minutes a refill, 12 stocked slots need 540 minutes. The usable budget is 408, and the aim split leaves 138 of it for price, orders and reads, so at most about 6 refills a day. The D1 aim (≥12.6) cannot be met at the current refill cost. The aim is not lowered here; the refill cost has to change, or the Strategist has to restate the aim on a measured cost.

## What maintains it

- Refills start without a checked depot read, so 405 minutes were lost on days 31, 79, 100 and 123. The rule of v4 was in memory, not in the work, so it did not hold. R-P-005's revert clause was met (stocked median below 9 with depot failures continuing) and no revert or redesign followed. This designer missed it at the day-133 point. Not reverting now: reverting brings back failed refills.
- The number of units per refill and the minutes per refill are unknown (spec open question 1, open since the first refill). No refill has been logged since day 135, so the swarm has no record of either.
- The minute measure disagrees with the log (zero on the chart, 365 to 410 in the log). The adapter is the system's, not the designer's, so it is recorded as a gap, not fixed here.
- The operator worked from aims that expired on day 168. AS-007 was not written (the aims file still says point 24).

## Change (smallest that fixes the cause)

1. **spec.md, version 5 → 6** (old copy: swarm/history/spec.v5.md):
   - D4a source: the operator's depot read before each refill block, logged, as well as the clock per refill call. Guardrail: a refill starts only when that block's depot read shows the product.
   - Open question 1: answered from logged refill lines; the 45-minute cost and the D1 aim's reachability are stated there.
   - Open question 7: the chart-versus-log conflict is recorded with the days. A day whose clock reading is below its logged refill minutes is unmeasured for D4, not zero, and the diagnostician is called. The adapter is not changed here.
   - Cadence, operator daily: one depot read per refill block; each refill call logged with units and clock minutes.
2. **rules/operator.md, version 4 → 5** (old copy: swarm/history/rules-operator.v4.md):
   - Refill rule: one depot read for the block, skipped slots logged, refill only the rest. Replaces the memory-based check.
   - New rule: each refill call logged with slot, product, units loaded and clock minutes.
3. **aims.md:** not changed by the designer. The point-24 aims expired on day 168 and no Strategist redirect has set point-25 aims since. The Strategist is brought forward (protocol: top measure below its floor line at points 23 and 25; slots_selling celeration wrong way). Until it reports, the operator works to the point-24 aims as the last set; their D1 aim is restated only with the measured refill cost.

Not changed: the goal, the aims, the 72-minute slack (a guardrail the Strategist set; it is worth about 1.6 refills a day and is the first thing to revisit if the measured refill cost stays at 45), D2 price and mix, the margin-per-slot method (open question 9).
