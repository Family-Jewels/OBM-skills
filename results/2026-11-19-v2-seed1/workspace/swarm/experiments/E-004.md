# E-004: price step-down on AI-HOUSE snack rows A-B (price response, one-time)

Driver: machine_revenue, summed over the slice, daily (recorded by the system)
Slice: AI-HOUSE:SNACK, rows A-B (six slots: A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, B3)
Control: rest (AI-LOUNGE, MAIN-LOUNGE and AI-HOUSE drinks keep current prices)

Price stays >= unit cost + margin on every slot (AI-HOUSE:SNACK A1 nuts 2.4 -> 2.16 vs cost 0.78; A3 stroopwafel 1.8 -> 1.62 vs 0.58; B1 choco 2.1 -> 1.89 vs 0.91; B3 gummy 2.0 -> 1.80 vs 0.72; A2 nuts 2.4 -> 2.16 vs 0.78; B2 stroopwafel 1.8 -> 1.62 vs 0.58). No other change on the slice.
Prediction: by day 217, units per slot on the slice rise 20% or more vs control's same-period change, and machine_revenue on the slice rises >= 5% vs control's same-period change (difference-in-differences against its own pre-start line).
Guardrail: price >= unit cost + margin on every slot; cash >= 144 before each purchase; no purchase above the 60% rule (park it); loading (known fix 15) and ordering (known fix 17) continue on slice and control alike, so the slice is never left unstocked to keep it clean. Stop and revert the price on the slice if cash falls below 120.
Capacity: about 5 min/day for price checks (within the 48 min/day experiment budget; no other experiment is running).
Start: 197 (point 29). The clock starts when the checker's fidelity check passes, but not before day 197, and not before rows A-B hold stock (PO-1044 nuts due 188, PO-1045 stroopwafel and crisps due 197; gummy-bears has no open order, see spec known fix 17).
End: 217 (21 days inclusive, the minimum window: points 29-31).
Note: this is the only new experiment in R-O-003. Its driver is per-unit (machine_revenue, per machine), so the slice is judged against the rest. The price response curve is unknown (E-001 read too small to read at +10% on drinks; see V-E-001).
