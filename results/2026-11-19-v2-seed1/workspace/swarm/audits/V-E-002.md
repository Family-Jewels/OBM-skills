## Why this is too small to read

- The effect is inside the bounce, and only 3 points are during the clock.
- MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS shows 0 units and 0 stocked slots in the latest unit table. Half the slice was empty during the test, so the reading mostly reflects SNACK alone.
- E-002's file header still shows start point 4 and end point 8 (days 22 and 56). The charts show the clock restarted at day 78. The file is stale and should be corrected by the designer. The day-21 campaign also overlapped E-001's slice, so its residual effect contaminates E-001, not this clock.

## Is a larger or longer test worth it?

Not now. Capital is idle (about 4,005 unused), so cost is not the constraint. The constraint is the bounce: a 2-unit slice with a x1.61 envelope needs a lift well beyond 1.61 to read, which takes many more points than remain at this pace. A longer test is worth running only after MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS is restocked, so the slice is not half empty. Then test SNACK alone, for at least 8 to 10 points after restock, with a fresh pre-start snapshot and a fresh start order from the designer.

## Ruling

Effect inside the bounce; prediction not met; guardrails held on the data checked. Revert is not required (no guardrail crossed, effect not beyond the bounce in the wrong direction). Keep is not warranted (no effect beyond the bounce in the right direction).

Verdict: too small to read
