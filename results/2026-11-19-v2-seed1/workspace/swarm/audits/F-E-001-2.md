### Criterion 3: no other running experiment touches the slice

`swarm/experiments/` holds E-001 and E-002. E-002 (MAIN-LOUNGE print campaign policy) has the slice MAIN-LOUNGE (both machines), which includes MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS.

Ground truth: the day-28 snapshot's marketing table holds one campaign, channel `prints`, day 21, location MAIN-LOUNGE, message "Thirsty or peckish? Fresh drinks and snacks now stocked at the MAIN-LOUNGE machines." It is absent from the day-21 snapshot and present from day 22 on. It is the E-002 change. Its duration was 7 days, so it covered E-001's slice from day 21 to about day 28.

E-002's file says it was applied on day 21, that this was in error, and that it was suspended on day 25 with its effect "stays in the record as contamination of E-001's MAIN-LOUNGE slice." So E-002 touched the E-001 slice during the period when E-001's change was made and started.

## Mismatch

1. **E-002 touched the E-001 slice.** The MAIN-LOUNGE campaign (day 21, 7 days) is in the ground-truth marketing record and targets the same location as MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS. It was applied during E-001's Start window, and E-002 was not removed from the queue until day 25. This breaks the third criterion. The campaign is not a price change, so the first two criteria are unaffected.

2. **Open point, stated so it can be settled:** E-002 is now suspended, and its 7-day window ends around day 28. The snapshots do not show whether the campaign is still active. I judge criterion 3 as not met because the overlap happened during E-001's Start window and is recorded as contamination of the slice. I am not treating a suspended status as making the overlap disappear. This would be settled by: (a) a ground-truth snapshot showing no MAIN-LOUNGE campaign active after day 28, and (b) the designer or auditor re-basing E-001's clock to start after that date, with E-002 kept out of the running until E-001 ends.

Criteria:

- Change made on every slice unit as written: met.
- Change made on no unit outside the slice: met.
- No other running experiment touches the slice: **not met** (E-002's MAIN-LOUNGE campaign, applied day 21, in effect until about day 28).

## Fix

To pass, the overlap must be closed in the record: E-001's clock is re-based to start after the MAIN-LOUNGE campaign window has ended (after day 28), with a ground-truth snapshot confirming no active MAIN-LOUNGE campaign, and E-002 stays out of the running until E-001 ends (day 56). The price change itself needs no rework. The fix belongs to the orchestrator and the designer; I have not changed any experiment or snapshot.
