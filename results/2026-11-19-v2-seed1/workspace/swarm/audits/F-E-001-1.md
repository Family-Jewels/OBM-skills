## Mismatch

1. **E-002 touches the E-001 slice.** The print campaign at MAIN-LOUNGE (day 21, duration 7 days) is in the ground-truth marketing record and targets the same location as MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS, the E-001 slice. E-001's own file says that E-002 was queued over the same slice, and E-002's correction says it was applied in error and cannot be reversed. This is a second experiment touching the slice, which the third criterion forbids. The campaign is not a price change, so it does not violate the first two criteria, but it does break the third.

Criteria:

- Change made on every slice unit as written: met (all six priced slots at +10%, no other slot on the slice exists).
- Change made on no unit outside the slice: met (AI-HOUSE:DRINKS and all SNACK prices unchanged).
- No other running experiment touches the slice: **not met** (E-002's campaign at MAIN-LOUNGE, in effect from day 21).

Timing is not a criterion here. The price change was made on day 21, before the day-22 check, and the clock is not held against it.

## Fix

To pass, E-002's day-21 MAIN-LOUNGE campaign has to be stopped from affecting the E-001 slice: either the E-002 campaign must end before E-001's clock starts (for example, its 7-day duration is allowed to run out and E-001's start is moved to after day 27), or E-001's slice is redefined to exclude MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS, or E-002 is removed from the queue. The fix belongs to the orchestrator and the designer; I have not changed any experiment or snapshot.

## Done well

The price change was made exactly as written: +10% on all six priced slots, nothing else changed on the slice, and no change leaked to AI-HOUSE:DRINKS or to any SNACK machine. The E-002 correction on day 22 was honest about the overlap.

Fidelity: fail
