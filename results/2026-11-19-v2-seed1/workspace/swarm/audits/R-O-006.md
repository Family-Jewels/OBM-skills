## Why the design caused it
The operator brief still carried a sales test on loading (drinks rows loaded only if they sold in the last 7 days, known fix 16) and the "never fill a slot that will not sell" default. An empty slot sells nothing, so it never passes the test: the same trap that known fix 18 closed for ordering.

## Changes
1. **Known fix 19 (whole system, now):** load every priced empty slot with depot stock, no sales test, on every machine (AI-HOUSE and AI-LOUNGE drinks included). MAIN-LOUNGE drinks hold kept. "Never fill a slot that will not sell" retired. Depot stock is already paid for, and at day 365 unsold stock is worth nothing, so a fill is the only route to cash.
2. **Known fix 20 (end-game purchase rule, from day 352):** orders sized to what sells by day 365 at the slot's recent rate per stocked slot-day; placed only if delivery is on or before day 362; only where price exceeds unit cost. This is new: the O-006 "two full loads" sizing would overbuy at the end of the horizon.
3. **Oat latte** ordered under the end-game rule (O-006 fix 2). This is an operator action under the existing rule, not a new rule.
4. **Experiments: none set up.** A 21-day minimum from point 3 cannot finish with 14 days left; any verdict would be "too small to read". The O-006 candidates are not adopted:
   - Mid-day reload: its driver is not a recorded measure ("units per selling slot-day" is not on the list), and the constraint is the start-of-day load, not sell-out before noon.
   - E-005 re-issue: retired. Its fill-rule lift is now whole-system (change 1). Its clock never started.
5. Operator brief rules 19 to 21 changed in place; the E-005 running clause is removed. Previous versions copied to `swarm/history/spec.v7.md` and `swarm/history/operator.v7.md`.
