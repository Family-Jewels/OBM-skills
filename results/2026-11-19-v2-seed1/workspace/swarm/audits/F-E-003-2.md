# Fidelity check F-E-003-2: E-003 fill empty slots on AI-LOUNGE (day 131 of 365)

## What I compared

Ground truth only: `swarm/snapshots/00112.md` (day 112, the last snapshot before the Start on day 113) against `swarm/snapshots/00131.md` (day 131, now). Day 113 (`00113.md`) was read as a cross-check for the first fills. Snapshots after day 131 (`00132.md` to `00134.md`) exist and were not used, since the check is on the state at day 131. The E-003 `Applied` line and the orchestrator's log (`swarm/log/progress.md`, D113 and D128) were read only to know what to look for. They are not evidence.

Experiment file: `swarm/experiments/E-003.md`. Slice: AI-LOUNGE (DRINKS and SNACK sections, both machines). Control: AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE. Change: a stocked slot on the slice is filled from depot stock even when the snapshot predicts no sales for its product, with duplicate-slot sharing unchanged.

Prior check: `swarm/audits/F-E-003-1.md` (day 114) failed on the same ground. Nothing in the snapshot schema has changed since: the slot table still has only slot, product, price, quantity, unit cost and loaded day, and no fill-rule column appears in any snapshot up to day 131.

## Checks and results

1. **Change on every unit of the slice.** Not verified.
   - Between day 112 and day 131 every stocked AI-LOUNGE slot was reloaded (loaded-day column moved past day 112): DRINKS A1 cola (106 to 127), A2 oat (106 to 127), A3 sparkling (106 to 120); SNACK A1 nuts (103 to 130), A2 gummy (107 to 124), B1 mint (49 to 125), B2 choco (99 to 129), B3 stroopwafel (103 to 126), C1 crisps (100 to 121). DRINKS B1 and B2 (energy-drink) were empty on day 112 and loaded on days 122 and 128.
   - SNACK A3 nuts-mix (loaded 99) is still empty on day 131, with no product sold since day 99, while the depot held nuts-mix (34 on day 131, 80 delivered on day 129). It is an empty slot on the slice that was not refilled. The change's trigger is a "stocked" slot, so this may not be a breach, and the 60% rule may explain it. The snapshot cannot tell these apart. Noted, not a criterion.
   - **The snapshot does not record the fill-policy setting.** The change is a fill rule (fill slice slots even when no sales are predicted, versus the default of predicted sellers only). No column, per-slot or per-machine, carries it. The reloads show that stock moved onto the slice; they cannot show that the change, rather than the default, governed each unit. The orchestrator's log (D128) says it has no tool to set a fill rule and implemented the change as restocking. That is a statement by the orchestrator, not evidence, but it is consistent with the gap in the snapshot. The lever the change uses is not covered by the snapshot, so this criterion stays unmet.

2. **No change on any unit outside the slice.** Met, as far as the snapshot can show.
   - Control prices on AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE are the same on day 112 and day 131 (for example cola 1.9 and 1.87, oat-latte 2.6 and 2.75, energy 2.42).
   - AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE also reloaded slots in this window (for example AI-HOUSE SNACK A2 at loaded day 130; MAIN-LOUNGE SNACK A3 at loaded day 130). That is ordinary core restocking on control, which the file does not count as a violation. The snapshot does not show any control slot receiving a fill-rule change, which is the criterion, but this is the same gap as in check 1.
   - AI-LOUNGE prices are unchanged between day 112 and day 131 (DRINKS 1.87, 2.75, 1.76; SNACK 2.2, 1.8, 2.2, 1.6, 1.9, 1.5, 2.9), so no price change came in with the E-003 window.

3. **No other running experiment touches the slice.** Met.
   - `swarm/experiments/` holds E-001, E-002 and E-003 only.
   - E-001 (slice AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS and MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS) is closed. Its verdict is `swarm/audits/V-E-001.md` ("too small to read"). Its standing drink prices remain on AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS. That is a standing confound, not a running experiment.
   - E-002 (MAIN-LOUNGE) is closed. Verdict in `swarm/audits/V-E-002.md`. The marketing table in both snapshots shows only the day-21 MAIN-LOUNGE campaign, so no campaign runs on AI-LOUNGE.

## Notes (not criteria)

- The `Applied` line's gummy top-up of 11 on SNACK A2 does not match the depot draw (the depot went 8 to 0 and the slot rose 7 to 15 on days 113 to 114). Not a criterion.
- Cash, the EUR 144 pre-purchase guardrail and the cost claims are not checked here. The system measures them.
- Crisps depot stock went from 80 (day 113) to 0 with no crisps slot loaded at that point, and later returned to 40 on day 117. Not an E-003 criterion.

## Mismatch

- **Criterion 1 is unmet and cannot be verified from ground truth.** The lever is absent from the snapshot, so the change cannot be confirmed on each unit of the slice, and it cannot be ruled out on the control units either. This is unchanged since F-E-003-1.

## What would settle it

- Add the fill rule per machine (or per slot) to the adapter's snapshot, for example a `fill_rule` column that shows `always` on AI-LOUNGE DRINKS and SNACK and `predicted-only` on AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE. Then re-run this check on a day after the change is applied, and compare that snapshot with the day-112 one.
- Confirm the status of AI-LOUNGE SNACK A3 (nuts-mix) in the next snapshot, to tell a breach of the "stocked slot" trigger from a 60%-rule park.

## Review record

- **Fix (criterion 1):** expose the fill rule in ground truth, then re-check. Without it, the experiment cannot be judged as designed; the clock should not be started on the current evidence.
- **Done well:** the restocking on the slice matches the depot draws in the snapshots, and none of the AI-LOUNGE reloads show up as a price change or as a campaign on the slice.

Fidelity: fail
