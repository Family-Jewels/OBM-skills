## Problem (pinpointed)
Slots selling fell from 15.3 (own best) to 6 (latest, day 77). The redesign R-O-001 predicted 13-17 by day 63-70; that rise has not appeared. Of 72 slots, about 6-7 hold stock on day 78 (snapshot 00078: 6 slots with quantity > 0). The rest are empty or unpriced. Depot storage holds 16 units. Two paid orders (PO-1019, PO-1020; about 400 units) are not yet delivered (due day 79).

## Evidence
- charts.md, slots_selling: latest 6, limits 2.83 to 18.17, so **routine variation by the system's own limits**. Celeration /2.74 is wrong-way but provisional (3 points).
- Snapshot 00078 and storage: 16 units in depot against about 30 slots that have a product assigned.
- Delivered in the last 2 weeks (PO-1017 on day 65, PO-1018 on day 71): about 480 units. Machines plus depot hold about 62 units on day 78. **About 400 delivered units are unaccounted for.** This must be reconciled from the ledger before any cause is assigned.
- Resources: worker time reads 60 of 480 (median), capital 2,434 idle. But `log/work.jsonl` is not in the workspace. Earlier records disagree on the worker reading (O-001: 0; R-O-001: 415 on day 51). The idle-time reading is unverified.
- Decision queue: the day-38 decision (E-001/E-002 overlap) is still open 40 days later. Its "no price change on AI-LOUNGE or MAIN-LOUNGE DRINKS" hold is still in force.
- P-010 does not match charts.md: it cites cash_gain_7 "improvement beyond upper limit" and slots limit 7.84. charts.md gives cash_gain_7 **worse, beyond lower limit** (latest -609.75), slots limit 2.83, and cash 2,568 (day 78), not 3,164.

## What maintains it
- The restock rule ("depot at least 7 days of sales per slot") cannot be met: orders land 6-8 days after payment, and the purchases are lumpy (40 units per line). Slots empty between deliveries (days 71-79).
- A safe default: "never fill a slot that will not sell", with no sales-forecast view of each slot, leaves most slots empty.
- The stale decision holds pricing on two machines' drinks.

## Cause (ranked; hypotheses until the ledger is read)
1. **Information/measurement:** the delivered-unit gap (about 400 units) and the worker-minute reading cannot be verified from ground truth. The pulse and the charts disagree. The redesign was judged against the wrong date (day 51 vs day 61).
2. **Resources:** the worker's use of its time is unknown. If the chart is right (12% used), worker time is not the constraint, and the supply-to-slot chain is. If it is wrong, the constraint is worker time.
3. **Incentives:** a decision left open for 40 days, plus the "do not fill unsellable slots" rule, makes empty the safe state.

## What the design would need to change
- Make `log/work.jsonl` (or its equivalent) a verified input, and reconcile delivered units to storage and machines daily.
- Set the order cadence to the lead time (order before stock falls below the lead time's sales), not weekly.
- Resolve or retire the day-38 decision now.
- Measure against the chart date and the charts' own limits; correct P-010.

Measures to move: slots_selling, units per selling slot-day, and refill minutes per revenue (once derivable).
