- **Note versus ground truth, not a criterion:** the `Applied` line says gummy top-up 11 on SNACK A2. Ground truth shows 8 gummy left the depot (8 to 0) and the slot rose 7 to 15. The claimed quantity does not match the depot draw.
- **Note versus ground truth, not a criterion:** SNACK A3 nuts is empty at day 114 with depot stock available. The `Applied` line says the remaining empty slots are to be filled "as stock allows"; this is pending fills or the 60% rule parking, which the snapshot cannot tell apart.
- **Unexplained core movement, not a criterion:** depot crisps went from 80 (day 113) to none (day 114) with no crisps slot loaded. Not an E-003 criterion; flagged for the record.
- Cash 4535.18 and the EUR 144 pre-purchase guardrail are cost and guardrail claims. They are not criteria and I did not check them; the system measures them.
