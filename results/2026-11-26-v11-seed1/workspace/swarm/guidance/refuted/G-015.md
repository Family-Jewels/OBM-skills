# Load stock the day it lands
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: When a delivery arrives, a strong period moves its stock into priced slots during the same working day, while worker time is still spare. Example from this run: PO-1033 landed on day 91 and the depot held 268 units on day 92, with no worker time used that day; 11 slots were loaded on day 93. Loading on day 92 would have used the time that was unused.
Why: Diagnosis D-A-002 (day 97). At least 11 days from day 21 to day 96 used 6% or less of worker time while the depot held 33 to 268 units. A stocked slot can only sell once it is stocked, and the lost sales show only in the next period's revenue, so the agent has no same-day signal. No current guidance covers loading arrivals.
Source: diagnosis D-A-002
Revisit: day 145

Fact check: C-010 refuted at day 133 (the "33 to 268 units" depot range is false (days 21–96 run from 2 units on days 52, 69, 89, 90, 91 to 330 on day 39), and the worker-time figures have no ground-truth measure.)
