# Load delivered stock into empty priced slots on the day it lands
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: Stock earns only once it sits in a slot that is stocked and priced. A strong period tends to move units from the depot into empty priced slots on the day they arrive, within the day's worker time, and to read how many units are still in the depot against how many slots are empty. For example, at the day 14 state the depot held 236 units across nine products while only 2 units sat in any slot, and capacity used on day 13 was 0%.
Why: Ground truth on day 14 (swarm/ground_truth/latest.md) shows units_in_storage at 236 across nine products after deliveries PO-1003 and PO-1005 landed on day 13, with 2 units in slots. The charts show capacity_used_share 0.0 on day 13 and revenue of 6.6 that day, against 148.5 on day 10 when slots were being filled (capacity_used_share 0.94). Opportunities O1 and O2 were open when the last day ended.
Source: audit A-001
Revisit: day 30

Fact check: C-002 refuted at day 29 (Figures hold (day 14: 236 units, nine products, 2 in slots; PO-1003 and PO-1005 delivered day 13; day 13 capacity 0.0, revenue 6.6; day 10 revenue 148.5, capacity 0.94; O1 and O2 open at day 14 end), but the claim that a strong period moves units on the day they arrive is false: no slot was loaded on day 13 or 14 (latest loaded day in the day-14 slots is 10), and day 10's stock was loaded on days 7-10 from deliveries made day 6 or earlier (PO-1001, PO-1002).)
