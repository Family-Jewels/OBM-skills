# Load delivered stock into empty priced slots the same day
Revises: G-003
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: A strong period treats an arriving delivery as work for that day. Once stock lands in the depot, it loads the priced slots that the stock suits, so spare worker time goes to slots that will earn. For example, on day 92 the depot held 268 units from an order that landed on day 91 while most priced slots were empty; a day spent loading that stock earns more than a day that ends with it still in storage.
Why: On days 85 and 92 capacity used was 0.0 (swarm/metrics_system.md), the day after an order landed (PO-1032 delivered day 84, PO-1033 delivered day 91; swarm/ground_truth/day-0097.md order table), with 143 and 268 units in storage. On days 86 and 93 capacity used was about 86% and 95%. On day 97, 24 of 31 priced slots were empty with 37 units in the depot (swarm/ground_truth/day-0097.md).
