# Read open orders against the stock already in the depot
Revises: G-001
Applies when: O1, O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period reads each open order's due day against the stock already in the depot before it pays for the next order. On day 297 the depot held 244 units across nine products while 23 of 31 priced slots were empty, and three orders paid on days 288 to 292 (PO-1076, PO-1077, PO-1078) were landing on days 290 to 296. In that position the stock on hand is the nearer question: stock arrived faster than priced slots were loaded. The lead time of the next order matters most when the depot is close to empty.
Why: swarm/ground_truth/day-0297.md (storage batches sum to 244; 23 of 31 priced slot rows at quantity 0; PO-1076 paid 288 due 290, PO-1077 paid 290 due 295, PO-1078 paid 292 due 296). swarm/metrics_system.md units_in_storage: 4 on day 290, 244 on day 297. Lead times on the record run from 2 to 8 days (PO-1075 paid day 277, due day 285), so a single lead-time rule does not pick the next order; the depot count does. The same question was raised for day 237 in a proposal that was refuted because its cited snapshot does not exist; this entry cites only the day-297 snapshot and the metrics.
