# Load delivered stock into priced slots on the day it lands
Revises: G-009
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: A strong period loads stock into empty priced slots on the day it is delivered, or on the next working day at the latest, so that a delivery does not sit in the depot while most priced slots stay at zero. On days 218 to 220 the depot held 173 units after PO-1061 (100 units) landed on day 219; on day 219 about 0.95 of capacity was used and the depot fell to 73, but on day 220 nothing was loaded (capacity used 0.0), the depot rose back to 173, and 22 of 31 priced slots were still empty. Recording each day's loads next to that day's deliveries makes the gap visible the same day it opens.
Why: swarm/ground_truth/day-0220.md (slot table: 22 of 31 priced slots at quantity 0; storage batches total 173; PO-1061 delivered day 219). swarm/metrics_system.md: units_in_storage 73 on day 219, 173 on day 220; capacity_used_share 0.948 on day 219 and 0.0 on day 220. Audit A-005 finds units_in_storage beyond its upper limit (swarm/charts.md) with the same pattern across days 160 to 220.
