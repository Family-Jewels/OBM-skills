# Load delivered stock into empty priced slots on the day it lands
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: Stock earns only once it sits in a slot that is stocked and priced. A strong period tends to move units from the depot into empty priced slots soon after they arrive, within the day's worker time, and to read how many units are still in the depot against how many slots are empty. For example, on day 14 the depot held 236 units across nine products after deliveries landed on day 13, while one slot was selling 2 units and capacity used was 13.5% of the 480 minutes. On day 21 the depot held 242 units after deliveries landed on day 20, no slot was selling, and capacity used was 6.2%.
Why: Ground truth on day 14 (swarm/ground_truth/day-0014.md) shows units_in_storage at 236 after PO-1003 and PO-1005 were delivered on day 13. Ground truth on day 21 (swarm/ground_truth/day-0021.md) shows 242 units after PO-1006 and PO-1007 were delivered on day 20, with every slot at quantity 0. In both cases most of the worker time was unused and the stock was not earning. A delay costs nothing on the day it happens, so the lost sale shows only in the next period's revenue; the reason to act on the day of arrival is that the stock is otherwise idle.
