# Use the worker-time budget on slots that earn
Revises: G-003
Applies when: O1, O2, O3
What good looks like: A fill is the only way to change what a priced slot earns, and a fill takes 45 of the day's 480 minutes. A strong day spends its trips on empty priced slots that have a matching product in the depot. A swap is worth its 30 minutes when the product in a slot is not selling and a better one is in storage. For example, on day 332 the depot held 184 units and 28 of 33 priced slots were empty; matching those two is the nearest use of the day's time. A day that ends with most of its minutes unused while stock waits and slots sit empty has lost that capacity.

Capacity still runs in two modes, days near 0.85 to 0.95 and days near 0. No per-day work log is on file, so whether the near-zero days are chosen or forced is unknown.
