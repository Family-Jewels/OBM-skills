# Read unit cost against the mix of lines bought, and against the depot
Revises: G-008
Applies when: O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period compares each new order line by line with the last price it paid for the same product from the same supplier, and reads the 30-day unit cost against the mix of lines bought. Before it places an order, it also counts what the depot already holds for each product against the priced slots that product could fill. For example, on day 332 the depot held 184 units while 28 of 33 priced slots were empty, and choco-bar-100g, nuts-mix-75g, gummy-bears-150g and stroopwafel-2pk were each in storage with empty priced slots waiting. Stock bought but not yet loaded earns nothing, so the unit cost paid is only realised once the units reach a slot.
Why: swarm/ground_truth/day-0332.md (slot table: 28 of 33 priced slots at zero; storage batches total 184; orders PO-1084 to PO-1087 paid days 321 to 330). swarm/metrics_system.md units_in_storage: 50 on day 331, 184 on day 332. unit_cost_paid_30d fell from 0.92 (day 160) to 0.83 (day 297) and was 0.839 on day 332, so the line-by-line comparison already in this entry stays. The depot-first check is this pulse's suggestion and is untested; the offer prices in force on each payment day are not in the ground truth.
