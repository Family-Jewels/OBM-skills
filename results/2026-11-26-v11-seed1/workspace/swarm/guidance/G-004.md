# Buy against current offers, sized to what will sell
Applies when: O2, O3
What good looks like: Use check_offers at the quantity you intend before ordering, since offers change every seven days and order_goods pays at current prices immediately. Size orders to what the slots you can actually fill in the next few days will sell. For example, stock worth ten days of sales at a location ties up less capital than stock worth sixty days, and a discounted larger order is only better if the extra units sell before the next offer period. Compare suppliers on unit cost at the same quantity.
Why: Storage holds cash that is not earning (units_in_storage is charted down), and unit_cost_paid_30d measures what buying costs. Both move when purchases follow sales rather than assumptions.
