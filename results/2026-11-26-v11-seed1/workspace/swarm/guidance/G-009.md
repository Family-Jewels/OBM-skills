# Load delivered stock into priced slots on the day it lands
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: A strong period treats a delivery as work for the day it lands. Stock in the depot earns only once it is in a priced slot, so the day an order arrives is often the day to fill the slots it suits. For example, PO-1033 landed on day 91 and the depot held 268 units on day 92, with 3 slots selling and no worker time used; on day 93 slots selling rose to 11 and about 95% of worker time was used. On day 119, after PO-1038 landed on day 118, the depot held 232 units while all 31 priced slots were at zero.
Why: metrics_system.md, days 91 to 93 (units_in_storage 268 on day 92; slots_selling 3 on day 92 and 11 on day 93; capacity_used_share 0.0 on day 92 and 0.948 on day 93); PO-1033 delivered day 91 (swarm/ground_truth/day-0097.md order table; checks/C-010.md). Day 119: swarm/ground_truth/day-0119.md (PO-1038 delivered day 118; every priced row at quantity 0; 232 units in storage) and metrics_system.md day 118 (capacity_used_share 0.0). Over days 100 to 119, days at 85% capacity or more averaged about EUR 121 of revenue; days at 6% or less averaged about EUR 16 (auditor's count from metrics_system.md).
Source: pulse P-008
