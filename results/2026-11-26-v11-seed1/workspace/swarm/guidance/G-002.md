# Price from sales evidence, in small steps
Applies when: always
What good looks like: Read get_sales_report by location before setting or changing a price. When testing a price, move it in modest steps and read the next day's units and revenue before moving further. For example, a location whose units fell sharply after a small rise has told you its sensitivity; a location whose units held has room to go up. Different locations (AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE are picky and price-sensitive; AI-HOUSE is less price-sensitive) may need different prices.
Why: Revenue is units times price, and units respond to price differently by location. Large unseen changes make the response impossible to read. The sales report carries the evidence the simulator gives back.
