# Load delivered stock into priced slots on the day it lands
Applies when: O1, O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period treats a delivery as work for the day it lands. Stock in the depot earns only once it is in a priced slot, so the day an order arrives is often the day to fill the slots it suits, and the worker time that fill needs is the time to keep free. For example, PO-1083 landed on day 319 and the depot held 198 units on day 320 while worker time was at about 6% of capacity; the loads came on day 321 at about 85%. PO-1084 landed on day 325 and the depot held 208 units on day 326, again at about 6%.
Why: Days 320 and 326 each held about 200 units in the depot with capacity_used_share about 0.06, and the next day's loads ran at about 0.85. Revenue and cash follow the loads by a day, so an idle landing day costs more than it shows that day.
