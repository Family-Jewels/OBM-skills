# Marketing as a bounded bet, read against sales
Applies when: O3
What good looks like: Check get_marketing_report for the location's current traffic multiplier, its limits and recent campaigns before running one. A campaign is most useful at a location whose slots are already stocked and priced, so extra footfall converts to sales. Moderate use keeps the boost; repeated use across channels creates fatigue. For example, one campaign followed by reading the sales report for a few days tells you whether the footfall converted.
Why: Marketing costs money immediately; its bonus expires and fatigue recovers slowly. It pays back only through sales, so it is worth more where stock and price are already ready. Copy wording does not change demand.
