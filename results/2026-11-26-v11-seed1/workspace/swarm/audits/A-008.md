## Diagnosis

The pinpointed problem: from day 321 the agent has bought and received stock faster than it loads it (eight orders in 24 days; 295 units in the depot on day 348) while 26 of 33 priced slots are empty and worker time is left unused on the days after a landing (days 340, 345, 347). The cue the agent acts on at the start of a day still names only O3, which holds whenever cash is above 2,000 and one slot is empty; the day-end line names O1 and O3, and O1 also holds by the spec's definition on the days it is idle. The changes that would move this (listed in D-P-010 and R-A-006) are system-side: the "open now" line built from the same evaluation as the day-end line; the O3 definition checked; the landing-day loading advice attached to a cue the agent sees on the day stock lands; and worker time chartable so it can breach its limits. Whether the idle days are chosen or forced is still unsettled: the per-day work log for days 340, 345 and 347 (calls, stop reason, context size) would settle it. The diagnostician should take the day-347 landing and the days after it first.
