# Audit A-004 (full, system-invoked on a chart signal: cash_gain_30d beyond its lower limit)

**Summary.** The goal is still moving the right way, but the engine that makes it move has slowed and is idle. Cash is 7,442.62 on day 189 (about 31 a day since day 150, against about 305 a day needed to reach the exemplar's 61,219 on day 365; the exemplar is a straight-line interpolation, not a reference path, so the gap is a finding for the human, not a change). The 30-day gain has fallen from its own best of 1,552 to 827 on day 189 and 771 on day 190, which is beyond its lower limit of 790.68: the signal that invoked this audit. The cause in the records is capacity that is not used: over days 160 to 189 the worker used 31% of capacity on average, and 17 of 30 days were at 6% or less, with O1 (empty slots, spare time) open. The redesign R-P-008 predicted this pattern would fall to 4 or fewer of 20 days in days 167 to 186; the actual count was 12 of 20, and its cash_gain_30d prediction failed too. Unit cost is the one clear gain (0.88 to 0.807, beyond its lower limit). The records have fallen behind the swarm: no pulse is on file since P-008 (day 117), the signal that R-P-008 added has no firing log, and the capacity chart still cannot bind. Diagnosis is needed.

## Findings

**1. Direction (cash right, pace and engine wrong).**
- Cash rose from 6,237 (day 150) to 7,443 (day 189). The phase signal (above its upper limit) is real but flattered by the phase reset. Judged against the pre-R-P-008 limits, cash_gain_30d is beyond its lower limit (charts.md "since redesign" table, centre 1,035).
- Revenue is falling: revenue over the last 7 days 531 on day 189 (490.9 on day 190) against 748 the same 7 days a month earlier (metrics day 189).
- The exemplar in the chart is a straight line between day 30 (3,228) and day 365 (61,219), not the reference operator's path. PIP at day 189 is about 4.1 on the level basis. A-003 asked for this to be corrected; it is not.

**2. Redesign predictions (R-P-008, due day 186).**
- Prediction A (days at 6% or less of worker time across days 167 to 186 at 4 or fewer of 20): **not met.** Actual 12 of 20 (days 167, 168, 171, 174, 175, 176, 179, 180, 181, 182, 185, 186).
- Prediction B (cash_gain_30d stays above its phase centre of 1,035): **not met.** 827 on day 189, 771 on day 190.
- Prediction C (the new signal fires within about 2 days of each idle run): **cannot be checked.** No signal log is in the workspace. slots_selling was 1 on days 166 to 168, so the rule should have fired from about day 167, and no pulse is on record for it.
- Revert clause (retire the signal if the prediction fails and at least half of firings found no idle run, or if the idle days were forced): cannot be settled without the firing log and the per-day work log, both requested in R-P-008 and D-P-008 and not on file.

**3. Idle capacity with empty slots (the pinpointed problem).**
- Days 160 to 189: 17 of 30 at 6% or less; the rest run at 35 to 95%. The share is bimodal: a day either uses almost nothing or runs to the day's end (0.948 is the record for "ran past 17:00"). Busy days alternate with idle ones (for example 183 at 0.948, 184 at 0.344, 185 and 186 at 0.0).
- On day 189 the depot held 2 units, O1 and O3 were open (hand-off, charts), and the cash sat above 7,400. The system shows O1 as open only at day end, not in the hand-off's "open now" list.
- Records outside the agent's control: the capacity_used_share chart still has limits of -0.85 to 1.53 on a 0-to-1 share, so it cannot bind (requested in A-003 and R-P-008, not fixed). The per-day work log that would show whether idle days were chosen is not on file.

**4. Replenishment (the stock behind the empty slots).**
- Orders landed 2 to 5 days after payment through day 185 (PO-1053: paid 177, due 181; PO-1054: paid 183, due 185). The next orders, PO-1055 and PO-1056, were paid on day 187 and are due on days 197 and 198: ten to eleven days. Why the due dates lengthened is not in the records (unverified: a supplier change, a stock-out, or the order size).
- Between the last delivery (185) and the next order's payment (187), the depot went from 32 units (day 185) to 2 (day 188). Priced slots were largely empty from then. Cash was not the constraint.
- Records disagree: latest.md marks several lines as loaded on day 186, but metrics show capacity_used_share 0.0 on day 186 and a fill costs 45 minutes. Either fills are not counted in capacity or one record is wrong. Unverified; the loading lag is not settled by this audit.
