# Diagnosis D-P-008 (day 150 of 365)

**Summary.** Cash is on an improvement signal (6,237 at day 150, above its phase limit of 5,927), but the business is earning less per 30 days than before R-A-002: the 30-day gain averages 851 since day 126 against 1,455 before it, and unit cost paid over 30 days has shifted up (0.87 against 0.85). The main cause of the lost gain is idle worker time with stock in the depot. Over days 100 to 150, 23 of 51 days used 6% or less of worker time; on those days revenue averaged about EUR 16 against about EUR 121 on days at 85% or more (G-019's count, which I could not re-verify from the day-0119 file). The records do not show whether these days ended by the agent's choice, by a call or context limit, or by the day closing with no action. The environment supports the choice reading: a no-action day costs nothing that day, the charts and signals cannot fire on it, and the hand-off does not show O1 as open now. Context does not explain it alone, since idle days also occur at 36k to 75k tokens. A per-day work log would settle the question.

## 1. Driver tree walk

- **Cash (goal):** improvement signal since day 126 (6,237; upper limit 5,927). Not the problem, but it is flattered by the phase reset: judged against the pre-R-A-002 limits it shifts up, and its gain is below its old centre.
- **cash_gain_30d:** improvement in the new phase (928 against centre 802), yet down against the pre-redesign centre (mean 851 against 1,455, shift beyond the lower limit). This is the measure that moved the wrong way; it is the symptom to explain.
- **unit_cost_paid_30d:** shift up since R-A-002 (0.87 against 0.85; beyond the upper limit). Routine on the new chart at 0.865. Secondary; see §4.
- **revenue, units, slots_selling, revenue_per_slot:** routine in the new phase (revenue 96 against centre 80). They are routine because they are averaged over the swings below, not because they are healthy: revenue varies between about 0 and 230 a day with the worker's loading.
- **capacity_used_share (driver):** routine on the chart, but the chart cannot signal it: its limits run from -0.85 to 1.53 on a 0 to 1 share, so it cannot bind.

## 2. Pinpoint

On days with stock in the depot, the worker often uses little or no capacity, and the priced slots stay mostly empty. Days 100 to 150: 23 of 51 days used 6% or less of worker time (days 103, 104, 105, 108, 109, 112, 113, 118, 119, 122, 123, 127, 128, 129, 132, 133, 134, 135, 137, 142, 146, 147, 150). Days 127 to 129 show the pattern in one run: capacity 0% with 36 to 192 units in storage. Busy days alternate with idle days rather than running at a steady rate.

## 3. What maintains it

- **Consequence:** an idle day costs nothing that day. Its lost revenue appears only in the next period, and fill costs worker time with no same-day return.
- **Signal blindness:** the cadence signals need revenue or slots_selling at exactly zero for two days (not_before 8). Idle days usually still sell a few slots (2 to 3), so none of the signals fire. The only trigger that did fire was pulse P-008 itself, which was late.
- **Hand-off:** on day 152 O3 (cash idle) is the only item "open now"; O1 (slots empty, worker time spare) appears only as "open when it ended". O3 holds almost every day, so it does not separate good days from bad ones, and it points at spending, not loading.
- **Unit cost:** buying at current offers pays the same day; the cost shows only in a 30-day average, so buying looks rewarded while price is worse.

## 4. Cause, environment first

1. **Information.** (a) O1 is not shown as open now, and the hand-off does not give the number of empty priced slots, the depot units, or the last delivery day. (b) The loading advice has not reached the library: G-008 and G-013 were refused and G-015 was refuted (false depot ranges and worker-time figures), so the only accepted advice is G-007 and G-003, which sit below buy-side guidance (G-004 to G-006) in the hand-off. (c) G-019 (proposed) rests on day-0119 and day-0092 evidence that the fact check has not yet run on. The information the agent needs is there in principle, but it is not presented at the point of decision.
2. **Resources.** Worker time (480 minutes) was available on every idle day, and the system counts a day as fully used only when calls run past 17:00. Context grew from 36k to 568k tokens. Idle days occur at low context (days 2 to 7, 12 to 14 at 36k to 75k), so context growth does not explain them. It may bear on late idle days; not confirmed.
3. **Incentives.** A no-action day is not penalised on the day, and loading is rewarded only in the next period's revenue. Buying is rewarded at once by cash. Nothing in cadence or the chart separates a zero-capacity day from a busy one.
4. **Knowledge or model tier.** Not reached. The agent's own notes earlier showed it knew the empty-slot gap (D-A-002), so the gap is not a knowledge failure.

**Most likely mechanism, unconfirmed:** the working day closes when the agent stops making calls. If so, a zero-capacity day is a day the agent ended with no action, and the environment does not gate or log that. This is the one reading the records cannot rule out; it needs the work log.

## 5. What the environment would need (for the designer; not a change)

- Show O1 as open now whenever slots are empty and worker time is spare, with the count of empty priced slots, depot units and last delivery day in the hand-off.
- Chart capacity_used_share as a clamped 0 to 1 share, or chart the worker-time share with limits that can bind. Add a process signal for idle worker time while the depot holds stock (for example, capacity 10% or less on two days with units_in_storage above 50), so it fires on the days that matter. The signal should not depend on revenue being exactly zero.
- Log the stop reason per day (work log: calls made, reason the day closed, context size), so a choice can be told from a call limit.
- Keep the loading advice in the library with examples checkable against ground truth, using days in the existing ground truth files only. Move it above buy-side advice on days when O1 is open.
- Measures to move: capacity_used_share on the first working day after a delivery; the share of days at 10% or less; revenue_per_slot; cash_gain_30d against its pre-redesign centre.
