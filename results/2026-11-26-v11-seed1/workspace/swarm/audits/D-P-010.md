# Diagnosis D-P-010 (day 332 of 365)

**Summary.** Cash is 12,248 against the exemplar's 55,506 (PIP 4.5), and cash_gain_30d has sat below its pre-redesign centre since day 311. Walking the tree, the measures that moved are on the cash-out side: revenue (90.9 against 90.4 before R-A-006), units, slots_selling, revenue_per_slot and unit cost are all unchanged in mean, while units_in_storage rose from 78 to 90 and the agent placed four orders in twelve days (days 316 to 328). The pinpointed operating problem is the same as in P-010: on the day after a delivery lands, worker time goes unused while priced slots are empty and the stock is in the depot. Days 320 and 326 each show this (capacity 0.06, 198 and 208 units in storage), and the agent loads the next day. Days 319 to 332 average 0.40 capacity, with 3 of 14 days at 0.10 or less. The cause sits in the environment. The "open now" line of the hand-off names only O3 (cash idle while slots are empty), although the day-end line names O1 and O2 as open, so the agent starts each day with a buy-side cue. The only guidance that says to load on the day stock lands (G-009) is attached to O1 and O2 alone, and its revision was refuted in C-016. Nothing charted can signal idle worker time, because the capacity limits (-0.40 to 1.30) cannot be breached. Whether the idle days are chosen or forced is still unsettled, because the per-day work log requested since R-A-006 is not on file.

## Walk down the tree

1. **Goal, cash:** 12,247.65 on day 332. Rate about 40 a day from day 311 against about 1,100 a day needed (PIP 4.5, P-010).
2. **cash_gain_30d:** "shift down" since day 311, mean 967 against a centre of 1,259 (charts.md). Latest 880 is inside the phase limits, so this is a shift, not a point signal.
3. **Drivers of cash_gain_30d, since adoption against before:** revenue 90.9 vs 90.4; units 41.5 vs 41.9; slots_selling 12.7 vs 9.9; revenue_per_slot 7.06 vs 7.00; unit_cost_paid_30d 0.84 vs 0.84; units_in_storage 89.6 vs 78.3. The sales side has not moved. The part that moved is cash out: orders paid (PO-1083 day 316, PO-1084 day 321, PO-1085 day 324, PO-1086 day 328) against stock that is not yet selling.
4. **Pinpoint, operating:** on days after a landing, the worker does not load. Stock landed on day 319 (PO-1083) is visible on day 320 (198 units in storage) and capacity is 0.062; PO-1084 landed on day 325 and is visible on day 326 (208 units), capacity 0.062. Loading happens on days 321 and 327 at about 0.85. Day 319 itself was 0.01 with 48 units in the depot and about 29 priced slots empty. Frequency: 3 of 14 days (319, 320, 326) at 0.10 or less. Cost: from the fact-checked counts in C-011, days at 6% or less earned about 16 a day against about 121 on busy days.
5. **Not the cause (checked):** context (about 72k tokens on day 320, against 900k before the day-311 reset); outages or missing credentials (none recorded); the depot (stock was present on each idle day).

## What maintains it

- **Before the behaviour (what the agent sees):** the hand-off for day 333 reads "Open when it ended: O1 (61); O2 (76); O3 (12127.65)" and "Open now: O3 (12202.25)". The list the agent acts on at the start of the next day drops the two opportunities that describe the idle stock. The guidance list in that note is G-001, G-003, G-004, G-005, G-006, plus "3 more". G-003 is attached to O3 (added by R-A-006) but says only "use the worker-time budget"; G-009 is not in the note.
- **Structural cue toward buying:** O3 holds whenever cash is above 2,000 and one slot is empty. Under the spec's definition (capital = cash plus stock value), cash is about 98% of capital, so the "at least half unused" test holds on every such day. The only open cue the agent sees therefore points at spending, and no buy closes it. The buy-side guidance it receives with O3 (G-004, G-005, G-006, G-008) matches what it does: four orders in twelve days.
- **After the behaviour (what the agent gets):** an idle landing day costs nothing that the agent sees on that day. Revenue falls one day later, and cash_gain_30d moves slowly. The one capacity-related measure cannot signal (below). The stock it leaves in the depot is tracked as units_in_storage (level, better down), which is inside its limits (-95 to 269) and so routine.
- **Shortcut:** an order pays visibly on the day it is placed (cash falls, guidance is satisfied); loading costs 45 minutes a fill and returns revenue on later days.

## Cause (environment first)

1. **Information (primary).** The "open now" line omits O1 and O2 while the day-end line names them. The agent acts on the first. Same fault as A-002 to A-006 and D-A-006; no change has reached the hand-off by day 333. The hand-off's own figures do not agree with the charts (see Records).
2. **Information (guidance).** The landing-day loading advice (G-009) is attached only to O1 and O2. Its O3 revision was refuted (C-016, the 21-of-39 count, and untestable claims), so it still does not reach the agent when O3 is the only open cue. G-003 does reach it, but its advice is general and its numbers (480 minutes, 45 and 30 per trip) are untestable, so the agent has little reason to act on it at the moment stock lands.
3. **Resources and feedback (chart).** capacity_used_share has limits -0.40 to 1.30 on a 0-to-1 measure with centre 0.45, so no signal, and no point beyond a limit, can occur for idle time. R-A-006 asked for a clamp or a worker-time share; not made.
4. **Incentives (open).** No consequence is visible on the idle day. The signal for slots_selling at 3 or fewer (two days, not before day 8) did not fire on days 319 or 320 (7 and 4), and no pulse has run since day 277, so the decision route for the cash signal lapsed for about 40 days.

## Chosen or forced? (not settled)

The capacity pattern is bimodal (near zero, or 0.85 to 0.95). The two idle days after a landing are followed by a full day on the next one, which fits a choice to work in batches, but it also fits a stop the harness forced. The records cannot tell these apart. The per-day work log (calls, stop reason, context size) for days 319, 320 and 326 would settle it. If the log shows the agent ended the day with no load call, the cause is in items 1 and 2 above; if it shows a stop or a failed step, the cause is in the harness or the procedure.

## What the environment would need to change (not written here)

- Make the "open now" list come from the same evaluation as the day-end line, and show O1 and O2 (with their counts: empty priced slots, depot units, last landing day) whenever their conditions hold. Measures to move: capacity_used_share, units_in_storage, slots_selling.
- Attach the landing-day loading advice to the cue the agent actually sees on a landing day (O1, O2, or a new cue for stock in storage above 50 with priced slots empty). Keep it advice only, and use only facts the checker can verify. Measure: capacity_used_share on the day after a landing.
- Chart worker time so that it can breach its limits (clamp to 0 to 1, or chart the share of days at 0.10 or less over 20 days). Measure: capacity_used_share.
- Review the O3 definition: as written, it holds on nearly every day with cash above 2,000 and an empty slot, so it cannot be closed by any action the agent takes. The designer should check this against the metric code before deciding whether O3 should name a loading cue.
- Restore the pulse cadence (every 5 days; none since day 277). Measure: the cash-gain shift must reach a decision within 5 days.
