The claim itself is supported by day 14 and day 21, and by the day-7 record. The library now holds no advice on it. Resources are not short: 480 worker minutes were available on each of those days.

Re-propose the load-on-landing advice with the day-14 and day-21 facts and a corrected example (proposed G-008 already uses day-14 facts).
