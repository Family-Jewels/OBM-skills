# Redesign R-P-010 (day 359 of 365)

**Summary.** Diagnosis D-P-010 traces the sales gap to idle worker time on the day after a delivery lands, with stock waiting in the depot and priced slots empty. Most of the named causes are outside this designer's reach: the "open now" line of the hand-off drops O1 and O2 (the system writes the hand-off), the capacity measure cannot breach its limits (the system computes them), and no pulse has run since day 277 (the system's scheduling). Inside the environment I can change, the one cause that reaches the agent is the guidance: G-009 is attached to O1 and O2 only, so the agent sees no landing-day advice when O3 is the only open cue, which is the cue shown at the start of each day. I therefore propose one guidance revision, G-010 (Revises G-009), which applies it to O1, O2 and O3 and drops the miscounted figure that caused the earlier revision to be refused (C-016). The spec, system.json and goal are unchanged, so no history copy was needed. Only six periods remain, so the prediction is short-range and partial.

## Change

- `swarm/guidance/proposed/G-010.md`: revises G-009 to apply on O1, O2 and O3. Advice only; it names no required or capped action. Its facts are limited to days 319 to 327, all checkable in ground truth.
- Spec: no change. The spec's guidance table already lists G-009 under O1, O2 and O3, so the library index (O1, O2) was the mismatch. G-010 brings the library into line with the spec.
- `system.json`: no change.

## Prediction (the charts can test this)

1. On any delivery day from day 360 onward, capacity_used_share is at or above 0.5 on the landing day or the day after (today it is 0.01 to 0.06 on such days).
2. units_in_storage falls from its current 102 toward its centre (86.85) by day 365.
3. cash_gain_30d does not shift back to its centre within the six days; that needs a longer window than remains.

**Revert if:** the fact check rejects G-010, or the first delivery after adoption lands with capacity at or below 0.10 on the landing day and the day after.

## Not changed, and why

- Hand-off "open now" line (system): the fault is in the system's hand-off generation. Flag to the system and the human.
- capacity_used_share limits and clamp (system): limits are computed by the system; the clamp or share-of-days chart needs a system change.
- Pulse lapse since day 277 (system): scheduling, not design.
- Hand-off figures: "last day used 57%" against 32% in charts.md and ground_truth/day-0332; P-010 slots_selling 7 against day-319 snapshot 4. Records, not design; flagged.
- Reference mismatch: D-P-010 says G-009's revision was refuted in C-016, but the library shows C-016 refers to G-003. The refuted G-009 revision (refuted/G-009.md) is the one the diagnosis meant; its miscount (21 of 39, not 20) is the reason.
- O3 definition: it holds nearly every day with cash above 2,000 and one empty slot, because capital is mostly cash. That is a real design issue, but changing O3 would change what the agent is told about buying on every day, with six periods left. Left for the next full audit.
