## Findings

**1. Direction (wrong way).** Cash, revenue, units and slots_selling all peaked on days 8 to 10 and have declined since; slots_selling fell from 12 (day 10) to 2 (day 13). The goal measure is below its start. Milestone M1 (revenue above zero on each of the first three days, budget first 3 days) was missed: revenue was zero on days 2 to 7 because the first delivery, PO-1002, reached the depot on day 6. That lag is an environment fact (orders paid day 1, due days 6 and 7), not an agent choice. No redesign has been made since spec v1, so no predicted shift is pending.

**2. Idle capacity with stock on hand (O1, O2 open).** Capacity used: 0.94 to 0.95 on days 8 and 10, then 0.38 on day 11, 0.01 on day 12 and 0.00 on day 13. On day 14 the depot held 236 units (ground_truth/latest.md) with 2 units in slots. The records do not show whether the day-13 deliveries landed before or after the agent's day-13 work; that timing decides whether the agent or the environment held the stock back. This is the main lost revenue and it is the pinpointed problem for diagnosis.

**3. Stock-out between sell-through and delivery.** Storage fell from 124 (day 8) to 9 (day 10) to 2 (days 11 to 13), while units sold fell from 73 (day 10) to 17, 8 and 4. The replacement orders, PO-1003 (paid day 9, due day 13) and PO-1005 (paid day 10, due day 13), arrived four days after payment. Whether a longer-dated order should have been placed earlier is not visible from the records alone.

**4. Unit cost (signal, wrong way).** unit_cost_paid_30d is 0.90 against an upper limit of 0.89 (provisional). My recomputation from the order lines (paid orders, 30-day window): PO-1001 and PO-1002 183.8 for 214 units; PO-1003 125.6 for 140 units; PO-1004 99.1 for 110 units; PO-1005 93.6 for 94 units; overall about 502 for 558 units, which is 0.90. The rise is partly mix and partly supplier choice: on the four lines PO-1004 and PO-1005 share, Bakker was cheaper on three (choco-bar 0.86 against 1.02, crisps 1.68 against 1.77, stroopwafel 0.49 against 0.59) and Zuidas on one (nuts-mix 0.78 against 0.94). Whether Bakker's offer was still open on day 10 is not in the records, so the choice is unverified rather than wrong. G-004 already says to compare suppliers on unit cost at the same quantity.
