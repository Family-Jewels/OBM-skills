# Redesign R-A-002 (day 115 of 365)

**Summary.** Diagnosis D-A-002 found two problems: unit cost paid over 30 days is beyond its upper limit, and worker time sat idle on at least 11 days (day 21 to day 96) while stock sat in the depot. The cause on the idle days is in the environment: the agent has no current guidance on loading arrivals (G-014 was refused, and the landing-day advice was never re-proposed), and no system field can signal idle worker time, because signals read only measures. This redesign therefore changes the guidance only: it proposes G-015 (load stock the day it lands), whose example is checkable against the ground truth (PO-1033 on day 91, 268 units in the depot on day 92, 11 slots loaded on day 93). The spec moves to version 3 and records the open question on whether the idle days were chosen or forced by a limit. `system.json` is unchanged, and the goal is unchanged. The unit-cost problem is not addressed by this redesign (see below).

**Direction check.** The drivers still move the top measure: cash is beyond its upper limit, and the gain ratio is 4.2 against 4.0 on day 68. The tree is sound. No design change to the tree was needed.

**Change.**
- `swarm/history/spec.v2.md`: copy of the previous spec.
- `swarm/spec.md`: version 3. Adds G-015 to the guidance table (marked proposed, fact check pending) and an open question on the idle days.
- `swarm/guidance/proposed/G-015.md`: "Load stock the day it lands" (applies to O1, O2). Advice and an example only; no "always", "never", "at least" or "do not".
- `swarm/system.json`: unchanged. Reason: O1's slot threshold (10 of 72) is always met and O1 already holds on the idle days, so changing it would not separate good days from bad. Signals read measures only, so no signal can express idle worker time. The gap is in what the hand-off shows ("open now" lists O3 only), which is system-side.

**Prediction (testable on charts.md).** After G-015 enters the library and is read by the agent, by day 145 the count of days with units_in_storage above 50 and capacity_used_share at or below 0.06 should fall from about 1 in 8 days (days 21 to 96) to 2 or fewer across days 116 to 145. Within a few days of each arrival, slots_selling should rise. The chart's latest capacity_used_share should be above 0.06 on the first working day after a rise in units_in_storage.

**Cost.** One proposed guidance file and one spec version. No spend beyond the fact check.

**Revert.** Retire G-015 (`Retires: G-015`) if by day 145 the prediction fails and the work log shows the idle days were forced by a call or context limit rather than by choice.
