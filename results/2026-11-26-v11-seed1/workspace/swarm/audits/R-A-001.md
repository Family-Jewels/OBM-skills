# Redesign R-A-001 (day 44 of 365)

**Summary.** The diagnosis D-A-001 (day 26) found that the swarm is still making money but is slow to put delivered stock into slots, and that the environment does not show it. The depot is not visible in the hand-off by arrival, and the opportunity rules never switch off: O2 opens for any stock above zero, O1 needs one empty slot out of 72, and O3 is always open because cash is most of capital. This redesign changes only those thresholds and proposes one piece of guidance, the load-on-landing advice rebuilt with facts that the ground truth confirms (the refused G-008 was refused on a false example and a claim the records do not test). The goal, the exemplar and the cash chart are unchanged. Two diagnosis items are system-side and are not changed here: the PIP computation (the chart still uses a level ratio, 2.3 at day 44, where the gain ratio is about 4.4) and the limits on unit cost. Day 26's unexplained worker time needs the work log, which the designer cannot add.

## Changes

1. **O1** (`system.json`): unstocked slots threshold raised from 1 to 10. With 72 slots, one empty slot is always true. Diagnosis part 5.
2. **O2** (`system.json`): storage threshold from above 0 to above 50 units. Days 11 to 13 (2 units) do not open O2; days 14 and 21 (236 and 242 units) do. Diagnosis part 1 and 5.
3. **O3** (`system.json`): adds cash above 2,000 to the existing conditions. This is a judgement, not derived from a stock plan; no stock-plan figure is available to the designer. Diagnosis part 5.
4. **Guidance proposed** `swarm/guidance/proposed/G-013.md`: "Load delivered stock into empty priced slots soon after it lands", with the day-14 and day-21 facts checked against `day-0014.md` and `day-0021.md`. It replaces the refused G-008 and does not require anything.
5. **Spec** version 2 (opportunity rows updated). Old versions copied to `swarm/history/spec.v1.md` and `swarm/history/system.v1.json`.

## Prediction (testable on the charts by day 54)

- `units_in_storage` falls below its centre (62) within two days of each delivery, and `slots_selling` rises by at least 3 in the following two days.
- O2 is open on days when storage exceeds 50 and minutes unused are at least 20%, and closed on days with 2 units in storage.
- O1 closes on days with fewer than 10 empty slots, and O3 closes on days when cash is below 2,000.

## Cost and revert

No spend change. The guidance proposal costs one fact check. Revert to `history/system.v1.json` if O2 stays open with storage at or below 50 or O3 closes on stall days where cash is above 2,000; revert to `history/spec.v1.md` if the spec text is found to shift the agent's goal (it should not).

- O2 shown open at 42 on day 26 with 99% of time used. The rule does not allow this; the system's display needs reconciling.
- Unit cost limits are tight on a flat series; the system computes them. No like-for-like chart is available with the measures the system reads.
- Day 26 worker time (99%, no slot loaded): needs `log/work.jsonl` for days 25 and 26, which the system must supply.
- Two guidance entries share the ID G-006 (index and `refuted/`). System-side.
- No ground truth for days 9, 15, 16, 23; no pulses since P-001 (day 7). System-side.
