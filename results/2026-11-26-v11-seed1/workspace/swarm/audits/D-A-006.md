# Diagnosis D-A-006 (day 277 of 365)

**Summary.** The pinpointed problem from A-006 is still there at day 277: worker time is unused on about half of recent days (20 of 39 days from 238 to 277 at 0.10 or less), while priced slots sit empty and stock waits in the depot (172 units and 8 stocked slots on day 276, the day after PO-1074 landed). Cash is the top measure and shows IMPROVEMENT, but the rate is about 38 a day (cash_gain_30d 1,154 over 30 days) against about 578 a day needed to reach 61,219 by day 365, so the chart hides the drift. The cause sits in the environment. The hand-off's "open now" list has shown O3 alone since day 68 (A-002), although O1 and O2 meet their conditions on most days, so the guidance for loading (G-003, G-009) never reaches the agent; the agent receives only buy-side guidance, and it buys and stocks. Nothing visible costs an idle day, and the review that would have caught it has not run since day 117. Whether idle days are chosen or forced is still not settled, because no per-day work log is on file. The fault has been named in A-002, A-003, A-004, A-005 and A-006 and the requests are still open, so the human should see this as a standing system fault, not a new finding.

## Pinpoint

- Days 238 to 277: 39 days recorded (no record for 255; day 277 values read from the charts and metrics, not a day file). Capacity used is 0.10 or less on 20 days (240, 241, 244 to 247, 251 to 253, 256 to 258, 263 to 265, 270, 271, 274 to 276). Busy days run to 0.85 to 0.96 and carry most revenue (days 243, 248, 254, 259 to 261, 266 to 269, 272 and 277 average about 180 revenue).
- Day 276: capacity 0.0, units_in_storage 172, slots_selling 8 (metrics). Day 275 slot table: 8 of 31 priced slots stocked. The rise from 22 to 172 units between days 275 and 276 matches PO-1074 (150 units) landing on day 276; that is an inference from the numbers, not a recorded delivery day. Day 277: capacity 0.948, units_in_storage 72, revenue 171. The agent did the loading on 277, not on 276.
- Cash gain: cash_gain_30d 1,154 at day 277 is about 38 a day. Required gain from day 277 to 365 is about 578 a day. The gap is not a noisy trend; it is a rate.

## What maintains it

- **Before the behaviour:** the hand-off's "open now" line lists O3 (cash idle while slots are empty) only (A-006 finding 3; the same in A-002 to A-005). O3 points at spending, not at loading (D-P-008). The guidance it brings with O3 (G-004, G-005, G-006, G-008) is buy-side. G-003 and G-009 are attached to O1 and O2 and are withheld.
- **After the behaviour:** an idle day has no visible cost. Revenue of zero on one day does not fire the "revenue zero two days" signal. The `slots_selling <= 3` signal added in R-P-008 has no signal log on file, and no pulse has been written since P-008 (day 117), so the signal is not reaching anyone. The cash chart reads IMPROVEMENT on the phase basis, which rewards buying.
- **Shortcut:** an idle day costs the agent no time-linked penalty and no visible loss; a loading day costs 45 minutes per fill and returns revenue only the next day. The buy-side actions pay visibly; loading is not shown as a target.

## Cause (environment first)

1. **Information (primary).** O1 and O2 are not in the hand-off's "open now" list although the spec's conditions hold on most days of this window (day 237: 62 unstocked slots, 95% of worker time unused, 89 units against a threshold of 50; day 276: 172 units, 0% used). The day-end count reads 1 to 3 while the "open now" list reads O3. The hand-off and the day-end evaluation disagree, and the "open now" line is the one the agent acts on. I could not read the hand-off itself (it is not under `swarm/`); this rests on the quotes in A-002 to A-006. The evaluation log for O1 and O2 would settle whether the fault is in the rule, the timing of the evaluation (for example, worker time read at a time when it is already used), or the hand-off selection.
2. **Information (chart).** Nothing charted rises or falls with loaded slots or with idle days. `capacity_used_share` cannot bind (limits -0.35 to 1.19 on a 0-to-1 share). The cash chart's phase basis reads improvement while the rate is far below the aim. The chart cannot show the pinpointed problem; A-003, A-004, A-005 and R-P-008 asked for both changes.
3. **Incentives (open).** The idle-day cost is invisible and the review chain is broken. Idle days show as 0.0 on the chart with no consequence; the early-end cadence (`early_end_unused_share`, `repeated_early_ends`) has produced no review on file. Management's own cadence has lapsed (about 24 pulse periods missed since day 117), so the designer has not been able to see the pattern for 160 days.
4. **Resources.** A fill costs 45 minutes, so a full busy day covers about 10 fills out of 31 priced slots; the design caps loading per day, and a delivery that lands late (PO-1074 due day 275, landed day 276) leaves the depot idle until it arrives. These are real limits, but they do not explain the zero-use days, which occur with stock in the depot.
5. **Not supported by the records (yet):** knowledge or model tier. Context is 837k tokens, and idle days occur at all sizes (day 3 at 37k, day 276 at 834k), so size alone does not explain them. A forced stop near the context limit cannot be ruled out for the later idle days; it is the question the work log answers.

## Chosen or forced? (not settled)

The capacity pattern is bimodal: zero, or 0.85 to 0.96 when the agent works. Busy days run close to the clock, which suggests the agent does use the day when it starts. What is unknown is whether a zero day starts no work at all (a choice to end, or a stop the harness forced) or fails at a loading step (a tool or procedure error). The records cannot decide this.

## What the environment would need to change (not written here)

- Show O1 and O2 in the hand-off's "open now" list whenever their conditions hold, with the count of empty priced slots, depot units and the last arrival day, and with the guidance IDs that attach to each. Measures to move: capacity_used_share and the share of days at 0.10 or less.
