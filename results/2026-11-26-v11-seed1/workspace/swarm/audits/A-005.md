# Audit A-005 (full, system-invoked on a chart signal: units_in_storage beyond its upper limit, wrong direction; day 220 of 365)

**Summary.** The goal is still growing but far behind its aim, and the signal is real. Cash is 8,419.19 on day 220 (up 976.57 over the last 31 days, about 31 a day), against about 364 a day needed to reach 61,219 by day 365; the exemplar line in the charts is a straight-line interpolation (3,228 on day 30 to 61,219 on day 365; 36,118 on day 220), so PIP 4.3 measures the gap to a line, not to a reference path, and the size of the gap is a finding for the human. The pinpointed problem is idle capacity next to stock: 173 units sat in the depot on day 220 with 22 of 31 priced slots empty and 0% of worker time used, after a 100-unit delivery landed on day 219. Redesign R-P-008 predicted that idle days would fall and cash_gain_30d would hold above its phase centre; neither prediction appeared on the charts (cash_gain_30d has been below its pre-redesign centre for 8 in a row). Diagnosis is needed. Unit cost is the clear gain (0.92 on day 160 to 0.809 on day 220), and revenue on busy days is strong. Several records A-004 asked for are still not on file, and no pulse has been written since day 117.

## Findings

**1. Direction.**
- The goal (cash) is up, but the rate is low: 31 a day over days 189 to 220 against about 364 a day required from day 220 to reach 61,219. At the present rate the goal will not be met; this is a finding for the human, not a change to the aim (the spec says the goal is never lowered).
- Revenue is recovering: revenue over the last 7 days is 569.3 on day 220 against 490.9 the same week a month earlier (metrics day 220). The recovery comes from busy days (for example day 219 at 0.948 capacity, revenue 187; day 208 at 0.948, revenue 230).
- Redesign R-P-008 (adopted day 183): its shift on cash_gain_30d went the wrong way. Since adoption, cash_gain_30d has run at a mean of 744 against a pre-redesign centre of 1,035 (charts.md, "since redesign" table; 8 in a row below the centre). The predicted shift in idle days (4 or fewer of 20 at 6% or less) was not met in A-004 (12 of 20). Both predicted shifts are on record as not appearing.
- Is anything in the chain not worth its cost? Management review spend is the question the records cannot answer (finding 6).

**2. Signals.**
- units_in_storage: 173 on day 220, beyond the upper limit of 159.45 (centre 53.75), worse direction (charts.md). Components on day 220 (day-0220.md): 173 units, of which 40 gummy-bears, 40 nuts... (see finding 3).
- cash: beyond its upper limit (phase basis, centre 7,527, latest 8,419). The phase basis resets when R-P-008 was adopted and is flattered by the reset; judged against the pre-redesign limits it is not a gain.
- cash_gain_30d: latest 976.57 is beyond its phase upper limit (965.63), but the value equals cash on day 220 minus cash on day 189 (8,419.19 − 7,442.62), a 31-day window. Cash on day 220 minus cash on day 190 gives 959.37. Minor, but the chart's window is one day wider than the label.
- unit_cost_paid_30d: 0.809, beyond its lower limit (0.81), improvement.
- capacity_used_share: latest 0.0; the chart limits (-0.38 to 0.96 on a 0-to-1 share) cannot bind, so the signal this measure should give is silent. Requested in A-003 and A-004, not fixed.

**3. Idle capacity next to stock (the pinpointed problem).**
- Days 160 to 220: capacity used is 0.0 on many days (I did not count them exactly; the zero runs are visible in metrics_system.md, e.g. days 179 to 181, 185 to 186, 213 and 220); the rest run at 0.35 to 0.95. Busy days carry most revenue. The share is bimodal: a day either uses almost nothing or runs to the day's end.
- Day 220 (day-0220.md, my count): 22 of 31 priced slots at quantity 0 across the three machines (AI-HOUSE 9, AI-LOUNGE 5, MAIN-LOUNGE 8). Storage holds 173 units, including 40 gummy-bears, 30 nuts-mix, 30 stroopwafel and 11 oat-latte. Opportunities open: O3 (cash idle while slots are empty; cash 8,419 is well above 2,000). O1 and O2 are also met by the snapshot, although the hand-off lists only O3 as open now.
- Day 219: capacity 0.948, 100 units from PO-1061 moved out of storage (73 left), revenue 187. Day 220: nothing loaded, capacity 0.0. So the load happened on the day of delivery, but the next day used nothing and the depot rose back.
- Whether the idle days were chosen, forced by a call or context limit, or caused by the loading procedure is not settled by the records. The working agent's context grew from 36k tokens (day 2) to 717k (day 220); the per-day work log that A-004 asked for (calls, stop reason, context) is not on file. The spec lists this as an open question.

**4. Replenishment.**
- Orders are still landing after 2 to 5 days in most cases (PO-1057 paid 200, due 202; PO-1058 paid 204, due 206; PO-1059 paid 208, due 212; PO-1061 paid 216, due 219). Lead time is no longer the main gap; the loading of stock that has already landed is.
- Records disagree on loading: latest.md marks several lines loaded on day 186, but metrics show capacity_used_share 0.0 on day 186, and a fill costs 45 minutes (spec). Either fills are not counted in capacity or one record is wrong. Still unverified.

**5. Keep-doing.**

- When stock is loaded, revenue usually follows: on the high-capacity days of days 200 to 219 revenue ran from about 90 to 230 (days 200, 208, 214, 219), with one exception (day 204: capacity 0.854, revenue 26.0, most slots empty). This is the behaviour G-003 and G-009 describe. Keep.

**Diagnosis: pinpointed problem.** Stock that has landed is not being loaded into empty priced slots, and worker time is idle in alternate days, while cash sits unused. Whether this is the agent's choice, a call or context limit, or a loading fault in the environment (for example capacity not counting fills, or a per-day stop the agent cannot see) is not settled by the records on file. The diagnostician should look at the per-day work log for days 200 to 220 and at the capacity accounting for fills.
