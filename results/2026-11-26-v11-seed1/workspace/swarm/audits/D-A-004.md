# Diagnosis D-A-004 (day 220 of 365)

**Summary.** The pinpointed problem in A-004 is still there at day 220: worker time is left unused on many days while machine slots are empty and stock waits in the depot. On day 220 capacity used was 0%, 9 of 72 slots held stock (by my count from the day-220 ground truth), and 173 units sat in storage. Cash is recovering (8,419 on day 220 against 7,443 on day 189), so the top measure now looks like it is improving, which hides the drift. The cause sits in the environment, not in the agent's knowledge: the system reports only O3 (cash idle) as open, although by the spec's own definitions O1 (empty slots with spare time) and O2 (more than 50 units in storage with spare time) hold on day 220. The guidance that tells the agent to use spare worker time on earning slots (G-003 and G-009) applies only when O1 or O2 is open, so it is never handed over. The agent therefore sees no prompt to load stock and no signal that idle time is the problem. The system should show O1 and O2 open when their conditions hold, and the designer should check that the hand-off surfaces the guidance for them. Whether idle days were chosen or forced by a call or context limit is not settled: the per-day work log is not on file.

## Pinpoint

- Days 160 to 189 (A-004): 17 of 30 days at 6% or less of worker time; the share is bimodal, either near zero or run to 17:00.
- Days 189 to 220: the pattern continues. Days at 0.0 capacity include 189, 190, 192 to 198, 206, 207, 211, 212, 213, 216, 218 and 220 (from `swarm/ground_truth/`). The day-end opportunity count is 1 to 3 across these days; the names are given only in the hand-off, which on day 220 shows O3 alone.
- Depot: 2 units on days 189 to 197 (stock-out while PO-1055 and PO-1056 were due on days 197 and 198); 173 units on day 220 with 63 of 72 slots empty by my count of the day-220 slot table. That table gives 9 stocked slots, but the `slots_selling` measure reads 13 on day 220; this disagreement is unresolved and should be checked against the machine state.
- The cash chart and the signal are not the problem: cash_gain_30d is above its phase centre and cash is positive every day on record.

## What maintains it

- **Before the behaviour:** the agent is told, in the hand-off, that the only open opportunity is O3, a cash opportunity. The hand-off's guidance list (G-001, G-004, G-005, G-006, G-008) matches O3 only. G-003 ("use the worker-time budget on slots that earn") and G-009 ("load stock the day it lands") are not in the list. Nothing the agent is shown says that empty slots and spare minutes are a gap.
- **After the behaviour:** the cash chart reads IMPROVEMENT on day 220 (cash beyond its upper limit, phase basis). Buying and marketing pay visibly; loading and selling do not appear as a target. The agent's own result (cash up) reinforces the choices it already makes.
- **Supply:** a stock-out between day 188 and day 197 gave the agent nothing to load on those days. That part is a timing problem in ordering (payment on day 187 for delivery on days 197 and 198), and the cause of the longer due dates is not in the records.

## Cause (environment first)

1. **Information (primary).** O1 and O2 are not shown as open. On day 220 the spec's conditions hold for both: slots with no stock are at least 10 (63 of 72), units in storage exceed 50 (173), and worker time is unused (0%). The hand-off and the day-end count show O3 only. Because O1 and O2 are closed, G-003 and G-009 do not reach the agent. The likely fault is in how the system evaluates the "unused" resource for `slots stocked at day end` and `worker time`, or in how the hand-off selects opportunities. The evaluation log for O1 and O2 on days 189 to 220 would settle which.
2. **Information (chart).** The cash chart and its phase basis show improvement while the driver that moves revenue has fallen. The charts show no measure that rises or falls with loaded slots. `capacity_used_share` cannot bind: on day 220 its limits are -0.38 to 0.96 on a 0-to-1 share, so a drop to 0 cannot be a signal. This was requested in A-003 and R-P-008 and is still open.
3. **Incentives (open question).** Zero-use days cost nothing visible to the agent. The `early_end_unused_share` and `repeated_early_ends` cadence settings exist, but no early-end review is on file since P-008. If the agent ends days early by choice, nothing stops it. This cannot be decided without the work log.
4. **Resources.** Replenishment lead time of 10 to 11 days (PO-1055, PO-1056) left the depot nearly empty for about 9 days. Whether this was a supplier change or the order size is not in the records.
5. **Not supported by the records:** knowledge or model tier. The agent's context grew from 36k to 717k tokens over 220 days, but zero-use days occur at all context sizes (day 19 at 102k, day 186 at 646k), so context size is not the sole cause. Confirm with the work log.

## What the environment would need to change (not written here)

- Show O1 and O2 in the hand-off's "open now" list when their conditions hold, and check the evaluation for `unused` on both the slot and worker-time resources. Name the measure: `slots_selling` and `worker time (minutes a day)` used share.
- Chart a measure that rises with loaded slots (or make `capacity_used_share` bind on 0 to 1) so the top-level drift is visible under the phase reset.
- Check that the hand-off's guidance list is driven by the same opportunity evaluation, so G-003 and G-009 surface when O1 or O2 is open.

## Evidence still needed

- The per-day work log for days 160 to 220: calls made, stop reason and context size on each zero-use day. This decides chosen against forced idle days (the spec's open question).
- The opportunity evaluation log for days 189 to 220 showing the resource values for O1 and O2.
- The signal firing log for `slots_selling <= 3` (R-P-008 prediction C and its revert clause).
- Why PO-1055 and PO-1056 were due 10 to 11 days after payment.
