# Compare offers on unit cost, and read the cost against the mix bought
Revises: G-008
Applies when: O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period checks current offers at the quantity it intends to order and compares suppliers on unit cost at that quantity. It also reads the 30-day unit cost against the products in each order, so a rise can be traced to price or to which products were bought. For example, a large order of oat-latte-can at 1.57 raises the average even when every line is bought at its offer price.
Why: The earlier entry said buying this way coincided with unit cost falling (0.88 on day 33 to 0.808 on day 53). Since day 70 unit cost paid 30d has risen from 0.783 to 0.871, beyond its upper limit (swarm/charts.md, day 97), while orders were still placed at current offers. Recent orders include higher-cost lines (oat-latte-can 1.10 to 1.57, nuts-mix-75g 0.78 to 1.11; swarm/ground_truth/day-0097.md, PO-1026 to PO-1034). The records do not yet separate price from product mix.
