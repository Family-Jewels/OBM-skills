# Read unit cost against the mix of lines bought, order by order
Revises: G-008
Applies when: O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period compares each new order's unit cost line by line with the last price it paid for the same product from the same supplier, and reads the 30-day unit cost against the mix of lines bought, not only its total. For example, PO-1065 (paid day 236, Noordzee Dranken) bought energy-drink at 1.14, oat-latte at 1.39 and cola at 0.85; the same supplier had sold cola at 0.60 on PO-1063 (paid day 225). The 30-day unit cost moved from 0.82 on day 236 to 0.851 on day 237, above its phase upper limit. Over days 160 to 220 the same measure fell from 0.92 to 0.809 while orders were placed against the lower offers, which is the gain to keep.
Why: swarm/ground_truth/day-0237.md order table (PO-1063, PO-1065). swarm/metrics_system.md unit_cost_paid_30d: 0.92 on day 160, 0.809 on day 220, 0.82 on day 236, 0.851 on day 237. swarm/charts.md: latest 0.851, upper limit 0.83 (phase since day 183). The offer prices in force on each payment day are not in the ground truth, so whether 0.85 was the offer or a poorer price is unverified; the mix effect is visible in the order table.
