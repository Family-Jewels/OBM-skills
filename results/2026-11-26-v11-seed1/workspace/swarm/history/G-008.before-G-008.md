# Read unit cost against the mix of lines bought, order by order
Revises: G-008
Applies when: O2, O3
What good looks like: A strong period compares each new order line by line with the last price it paid for the same product from the same supplier, and reads the 30-day unit cost against the mix of lines bought, not only its total. For example, Zuidas Groothandel B.V. supplied energy-drink-500ml at 0.90 on PO-1074 (paid day 272) and at 1.29 on PO-1076 (paid day 288); gummy-bears-150g from the same supplier was 0.51 on PO-1075 (paid day 277) and 0.73 on PO-1078 (paid day 292). Lines whose price moved most since the last order from that supplier are the ones a period can check first, before the order is placed.
Why: swarm/ground_truth/day-0297.md order table (PO-1074, PO-1075, PO-1076, PO-1078 lines and prices). swarm/metrics_system.md unit_cost_paid_30d: 0.862 on day 238 and 0.827 on day 297. swarm/charts.md: unit_cost_paid_30d latest 0.827, beyond its lower limit (phase since day 277). The offer prices in force on each payment day are not in the ground truth, so whether each move came from the offer or from the choice of supplier is untested; the order table shows the prices paid. The example in G-008 (PO-1065, day 236) is superseded by these more recent orders; the advice itself is unchanged.
