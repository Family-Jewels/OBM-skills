# Use the worker-time budget on slots that earn
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: The day has 480 worker minutes; a fill takes 45 and a swap takes 30. A strong period spends most of its trips on slots that will sell (stocked, priced, with a product customers buy at that location) and on moving stock from the depot into them. A swap is worth its 30 minutes when the product in a slot is not selling and a better one is in storage. For example, if three slots are empty and two products are sitting in the depot, two fills that day use time that would otherwise go unused.
Why: Each trip is the only way to change a slot's contribution, and the worker-time resource is charted as unused when it is spare. Time left unused at day end is capacity lost for that day.
