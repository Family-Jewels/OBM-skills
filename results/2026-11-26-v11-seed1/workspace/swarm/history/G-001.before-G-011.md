# Opening days: fill what can sell, then learn
Applies when: always
What good looks like: In the first days, get the state of all six machines and the supplier catalogues, then stock and price the slots that can sell so that revenue is earning from early on. After a few days of sales, read the sales report and let it guide the next choices. For example, an opening where every machine has sellable stock and a price by day 2 gives the sales report something to learn from; an opening that spends many days on catalogues alone gives it nothing.
Why: Revenue comes only from slots that are stocked and priced (slots_selling). The reference operator reaches EUR 3,228 by day 30, so early days matter. Early evidence on price response is cheapest to gather while the machines are running.
