# Diagnosis D-P-002 (day 17 of 365; from pulse P-002, day 14)

**Problem (pinpointed).** The top measure (cash) has no chart signal, but it is far behind the exemplar (PIP 20.5; cash 1,547.78 vs 2,479.2 at day 17). The driver that moved is slots_selling, the constraint: 17 at day 7 against the M1 aim of at least 36 (missed), 25 at day 17. Snack slots run dry between deliveries (D10–11 empty, D13 crisps and MAIN energy empty). Each order is about €80–120 and covers only a few slots, while the spec allows up to 85% of deployable cash into stock. At day 17, capital is 15% in use (1,384 idle) and 63 of 72 slots sit unstocked at day end.

**Evidence.**
- charts.md: slots_selling 25 latest, stocked-at-day-end median 9 of 72; capital 15% in use.
- progress.md: D1 orders total about €363 across three suppliers (crisps 60, gummy 36, dropjes 36 per supplier). "Zuidas stock fully allocated" (D4). Deliveries land the morning after due day, 2–11 days out. D10: "machines ran dry". D5: "stopped refilling at 15:20 (only duplicate lines left)".
- P-001: committed orders €483 against about 85% of deployable cash.
- Kanaal-cash-carry is marked "expensive for most lines"; no log entry shows it was priced at bulk quantity.

**What maintains it.** Supply lead time and small per-offer quantities, not refill effort, cap slots. Nothing in the brief tells the operator how much stock to hold per slot-day or how far ahead to order, so orders are sized by feel. The only visible consequence is solvency: cash is far above €120, so the safe choice (hold cash) costs nothing visible. Idle slots and idle capital have no consequence until a pulse reports them 7 days later.

**Cause.**
- *Information:* aims.md sets a slot target but no order cadence, depot cover or lead-time allowance; the M1 aim ignored delivery lags of 2–4 days. Open question 1 (restock fills one slot) was measured, but sourcing capacity was not.
- *Resources:* supplier breadth is unused. Four suppliers are known; only two were priced at volume. Worker time is not the cause while the depot is empty.
- *Incentives:* holding cash is never penalised; an unfilled slot is reported only at the pulse.

**Conflicting records to settle before the designer relies on them.**
- charts.md shows worker time 450 of 480 min median used (94%); P-002 read 150 min median at day 14; the log records clock ends at 15:20–16:15. Need the simulator's daily clock readings and the missing `log/work.jsonl` (T-0001 required it; it does not exist).
- slots_selling (25) vs stocked at day end (median 9): definitions still differ (P-001 raised this).
- Complaints CMP-001–003 are unpaid claims 4–10× item price; reputation 0.88. Sales rose after day 14, so no effect is shown yet. The complaint rule (brief: "full amount") needs a designer decision.

**What the design would need to change.**
1. Measure: add a depot-cover measure (days of stock on hand vs sell-through) and an order-cadence rule tied to supplier lead time. Target slots_selling.
2. Aims: rebase the aim on lead time, and do not set day-7 slot aims that depend on a delivery landing after day 4.
3. Sourcing: have the operator price all four suppliers at bulk quantity, so that orders match the 72-slot capacity.
4. Incentive: make idle capital and dry slots visible within the point, not only at the pulse.

Strategist's redirect due at day 21 (S-001) should take the constraint as supply-to-slot, not worker time, unless the clock evidence above shows otherwise.
