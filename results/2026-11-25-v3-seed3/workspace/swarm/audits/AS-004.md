## Why the redirect cannot be met as the work is done

1. **Refill budget.** The Strategist asks for seven 45-min refill trips (315 min, 66%). The spec's milestone M1 caps refills at ≤ 60% of day-minutes (288 min), and aims.md v4 kept to that. Six trips is the most the current spec allows. Seven trips needs the budget raised in a redesign. I have not changed the spec.
2. **The constraint is not measured.** Depot cover (spec 2a) and sell-through are still not charted, after three strategies. Without them the stretch aim for slots_selling (24) has no check that shows refill minutes are buying sales. Spec open question 8 is still open.
3. **Operator allowance is not published.** Spec open question 9 is unanswered. The day-70 chart showed 0 minutes used while the log's last entry was day 64. Whether the operator is out of budget is unknown, so the aim cannot assume it has spare calls.
4. **Worker-time figures disagree.** S-004 cites median 180 min used (38%). charts.md, the system's measure, shows median 75 (16%) and 3 unused days in 7. I have used the chart figure. Open question 6 should be closed by the system's daily clock readings, not by either report.
5. **Refill rate is unknown.** Open question 1 (does one `restock_machine` fill one slot or a machine) is not recorded as settled. Without it, six trips cannot be shown to reach 24 selling slots.

Items 1–3 block the aims as written. Items 4–5 are measurement gaps the system should close.

- Raise the refill budget in spec Milestones M1–M3 to the level the Strategist's split needs (66%, or a stated ceiling), or keep 60% and accept that slots_selling 24 is not reachable this way.
