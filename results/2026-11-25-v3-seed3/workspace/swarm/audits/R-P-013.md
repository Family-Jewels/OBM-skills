# Redesign R-P-013 (invoked day 215; applied on the record as it stood at day 267, point 39)

Trigger: diagnosis D-P-013 (day 203, point 29): slots stopped being refilled while depot stock existed; aims not reaching the operator; operator-written rules (8 units per slot, "one week of sales") in place of the spec; clock and log disagree; allowance unpublished.

Record note: the charts this redesign was read against are dated day 267, not day 215. Aims v11 (AS-010, day 243) was already in force when this change was made. Nothing here changes it.

## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The tree adds up (revenue = slots_selling × revenue_per_slot; cash = opening + revenue − purchases − fees − refunds). The link that matters is refill trips landing depot stock in slots. Depot stock is on hand (day 211: choco 44, crisps 44, nuts 37, stroopwafel 38), so supply is not the binding link. Nothing here lowers the goal or an aim.

The direction is right, but the link cannot be seen. The log records loads; the machines read zero after them (D195, D214, D225, P-022); the simulator clock reads zero minutes. Each redesign since day 126 changed information about supply (cover formula, DEPOT line) and left the refill-to-slot link unmeasured (A-002, finding 2). That is the cause to fix.

## 2. Is the design causing it?

Yes, in information and in rules the spec never set:
- **No refill target and no load size in the spec.** The operator wrote its own (8 per slot, a loose trip count). Loads were small and few (3 to 5 a day against six needed).
- **No read-back.** Nothing requires slot stock before and after a trip, so a load that does not land is never seen.
- **Open question 1 never closed.** The log's D4 entry says one call fills one slot in 45 minutes. No read-back has confirmed it on the machine.
- **Clock and log conflict** (spec open question 10). The system's daily clock reading is not yet published.

Not the cause, so not changed here: the operator allowance. The log's budget line reads about $0.09 a day against an allowance of about $0.14 (A-002). Budget did not stop the refills. The allowance is still unpublished, so this rests on the log.

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)

Spec v7 → v8 (`history/spec.v7.md` kept):
1. Budgets: refill rule. Each working day after the core reads, one 45-minute trip per empty or short slot whose product has depot stock, up to six trips. Load = full capacity (18 small, 10 large) while the depot holds that line, else spread the line's stock over its empty slots. Each trip read back (stock before and after).
2. Budgets: refill cap set at 60% of day-minutes (288 min) for every milestone, resolving the conflict flagged in AS-008 and A-002.
3. Checks: a refill-target row. A trip passes only if the clock shows 45 minutes and the slot's stock rises. A log line alone does not pass.
4. Open questions 1 and 10: the first trip after v8 reads one slot's stock and the clock to settle open question 1; the clock and log both go in each event line until the system's reading settles open question 10.

Operator brief v7 → v8 (`history/operator.v7.md` kept): the refill target, load and read-back rules in the spec's words; the first-trip test; both clock and log numbers on a conflict; no load or order numbers except the spec's.

Aims: not changed. Aims v11 stands (AS-010).
