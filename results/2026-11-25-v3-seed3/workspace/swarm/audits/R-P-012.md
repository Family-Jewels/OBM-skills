# Redesign R-P-012 (day 169, point 24)

Trigger: diagnosis D-P-012 (day 161 charts). Spec v6 → v7; operator brief v6 → v7. Aims (v8) unchanged.

## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The top measure is cash. The tree is right: slots_selling and depot cover are the drivers that move cash, and D-P-012 locates the constraint in supply to slots (62 of 72 slots empty at day end, 8,531 capital idle). The aims name the same constraint. Nothing here lowers the goal or an aim.

## 2. Is the design causing the problem?

Yes, in information, not in the aims:
- Depot cover is the supply link, but it depends on units sold per day, which comes only from the sales report. When the sales report is unread, the DEPOT line cannot give a cover figure, so the off-round order that v6 requires never fires. Depot cover is also uncharted (requested in R-P-004 and R-P-010, still open).
- The order-size rule in v6 says "cover to lead time + 2 days" but gives no number the operator can check against.

Not designed here, and not assumed: the operator allowance (unpublished, open question 9) and the worker-time record conflict (open question 6). The latest chart shows 0 minutes used and 3 idle days in 7. This may be an allowance or stall problem; the system must settle it (R-S-001). The change below does not depend on it.

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses the cause)

1. Spec 2a: units sold per day can be taken from the slot-stock fall between day-end reads, so cover is computable from the core reads alone.
2. Spec Budgets: the cover check moves into the core reads, funded first. Order quantity is stated as a number: max(0, units sold per day × (lead time + 2) − depot − in slots), whole cases, after check_offers.
3. Operator v7: the same check each working day, using the spec's quantity.

No change to checks, milestones, cadence, decision rights or aims.

## 4. Prediction

- Measure that should shift: slots_selling, up. Its centre has been 14.3 with eight points below it; the direction is wrong now.
- Expected: eight points in a row above the current centre by day 196 (point 28), or the celeration flat at least, from the current /1.39.
- Also expected: the DEPOT line written on every working day from day 170, and an off-round order on any day its cover is below lead time + 2 (count these in the log).
- If not: by day 196 slots_selling still shows a wrong-way signal, and the log shows cover below lead time + 2 on two or more days with no off-round order, then the cause is not the brief. Re-diagnose; do not repeat this change.

## 5. Cost and revert

- Cost: one check_offers round on low-cover days, a few calls. Funded before price and reads.
- Revert to spec v6 and operator v6 (both in swarm/history/) if the operator's core reads are cut on two or more days in a week, or cash falls below the floor line for three points in a row.

## 6. Requests to the system (not designer changes)

- Chart depot cover per fast seller (R-P-004, R-P-010 still open).
- Publish the operator allowance and the minutes by tool each day (R-S-001; open questions 6 and 9).

Design change needed: no (no aim changed).
