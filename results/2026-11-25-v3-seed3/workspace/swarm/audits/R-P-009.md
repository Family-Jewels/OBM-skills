# R-P-009: redesign after D-P-009 (day 102, point 14/15)

**Direction.** Right. slots_selling is the constraint and depot cover is the supply link that feeds it; the driver tree and the aims (aims.md v5, 270 min of refills, inside the spec's ≤60% refill budget of 288 min) follow from that. The tree does not need to change. Cash is above its floor and the Strategist's aim is not lowered.

**Cause addressed (environment: how the work is done).** The off-round order rule fires only when a fast seller's cover falls below its lead time (2–11 days). A delivery lands after the lead time, so by the time the rule fires the slot has already gone dry for the whole wait. Combined with a point-day round every 7 days, stock is bought in lumps and slots sit empty between rounds. Refills then have nothing to load, which is consistent with the 5–15 min median used and the 59 idle slots.
