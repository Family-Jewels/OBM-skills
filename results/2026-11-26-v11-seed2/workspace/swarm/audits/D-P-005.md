# Diagnosis D-P-005 (day 60 of 365)

**Summary.** Cash (€2,374) is still beyond its upper limit, but the driver under it has broken down. From day 40 to day 60, 10 of 21 days used under 10% of worker time while stock waited in the depot (15 units on days 41–43, 21 to 121 on days 49–50, 53 on days 55–58) and most of the 72 slots were empty. The stocked-slot count fell to 1–3 on days 55–58 and the 7-day revenue fell from €684 (day 51) to €258 (day 60), its lowest since day 44. The pulse P-005 saw the first episode and did not see that it recurred. The cause sits mainly in information: the system's own opportunity labels tell the agent to buy stock on the same idle days that O2 says to clear the depot, nothing ties the size of a purchase to loading time, and no signal fires until a measure reaches zero, so a slide to 1–3 slots is silent. Incentives add to it: an idle day costs nothing visible, and a purchase lowers the top chart at once while its return comes only after loading. The agent's own reasons for the idle days are not in the record, so the cause is not fully confirmed; evidence needed is listed below.

## Driver walk
- **Top: cash**, IMPROVEMENT since day 34 (unit_cost_paid_30d fell 1.06 → 0.88). Reads well, but it rests on cheaper stock, not on more sales.
- **Revenue (7-day sum)**: 684 (day 51) → 258 (day 60). Daily revenue is "routine" on the chart. The 30-period check in the spec still passes (€1,913 for days 31–60 against €1,779 before), so it does not catch the slide.
- **slots_selling × revenue_per_slot**: slots_selling 6, 4, 5 on days 41–43; 3, 2, 1, 2 on days 55–58. Revenue per slot is routine. The moving driver is stocked slots.
- **Capacity used / worker time** (the resource behind stocked slots): under 10% on days 41, 42, 43, 49, 50, 52, 55, 56, 57, 58. Units sold on those days are mostly 1–6 (two exceptions, 23 on day 41 and 36 on day 52, from stock already in machines).

## Pinpointed problem
From day 40 on, worker time goes unused on repeated stretches while stock waits in the depot and most slots stay empty. Each stretch (days 41–43, 49–50, 55–58) is a run of days with units_in_storage > 0, O2 open, and capacity under 10%. The first such run was day 32–33 (226 units in storage, 0% and 5% used).

## Evidence
- Ground truth day 41 to 60 (`swarm/ground_truth/`): capacity 0.021, 0.062, 0.0; storage 15 on days 41–43. PO-1014 (€299, paid day 41, delivered day 43, 322 units) lifted storage to 332 on day 44. Cash fell 2,156 → 1,885 on days 41–42.
- Storage 21 → 121 on day 49–50 (PO-1015 landed day 49); capacity 0.0 on both days.
- Storage stuck at 53 with capacity 0.0 on days 55, 57, 58 and 0.062 on day 56; slots_selling 3, 2, 1, 2.
- `charts.md`: units_in_storage beyond its upper limit; slots_selling and revenue have no signal, since the signals in `system.json` fire at zero only (slots_selling zero for 2 periods, revenue zero for 3).
- Opportunities: O2 open on the idle days; O1 and O3 also open on days 41–43 (3 open at day end).
- Working agent's context: 200.2k tokens on day 41, 210k on day 44, 258k on day 60. The first idle run (days 32–33) was at about 172k, so context size does not explain it.

## What maintains it
- **Buying is what the environment rewards on idle days.** O3 reads "Cash is available to spend and worker time is unused: buy stock or run a price test", and G-004 advises "use both" on such days. The idle day produces a buy, which adds stock to the depot that the same idle worker time has to load.
- **Idle costs nothing visible.** With 1–6 units a day and slots_selling above zero, no signal fires. Cash still rises from cheaper stock, so the top chart reads IMPROVEMENT.
- **A purchase hits the top chart at once** (cash −€271 on day 42), while its return comes only after loading. Idle loading has no matching cost.

## Cause
- **Information (main).** (a) O2 says clear the depot onto empty slots (G-007), O3 says buy stock on the same idle days, and no guidance links purchase size to loading time. G-007 and G-008 have passed their revisit dates (day 40 and day 50); G-001, G-002 and G-005 show day-30 revisits with no recorded outcome. (b) The revenue and slot signals fire only at zero, so a fall to 1–3 slots is silent. (c) The handoff carries the day-45 pulse as current, and no event note for the three early ends on days 41–43 (the cadence's early-end rule) is in the record.
- **Information, known but not yet fixed.** `slots_selling` still counts sellers, not stocked slots (R-A-001 request 1). Days 41 and 43 match ground truth (6 and 5), so this did not cause the idle days, but it blurs the slot count the agent sees.
- **Incentives.** Idle days carry no visible cost; purchases carry an immediate cost on the top measure. Check 3 covers a 30-period window, which hides a 9-day slide.
- **Resources (not confirmed).** Worker time is 480 min a day and a fill is about 45 min; a 322-unit delivery takes several days to load. Context near 200k on day 41 may matter; check the run log for a compaction or reset on days 41–43.
- **Not supported by the record:** an outage, a missing credential, or a usage limit. No environment error is recorded for these days.

## What the environment would need to change (not written here)
1. O3's label and G-004 should not prescribe buying while units_in_storage > 0 and slots are empty; point to loading first. Moves capacity_used_share and units_in_storage.
2. Add an event on idle days with stock waiting: capacity_used_share < 0.1 on two consecutive periods with units_in_storage > 0, delivered in the handoff. Moves capacity_used_share and slots_selling.
3. Chart the 7-day revenue sum and stocked slots against limits, with a signal on a sustained fall, so the slide is visible. Moves revenue.
4. Apply R-A-001 request 1 (count stocked, priced slots with stock). Moves slots_selling.
5. Extend the P-005 proposal to cover purchases on idle days, and record the revisit outcomes of G-001, G-002, G-005, G-007, G-008.
6. Record the agent's stated reason for each early end in the day's notes, so the next diagnosis can read it.

## Evidence that would settle the cause
The agent's call log and reasoning for days 41–43, 49–50 and 55–58: whether it ended the day by choice, whether it saw the depot stock and empty slots, and whether any tool error or context compaction occurred. Also the event notes for days 41–43 (were they sent?).

Environment change: none (diagnosis only; the designer decides).
