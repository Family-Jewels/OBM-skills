Storage went from 15 (day 41) to 259 (day 60). Orders were not sized to the stocked slots' sales rate, and loading lagged delivery (G-003; G-007).

Each day's end showed three opportunities open (ground truth, opportunities_open_at_end = 3). The record does not show why. Context is not the whole story: days 3 to 5 were idle at about 40k tokens, while the agent was at 258k on day 60.

The request for this was made at day 30 (R-A-001 request 1) and repeated in P-005; it is unmet. The PIP is charted as 3.5, a level ratio. On the spec's gain definition it is about 7.9 (exemplar gain of about EUR 6,921 against about EUR 874). The handoff's "Open now" lists one opportunity (O2) against the chart's three.
4. **The redesign's prediction is untested in its stated form.** Its preconditions (requests 1 and 2) are not met, so its revenue shift cannot be read as a fair test. The revenue part did not appear anyway.
5. **Records are behind.** Six guidance entries are past their revisit days with no revisit record (G-001, G-002, G-005, G-006 at day 30; G-007 at day 40; G-008 at day 50). G-008 is used twice: the refuted proposal (refuted/G-008.md) and the live entry. There are no pulse records for days 50 or 55 (the spec sets pulses every 5 periods); the last is P-005 at day 45. The proposal P-005 is still awaiting its fact check.
