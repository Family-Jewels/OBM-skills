# Redesign R-A-005 (answers D-A-005; day 344 of 365)

**Summary.** D-A-005 found that the empty machines between deliveries are driven by an order volume the agent cannot see a target for: the charts show slots_selling and cash but no open gap between priced-and-empty slots and the depot, two charts cannot signal (capacity_used_share and units_in_storage_change have limits the system computed too wide), and the guidance G-004 and G-007 conflict. Within the environment files I write, this redesign makes the smallest change that gives the agent a visible purchase gap: opportunity O4 (stocked slots empty and no stock in the depot), a `units` chart in place of the storage-change chart that cannot signal, and a proposed guidance entry G-010 that revises G-004 and reconciles it with G-007 as advice. It does not change the goal, the exemplar, the milestones or any limit. Three diagnosed faults are not in my reach and are flagged to the system below. The cause is not fully settled: the tool-call log that would show whether some zero days were tool refusals or budget limits is not in the workspace, so this change assumes the information cause D-A-005 names and would be wrong if the log shows otherwise.
