# Diagnosis D-A-003 (requested by A-003; records to day 149 of 365)

**Summary.** Cash is EUR 3,926 at day 149 (PIP about 6.1 against the exemplar's EUR 23,828 at this day), and the top measure is still rising but slowly. The moved driver is worker time: capacity used averaged 0.22 since R-P-005 against 0.28 before it, and it was 0% on 14 of days 120 to 149. The pinpointed problem is narrow and repeatable: on the first day after a delivery lands, the worker uses 0 to 13% of its time while 80 to 170 units wait in the depot and slots are empty, then loads on the next day at 57 to 85%. This held on all 8 delivery days since day 110 (days 111, 117, 123, 128, 134, 139, 145, 149), so each delivery costs one selling day. It is maintained by the environment, not by a reward: a delivery day has nothing visible to the agent that asks for action, and no chart or signal reaches it. Cause (information, most likely): the arrival is not reported as an event, and O3, which fires on almost every idle day (445 day-ends), reads as "buy or price", so the one thing the depot needs is not what the agent sees. Not supported by the records: a resource limit (loading takes about 45 minutes and the next day's loads reach 85%) or a context limit (idle days occur at 40k and 435k tokens alike). Cannot be settled from the workspace: the agent's notes, the tool-call log (log/work.jsonl) and the orchestrator log are absent. Settling evidence: for days 111, 134, 145 and 149, the delivery message the worker received and its tool calls for the day; whether any opportunity note was sent on those days (the early-end rule should have fired). For the designer: the environment should make an arrival visible on its day (an event line in the hand-off: "PO-n delivered: X units, Y slots empty") and give the capacity measure a chart that can signal below its centre. Do not change the agent; no rule or cap.

## 1. Walk down the tree

- **Top: cash** (chart: IMPROVEMENT beyond upper limit since day 117). Rising, but the 30-period gain fell from about EUR 24 a day to about EUR 15 (uncharted; no chart carries a 30-period gain).
- **Revenue** (centre 46.7; since R-P-005 mean 42.55 against 58.06 before; inside limits). Routine on its chart, but the level fell.
- **slots_selling** (6.26 vs 6.8 before) and **revenue_per_slot** (5.86 vs 6.63 before). Both fell; both inside limits.
- **worker time (capacity_used_share)** (since R-P-005 mean 0.22 vs 0.28 before). **This moved.** Its chart limits are −0.81 to 1.30, so it cannot signal below the centre; only the 8-in-a-row rule could, and it has not fired.
- **units_in_storage** (mean 35 vs 75 before; latest 96 at day 149). Storage fell since R-P-005, so the redesign's storage prediction held. The depot is being cleared more slowly than it fills at each delivery.

Routine variation inside the limits is not diagnosed. The driver that moved is capacity used, on delivery days.

## 3. What maintains it

- **Before the behavior:** the delivery is recorded in the purchase table on its due day, and the stock appears in storage on the next day's read. The day-149 hand-off names only "O2 (96)" as open, with the same wording as on every other open day, and O3 (the "buy or price" opportunity) as open on 445 day-ends. Neither says a delivery arrived.
- **After the behavior:** a zero-call day costs nothing visible and the next day loads at 76 to 85%. Nothing in the record punishes the idle day. The early-end rule (repeated early ends, window 5) should send a note, but the workspace holds no note for the idle post-delivery days, so the consequence is absent, not weak.
- The problem persists because the one signal that matters (a stocked-day gap after arrival) is not shown to the agent or charted.

## 4. Cause

- **Information (primary).** The arrival is not reported as an event; O2 and O3 are described the same whether stock just landed or has sat for weeks; O3 is open on nearly every idle day, so it carries no information; the hand-off shows the day-end state, not the day's arrival. Whether the agent knew of the delivery on the day cannot be checked here (see evidence below).
- **Incentives (secondary).** The early-end note did not appear in the record for these days; ending a day early has no visible cost to the agent.
- **Resources.** Not the cause. Loading needs about 45 minutes per fill and the loading days reach 76 to 85% of the 480 minutes. Storage and cash were not limiting (cash above EUR 3,000).
- **Knowledge / model tier.** Not diagnosed; the same pattern at 40k and 435k context (idle days at 40k in day 3; at 324k in days 87 to 89) does not point to context.

## 5. Evidence needed to settle it

1. The worker's tool calls and any end-of-day message on days 111, 117, 134, 145 and 149 (log/work.jsonl; not in the workspace).
2. The hand-off of days 110, 116, 133 and 144 (not in the workspace; only day 149 is).
3. Whether any opportunity or early-end note was sent on those days (the spec says each early end with O1 to O3 open gets one).

If (1) shows the worker made no calls and (2) shows no arrival in the hand-off, the cause is information and the fix is in the environment. If (1) shows calls that failed or were refused, it is a resource or tool issue to report as environment, not performance.

## 6. What the environment would need to change (not written as a change)

- Show the arrival on its day: a hand-off line per delivery ("PO-n delivered: X units; Y slots empty"). Measure to move: capacity_used_share on delivery+1 days; units_in_storage.
- Make O2 say how many units wait and how many slots are empty, so it differs between a fresh arrival and a long wait. O3 should not read as the default for an idle day.
- Give capacity_used_share a chart whose lower limit can be reached (its limits, −0.81 to 1.30, cannot signal a fall below the centre), or a run rule on it. Measure to move: the 30-period mean of capacity used.
- Keep G-004 proposed revision A-003 as advice; its revisit (day 143) is past, so the auditor should check it.
