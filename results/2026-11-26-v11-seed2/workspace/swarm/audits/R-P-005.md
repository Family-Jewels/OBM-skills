# Redesign R-P-005 (day 88 of 365)

**Summary.** D-P-005 found that the environment labelled buying as the open use of idle days (O3 "buy stock or run a price test", G-004 "use both") on the same days that stock waited in the depot with worker time unused, and that no chart or signal showed the slide in 7-day revenue. The direction of the work is right: the driver tree still runs from cash through revenue and stocked slots to worker time, so the goal and the tree stand. The change is small. O3's label and the spec row for it now point to loading waiting stock first; G-004's revision is proposed for the fact check; and a chart of revenue's change over 7 periods is added so a sustained fall reaches the system's own limits. Expected effect: capacity used on days with stock waiting rises, and units in storage falls back within its limits. Not changed: the goal, the exemplar, the cadence, and the system's early-end rule (which already fires; the missing event note is a delivery gap for the orchestrator and system to check).

Trigger: D-P-005 (day 60; redesign deferred to day 88 by the system, which spaces redesigns 20+ periods apart; last redesign R-A-001 day 39).

Why: the diagnosis pinned the cause to information: the O3 label and G-004 prescribe buying while stock waits and worker time is idle (diagnosis cause (a)), and the revenue signal fires only at zero (cause (b)). The chart addresses (b) without setting a limit; the system computes the limits from its own data.

Limits of this change:
- The interface has no rolling 7-day sum, so `revenue_change_7_periods` is a change over 7 periods, not the 7-day sum the diagnosis named. A sustained fall should still show as a run of points below the centre or beyond the lower limit.
- A signal on "capacity used with stock waiting" (diagnosis item 2) is not expressible: signals take one measure, so it is not added. The O2 opportunity already shows when stock waits with slots empty.
- Stocked-slot count (R-A-001 request 1) is still pending as a system fix; not changed here.
- G-007 (revisit day 40) and G-008 (day 50) are past their revisit dates; G-001, G-002 and G-005 are at day 30. These need the auditor's or the system's revisit record, not a designer change.

Prediction (testable on swarm/charts.md):
- From day 89, on each period with units_in_storage > 0 and slots empty, capacity_used_share averages at least 0.3 over days 89–100 (latest reading 0.0 on day 88; the 39-period mean is 0.28).

Cost: a purchase on an idle day may be delayed, so cash could sit flat for a few periods; cash_change_per_day is the watch measure. Check 3 (revenue trend) still guards against a lull.

Revert if: units_in_storage stays beyond its upper limit for 5 periods after day 95, or cash is beyond its lower limit for 5 periods, with capacity_used_share still below 0.1. Revert to `swarm/history/system.v2.json` and `swarm/history/spec.v2.md`, and withdraw the proposal.
