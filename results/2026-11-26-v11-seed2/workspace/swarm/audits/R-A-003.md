# Redesign R-A-003 (day 172 of 365; answers D-A-003)

**Summary.** D-A-003 finds that worker time is idle on the first day after each delivery (8 of 8 since day 110) while stock waits in the depot, and that the cause is most likely information: the arrival is never reported as an event, and O2 and O3 read the same whether stock just landed or has waited for weeks. The design can show a change in storage on its day through the existing chart mechanism, so I added one chart, `units_in_storage_change` (diff 1, better down, in `swarm/system.json`; prior version at `swarm/history/system.v1.json`). The spec, the goal, the exemplar and the guidance are unchanged. The fix the diagnosis asks for, a hand-off line per delivery ("PO-n delivered: X units; Y slots empty"), needs the system's hand-off to carry a delivery event, which the designer cannot write in `system.json`; it is requested from the system owner below and is not made here. The evidence in D-A-003 section 5 (tool-call log, earlier hand-offs, early-end notes) is not in the workspace, so the cause is not yet settled.

## Change

- `swarm/system.json`: added chart `units_in_storage_change` (from `units_in_storage`, diff 1, better down). The 1-period change is the only figure in the design that jumps on the arrival day.

O3 was considered for narrowing (it is open on 445 day-ends); not changed here, since O2 already covers the post-delivery state and narrowing O3 would move a guidance target with no evidence on what the agent does with it.

## Prediction (testable on the charts)

1. `units_in_storage_change` shows a signal (beyond its upper limit, the rise on the arrival day) on each delivery day, within the next delivery cycle (about 6 periods, by day 180).
2. Capacity used on the day after delivery moves above 0.5 on at least 4 of the next 6 post-delivery days. If it does not, the cause is not information alone, and the next diagnosis should look at the tool log and the early-end notes (D-A-003 section 5).
