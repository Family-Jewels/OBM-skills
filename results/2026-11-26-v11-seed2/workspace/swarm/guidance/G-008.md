# Fill several slots in one sitting
Applies when: O1
What good looks like: A day that starts with stock in the depot and worker time to spare can load several slots in one stretch, then read the sales report for the prices. On day 34 about 88% of the 480-minute day was used, seven slots sold 62 units, revenue reached €126 and the cash balance a new high of €1,858 (swarm/ground_truth/day-0034.md; swarm/charts.md).
Why: Capacity went from 5% of the day on day 33 to 88% on day 34, and revenue per slot rose to 18.04, beyond the chart's upper limit of 14.32. Stock becomes sales only once it is in a machine, which is the point of G-002's example of a strong day.
