# Stocked and priced slots are the earning capacity
Applies when: O1
What good looks like: A period that ends with empty slots and unused worker time tends to have left earning capacity on the table. An example of a strong day: the worker fills the slots that will sell through in the coming days first (the fill takes about 45 minutes), sets prices on each stocked slot, and reads the sales report before the day ends. A day of 480 minutes can hold several fills plus reads and price changes.
Why: slots_selling and revenue_per_slot are the two drivers of revenue, and revenue drives cash. An empty slot earns nothing overnight; a stocked, priced slot earns on every night it has stock.
