# Empty slots: load the depot first, then order for the slots
Applies when: O3, O4
What good looks like: When stocked slots are empty and the depot holds stock, loading that stock is usually the earning use of worker time, and idle cash can wait for it. When the slots are empty and the depot is empty too, a strong period orders for the empty priced slots, sized to what those slots can sell before the next delivery, not only to the depot's current level. Example: a day that ends with 10 units in the depot and two priced slots empty is a day to load the 10 units first, and to place the next order for the slots that stay empty.
Why: On days 271–272 of this run the machines were empty with 20 units in the depot and no loading; on day 309 two priced sparkling-water slots were empty with 10 units in the depot, and worker time was unused (D-A-005). Orders timed to a low depot left the machines empty between deliveries, while cash was about EUR 5,800.
