# Idle day, idle cash: use both
Applies when: O3
What good looks like: When a day ends with much of the worker time unused and cash on hand, a strong period uses one of the two: a restock or a supplier order for the slots that are selling, or a small price test on one slot whose result will show in the next sales report. An example: spend the unused minutes on a test that tells you something (one price change, one read of the sales report) rather than leaving the day idle.
Why: The day is 480 minutes and unused minutes earn nothing. Cash sitting idle while the machines have room is the same capacity problem as O1, seen from the money side.
