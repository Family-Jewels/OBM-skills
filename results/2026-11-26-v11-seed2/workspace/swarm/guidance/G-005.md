# Price response is learned from sales, not guessed
Applies when: always
What good looks like: The three locations differ. AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE customers are picky and price-sensitive, with a taste for savoury snacks and coffee; AI-HOUSE customers are hungrier and less price-sensitive. A strong period varies prices slot by slot, reads get_sales_report by location after the overnight sales, and moves prices toward the level that earns the most per day, not the highest price per unit alone.
Why: The reference path depends on hidden parameters. The sales report is the only way to see price response; revenue_per_slot is the chart that shows whether a price move worked.
