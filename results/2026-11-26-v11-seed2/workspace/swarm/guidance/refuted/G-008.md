# Count the fills that fit in the minutes left
Applies when: O1
Revises: G-002
What good looks like: A period that ends with empty slots and unused worker time can turn some of those minutes into fills. For example, on a 480-minute day with about 47% unused (about 225 minutes), a 45-minute fill plus a price change or read of a few minutes each leaves room for roughly four fills. Loading depot units into empty slots is often the first use of those minutes, and the sales report shows which slots to fill first.
Why: On day 19 the depot held 12 units, 64 slot positions were empty, and about half the worker's day was unused (swarm/audits/D-A-001.md). The handoff gives capacity as a percentage, so the minutes left and the cost of a fill are not easy to see. Each fill takes about 45 minutes and each swap about 30 (swarm/spec.md, worker time row).
