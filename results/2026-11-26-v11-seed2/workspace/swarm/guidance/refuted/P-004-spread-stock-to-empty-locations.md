# Spread loaded stock to empty locations
Applies when: O2
What good looks like: When stock is waiting in the depot, a strong period checks the machines at every location, not only the ones it has been filling, and loads the empty ones alongside. On day 34 the AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE machines held no stock across their 48 positions while 124 units waited in the depot, and that day's sales came only from the six stocked AI-HOUSE slots.
Why: Units in storage reached 210 on day 23 and 226 on day 33, both beyond the chart's upper limit of 164.9 (swarm/charts.md). Stock in the depot earns nothing, and a stocked slot earns on its own sales wherever it is. Evidence: swarm/ground_truth/day-0034.md.
