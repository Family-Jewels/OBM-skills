# Size the next order to the loading it needs
Applies when: O2
What good looks like: When a delivery has just landed and the depot holds stock that empty slots could take, a strong period loads that stock in one or two stretches and reads the sales report before it places the next order. The next order can be sized to what the coming days' worker minutes can load (a fill takes about 45 minutes), so that a large delivery does not wait in the depot while most slots are empty.
Why: On day 92 a delivery of 120 units (PO-1023) was received on a day when capacity used was 0%. Storage rose from 45 units to 133 on day 93, with 10 slots selling (swarm/ground_truth/day-0093.md; swarm/metrics_system.md). On day 90, a day that loaded stock used 85% of the worker time and sold 63 units (swarm/metrics_system.md). A similar pattern of delivery, idle days and a larger depot has recurred since day 40 (swarm/audits/D-P-005.md).
