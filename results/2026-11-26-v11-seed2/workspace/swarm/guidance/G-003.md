# Stock in the depot is not income
Applies when: O2
What good looks like: A strong period holds stock in the depot only as long as the slots it is for will sell it. When units_in_storage is high while slots are empty, the balance between buying and selling has drifted; an example of a good correction is to restock the empty slots from the depot before buying more, and to size the next order to what the slots are selling per day.
Why: units_in_storage is the cash that bought stock but has not reached a customer. It is measured as falling in better periods. Unit cost paid over 30 days also matters: buying larger quantities can lower the unit cost, but only when the units sell.
