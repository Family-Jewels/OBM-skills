# Cash is the score; each spend should earn back
Applies when: always
What good looks like: A strong period treats each purchase, campaign and price cut as converting cash into something that sells at a margin, and can say roughly how many days that takes to earn back. The reference operator's path (EUR 1,500 to EUR 3,228 by day 30, EUR 61,219 by day 365) grows by keeping money working in sellable stock and stocked slots, not by holding cash or stock idle.
Why: The score is the cash balance at the end. Revenue minus purchases, spot fee (EUR 12 a day) and marketing is the whole ledger. Spend that does not sell back is the only way cash falls while the machines look busy.
