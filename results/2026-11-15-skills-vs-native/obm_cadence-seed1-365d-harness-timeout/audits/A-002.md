- **Core progress:** the goal's main number advanced across M2 (days 31–60). It is now declining. Supporting work (records, logs) did not gate the sales drop; the sales drop is supply.
- **Survival:** not threatened. No bankruptcy, no unpaid-day signal in the operator's log, cash €2,313 above the floor. Not a pause condition.

## 6. Over-control and drift

- **Purchase ceiling (over-control, the main one):** the €200-per-call rule is a fallback for a checker that has never been wired (no packets, no reviews). It limits the size of each call; it does not limit the number of calls. Meanwhile cash sits at €2,563 against a €250 floor, and the machines are empty. Whether the ceiling is binding depends on a reading the spec does not settle: whether repeated tranches in one day are allowed. Designer decision (D-4).
- **Record gates (over-control, moderate):** nine brief versions and eight rule-change records in 60 days. Each added a logging or gating step, and six backfill blocks were written at pulses. The brief is at its word budget. I found no evidence that a gate blocked a restock: on days 54–58 the log says storage was empty and nothing was in stock to restock. The revert conditions in RC-006 to RC-008 (sales lost to a blocked restock) cannot be checked without sales-by-machine and stockouts.
- **Refund policy v2 (under-control, small money):** about €129 paid across six complaints, about 3% of revenue. Reputation rose from 0.84 to 1.00, but no measure links reputation to sales, so the benefit is unmeasured. Some payments conflict with the spec's refund right ("a CMP- reference with a matching complaint and full amount"): CMP-002 and CMP-004 were refused for having no matching sale and later paid in full under v2; CMP-007 paid €23.60 for a €1.80 item. The spec's wording is ambiguous, so this needs a decision (D-5), not a finding of breach. The orchestrator asked for this cost review at day 60; this is it.
- **Drift:** the swarm is still producing cash. Records and rule changes have been taking more of the orchestrator's attention than supply. Of P-008's three pivots, two are records and one is supply.
- **Gold-plating:** none found in purchasing or pricing beyond the above.
