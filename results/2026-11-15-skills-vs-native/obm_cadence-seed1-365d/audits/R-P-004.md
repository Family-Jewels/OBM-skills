# Redesign R-P-004 (day 26 of 365)

**Trigger:** Diagnosis D-P-004 (day 22): slots go empty within about two days of each load; restock is reactive and trips are not ranked by value.

**Reading taken:** The direction is right. slots_selling is the lever that moves revenue, and the spec's driver tree and push order (slots_selling first, via restock) are correct. The tree itself is right. The fault is in the environment: the operator brief lacks the information the spec already requires (lead times, supplier minimums, the trip-value rule), its only restock rule fires after a stock-out, and it lists a file that does not exist (`swarm/index.md`) while not listing the pulse pivots. So the change is to the brief, not to the spec, tree, measures, charts or goal. No input was pinned, no limit was set, and no criterion was lowered.

**Change:** `swarm/rules/operator.md` v1 -> v2 (old copy: `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`).
- Inputs: removed `swarm/index.md`; added the latest pulse, any diagnosis, and `check_offers`.
- Added the facts the design depends on: 2–4 day lead time, supplier minimums (read from `check_offers`), drinks selling out in about two days, 10–18 unit trips. Lead times and the minimum are taken from D-P-004 and must be re-checked against the sales report and offers.
- Added a cover-days rule: order when cover days fall below lead time plus 1, so stock lands before the stock-out, not after.
- Added the trip-value rule from the spec, ranked by expected sales per trip.
- Added a two-day response rule for pulse pivots.
- Added timing of orders ahead of the expected trough around days 29–30 (a hypothesis to confirm).
- Unchanged: solvency rules, the 15% reserve, the decision rights, the chart rules.

**Why:** The operator cannot rank trips or time orders without lead times, minimums and cover days, and the pivots never reached it. Each is a missing input, which is a design fault.

**Prediction:** `slots_selling` shifts **up**: back to 16 or more by day 28 (it was 16 on day 20, 6 on day 22), and it stays at or above 16 through day 30. The next trough, if the eight-day cycle holds, should be shallower: `units` at or above the centre (54) on day 30, not 11. Measured on the charts against the old limits (`charts.md` centre 11.37 for slots_selling; 54.0 for units).

**Cost:** The brief is longer; no change to spend, budget or review share. Possible cost: extra orders to fill cover, which the reserve rule still limits.

**Revert if:** `slots_selling` stays below its centre (11.37) on days 28 and 30 while cover days are at or above lead time plus 1 (so the cause is not stock), or cash falls below EUR 150 on any day because of extra orders. Revert means restoring `swarm/history/operator.v1.md` to `swarm/rules/operator.md` and writing the revert here.
