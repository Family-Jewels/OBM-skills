## Pinpointed problem

The operator can refill only one slot per restock call, and each call uses one 45-minute trip. An 8-hour day allows about 8 to 9 trips, so about 8 slot refills a day for 72 slots. Slots selling stays in the 8–19 range; drinks sell out within 1–2 days in hot weather, and snacks run out for days at a time (`log/progress.md`, days 115–119: "the 8-trip day is binding").

## What maintains it

- The operator's choices are sound under the ceiling: highest-value slots first, storage checked before each trip, trips skipped when the depot holds little stock. Nothing in the records shows a wrong incentive, a false report or a skipped task.
- What keeps the shortfall in place is the trip ceiling. Each redesign that targeted it (R-P-020, R-A-003) assumed several slots per visit. Neither could have worked on one-slot calls, and neither prediction was met.

## Cause

- **Information (primary):** the question "does one restock call fill several slots?" was left open in the spec until day 200, and both redesigns were built on the answer being yes. The operator had named the trip clock as the constraint by day 87 and again on day 115, but no spec measure or redesign made the one-slot-per-trip ceiling explicit.
- **Resources (primary):** the trip clock is the binding resource. The aim of 60% of slots selling (M3, 43 of 72) needs about 2 to 3 times the refill capacity the day allows, under the current one-slot-per-call tool.
- **Incentives:** no cause found.

## Evidence strength and what would settle it

- The one-slot-per-call finding rests on the operator's own records (`log/work.jsonl` shows one slot per restock action from day 67). It is not yet checked against the system's restock returns. The system's per-call log (owed since day 200) would settle it.
- The claim that the trip clock limits slot refills rests on the operator's notes and the metrics. The split of cash_gain_7 (owed by the system) would show whether days 178–187 came from stock-outs or from a fall in revenue per slot.
- Not verified: the tool's documented parameters for multi-slot calls. The designer should read these from the tool's documentation (not simulator internals).

- Chart the system's per-call and split measures as owed, before judging R-P-029.
