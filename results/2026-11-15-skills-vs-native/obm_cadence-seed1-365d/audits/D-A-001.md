## What maintains it

- The swarm is pushed on slots first (spec: "cheapest lever"), and `slots_selling` counts a slot as stocked. Restocking shows up in the measure at once. Yield (units per slot, price, mix) pays off later and has not been tested. Price and mix have not been changed (per A-001).
- Late pivots and missed predictions have no consequence. The two-day pivot rule was not kept with no written reason. R-P-004's revert rule (slots below 11.37 on days 28 and 30) could not fire because it only tested slots, not units or yield. The failed prediction stood unchallenged.
- Ledger cash rose while sales fell, so the cash signal hid the problem (A-001).

## Cause

- **Information (primary):** The brief gives no stock or demand cycle, so the operator is not told that deliveries arrive after the weekly trough. The spec's price and mix questions have no due date. The charted `cash_gain_7` is a ledger change, not the spec's revenue − COGS − fee − marketing − write-offs. The chart's "no shift yet" column conflicts with the means above (A-001 raised this).
- **Incentives (primary):** Counting stocked slots rewards restocking on arrival over timing the buy to the trough. The revert rule and pivot rule carry no consequence.
- **Resources:** No `runs.jsonl`, so spend cannot be measured. COGS per unit and write-offs are in the tree but not charted.
- **Knowledge / model tier:** Not indicated by the records. Not the first cause.

## Design change needed (not written here)

- Move the measure the operator is pushed on from `slots_selling` to `revenue_per_slot` and `units`, with `slots_selling` as a check.
- Give the operator the weekly cycle and a rule to time each buy to the trough, using lead time.
- Set a dated price and mix test per location.
- Make revert rules cover units and yield, and make missed predictions trigger a review.
- Split `cash_gain_7` into revenue, COGS, fee and write-offs, and add `runs.jsonl`.
