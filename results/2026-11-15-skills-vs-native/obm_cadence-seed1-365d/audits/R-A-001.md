# Redesign R-A-001 (day 69 of 365)

Trigger: Diagnosis D-A-001 (day 66) and full audit A-001 (day 50). Charts read: `swarm/charts.md` (day 69). Spec read: v1.

Change: `swarm/spec.md` v1 -> v2 (old copy: `swarm/history/spec.v1.md`); `swarm/rules/operator.md` v2 -> v3 (old copy: `swarm/history/operator.v2.md`).

## Direction first

- The goal (final cash) and the top of the tree are right. No aim or limit was lowered. The exemplar is unchanged.
- The tree adds up: revenue = slots × revenue per slot, and cash follows revenue less costs.
- The push order is wrong. The spec pushes `slots_selling` first. That is the measure the operator can raise at once by restocking, so it moved (11.7 to 13.0 since R-P-004). Units and revenue per slot, which it feeds, fell 20–26%. The lever that moves revenue is units per week, and the trough is what caps it.

## Cause

- **Information (primary):** the brief does not tell the operator that demand troughs every seven days (days 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, 50, 56, 63), so its restock rule fires after the stock-out instead of before the trough. R-P-004's timing hypothesis was never made a rule the operator could act on in time.
- **Incentive (primary):** the first rule in the brief and the first push in the spec both reward a filled slot. A slot that is filled but sells nothing counts the same as one that sells.
- **Environment, not agent:** the spec's 15% reserve is missing from the brief (since v1), and the revert rule of R-P-004 tested slots only, so it could not fire on units or yield.

## Change

1. **Push order (spec):** `Units` is now the first push, timed to the trough. `slots_selling` is a check, not a push. `revenue_per_slot` is pushed after the timing change is judged.
2. **Brief (v3):** the trough cycle is stated as a fact; a units-first rule times each order to land before the next trough; restock fires only for a slot whose product sells; the 15% reserve is written in; a missed prediction must be written the same day.
3. **Price and mix:** deferred to a dated decision on day 90, so the timing change is not confounded. Not changed now.
4. **Cadence (spec):** a missed redesign prediction triggers an immediate pulse; a weekly trough watch is added.
5. **Instrumentation (spec, asked of the system and orchestrator, not changed by the designer):** split `cash_gain_7` into revenue, COGS, fee and write-offs from the ledger; create `log/runs.jsonl`; chart COGS and write-offs.
