## Pinpointed problem

## What maintains it

- Each restock call fills one slot (log, days 221, 233, 257, 276). Six to ten fills a day cannot keep 72 slots selling when drinks sell out in about two days.
- Trips are ranked by units per trip (R-A-004), so fills go to the highest-value slots and the rest stay empty.
- Late noordzee deliveries (PO-1092, 11 days) left drinks at zero depot stock on days 244–255 and 270–276.
- Redesigns changed lead times and push measures, not the fill rate. The R-P-037 prediction failed on its date.
