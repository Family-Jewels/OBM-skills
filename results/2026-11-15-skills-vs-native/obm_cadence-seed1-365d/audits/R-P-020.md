Restock trips are the cap on slots selling (D-P-020 §3), and no tree row or chart carries them. So the tree points at the right measure but not at the thing limiting it.

The cash line also hides the cost of empty slots, and cash_gain_7 now signals downward with no ledger split to explain it. That split has been requested eight times (days 66–110) and not produced. It is escalated to the orchestrator, not solved here.

## Cause and the smallest change

The operator's drinks rule loads each drinks slot to "capacity, or two days' demand if that is less" (operator v4). Drinks sell out in about two days, so that rule forces a return to the same slot about every two days. Restock trips are the scarce resource, so the rule spends them on small loads. The brief names the cause in its own wording.

One change, two artifacts:
1. **Tree (spec v3 -> v4):** two rows added under slots_selling, "Slots restocked per trip" and "Restock trips per day", both pushed on now (yes, now). Units stays a push, but the binding constraint is now named. Instrumentation gap added: the system must log trips per visit and chart them. The operator's progress lines are a stopgap only, not ground truth.
2. **Brief (operator v4 -> v5):** the drinks rule loads each drinks slot to its capacity, not to two days' demand. The operator logs trips and slots per trip each day.

Old versions: `swarm/history/spec.v3.md` and `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`. Current: `swarm/spec.md` (v4) and `swarm/rules/operator.md` (v5), at their usual paths.
