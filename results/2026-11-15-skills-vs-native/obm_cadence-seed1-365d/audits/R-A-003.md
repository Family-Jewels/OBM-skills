# R-A-003: redesign at day 170

Trigger: D-A-003 (day 166, "Diagnosis needed: no", complete). Cause: resources and information. Slots selling fell from a prior centre of 13.45 to about 11 since day 140; revenue per slot and units show no shift, so the drop is in slots, and trips (one slot filled per visit, 8–9 visits a day) are the cap.

Records read: swarm/audits/D-A-003.md, swarm/audits/A-003.md, swarm/charts.md (day 166 charts, read at day 170 per the designer's record), swarm/spec.md (v4), swarm/rules/operator.md (v5), swarm/audits/R-P-020.md.

## Is the direction right?

For the top measure, mostly. Final cash is the human's goal and is unchanged. The tree points at the right chain (cash → revenue → slots → trips), and the trip driver was added at R-P-020. But R-P-020 pushed on the wrong lever: it changed how full each drinks load was, not how many slots a visit covers. The D-A-003 trip logs show one slot per trip, and fuller loads did not change that count. So the tree's trip driver is right, the brief's rule for using it is wrong.

Is the tree itself right? Yes, with one gap: slots per visit and trips per day are in the tree but not charted, so no signal can confirm them. That is a system task (instrumentation), not a design change this record can make.

## Cause and the smallest change

The brief never says how many slots one visit may fill. The operator reads it as one slot per trip. One change, two artifacts:

1. **Brief (operator v5 -> v6):** one restock visit covers every empty or low selling slot on the machine it reaches, loaded to capacity. A trip is worth making when it can fill at least one empty selling slot; a skipped trip is logged with its reason and the empty slots it leaves. Slots filled per visit is logged each day until the system charts it.
2. **Spec (v4 -> v5):** the trip driver row now names the visit rule as its push. A prediction cadence row is added.

Old versions: `swarm/history/operator.v5.md` and `swarm/history/spec.v4.md`. Current: `swarm/rules/operator.md` (v6) and `swarm/spec.md` (v5), at their usual paths.

Assumption, stated so it can be tested: a visit can carry more than one slot. The brief says the visit is about 45 minutes and nothing says it is limited to one slot. If the operator finds otherwise, it writes so in `log/progress.md` the same day (the brief now requires this), and the redesign is reverted.

Not changed on purpose (one change at a time):
- The landing-lag cover trigger (D-A-003 names it). Brief already uses upper lead times and the receipt's actual day. Next redesign, not before day 190.
- Price and mix test: still held per the day-90 decision and day-105 start; not part of this change.
- PIP basis alignment and the day-label offset (A-002): next redesign.
- Ledger split of cash_gain_7 and the trip chart: system and orchestrator items (A-003 section 9).
- M3's aim (60% of capacity by day 120) was missed and stands as written. No aim is lowered. M3 needs its own re-plan at the day-180 audit, not this redesign.

Stops the core work? No. Buying by cover, restocking, and solvency checks continue. Fuller visits use more cash per visit, inside the 15% reserve rule.

## Prediction

Measure: `slots_selling`. Shift: up. By: day 190. Test: eight consecutive daily points above its prior centre of 13.45 by day 190 (charts).
Why this measure: if a visit fills every empty selling slot, the empty slots that drop slots_selling fill on the same visits; the trip count does not change.

Secondary (read from `log/progress.md` until the system charts visits): slots filled per visit averages 2 or more on the days with trips, by day 180.

## Cost

More stock in depot and slots at once; more cash tied up within the 15% reserve; possible write-offs if a slot's product does not sell (write-offs are in the tree as "down"). Operator minutes per visit are unchanged; skipped trips should fall.

## Revert if

- Slots filled per visit stays at 1 by day 180 (then the assumption is false; diagnose the visit capacity, do not push harder).
- `slots_selling` is not above its prior centre by day 190 (prediction unmet; immediate pulse).
- Write-offs on the ledger are above their day-155 level for two weeks in a row.
- Cash falls below the 15% reserve or the EUR 150 floor because of a restock purchase (parked per decision rights, then reverted).
