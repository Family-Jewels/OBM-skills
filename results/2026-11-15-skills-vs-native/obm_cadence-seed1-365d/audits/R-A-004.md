Spacing: last operating redesign R-A-003 (day 170) and R-P-029 (day 195); the next operating redesign is allowed from day 215. Met.

## Is the direction right?

Partly. The tree's chain is right: final cash → revenue → slots_selling → trips per day × slots per trip. The drivers pushed since R-P-020 (trips, slots per trip) are the ones that move slots_selling. The direction is wrong in one place: R-A-003 and the v6 brief (rule 19) required a visit to cover every empty slot, assuming a multi-slot restock call. Nobody tested that. The operator's logs (days 67–89) show one slot per trip every time. Both redesigns that targeted the trip ceiling therefore could not work as written, and the slots_selling charts show no shift from them (since day 200 centre 9.7, shift down).

## Is the design causing it?

Yes, in one place: the tree has no measure for what one trip is worth, so the ceiling is never stated where the operator reads the spec. The ceiling itself is the environment (45-minute trips, 8–9 per day), not a design fault. The aim (M3, 43 of 72 slots) was set without that ceiling in view.

## Change (smallest that addresses the cause)

1. **Tree (spec v6 → v7):** add "Units per restock trip" as a driver to push on now, ground truth system `units` and restock events. This makes the ceiling a measure, not a hidden assumption.
2. **Open question settled by a test:** the operator makes one multi-slot restock call on the first trip after day 218 that reaches a machine with two or more empty selling slots, and writes the signature and return the same day. Due day 225. The answer decides the plan, not the brief.
3. **Brief (v6 → v7), rule 19 and the trip-ranking rule:** replace "fill all on every visit" with the test and, if one slot per call, rank trips by units per trip.
4. **Not changed:** the M3 aim and its date, the goal, the exemplar, solvency, the reserve. The swarm cannot lower an aim. D-A-004 estimates the 60% aim needs 2–3× the current refill capacity under one slot per call; that decision is parked for the human.

## Prediction (testable on the charts)

- **Units per restock trip** (system chart from day 219): its mean over days 219–240 is above its mean over days 200–218 by **day 240**.
- **slots_selling** (chart centre 9.7 since day 200): not above 9.7 by day 260 means the trip ceiling, not trip ranking, is the binding constraint. That is the signal to take the parked aim question to the human.

If the operator reports a multi-slot call works by day 225, the prediction about slots_selling becomes the main test and the aim question may be re-timed rather than parked.

- Cost: one system chart (units per restock trip, the owed per-visit log), a same-day note per test, and a few lines in `log/progress.md`. No change to operating loads, prices or the cover rule.

Revert the brief to v6 only if the test shows rule 19 caused a loss (for example, a skipped trip with empty selling slots left unfilled).
