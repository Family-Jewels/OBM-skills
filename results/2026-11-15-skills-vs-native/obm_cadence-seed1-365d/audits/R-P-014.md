# Redesign R-P-014 (day 90 of 365)

Trigger: D-P-014 (day 87): stock-outs on days 79–86 drove slots_selling and units to their lows, and the cause is a wrong lead-time fact in the operator brief.

Change: operator brief v3 -> v4 (`swarm/rules/operator.md`; v3 kept at `swarm/history/operator.v3.md`). Spec stays at Version 2: its tree already pushes on units first and checks with slots_selling, so no driver or tree change is needed.

## What changed (smallest set that addresses the cause)

1. **Fact corrected:** lead times read from the order receipts, not the 2–4 day figure. Noordzee 2–5, zuidas 2–11 (usually 3–4; 10 and 11 on PO-1021 and PO-1031), bakker-en-zoon 6 and 15. Planning uses the upper figure.
   - Note: the ledger differs from D-P-014 on zuidas. I counted 2–11, not 4–11. Noordzee 2–5 (D-P-014 said 2–6). The planning figure of 11 is unchanged, so the decision does not depend on the difference.
2. **Buy by cover, not by weekday or offer validity:** a daily cover-days line per product in `log/progress.md`, with an order-by date for any product under 20 days. Order when cover is under the supplier's upper lead time plus 1 day.
3. **Drinks load from slot capacity** (`get_machine_inventory`), or two days' demand if lower. If capacity cannot be read, that is written down. This resolves the open evidence on whether 10-unit loads were a choice or a limit.
4. **Trough dates** moved to days 84, 91, 98. Trough weekday left unchanged (open evidence: day 83 labelled Sat and "Sunday trough").
5. **Prices held until day 105** (was: day-90 decision). The price and mix test is deferred so the buy-timing change is judged alone. This is a choice, and the human can reverse it.

## Not changed, and why

- Goal, exemplar, done criteria, reserve (15%), solvency floor, tree: unchanged. The goal and aims are not touched.
- Stock was not short overall (about 300 units in the depot on day 85). The fault is timing, so no purchase limit or price change was made.
- The incentives and knowledge explanations in D-P-014 were not supported, so no change was made for them.
- Orchestrator items (ledger split of `cash_gain_7`, missing `log/runs.jsonl`) are not design changes. They stay open in the orchestrator's queue, and they block the budget check, not this fix.
