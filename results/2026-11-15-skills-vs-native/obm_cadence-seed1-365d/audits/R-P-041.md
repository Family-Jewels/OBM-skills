## Is the direction right?

Partly. Final cash is still the right top measure, and the goal is unchanged. The push on units per restock trip is not enough on its own. Its ranking sends each trip to the highest-value slot, and the diagnosis found drinks slots left empty while the depot held their stock. The push list had no measure for that gap. The tree needs one new driver, not new branches.

## Is the design causing it?

Partly. The cause is the one-slot-per-call restock clock (fixed by the tool), plus the operator's rules, which do not send trips to empty stocked slots first. The brief's one-call test (day 225) is stale, and it still tells the operator to plan by units per trip when a call fills one slot. Stock timing and late deliveries (PO-1092) are the other cause; the cover rule already covers those, so I did not change it.

Not changed: the goal, the M3 aim (still parked for the human), the exemplar, the cover rule, the 15% reserve, prices, the depot rules.
