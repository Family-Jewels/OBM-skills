# D-P-036: Diagnosis at day 230

Method: I recomputed the cash identity from `swarm/metrics_system.md` (cash, revenue) and `swarm/ledger.md` (orders, complaints). I had no sim tools, so nothing here is checked against `get_status`, `get_machine_inventory`, `get_storage_inventory` or `list_emails`.

**What is going wrong**

1. **Cash leaves the account with no record.** Expected cash from day 94 (€4,318 + revenue €9,494 − fees €1,632) is €12,180. Actual is €7,464 (day 230). Gap: €4,715. P-036's figures check out.
2. **The gap starts on day 95, the first day with no ledger line.** Over days 70–94 the ledger reconciles to within about €27. So the leak is new from day 95, not a standing error.
3. **The gap is steady, about €35/day, and is about half of revenue.** Gap as a share of cumulative revenue is 48–52% at every checkpoint (day 124: 1,390; day 164: 2,598; day 204: 3,815; day 230: 4,715). Gap at day 214 was 4,272 and at day 230 it is 4,715 (about €28/day over that window).
4. **The control loop has stopped.** No ledger line since day 94, `slots_stocked` never recorded, all five requests overdue since day 215 with none closed.
5. **Sales shift from day 98** against the frozen 11.7 centre (days 91–98 all below it). Revenue per slot has stayed near 6–8, against about 11 earlier. The physical cause is not established.

**Why**

- The leak began on the day the ledger stopped. The likeliest cause is unlogged purchases. Pre-day-95 purchases ran at about €44/day, close to the leak rate. A revenue-linked deduction (such as a location share) also fits the 50% ratio. Zero-sales days 203–204 showed almost no drain, which fits both.
- The drain continued through days 206–230, after the plan froze all buying. Either the freeze was not obeyed or the cause is not purchasing. This is the most useful thing to check first.
- Every gate (purchase gate, cash watch, stall rule) reads the operator's own ledger. When the ledger lapsed, nothing froze purchases. No system-written measure tracks the cash identity residual.
- Loading is about 6–7 slots a day against 72 slots. Stock cannot be refilled fast enough, which may explain the plateau. Unverified until slot counts are recorded.

**Not established:** whether purchases went off-loop; the non-purchase cause if they did not; the physical cause of weak sales. Settling these needs `get_status` fee lines, order history, and machine and storage inventory.

The plan is not changed.
