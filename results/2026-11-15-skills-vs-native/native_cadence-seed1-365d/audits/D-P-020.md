# D-P-020: Diagnosis at day 121

Sources: `swarm/audits/P-020.md`, `swarm/charts.md`, `swarm/plan.md` (v6), `swarm/ledger.md`, `swarm/metrics_system.md`, `swarm/audits/D-P-016.md`, `swarm/audits/P-019.md`. No live sim tools were available in this session, so nothing below is checked against `get_status`, `get_machine_inventory`, `get_storage_inventory` or `list_emails`. Arithmetic is from the files; causes are marked where unverified.

## What is going wrong

1. **The operating record has lapsed, and the stall rule was not applied.** The ledger ends at day 94. Days 95–121 (27 days) have no ledger line. Plan v6 says a missed day triggers the stall rule, which stops purchases. Cash still moved without sales to explain it. Sales days 95–117 were €2,282; less €12/day fees (€276) cash should have risen about €2,006. It rose €870. The gap is about **€1,136** (±~€100: the fee assumption, and a €93 residual already present in the d70–94 window).
2. **Five large unexplained cash drops**, each on a day with little revenue: d99 −€175 (sales €20), d104 −€123, d107 −€190, d111 −€100, d117 −€37. Their size and spacing match purchase orders. Unverified: order history. **Lead:** the d99 drop, after the €12 fee, is ~€163, the same size as PO-1031 (€162.60). The ledger records PO-1031 as paid on d93. Either its payment date is wrong or a second order of the same size was placed. Either way the ledger is incomplete.
3. **Selling slots are capped by restocking throughput, not demand or cash.** Selling slots have stayed around 10–15 since day 52, out of 72. Sales run about 5 units per selling slot per day. Visits run about 6.5 per day (45 min per restock, 8-hour day; not measured in the sim). Steady state gives selling slots ≈ 1.3 × units per load. Around 13 selling slots implies a load of about 10 units. This is consistent with the plateau. It is the same mechanism D-P-016 proposed, now supported by the numbers but still unverified because `slots_stocked` and slot capacity were never recorded.
4. **Cash is not the constraint.** €5,285 on day 121, against a reserve of about €150.

## What is not a shift

- `slots_selling` was below 11.7 for nine days (d91–99). That signal fired on d98 and ended on d100 (12). Since then the longest run below 11.7 is three (d118–120). The shift rule is not met.
- `revenue_last_7` was €607 on d117 against €690 a month earlier (−12%). It is one week, not two in a row. The stall rule's revenue trigger is not met.
- Weekend and early-week troughs recur (d96–99, d104–106, d111–114, d118–120). Several of these may be weekly pattern rather than stock.
- The day-120 target (`slots_selling` ≥ 11.7) was missed on its one day (9). That is a single reading inside the limits. `slots_stocked` has no baseline, so its target cannot be tested.

## Chart and metric errors

- **PIP is miscalculated.** The chart gives 4.6x at day 121 (exemplar 18,980.78 / actual 5,285.22). The division gives **3.6x**. The plan's day-104 figure of 4.6x is also wrong: 16,037.95 / 4,686.33 = **3.4x**.
- **The `slots_selling` "before" centre differs across files:** 11.7 (plan), 8.45 (D-P-016), 11.93 (charts). The plan's shift rule uses 11.7. The chart's 11.93 is fitted on the decline, so it should not be used for the rule.
