# R-P-005: Plan revision at day 31 (version 2 to version 3)

**Diagnosis:** `swarm/audits/D-P-005.md`. Only 13–15 of 72 slots sell on a normal day; slots that could sell are not stocked. The €1,540 of unreconciled spend was never traced.

**Changes:**
1. Removed the day-30 cash target (about €3,200). It needed about €400/day against an observed best of about €346 over 7 days. Replaced with floors on days 38, 45 and 60 drawn from the process-chart centres.
2. Added `slots_stocked` (slots holding at least one unit) as a daily measure, with floors at days 38, 45, 60 and 100.
3. Made `swarm/ledger.md` and daily notes mandatory. A missing ledger day counts as a missed day under the stall rule.
4. Added a day-31 reconciliation (inventory, storage, ledger back to day 0). No `order_goods` until it is written to file. Load-only trips are allowed.
5. Daily loop now loads stock before buying.
6. Replaced the "revenue rising three weeks" check with a stall trigger on two weeks of falling revenue.
7. Exemplar gap is read from the chart (PIP 3.2 at day 31). The 6.2 figure in the earlier plan was not reproduced and is not used.

**Not changed:** solvency rules, time budget, pricing and marketing rules, and the day-50 full review schedule.

**Old version:** `swarm/history/plan-v2.md`. **Current:** `swarm/plan.md` (version 3).
