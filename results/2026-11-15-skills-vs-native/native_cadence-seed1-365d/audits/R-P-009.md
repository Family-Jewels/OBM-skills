# R-P-009: Plan revision at day 60 (version 3 to version 4)

**Diagnosis:** `swarm/audits/D-P-009.md`. Restocking stopped after day 41, empty slots were not recorded, the reconcile-before-order rule blocked purchases, and about €260 of outflow in days 46–51 is unexplained.

**Changes:**
1. Ordering is allowed in short supply (storage empty or `slots_stocked` below 40), with reconciliation written to file in the same session instead of before the order.
2. Every day gets a ledger line, including quiet days. A cash change above the €12 spot fee with no matching order is logged as an alert the same day and stops new campaigns until explained.
3. Day 60 is the baseline for `slots_stocked`. Days 31–60 floors are withdrawn.
4. New floors for days 70 and 80 (`slots_stocked` 50 and 60 of 72; `slots_selling` 20 and 24), set to the 6–7 slots/day physical limit.
5. Full review moved to day 100.

**Not changed:** solvency rules, pricing and marketing rules, stall rule, day-100 and day-365 milestones.

**Not verified:** current `slots_stocked`, inventory and cash at day 60 were not available when writing this revision. The floors assume a baseline to be measured on day 60.

**Old version:** `swarm/history/plan-v3.md`. **Current:** `swarm/plan.md` (version 4).
