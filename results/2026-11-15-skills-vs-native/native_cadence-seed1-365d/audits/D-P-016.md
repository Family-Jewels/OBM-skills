# D-P-016: Diagnosis at day 99

Sources: `swarm/audits/P-016.md`, `swarm/charts.md`, `swarm/plan.md` (v5), `swarm/ledger.md`, `swarm/metrics_system.md`, `swarm/audits/D-P-012.md`, `swarm/audits/R-P-012.md`, `swarm/audits/P-015.md`. No live sim tools were available in this session, so `get_status`, `get_machine_inventory`, `get_storage_inventory` and `list_emails` were not checked. Causes below are inferred from the files and marked where unverified.

## What is going wrong

1. **Stocked slots are falling, and sales follow.** `slots_selling` averaged 14 on days 89–90 and 6 on days 97–99. `revenue_last_7` fell from €765 (day 94) to €514 (day 99), about 33%. Cash is not the problem: €4,406 on day 99.
2. **A shift signal has fired, and the charts do not show it.** The plan's own rule uses a pre-redesign centre of 11.7 for `slots_selling`. Days 91–98 are all below 11.7 (6, 4, 10, 11, 9, 5, 5, 6): eight in a row on day 98, nine on day 99. The chart reports "routine variation" because its limits are fitted to the decline. Its "before" centre is 8.45, not 11.7. The plan and the chart disagree on every pre-redesign centre (for example revenue 105.53 vs 83.72). The chart's "before" centre is fitted on the decline and hides it.
3. **The records still cannot show why.** `slots_stocked` has not been recorded since the start. The day-89 baseline required by plan v5 is not in the ledger. No trip log or stock trace exists for PO-1025 to PO-1031 beyond delivery days.

## Why (most likely, not verified)

- **Restocking is the binding constraint.** The plan limits loading to 6–7 slots a day. At day 94 about 70 units a day sold through about 11 slots, roughly 6 units per selling slot per day. A loaded slot may therefore empty in a few days (slot capacity is not in the files). If so, 6–7 loads a day cannot hold more than a low-teens count of slots. That matches the plateau since day 60. Verify with `get_machine_inventory` (capacity per slot and current units).
- **Cash is idle.** About €4,300 is unused while stock orders continued at about €45 a day. Spend is not reaching stocked slots. Goods may be sitting in storage (unverified: `get_storage_inventory`).
- **The plan's checkpoints cannot be met as written.** `slots_stocked` ≥ 60 by day 100 needs about 8 days at 7 loads a day from a baseline that was never recorded. The day-100 checkpoint is effectively already missed.

## Corrections to P-016

- "The ledger ends at day 69": false. `swarm/ledger.md` has a days 70–94 section.
- "About €1,140 of spending has no ledger entry": mostly explained. Orders PO-1025 to PO-1031 (d74–d93) total €1,091.80, and CMP-011 is €23.89. That leaves about €26 unexplained, which may be fee timing. Confirm against `get_status`.
- "Revenue recovering": revenue_last_7 peaked on day 94 and has since fallen.
- "Slots selling 11 is routine variation": the shift rule fired on day 98.
- "Floors of 20 at day 70 and 24 at day 80": withdrawn in plan v5 (day 89) and based on a superseded limit.
- "Revenue per selling slot above centre": this ratio rises when few slots sell, so it reflects the shortage and is not a sign of health.
