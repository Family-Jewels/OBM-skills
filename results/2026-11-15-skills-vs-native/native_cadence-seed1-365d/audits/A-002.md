## Findings

**1. Stock is still not reaching the selling slots, and the signal has not been acted on.**
- `slots_selling` was below the pre-redesign centre of 11.7 on eight days in a row, days 91 to 98 (6, 4, 10, 11, 9, 5, 5, 6), and nine days to day 99. Under the plan's shift rule (plan.md, "Shift centre") this is a signal. The rule says run the stock trace first. Nothing in the swarm records shows that trace.
- The chart shows `slots_selling` as "routine variation" because its limits were fitted after the day-98 redesign (centre 8.45). The plan says to judge against the pre-redesign centre, so the chart's status should not be read as clearance.

- The same pattern has been flagged at day 45, day 76, day 89 and day 99 (A-001, D-P-012, R-P-012, P-017). Each time the stock trace was asked for and none is on file.

**2. Deliveries are not visibly reaching the slots.**

- PO-1031 (Zuidas, €162.60, paid day 93, due day 96) has no delivery recorded. `slots_selling` stayed at 5 to 6 from day 96 to day 98. Whether PO-1031 arrived and whether it was loaded is unknown.

**3. The records stopped at day 94.**
- The ledger has no lines for days 95 to 102: eight missed days under plan principle 8.
- The stall rule requires that these days be marked and that purchases and campaigns stop until the Immediate actions are written. The file does not show that this was done. The ledger shows no purchase since day 93 and no campaign since day 55, which is consistent with a stop but does not record one.
- `slots_stocked` has never been recorded in the metrics, the chart or the ledger. The plan's central stock measure is therefore missing 102 days in.

**4. Cash has an unexplained outflow of about €187 over days 95 to 102.**
- Cash went from €4,317.99 (day 94) to €4,675.43 (day 102), a rise of €357.44.
- Revenue over the same eight days was €640.20 and spot fees were €96.00, so the expected rise was about €544.
- By day 99 the gap was about €80 (P-017 found the same). Over days 100 to 102 it was about €107.
- No order, complaint or marketing line in the ledger explains it. It could be an unlogged order or refund. Until it is traced, it counts as an alert under principle 8.

**5. Revenue is at a lower plateau, not recovering.**
- Last-7-day revenue was €882.40 on day 41 and €905.80 on day 30. It was €718.80 on day 91 and €523.80 on day 102, about 41% below day 41.
- Against the same week a month earlier (€529.50) it looks flat, but that comparison week was itself depressed. Revenue has stayed between about €485 and €750 since day 46.
- The two-week test has not yet been met on a weekly comparison. The stock-trace requirement from Finding 1 applies regardless.

**6. Solvency is not the problem.**
- Cash is €4,675.43, up from €1,500 at the start, and the chart's cash_gain_7 is inside its limits. The rise is about €45 a day over the last eight days, after the unexplained outflow in Finding 4.
- The exemplar gap (PIP 4.5 in charts.md) is a reading only and is not used for any target.
