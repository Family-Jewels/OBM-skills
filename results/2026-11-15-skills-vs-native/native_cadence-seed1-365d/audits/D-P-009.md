**What is going wrong:** Cash is healthy (€2,734.80) but the stock is not in the machines. `slots_selling` has been 10–14 of 72 since day 37, and fell to 4 on days 49–50 (5 units sold on each). 7-day revenue fell from €905.8 (day 30) to €550.2 (day 51). Cash has been sitting unspent while slots run empty. Solvency is not the problem.

**Why:**
1. **Restocking has stopped.** The last order is PO-1016/PO-1017 (€263, day 41). PO-1017 (due day 43) is still undelivered, and PO-1016 is due day 52. Nothing is arriving.
2. **The operator is not seeing the problem.** No ledger lines for days 43–51, and `slots_stocked` has never been recorded. Empty slots are invisible, which is the failure the plan's measure 7 was added to fix.
3. **The process rules block the restock cycle.** The "reconcile before any order" rule has never been written to file, so the operator cannot buy, and load-only trips are not happening either.
4. **Unexplained cash outflow.** Using the metrics table (fees €12/day from day 2), cash is about €130 below the rebuilt figure at day 45 and about €390 below at day 51. Most of the day 46–51 gap (about €260) has no logged order or complaint. This is unexplained, not confirmed spend.

**Chart correction to P-009:** `charts.md` shows no signal for `cash_gain_7` (status "no shift yet"; since-mean €229 is below the old centre). The "8 in a row above centre" claim is not supported.

**Physical limit:** A restock is 45 minutes, so 6–7 slots per day. Refilling 72 slots takes about 11 full days of trips. The plan's day-38/45 slot floors are not reachable from a standing start.

**Not checked:** Inventory, storage, sales-by-product and `get_status` were not available in this session, so causes 1–2 are inferred from files and the leak in 4 is unconfirmed.

**For the revision:** Add `slots_stocked` to the metrics. Require the daily ledger line. Allow ordering when stock is in short supply, with reconciliation done alongside it. Set slot floors that the time budget can reach.
