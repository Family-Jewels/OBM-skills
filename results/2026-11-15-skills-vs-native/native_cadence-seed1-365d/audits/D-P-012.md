# D-P-012: Diagnosis at day 76

**What is going wrong:** Stock is not reaching the machines. Cash is not the problem (€3,582 on day 76). `slots_selling` averaged 8.3 over days 67–76, against 11.7 before the redesign. Revenue per selling slot is normal (€9–13 on most days; €4.81 and €5.97 on days 70–71 only because so few slots were selling). Demand per stocked slot has held. Fewer slots hold stock. Revenue last 7 days is €550 on day 76, about 29% below the same week a month earlier.

**Why:**
1. **Restocking is not measured, so it cannot be seen.** `slots_stocked` has never been recorded. The day-60 baseline and the day-70 floor (50 of 72) cannot be checked. Whether the 6–7 restocks per day actually happen is unknown.
2. **Stock arrives in batches and runs out.** Deliveries came on d51, d52, d59 (two orders) and d65. Machines ran dry around d62–64 (revenue €20 and €12). PO-1023 (due d71) is the next delivery and has no recorded trace.
3. **The record broke at day 69.** There are no ledger lines for days 70–76. Under plan principle 8 these are missed days, and the stall rule should have fired. No stock trace has been written for PO-1023.
4. **Cash does not reconcile.** Cash fell €185.49 on day 76 with €98.50 revenue, and no ledger line explains it. On day 67, cash rose €87.80 when PO-1023 (€207.90) should have left. "Already paid" for PO-1023 and PO-1024 is therefore unverified.
5. **Plan defect.** The day-70 floor "slots_selling at least 20 (chart upper limit)" is not the chart's limit. The current upper limit is 15.39 (provisional). The figure 20 comes from the pre-redesign limit of 20.52. The floor is also unreachable from about 8 without a sustained restock run.

**Corrections to P-012:**
- "Below their centres every day since day 62" is false. `slots_selling` was above 8.3 on days 62, 65, 66, 69 and 73–76.
- The chart does show a shift against the pre-redesign centre. `slots_selling` was below 11.7 on each of days 67–74 (eight in a row). The chart reports "no shift yet" because it judges against the new centre (8.3), which is fitted to the decline itself. This is a choice the review must make explicit. It is not routine variation.
- Units show no run of eight on either centre.

**Not verified:** Only files were available. `get_status`, `get_machine_inventory`, `get_storage_inventory` and `list_emails` were not checked, so causes 1–2 are inferred. `metrics_system.md` also has a day-77 row (cash €3,644.59), which this audit does not use as a basis.

**For the revision:** Record `slots_stocked` daily. Restore the daily ledger and run the stock trace on PO-1023. Explain the day-76 €185 outflow. Set the shift rule's centre explicitly. Set floors from the chart's limits and the restock budget.
