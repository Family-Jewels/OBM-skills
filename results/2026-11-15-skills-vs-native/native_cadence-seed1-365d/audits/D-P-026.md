Days 166–167 (13, 14) broke the run; latest is 9 at day 170. The chart reads "no shift" only because its centre (8.9) was refitted on the decline.

## Why

- **The control loop depends on its own ledger.** Purchase gate check (a) needs a ledger line for the previous day. The ledger lapsed at day 94, so the gate cannot pass and the plan's stop rules rely on an operator who has stopped writing. Money still leaves.
- **Requests are re-issued, never closed.** The stock trace and ledger backfill have been asked for since day 45 (A-001 to A-003, P-024, P-025, P-026). None is on file. Nothing assigns an owner or checks completion.
- **Centres ratchet down.** Each revision refits the chart's centre to the decline (11.7 → 11.93 → 8.45 → 8.9), so the level can fall without a signal.
- **Likely physical mechanism (hypothesis, unverified).** Stock is bought faster than it can be loaded. Loading is about 6–7 slot visits a day against 72 slots, and MAIN-LOUNGE empties in 1–2 days. Unexplained cash may be unlogged purchases sitting in storage. Slots then run empty and `slots_selling` falls.
