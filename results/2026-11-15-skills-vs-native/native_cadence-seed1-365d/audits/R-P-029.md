# R-P-029: Revision to plan v10 (day 206)

Response to `swarm/audits/D-P-029.md` (diagnosis at day 191) and the system's revision request at day 206.

Previous version: `swarm/history/plan-v9.md`. New version: `swarm/plan.md` (v10).

## Changes
1. **Self-enforcing freeze.** Purchases stop automatically on a missing ledger line, an overdue request, or a failed cash watch. Lifting needs four recorded checks (backfill, cash reconciliation, drops matched, `slots_stocked` recorded). No purchase under any heading until then.
2. **Interim order cap.** €150 per order, one open at a time, until loading is measured (Immediate action 4).
3. **Deadlines set by review.** Operator cannot extend a due day. Seven days past due sets the spend cap to zero until a review closes or withdraws the request (principle 15).
4. **New due day 215** for Immediate actions 0–5 and the Open requests table; day-200 checkpoint withdrawn; new checkpoint day 230.
5. **Shift start recorded at day 98**, with an order cap of 3 days of sales at the current `slots_selling` rate until loading is measured.
6. **Chart cross-check.** Where a chart centre differs from the frozen plan centre, the plan centre is used and the mismatch is logged as an alert.
7. **Cash drops extended** to d184 and d188 in the Open requests table.

## Not changed
- Core strategy, product mix and pricing (no sales-based evidence yet; the diagnosis asks for the loading and order records first).
- Frozen shift centres (11.7 etc.).
- The PIP reading rule.

Sim tools were not available in this session. The €3,481 gap, the purchase-size match, the day-98 signal start and the loading hypothesis are all from the diagnosis's hand calculations and are not checked against the sim. Steps that would settle them are in Immediate actions 0–4 of v10.
