## What is going wrong

1. **Unexplained outflow, still running.** Cash day 94 €4,317.99 + revenue days 95–191 €6,926.30 − fees 97 × €12 (€1,164) = €10,080.29 expected. Actual €6,599.69. Gap **€3,481**, about €36/day. P-029's figures check out: revenue 95–184 €6,517 and gap €3,348 at day 184 (€3,082 at day 177). Days 185–191 added another €133 net, so the gap is still growing.
2. **The gap is lumpy, not a daily leak.** Residual (change in cash − (revenue − €12)) is small or positive on quiet days but large negative on d99 (−183), d104 (−236), d107 (−316), d111 (−251), d117 (−128), d184 (−228), d188 (−132). That looks like discrete payments. Its size matches the ordering the ledger shows before day 94 (about €54/day, days 67–93). Off-ledger `order_goods` is the likely source. Not proven.
3. **Demand shift began earlier than the plan says.** `slots_selling` was below the frozen 11.7 centre for nine days running (d91–d99), so the eight-in-a-row rule fired by about d98. The plan dates it from d137. It has also been below 11.7 on d137–165, d168–186 and d189–191 (d187–188 at 12 broke one run).
4. **The chart hides it.** The centres were refitted again (11.7 → 11.93 → 8.45 → 8.9 → 7.0). `charts.md` gives a "before" centre of 5.1 for `slots_selling`, which conflicts with the 11.93 that `plan.md` cites for the same measure. Judged against 7.0, the signal reads "no shift yet". This is the ratchet D-P-026 described.
5. **PIP.** The chart's 5.8 at day 191 is correct on its gain basis: (31,098.3 − 1,500) / (6,599.69 − 1,500) = 5.80. P-029's "should be 4.7" uses a cash ratio (29,886.55 / 6,407.65 = 4.66), which the plan rules out. The exemplar figure 29,886.55 is not in any file I can read.
6. **Control record.** No ledger line since day 94 (97 days). `slots_stocked` has never been recorded. Every Open request is past its day-182 due date.

## Why

- The purchase gate (principle 10a) needs a ledger line, and the ledger stopped at day 94. The gate cannot pass, so the freeze depends on an operator who has stopped writing. Cash still leaves. Either purchases continued off-loop (principle 11) or something else is taking cash. The pattern fits the first. I cannot confirm it without order history.
- Requests are re-issued with no closing record, and nothing enforces their due days.
- Physical cause unknown. Loading is 6–7 visits a day against 72 slots, so one full refill takes about 11 days. If stock is bought faster than it is loaded, storage builds up while slots run empty. Hypothesis only; `slots_stocked` and capacity were never recorded.

- Judge the shift against the frozen 11.7 centre, with the signal start at about d98.
