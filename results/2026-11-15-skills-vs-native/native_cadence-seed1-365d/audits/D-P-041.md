# D-P-041: Diagnosis at day 287

Basis: `swarm/metrics_system.md`, `swarm/ledger.md`, `swarm/charts.md`, `swarm/plan.md` (v12), prior audits. No sim tools were available, so nothing here is checked against `get_status`, orders, or inventory. Sums were added by hand from the metrics file (revenue summed from the seven-day windows, which partition days 95–287).

**What is going wrong**

1. **Cash leaves with no record, at about €35/day.** Days 95–287: revenue €13,262, spot fees €2,316 (€12 × 193), cash change +€4,135 (€4,318 → €8,453). Unexplained outflow €6,810, about 51% of revenue. P-041's €1,125 gap (day 282) is the same leak measured over a shorter window.
2. **The drain did not stop with the freeze.** Days 227–287: revenue €3,837, fees €720, cash +€1,268. Gap €1,849, about €31/day, 48% of revenue. The rate is unchanged after the day-227 freeze.
3. **Correction to D-P-040.** From day 255 the gap is about €30/day (revenue €1,838, fees €384, cash +€482, gap €972 over 32 days), not €14/day.
4. **Sales are below the frozen centre.** `slots_selling` has been under 11.7 on 25 of the last 26 days. The plan's stall rule (8 points below 11.7) fired around day 242, but no action followed.

**Why**

- **Charts hide the shift.** Centres are refitted after each change (now 5.2 and 6.37). A chart centred on the low phase cannot flag low values as a signal, so "routine variation" is not evidence of no change.
- **Most likely cause of the outflow: unlogged purchasing at the old rate.** Ledgered orders ran at about 50% of revenue before day 94 (days 67–93: about €1,400 against about €2,600 revenue). The post-95 gap is about 50%. A revenue-linked fee starting day 95 would fit the ratio equally well. Cash alone cannot separate the two.
- **Likely physical cause (unverified):** loading is capped at 6–7 slots a day against 72 slots, so stock bought faster than it is loaded would pile up in storage and slots would run empty.
- **Why it persists:** no sim reading has been recorded since day 94. The same requests have been re-issued in about 30 audits since day 45 and never closed. The day-263 escalation passed with nothing closed.

**To settle it (operator, with sim tools):** `get_status` fee lines and order history from day 95. If orders sum to about €6,800, the cause is unlogged purchasing. If not, look for a fee line. Also `get_storage_inventory` and `get_machine_inventory` to test the storage build-up.

Plan not changed.
