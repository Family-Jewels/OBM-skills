## What changed
1. **Gates read the sim, not the ledger.** Purchase gate, cash watch and stall rule now use `get_status`, `get_machine_inventory`, `get_storage_inventory` and `get_sales_report`. A failed or unrun read freezes purchases.
2. **Freeze lift conditions are sim-based.** Four conditions (cash explained from day 95, order history listed, slots and capacity read, storage reconciled). Ledger backfill is no longer a condition.
3. **Chart centres are not used for rules.** Frozen plan centres govern the shift rule. The day-98 `slots_selling` shift is named as active.
4. **Requests have sim-verifiable closing records and new due days (day 235).** Overdue for 7 days sets spend cap to zero.
5. **No new rules without retiring one (principle 14).** v11 removes ledger-dependent rules and adds none.
6. **Sim readings before figures (principle 15).** Figures without a same-day sim reading are marked unverified.
