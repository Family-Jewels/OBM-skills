## Changes

1. **Shift centre fixed.** The shift rule uses the pre-decline centres in the plan (`slots_selling` 11.7), not the chart's "before" centres (8.45). The `slots_selling` signal that fired on day 98 is recorded as active.
2. **Baseline required.** Day 104 `slots_stocked` and `slots_selling` with a full reconciliation replace the missed day-89 baseline.
3. **Measure before buying.** Slot capacity and days-to-empty are measured from `get_machine_inventory`. The 6–7 visits/day limit is marked unverified until then.
4. **Fill visits fully.** Each visit fills the slot as far as storage allows, empty slots first.
5. **Purchases capped.** Standing orders are limited to about 3 days of loading. No order while stock from an earlier order is unloaded.
6. **Checkpoints reset.** The missed day-100 checkpoint is not restated. Day-120 targets are set from the day-104 baseline: +6 slots per loading day, capped at 72, and `slots_selling` at least 11.7.
7. **Ledger and order gaps.** Days 95–104 and the about €26 unlogged spend are checked. The day-76 cash fall stays an open alert.
