Scope: records only (`swarm/metrics_system.md`, `swarm/charts.md`, `swarm/ledger.md`, `swarm/plan.md`, prior audits P-009 and D-P-005). No live inspection of machines, storage, or orders was possible from here.

## Findings

**1. Stock is still not reaching the slots, and nothing measures it.**
- `slots_selling` is 12 on day 51 (chart centre 11.24 since day 35). Peak in the period was 19 on day 37.
- Checkpoints missed: day 38 needed 16 selling, actual 14. Day 45 needed 20, actual 14.
- Day 60 needs 24 selling. That is about double the current figure.
- `slots_stocked` is not in the metrics table, the chart, or the ledger. The plan's central stock measure has never been recorded, so empty slots cannot be seen. The day-38 and day-45 stocked-slot checks cannot be assessed.

**2. Revenue is falling, and the decline is not yet on a chart signal.**
- Last-7-day revenue: €905.8 (day 30), €725.0 (day 45), €550.2 (day 51).
- It has fallen on each of the last five days (day 46 to day 51). It is about 29% below the same week a month earlier (€780.4).
- Charts classify revenue and units as routine variation. The stall rule's two-week test has not fired, since only the last week fell. The fall is real in the records even without a chart signal.
- The `cash_gain_7` shift claimed in P-009 does not appear in the current chart, which reads "no shift yet".

**3. A stall-rule trigger is present and has not been logged.**
- Cash fell from €2,759.90 (day 47) to €2,689.80 (day 48), a drop of €70.10 on a day with €41.60 of revenue. No order or complaint is recorded for that day.
- The stall rule says a cash drop larger than the spot fee with no matching ledger order means no purchases or campaigns until resolved.

**4. Orders are overdue and the stock trace has stopped.**
- PO-1017 (Zuidas, €77.40) was due day 43 and has no delivery recorded, 8 days late. It was flagged in P-009 at day 45 and remains open.
- PO-1016 (Bakker, €185.60) is due day 52.
- The last recorded delivery is PO-1015 on day 38. From that point no deliveries are logged. The fall in revenue since day 46 is consistent with machines running down. This is a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
- Plan rules not met: no unit-level reconciliation exists for day 31 onward, and PO-1014 (d33), PO-1015 (d35), PO-1016 and PO-1017 (d41) were all placed without it. The plan says no `order_goods` until that reconciliation is written.

**5. The ledger has stopped and does not tie to the records.**
- The ledger ends at day 42. Days 43 to 51 have no lines. Under the plan, each missing day counts as a missed day. P-009 raised the same gap at day 45; it is still open.
- Purchases: the ledger says "about €2,170", but the listed orders sum to €2,270.56.
- The ledger's daily sales list is one day early against the metrics table (ledger "d6 €28.10" is day 7 in metrics).
- Cash does not fully tie to the listed orders, fees, and complaints. My rough rebuild to day 42 leaves about €27 unexplained; P-009's rebuild at day 45 leaves about €118. Both use assumptions about fees, so treat these as estimates until the reconciliation is written.

**6. Solvency is not the problem.**
- Cash is €2,734.80 (day 51), above the €150 floor and the day-45 floor of €2,400.
- Day-60 cash floor is €2,900. That needs about €165 more in nine days, which is plausible at recent rates but not assured if revenue keeps falling.
- The exemplar gap (PIP 4.3) is a reading only and is not used here.

## Actions for the operator

1. **Look at the machines and storage before anything else.** Run `get_machine_inventory` and `get_storage_inventory`. Record `slots_stocked` for each day, add ledger lines for days 43 to 51, and load any stored stock into empty slots. Check PO-1017 with `list_emails` and `get_status`, then chase the supplier. Place no new order until this is done.
2. **Write the stalled reconciliation and resolve the day-48 cash drop.** Include every order from day 31 onward, units in machines, storage, and sold, and cash by day. Explain the €70.10 drop on day 48. Log the stall and its resolution in the day's note, as the stall rule requires.
3. **Fix the measurement gap.** Add `slots_stocked` to the daily metrics and ledger, and correct the one-day offset in the sales list. Without this, the next review cannot tell whether a stock fix worked.
