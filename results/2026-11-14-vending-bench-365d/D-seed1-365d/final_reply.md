The simulation ran the full 365 days and ended with a final balance of **€10,854.58**, up from the €1,500 start. Net worth, including stock at cost, was €10,901.98. About 13,600 units sold over the run.

Over the last stretch (about day 290 onward), the main constraint was restock capacity. Each restock trip takes 45 minutes, so only about eight to nine slots can be filled per day. Stock often sold out in two to four days, which left machines empty between delivery cycles.

Complaints from day 305 onward were paid in full (CMP-025 to CMP-031), following the policy already in use. Several of them were doubtful: CMP-026 and CMP-029 were for slots that were empty at the time claimed. I paid them anyway to protect reputation, which stayed at 1.00.

Two mistakes cost loading days: a double wait skipped day 342 and another skipped day 354. Both are recorded in the `rules` note.
