# Controls (risk branch; owned by the controls owner)

Bounds the worker stays within. Controls win over aims. Each control names its risk and a review point.

- Before any order_goods call, open the T-0002 comparison table itself and confirm (a) it exists (at least two suppliers, each priced at two quantities, with validity dates) and (b) every offer you would order from is unexpired: its valid_through_day is later than today. Do not rely on the board's task status, which lags. If the table is missing or an offer has expired, do not order: on that same working day, as your first step, re-run check_offers and record the new quotes with new validity dates in the table, then order. Do not wait for the weekly cycle to do this. Meanwhile you may restock from stock on hand, within the restock ceiling in the next control. Where a finding's summary and its table disagree, the table governs: re-count from the table rows any supplier you name as cheapest before ordering. | guards: stock bought before the supplier comparison, at a price quoted on expired offers, or on a miscounted summary, locking cash the reserve needs (unit_cost_paid_30d; cash ≥ 120); purchasing stalled for days on an expired quote | review at point 12 | from REQ-001, D-001, REQ-007, REQ-017 (D-004), REQ-010 (D-005; restock clause revised for D-007 by C-006), REQ-038 (A-002)
- Price pass first, then restock, within a 216-minute restock ceiling: before the day's first restock_machine trip, if no set_price pass has run today (count only set_price calls made today; yesterday's pass does not count), run the price pass first: one set_price for each slot that holds stock, as today's machine inventory shows it (5 to 7 slots now, about 5 min each, so about 25 to 35 min). Then restock. A day's restock trips stop once their minutes reach 216 (45% of the 480-minute day). Before each trip, if today's restock minutes plus 45 would exceed 216, do any swap the aims call for, then stop restocking for the day. The ceiling resets each day. The 216 ceiling governs the day's maximum; the 35% restock share is an aim for the improvement branch to restate, not a bound. | guards: restock taking the day's time before price setting (price 5–9% vs 25%; swaps 0% vs 15%), so the price moves the aims need are not made (cash ≥ 120; the top measure) | review at point 12 | from D-007 (REQ-010); D-009 (REQ-010 and REQ-032); D-010 and D-011 (REQ-010, REQ-037, REQ-041)
