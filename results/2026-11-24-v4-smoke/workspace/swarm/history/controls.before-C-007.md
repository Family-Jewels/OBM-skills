# Controls (risk branch; owned by the controls owner)

Bounds the worker stays within. Controls win over aims. Each control names its risk and a review point.

- Before any order_goods call, open the T-0002 comparison table itself and confirm (a) it exists (at least two suppliers, each priced at two quantities, with validity dates) and (b) every offer you would order from is unexpired: its valid_through_day is later than today. Do not rely on the board's task status, which lags. If the table is missing or an offer has expired, do not order: re-run check_offers and record the new quotes with new validity dates in the table first, then order. Meanwhile you may restock from stock on hand, within the restock ceiling in the next control.
