## Result
- Units per price point are 1 to 3 per location per day. Stock-outs (slots empty for days at a time) dominate the reads, so a demand difference between 2.50 and 2.75 is not visible.
- No price was set below unit cost (energy cost 1.03-1.29; sparkling 0.41-0.51; crisps 1.57-1.96).
- What the data does show: in every location, the products sell out within 1-2 days at 2.50-3.30 when stocked. Demand is capacity-limited (stock on hand), not price-limited, at these prices.
- Revenue per slot: best realised days were days 15 and 23 (115 and 137 units network-wide, from about 9-10 slots stocked).
