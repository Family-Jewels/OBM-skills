# D-004 diagnosis: misreport (from K-002)

Invoked by the risk branch. Fault: K-002 found one of three sampled claims in T-0002's finding does not match its own table.

## Pinpoint

The finding in swarm/log/T-0002.md line 26 says zuidas-groothandel is cheapest on 7 products. The table (lines 10-24) lists zuidas as lowest at qty 60 on 8 rows (sparkling, energy, dropjes, gummy, mint-tin, tortilla, protein bar, oatmeal). The worker left out mint-tin. The bakker count (7) matches. The two order amounts and the "no excess cost" conclusion cannot be checked against ground truth.

## What maintains it

- Environment, information: the finding is a summary typed once, after the table, with no step that counts rows from the table. Nothing in the log or the harness compares a summary count to its table.
- Environment, incentives: the log is the record later steps act on (the order gate reads these tables), so a wrong summary feeds real purchasing decisions, but no measure or check reads summaries. The checker samples claims only every second point and only the last point's window, so a claim like this is rarely looked at.
- Environment, information: the log says PO-1001 and PO-1002 were placed "before this table existed", yet concludes no excess cost. The conclusion has no measure behind it.

## Cause

Not the worker's skill. The record has no check between a summary and its source, and the check that exists samples too few claims to catch this kind of error.

## What would change (for controls)

A control requiring that any count in a supplier finding be generated from its table, or a check on every claim in a finding rather than a sample.

## Facts

- Zuidas-groothandel is lowest at qty 60 on 8 rows of the T-0002 table, not 7 | source: swarm/log/T-0002.md lines 20-22, 26
- The T-0002 quotes expired on day 7; the finding's conclusion on the day-1 orders has no ground-truth support | source: swarm/log/T-0002.md lines 3, 27; swarm/metrics_system.md (day 2 is the first cash row)
