# C-005: controls change on D-005 (capacity-split-off-plan), day 36

## What I changed

- Copied the previous controls.md to swarm/history/controls.before-C-005.md first.
- swarm/controls.md line 5 (the order gate), one clause changed. The old clause "Keep restocking from stock on hand meanwhile" made restocking the default the controls leave open for the whole day. It now reads: restocking from stock on hand is allowed, but before each restock run the worker checks that the day's remaining minutes still cover one price setting pass and any swap the aims call for; if not, those go first.
- Review point moved from 5 to 6 (control_gap = 1), because the gate is still due and still earns its cost.
- Added REQ-010 (D-005) to the "from" list on that line.

Nothing else changed. The order gate's checks (comparison table, unexpired offers, cheapest supplier re-count) are untouched.

## Why

D-005 finds the capacity split off plan (restock 77-81% against a 45% plan; price setting 0-1% against 15%; swaps 0% against 15%). Its maintaining causes are mostly environmental: the brief and the aims' day order put fills first, and the charts carry no restock share. Those belong to the designer and are not controls work. The one part a control can close is the controls-side cause D-005 names: line 5 left restocking as the default for the minutes that the gate's wait left free. The new clause is a limit on what restocking may crowd out. It does not set a share, a target, or an order of work, so it does not stop the core work and does not replace the designer's aims.

Risk guarded: restock minutes crowding out price setting and swaps, the levers the aims name for the cash gain. Cash is improving (cash_gain_7 signal: 8 in a row above centre), and the records do not yet show that a lower restock share would raise cash (D-005's caveat). So this control is kept narrow: it removes starvation of the price and swap work, and makes no claim that restock is wrong. The Pursuit table will show whether price and swap shares recover; if they do not, the control is removed at point 6.

Cost to the top measure: small. The clause only applies when the day's minutes would run out, and it puts a price pass and any aim-called swap before a restock run rather than dropping restock.

The order gate's own risk is still live: unit_cost_paid_30d is the one measure moving the wrong way (x1.02 per point, level, down is better), and the gate is what keeps stock from being bought on expired or unverified quotes. The gate stays, with review at point 6.
