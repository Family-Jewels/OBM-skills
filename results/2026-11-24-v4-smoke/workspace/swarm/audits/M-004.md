# M-004: method change (designer), day 77 of 91

## What changed

1. **Purchasing moved ahead of the restock fills.** The aims' order of the day put check_offers and order_goods last, after restock, and the 216-minute restock ceiling (rule 16) ends restock trips before purchasing runs. Purchasing ran at 2% against a 20% plan. Rule added to rules/operator.md (v5): run check_offers and order_goods after the price pass and before the first restock trip when an offer is due within 2 days or a product's depot stock covers fewer than 7 days of units, sized to 7 days of units per product within the cash guardrail.
2. **Capacity split re-planned on the 480-minute basis** (aims.md v7). The 20% purchasing plan (96 minutes a day) was larger than one offer cycle needs: one check_offers and one order_goods is 50 minutes per 7-day cycle. The 12% reads plan was below the opening reads (get_status, get_storage_inventory, get_sales_report: about 55 minutes). Restock held at 35%, inside the 216-minute ceiling. Price share is now computed from stocked slots (9 × 5 minutes = 45 minutes, 9%).
3. **Price floor** (rules/operator.md v5): no price below the latest unit_cost_paid_30d (1.105 at day 77). The aims guardrail already read the latest cost.
4. **Not changed:** spec.md (its exemplar and basis are already day 91), controls.md (the risk branch's), and the tree.

Archived first: `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`, `swarm/history/aims.v6.md`.
