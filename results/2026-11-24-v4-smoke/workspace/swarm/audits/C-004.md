## Why

- **D-004 (misreport):** the finding named 7 products for zuidas-groothandel where the table has 8 (mint-tin omitted). The order gate already makes the worker open the table itself, so a summary miscount should not drive an order. The added clause makes that explicit at the point of ordering, for one sentence of cost and no new stop. The residual gap, that findings can be miscounted and nothing samples them closely, is a checker-coverage question for the designer, not a control.
- **D-003 (capacity split):** no control added. The 50% split is an aim, and controls win over aims. A control that enforced it would be a restock ceiling, which is a method change (designer, REQ-014) and would stop core restock work on a premise D-003 itself says is untested (restock may be the best use of time for cash now). No guardrail is near: cash is 1996.73 against a reserve of 120. The cause D-003 names most firmly, the swap trigger timing, is also the designer's (REQ-008).
