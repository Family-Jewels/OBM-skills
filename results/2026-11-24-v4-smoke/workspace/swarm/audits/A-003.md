2. **Fix (fault): T-0002 holds rows dated after today.** The file has a "Re-quote, day 70" block (eight prices, "No order placed") and a "Re-quote, day 71" block that says "Ordered on day 71: oat 60, stroopwafel 60, nuts 60, energy 60 (see day-71 log)". The order gate reads this file, so a row for a day not yet reached could pass it. Fix: controls to require that the gate read only rows dated on or before today; the worker to strike the day-70 and day-71 blocks, since no such order exists in the ground truth.

3. **Fix: the 216-minute restock ceiling is over-controlling.** On days 66 to 69 the worker made four fills (180 minutes). A fifth would reach 225, over the ceiling, so the day stopped with 170 to 180 minutes idle (Facts). Empty slots with stock on hand: day 67, energy, cola and oat empty at all three machines with depot 60, 60 and 41; day 69, cola empty at two slots with depot 50 and sparkling empty at two with depot 70. A stocked slot has averaged about EUR 10 a day (revenue_per_slot, days 60 to 69). The price pass already runs first (controls line 12; logs), and swaps have no candidates, so the ceiling now only caps fills. Fix: controls to let fills continue past 216 once the price pass and any swap are done and minutes remain, with the 480-minute day as the cap. Review at point 11: idle minutes and empty-with-stock slots.

4. **Fix: two measures are on the wrong basis.** (a) The top measure's floor and aim lines (charts.md lines 54 and 56) still aim at EUR 61,219, the day-365 exemplar. The spec's day-91 basis is EUR 5,500 to 6,500 (spec.md line 12). On the day-91 basis, cash needs about x1.12 per 7 days from 3,877 (day 69). The measured pace is x1.10 (days 48 to 69) and x1.05 (days 62 to 69). S-004 states x1.13, which the ground truth does not reproduce. Being below that line is the Strategist's to act on, not a fault. (b) The Pursuit table sets plan as a share of 480 minutes and actual as a share of minutes used. On the 480 basis at point 9, restock was 36% against a 35% plan, which is on plan. The real gaps are purchasing 2% against 20%, swaps 0% against 13%, price 7% against 15%, and reads 15% against 12%. The charts' 19-point restock gap is mostly the basis. REQ-047 is already open for this; no new ask.

5. **Fix: the request ledger does not show what is open.** charts.md lists three open rows (REQ-046, 047, 048) but System health says "Requests open: 25". Open human asks in escalations.md (REQ-015, 016, 024, 028 to 031, 036, 043 to 045) are missing from the table, including REQ-015 (the lines in item 4), which A-002 raised. REQ-048 (price floor) is "open" in the table, but AS-004 says it is done, and AS-004's claim that rules/operator.md still carries a 1.02 to 1.05 floor is wrong: the rules file has no unit-cost floor at all. The depot-stock ask (REQ-044 to human) is also missing from the table.
