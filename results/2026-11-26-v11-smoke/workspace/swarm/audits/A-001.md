# Audit A-001 (full audit, signal-triggered)

Invoked by the system: no sales two periods running (revenue and slots_selling both 0). Records read through day 7 (latest state), day-6 measures, charts and hand-off dated day 5; the invocation said day 3, so this audit uses the latest records available.

## Summary

The swarm is not selling. Cash is at EUR 1,203 on day 6 against the opening EUR 1,500 and the exemplar's path of about EUR 1,788 by day 5. Revenue and units have been zero every day, slots_selling is 0 of 72, and the agent used 0% of its worker time on days 3 to 6 while O1 and O2 were open at every day end. It spent EUR 236.55 on 275 units (cash reconciles to those orders and the EUR 12 daily fee) and by day 7 had 135 units in the depot with no machine loaded. The driver tree is sound and the records I could check reconcile, so this is a stall in the working agent's use of capacity, not a measurement fault. The M1 milestone (all six machines selling by day 3) has been missed. Cause is not visible in the records I can read, so a diagnosis is needed.

## Findings

**Direction (problem).** The tree adds up to the goal and the measures are the right ones. No redesign has been made, so no predicted shift exists to check. To reach the exemplar's EUR 3,228 from EUR 1,203 on day 6 the business needs about EUR 84 a day net after fees over the 24 remaining days. Nothing yet produces that. M2 (by day 15) is at risk for the same reason.

**Signal (problem).** `revenue` = 0 and `slots_selling` = 0 for two periods from day 3 (system.json signals; ground_truth/day-0006.md shows 0 slots loaded). This is the signal invoked. `cash` below EUR 1,500 has run since day 2; its own signal starts at day 8 and will fire by day 10 if the decline continues.

**Capacity not used (problem).** capacity_used_share is 0.469 on day 2 and 0.0 on days 3 to 6 (charts.md, metrics_system.md). Opportunities open at each day end: 2 (O1 and O2, days 3 to 5). This is the pattern the hand-off and the spec's guidance (G-002, G-004) address, and it was still present on day 6 after the day-5 hand-off. The agent's notes that would show why are not in the workspace (see Records), so I cannot say whether it believed the fills were blocked, planned them for later, or did not see the open opportunities. I do not judge the agent on guidance it had one period to use.

**Ordering ran ahead of placement (problem).** Two orders paid on day 1: PO-1002 (125 units, delivered day 6) and PO-1001 (150 units, due day 7, not yet delivered). 135 units are in the depot and 0 of 72 slots are loaded. The guidance on this point (G-001 supplier comparison, G-005 stock on hand) does not say how to size a first order against how many machines can be loaded. Proposed G-007 addresses that gap.

**Hand-off disagrees with the charts (record, system).** The day-5 hand-off says "Open now: O1" only. charts.md (day 5) and metrics_system.md (day 5, `opportunities_open_at_end`) both say 2, O1 and O2. O2 is the opportunity about unused worker time, which is the one most in view with 0% capacity. The hand-off's list is the one the agent reads. This is the system's record, not the agent's.

**PIP is labelled as gain, computed on levels (record, system).** charts.md says PIP = exemplar gain / actual gain and shows 1.5 at day 5. The value matches the level ratio, 1,788 / 1,215.45 = 1.47. On gains the exemplar has gained EUR 288 and the actual gain is minus EUR 284.55, so a gain-based PIP is not meaningful yet. The gap as displayed is not the gap the spec defines.

**Record timing (record, system).** charts.md and handoff.md stop at day 5, metrics_system.md at day 6, latest.md at day 7. The day-6 `units_in_storage` is 0, yet latest.md shows PO-1002 delivered on day 6 with 135 units in the depot. The day-6 snapshot shows the order as still paid and undelivered. Whether the day-6 measure should include a same-day delivery is worth checking.

**Budget figures differ (record).** The spec says about USD 2.55 of USD 3.00 remains. This session shows USD 1.5 remaining for the audit. The management roles' cost so far is "none yet" in charts.md. Cost per result is undefined because there are no results.

**Not verifiable (records absent from the workspace).** The spec's cadence calls for a short review note when an agent ends a period early with an opportunity open. The agent used 0% of its time on days 3 to 6, which meets the early-end rule (early_end_unused_share 0.5, three in five periods). I found no review note, no orchestrator log, no agent notes, no `audits/probes.md`, no `checks/policies.md`, and no checker record in the workspace. I therefore cannot confirm whether the review fired or how the orchestrator routed the stall. Marked unverified, not wrong.

**Guidance state.** The fact-check column in guidance/index.md is blank for all five entries, so I cannot confirm the system's checks passed. G-001 to G-005 read as advice: each says what good looks like with a why, and none uses an order.

**Re-verification sample and probes.** No work has passed a check, so there is no pass to re-verify (sample is empty). No probe was run: the probe register is not in the workspace and a seeded error would touch the simulator, which is out of scope here.
