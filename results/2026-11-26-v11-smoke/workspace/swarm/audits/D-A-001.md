## Summary

Cash is EUR 1,216.59 on day 15, below the opening EUR 1,500 since day 2 and well short of the exemplar path (EUR 2,364). The stall A-001 was raised for (no sales, days 3 to 6) has resolved: sales resumed on day 8 and slots_selling is 13 of 72. The live problem is that the agent buys ahead of sell-through and places stock slowly: 535 units were bought on days 9 to 14 (about EUR 506 committed across PO-1003 to PO-1007) against 348 units sold on days 8 to 15; on day 14 the depot held 143 units with 63 slots empty, and EUR 172 more was ordered that day. Revenue per slot fell from 14.7 (day 9) to 4.0 (day 15), and prices were cut on several slots on day 14 at more than one location in the same period. The environment gives the agent the wrong picture in three places: the hand-off's "Open now" lists only O1 while three opportunities were open; the O4 condition (`revenue <= 0`) contradicts its label ("flat or falling"), so the price-response guidance (G-003) and the always-applicable supplier guidance (G-001) were not routed; and the unit-cost measure rewards volume buying with no visible cost of unsold stock while worker time is busy. Why 384 of 480 worker minutes went unused on fills on day 15 is not in the records.

## Problem (pinpointed)

1. **Buying runs ahead of placement and sale.** Orders PO-1003 (day 9), PO-1004 (day 9), PO-1005 (day 10), PO-1006 and PO-1007 (day 14). Storage: 215 (day 8), 117, 46, 3, 123, 53, 143, 59 (day 15). Slots empty at day end: 59 to 63 on days 14 and 15.
2. **Prices moved together and unread.** Day 14 ground truth shows cola at 1.8 and energy drink at 2.5 at AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE/AI-LOUNGE; day 15 shows them back at 2.0 and 2.9. Revenue per slot went 14.74 (d9), 13.62, 6.43, 6.26, 11.64, 3.11, 4.02 (d15).
3. **Unit cost worse beyond its limit** (0.916 on day 15, centre 0.88), while `units_in_storage` is routine and carries no signal.

## What maintains it

- Ordering is the step the agent can do at once and sees succeed; the cost of unsold stock arrives late (storage is routine variation, no signal) and O3 only opens when worker time is idle, so it is silent when the agent is busy.
- The unit-cost chart is volume-weighted, so bulk buying improves it (down is better). Nothing on the chart pairs cost with sell-through.
- Price moves are rewarded by the next day's revenue, but with several moves at once the response cannot be read (the reason G-003 exists), and the guidance that says so was never handed to the agent.

## Cause (ordered)

1. **Information (primary).** (a) O4's condition does not match its label, so it cannot open while revenue falls above zero (revenue fell 190 to 58 on days 10 to 11 with no O4). (b) The hand-off's "Open now" hides O2 and O3, and O3 is the opportunity that matches the stock build. (c) The always-applicable G-001 (unit cost) and G-003 (price response) are not routed. (d) The O2 figure is inconsistent with the capacity share.
2. **Incentives (secondary).** `unit_cost_paid_30d` is charted down-is-better with no sell-through pair; `units_in_storage` has no signal before the day-8 cash signal, which fires only at three periods below EUR 1,500.
3. **Resources (unknown).** 384 worker minutes were used on day 15 but only about 2 machine fills (45 minutes each) were needed to fill the free slots. Whether travel, price checks or other calls took the time cannot be read from the records. Ruled in or out only by a per-call minute log.
4. **Knowledge / model tier.** Not considered until 1 to 3 are settled.

## What the design would need to change (no agent rules)

- Hand-off: list every open opportunity under "Open now" and include the always-applicable guidance each period. Moves unit_cost_paid_30d and revenue_per_slot.

- Chart: pair unit_cost_paid_30d with sell-through so a lower cost cannot be bought with unsold volume.
