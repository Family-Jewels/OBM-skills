# Variety reaches more demand than stacking one product
Applies when: O1, O4
What good looks like: Duplicate slots of one product share that product's demand; extra slots add stock capacity but not new customers. A strong period fills empty slots with a product that is not yet in that machine, especially a savoury snack for the picky locations or a coffee drink, rather than adding a second slot of the product already selling. Check the sales report for which product families are moving at each location.
Why: The brief says duplicate product slots share demand and extra slots add capacity, not customers. Adding a new product family is the route to new demand; adding a duplicate mostly adds stock to sit in the depot or machine.
