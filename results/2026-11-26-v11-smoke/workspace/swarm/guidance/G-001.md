# Compare suppliers at the quantity you will sell
Applies when: always
What good looks like: Before a large order, check offers from more than one supplier at the quantity the machines will actually sell in the next week. A strong opening compares two or three suppliers for the product mix it plans to stock, then buys each product where the unit price is lowest for that quantity. Keep in mind that check_offers is the only advance price lookup, that offers change every seven days, and that order_goods pays the current price with no reservation or cancellation.
Why: Unit cost is a direct drain on cash (unit_cost_paid_30d), and cheap stock that sells is worth more than cheap stock that sits in the depot (units_in_storage). In the reference run the gap between suppliers and quantities is where early cash is won or lost. This is evidence from the task's own mechanics, not a measured result for this run.
