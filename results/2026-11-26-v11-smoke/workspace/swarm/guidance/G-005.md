# Stock on hand is cash that earns nothing
Applies when: O3
What good looks like: Stock in the depot is money spent and not yet earning. A strong period matches what it orders to what the machines will sell before the next delivery window, and when units are in the depot while worker time is free, fills the empty slots they suit before ordering more. Example: with 60 units of drinks in the depot and a free hour in the afternoon, fill two drinks slots that are running low instead of buying the next batch.
Why: units_in_storage is a level that should fall as stock moves into machines and sells. Cash spent on depot stock does not count toward the day-30 balance unless it sells. Evidence is the brief's own accounting (cash counts; inventory is not money).
