# Buy to the depot and the empty slots you can fill
Applies when: O5
What good looks like: A strong period reads the depot count and the empty slots together before ordering. Stock that is already in the depot, plus what the next fill can place, is the amount most worth ordering; a delivery much larger than that waits in storage while slots stay empty. Example: with 143 units in the depot and 60 empty slots, a period that places part of the depot first and orders the next delivery in smaller lots keeps the depot turning over.
Why: Ground truth on days 9 to 15 shows 535 units bought against 348 sold, with 143 units in the depot on day 14 and 59 to 63 slots empty on days 14 and 15 (diagnosis D-A-001). Storage was routine variation on the chart, so the stock build had no signal of its own. O5 makes the build visible whether or not worker time is free. This is advice drawn from that record; it is not a measured result.
