# Size an order to the free slots and the depot already
Applies when: O1, O3
What good looks like: Before placing an order, count the slots that are free to load in the next fill and the units the depot already holds. A strong opening buys for what the next few fills can load, so stock on hand does not run ahead of placement. Example: a first order is sized to the products the first fills will load, rather than to the full range of products on offer.
Why: The latest ground truth (day 7) shows two orders totalling EUR 236.55 for 275 units. PO-1002 (125 units) was delivered on day 6 and 135 units are in the depot, while 0 of 72 slots are loaded. PO-1001 (150 units) was paid on day 1 and is due on day 7. Cash reconciles to these orders and the EUR 12 daily spot fee, so the spend is real and sitting in stock. This applies the stock point of G-005 at the ordering step; it is not a measured result for this run.
