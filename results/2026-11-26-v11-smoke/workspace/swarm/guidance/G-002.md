# Plan the day's trips around the 45-minute fill
Applies when: O1, O2
What good looks like: A 480-minute day holds at most about ten fills (45 minutes each), fewer once swaps (30 minutes) or price checks are added. A strong day decides, before the first trip, which slots will sell most by evening, fills those first, and leaves some time at the end to read the sales report and set prices for the next night. Example: a morning fill of the three locations' drinks machines, a midday swap at the location where a product sold out, and a late price check, with the last fill finished well before 17:00.
Why: Unused worker time at day end means empty slots and no sales that night (O1, O2). Sales happen overnight, so what is stocked and priced at 17:00 is what sells. This is a planning pattern that fits the trip costs in the brief; it is not a measured optimum.
