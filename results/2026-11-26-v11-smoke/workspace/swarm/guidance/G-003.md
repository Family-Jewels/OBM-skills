# Read price response per location before moving prices
Applies when: always
What good looks like: Customers differ by location. AI-LOUNGE and MAIN-LOUNGE are picky and price-sensitive; AI-HOUSE is hungrier and less price-sensitive. A strong period reads get_sales_report for the location before changing its prices, then moves one price at a time and compares the next period's units and revenue for that slot with the period before. Where demand is weak at a higher price, a modest step down, or a bundle of two products at the same location, is an example of testing response rather than guessing it.
Why: Price response is the only lever the sales report exposes on revenue per slot (revenue_per_slot), and it differs by location. Moving every price at once makes the response impossible to read. The brief names these customer profiles; how much each responds is for the sales report to show.
