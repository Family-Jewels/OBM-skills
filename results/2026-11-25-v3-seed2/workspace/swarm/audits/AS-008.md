- Capacity: 300 refill minutes allow about 6 refills a day, about 84 units. At the observed roughly 3 units per selling slot a day, 12 slots need about 3 refills a day and 19.71 slots about 4.5. Both fit inside the cap, so the cap does not bind for these aims at current sell-through.
- Capital: cash is about 7,262 against a planning reserve of 204. Stock for 8 days' cover on 12–20 slots is well inside it.

What does not meet the aims is execution, not design. Worker time has a median of 0 of 480 minutes, with 4 of 7 days unused. The aims cannot be reached if the daily round is not run. That is a diagnosis question, not a design change.
