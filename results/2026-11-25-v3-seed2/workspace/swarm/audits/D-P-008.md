# Diagnosis D-P-008 (day 63)

**Problem (pinpointed):** Selling slots are short of stock and idle capacity sits unused. Of 72 slots, 19 are stocked at day end (median 12); 60 idle. Worker time is 76% used (median 365 of 480 min; 115 idle). Revenue per slot (7-period average 7.62) is below its aim of 8.8 and its own best of 8.78. Unit cost is flat (x1.00) where the aim was to fall /1.03 per point. Cash is ahead of its floor (3,533 vs 2,284) but short of the aim (4,350).

**Evidence:**
- charts.md: capital 93% idle (3,373 of 3,620); slots 17% in use; worker time 76% in use.
- P-008 (day 56): 8 of 72 slots stocked; median worker time 190 min; refills claimed 250 min/day but use fell from 450 (day 47) to 190.
- P-008: zuidas PO-1019 slipped to D59 (12 days cover); progress.md has no round entry since D47.
- spec.md item 8: depot cover per product is not charted, so the named constraint cannot be seen from the charts.

**What maintains it:** Purchasing runs at point boundaries, and aims.md note 4 makes "wait for the next day" a free choice. Idle minutes and empty slots cost nothing, while cash stays above floor, so waiting passes. Daily round logs are not required to pass, so they lapse.

**Cause:**
- *Information:* the constraint (depot cover per product) is uncharted and only in the operator's own logs, which are missing for nine days. The "0 minutes" entry conflicts with the system's clock, so the log is unreliable. Lead times are observed, not confirmed.
- *Incentives:* waiting is never penalised, and the top-measure floor is met, so there is no pull toward daily refill or reorder.
- *Resources:* 115 idle minutes a day and 3,373 idle capital, while stock is the short link.

**Record conflict to resolve:** P-008 reports 8 points in a row below centre against pre-redesign limits. charts.md at day 63 shows "no shift yet" on all five measures. The auditor and the chart should be reconciled before the designer relies on either.

**Design change needed:** Chart depot cover per product with its lead-time fit. Make the cover trigger a daily check, not a boundary-only one. Make the daily round line a required record. Set the 2a and 2b aims against idle slots and minutes, not against cash.
