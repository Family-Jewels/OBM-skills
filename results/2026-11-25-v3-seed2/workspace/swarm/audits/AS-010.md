## Aims written
- Constraint: stock reaching selling slots, via depot cover (spec driver 2a-i). Worker time is idle capacity, not the limit (kept from AS-009; S-010 names worker time, see discrepancies).
- Point 44 (days 302–308), carried from S-010:
  - Refill and cover block: ≥ 300 min every working day, counting only refills, cover checks and logged waits. No padding.
  - Slots selling, 7-period: ≥ 8 at point 44; ≥ 12 at point 45 (S-010's point-43 aim, carried, not lowered).
  - Revenue per slot, 7-period: ≥ 8.0 at point 44, price ≥ unit cost × 1.2.
  - Depot cover: ≥ 8 days per selling product at every daily check, order at L + 8.
- Split of 480 min: days 302–307 are 300 / 60 / 60 / 40 / 20; day 308 (boundary) is 300 / 120 / 40 / 20 / 0. Both total 480 and both are within spec caps.
- Guardrails: cash ≥ 120 at every close; cash ≥ 204 after any cover order; refill minutes ≤ 300/day; never buy beyond expected sales before day 365.

## Can the redirect be met as the work stands?
No. What stops it:
1. **Refill minutes need stock in the depot.** At 45 min a refill, the 300-min block fills only if the depot holds stock for the slots. Charts show 0 of 72 slots stocked at day end (median 0), and S-010 reports 1 of 72, with 7,957 of capital idle. Minutes cannot turn into refills with no stock. Aim 1 can be reached only by padding (cover checks and waits logged to fill the clock), which the spec forbids: minutes are caps, not targets ("not by pushing time", spec v8 driver 5). So aim 1 is not reachable as a real output.
2. **Stock is set by a purchase rule with no target.** Spec v6 sizes each order as expected sales × "slots of that product to be stocked". The number of slots to be stocked is the operator's own planning figure; nothing in the spec ties it to the slot aim (8, then 12). An order can therefore stay small while the aim rises, so the slot aim and the purchase size are not connected in the design.
3. **Cover cannot be checked from the charts.** Depot cover per selling product is still not charted (spec open questions 10, 11). The 8-day cover rule, the one control on stock, is invisible to the system and to the auditor's charts; the auditor recomputes it by hand.
4. **Unconfirmed environment cause.** D-P-014 cause 1: zero-minute days may be environment errors, which spec v8 classes as "environment" when an error is recorded. If so, the refill block is blocked by the environment and no design change to the aims would help until that is answered. The round lines I could read stop at day 40, so I could not check this.

Because of 1–3, the aims cannot be met by the work as designed, and changing the work (the purchase rule and the cover chart) is needed. Per the rules, that change is for a redesign after the diagnosis, not made in this aim update.
