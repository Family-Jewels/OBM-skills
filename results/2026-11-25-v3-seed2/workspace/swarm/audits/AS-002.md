## Can the aims be met as the work is done now?
No, not as written:
1. **Refill throughput.** Slot 2a needs ≥ 20 selling on the 7-period average, from 18.57. Each refill takes 45 fixed minutes (spec), and 250 minutes is about 5–6 refills a day against the 9 a day the log shows. The spec's refill cap is 300 minutes a day. Raising selling slots past depot stock needs more output per minute of refill, which is a change in how the refill work is done, not a change in the aim.
2. **Measure gap.** The depot-stock constraint cannot be seen in the charts, so the aim's constraint cannot be checked. Adding the measure changes the spec.
3. **Time split.** At 450 of 480 minutes in use, the 110-minute buffer in the S-002 split is not free. The split holds only if minutes per refill or per quote fall.

Predicted outcome if the change goes in: refill minutes per selling slot fall, and depot cover ≥ 8 days in every selling product within 2 points. Revert if cash falls below 120 or cover orders push cash below 204.
