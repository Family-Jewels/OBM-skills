# D-P-012: Selling slots fall while worker time and cash sit idle (read at day 189, point 27)

Note on the trigger: P-012 is the day-91 pulse. Its signal (cash below the old centre) is superseded. This diagnosis uses the day-189 charts, the latest records.

**Problem (pinpointed).** Stocked, selling slots fell from a median of 7 (charts, 7-day) to 4 at day end. slots_selling latest 5 against centre 10.11, celeration /1.67 (wrong way, 3 points, provisional). Cash x1.02 against the floor's x1.08; latest 6,950 vs floor line 9,048. Revenue /1.48 and units /1.38 follow it.

**Walk down.** Top: cash (below floor) <- revenue (/1.48) <- slots_selling (/1.67, the largest wrong-way driver) with revenue_per_slot (/1.23) secondary. The driver that moved is 2a, through its constraint 2a-i (depot cover). All individual points are inside their limits; the shift is in celeration, so this is a trend, not a single signal.

**Evidence.**
- Resources: worker time latest 0 of 480 min, median 0, unused 5 of the last 7 days; capital 4% used (6,800 idle); slots stocked 10%.
- Records conflict: rules say a round line is logged daily. work.jsonl has day-level lines only to day 89; days 90–146 and 147–190 are summaries. The 147–190 summary reports "refills 2–9/day", but the system's clock shows 0 minutes used. Either calls are not reaching the clock, or the summary is not a record of actions. Not resolved here.
- aims.md is dated day 155 (point 23). S-007 (day 175) set aims for point 26 and a new split; no aims for points 27 onward, and no S-008 on record.
- Depot cover per product is still not a system chart (requested in D-P-008, D-P-011, S-007).

**What maintains it.**
- No cover signal: the constraint is unmeasured, so a near-empty depot gives no alarm to operator, pulse or Strategist.
- Idle minutes have no pull: the 300-min refill block is a cap, and "unspent minutes are logged, not moved". Refills need depot stock, so an empty depot makes the time idle, and nothing requires buying more.
- Idle cash has no pull: no rule converts it into stock unless a cover reading shows a breach.

**Cause.**
- Information (primary): cover per product is unmeasured. The L+8 trigger cannot be checked and the depot can run dry unseen.
- Resources: idle minutes and cash are not turned into depot stock; the cause is the purchase trigger, not time.
- Not settled: whether orders were placed and under-sized, or not placed. Evidence needed: get_machine_inventory depot counts and order sizes for days 175–189, and the simulator call log for the same days (not the summary).

**What the design would need to change.**
1. Chart depot cover per selling product (driver 2a-i) as a system measure with an 8-day guardrail.
2. Require one dated round line per day, and make a missing line a pulse signal.
3. Issue aims for points 27 onward and reconcile the work log against the clock.
Measures to move: slots_selling, units, cover days, then cash.
