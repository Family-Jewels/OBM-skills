Mostly. The evidence for depot stock as the constraint holds: on day 38 the round made zero refills, the depot was empty until PO-1015 landed on day 39 (work.jsonl), and capital is about 88% idle (charts.md, 12% in use). Worker time is not the limit (73% used, median 350 of 480).

## 2. What is wrong in the design (the cause)
The cover rule is live (operator brief, v2), but it orders at 8 days of cover. The observed lead times are zuidas 2–4 days and bakker 8–11 days (progress.md; PO-1009: 11 days). An order placed when cover is 8 days lands too late for bakker and often for zuidas, so slots run dry. The rule is in the environment; the rule's threshold is the cause, not the operator's effort.
