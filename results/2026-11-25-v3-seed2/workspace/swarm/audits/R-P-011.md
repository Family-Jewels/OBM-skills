## What in the design causes the fault
The cover rule sizes orders and judges cover against the last 7 days' average sales. A product that is out of stock sells nothing, so its average falls, its expected sales fall, and both the cover reading and the order size fall with it. The rule therefore holds stock low: a loop the operator cannot see on its own round line. Progress.md shows rounds and orders continuing to day 146 (PO-1044 day 140, PO-1045 day 144), while log/work.jsonl has no round line after day 89. The cover chart requested since D-P-008 is still absent.

## Change (smallest that addresses it)
1. Driver 2a-i: expected sales per day = units per stocked slot-day over the last 14 days in stock × slots to be stocked. Not the 7-day average.
2. Order size: fill every selling slot of the product to full capacity first, then cover to L+8 days of that expected rate. Trigger unchanged (cover < L+8).
3. Open question 10: request the depot-cover chart and expected-rate basis from the system (again).
