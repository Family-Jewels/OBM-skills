## Can the aims be met as the work is done now?
**No. Design change needed.**

1. **Cover is zero on the selling products while the rule is in force.** work.jsonl shows cover_days of 0 for crisps, energy, cola and choco on days 76, 79, 86 and 89 (e.g. day 89: crisps 0, energy 0, cola 0, choco 0). Stocked slots have stayed at 8–13 since day 63.
2. **The order trigger is too short for the observed lead times.** The spec v4 rule (and operator rule) triggers at lead time + 1 with zuidas 5 and bakker 12 days. Observed: bakker ordered on day 79 was due day 94 (15 days); the day-89 zuidas order is due day 91, two days after cover reached zero. An order triggered at 8 days' cover arrives after the slot has run dry, so the depot cannot keep selling slots filled.
3. **The 8-day aim cannot be met from bakker at its observed lead time.** 8 days of cover is less than a 14–15 day lead time. Only zuidas and kanaal can hold the aim for products they supply. The spec's supplier split, lead-time figures and trigger need changing, which is a spec and rule change.
4. **The 20-slot aim may hit the refill cap.** At 45 min a refill, 300 min allows about 6 refills a day. Day 89 used 8 refills (360 min). If stock-out counts do not fall, the cap binds before the aim. Not a design change yet; for the Strategist to watch.

The worker's time is not the limit (115 min idle). The fault is in the depot: order timing and supplier lead times. Changing the minutes or the aims would not fix it, and I am not permitted to change the spec or rules here.
