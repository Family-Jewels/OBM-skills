## 2. What is wrong in the design (the cause)
The cover rule exists (operator brief v3, spec v3 driver 2a-i) but the environment does not make it run:
- *Information:* cover per product is not charted, so no one sees a breach until slots run dry. The operator's log is the only record, and it lapses.
- *Incentives:* waiting costs nothing in the score (aims.md note 4 called a wait "its own call"), so idle minutes and empty slots pass unpenalised while cash stays above floor.
- *Checks:* a day with no round line is not counted as a failure, and the cover check is not a named step in the per-day order.
