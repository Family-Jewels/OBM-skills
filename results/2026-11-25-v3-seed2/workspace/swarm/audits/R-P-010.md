## 2. Is the design causing it?

## 3. Change (smallest that addresses it)
1. **Trigger, all selling products, daily:** order when cover < L + 8, where L is the chosen supplier's longest delivery in its last 3 receipts. Size to L + 8 days of cover counting stock on hand. This keeps cover ≥ 8 through delivery.
2. **Supplier choice:** lowest of ≥ 2 checked quotes, unchanged. Bakker is not barred: at L = 15 its trigger is about 23 days' cover, which cash can carry.
3. **Charts:** depot cover is still not a system chart (spec open question 8, requested at D-P-008). This is a request to the system, not a designer change. Until it exists, the operator logs cover per product each round; the auditor recomputes it from ground truth.
4. **Aims:** targets unchanged (slots ≥ 20, price ≥ 8.8, cash on the system's floor and aim lines). One sentence in aims.md on bakker is aligned with the new trigger.

Not changed, and why: the goal, the aims' targets, the worker-time split, guardrails and the charts. Core work does not stop under this change.
