- Refill capacity: 300 min at 45 min a refill gives at most 6 refills a day (about 5 if cover checks take about 30 min of the block). Need is about 3 refills a day for 12 slots and about 4.5 for 19.71 slots (spec v7 known limit). The cap does not bind.
- Stock: cover at L + 8 days for 12 selling slots at about 3 units per slot a day is roughly 290 units. Cash of about 7,670 covers that with the cash ≥ 204 rule intact.
- Price: the price ≥ unit cost × 1.2 rule and the clearance date are unchanged.

What stops the aims is not the design. The worker used a median of 0 of 480 min and the refill and cover block did not run, so the work the spec sets out is not being done. That is a performance fault for the diagnostician; S-009 already states it. If the diagnostician finds that the work cannot be done as the brief and tools allow, that would be a design fault, and the redesign would follow.
