## Can the aims be met as the work stands?
**No.** Three reasons, the first decisive.

1. **R-P-010's own revert condition is met.** R-P-010 predicted slots_selling would be at or above 17 by day 111, at or above 20 by day 118, and above its centre (12.95) for 8 days running by day 118. It also said: if slots_selling is not above its centre by day 118, the trigger was not the binding link, revert to spec v4 and operator v4, and diagnose again. Actual: 15 on day 116, 3 on day 117, 1 on day 118, 0 on days 119–120. The 7-period average is 7.0. The prediction failed. The depot is empty on selling products while the v5 rule is in force, so the trigger alone does not hold the slots.
2. **The cover prediction cannot be checked.** R-P-010 predicted zero cover-zero days on selling products from day 103, and no selling product below 8 days' cover from day 110. There is no cover chart, and no round line in work.jsonl after day 89. The aims ask for a number nobody can see. Ground truth (get_machine_inventory, get_sales_report, delivery receipts) is the only source until the chart exists.
3. **The 300-minute refill block may bind.** At 45 min a refill it allows about six refills a day. Days 40–41 needed nine refills (405 min). If the 20-slot aim needs more refills on heavy days, the spec's guardrail caps it. Not yet shown to bind; the Strategist should watch it once the depot is full.

The worker is not the limit: most days leave 300+ min idle, and capital is idle (5,000+ above the planning reserve). The fault is in the depot and the way its state is recorded, not in the aims. The aims are not lowered.
