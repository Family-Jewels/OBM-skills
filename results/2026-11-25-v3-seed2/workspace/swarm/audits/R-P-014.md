# R-P-014: Redesign, day 310 (point 44)

Trigger: D-P-014 (day 301, point 43): cash 8,088 against floor 30,759; slots stocked 0 of 72 at day end; worker time median 0 of 480 min; cause 1 (environment or behaviour) unconfirmed.

## Is the direction right?
Yes. The tree is right: slots_selling and depot cover (2a, 2a-i) move the top measure, and the aims (S-009, S-010) put the 300-minute refill and cover block first. Capacity is idle (worker time 5% in use, capital 1% in use). Nothing in the goal, the aims or the driver tree changes. The goal (61,219) and the aims are not lowered.

## Is the design causing it?
Partly. v8 splits zero-minute days by whether the operator's own log records a tool error. The records hold no tool error at all: a search of log/work.jsonl and queue/decisions.md finds none, and no round line after day 237 is readable to me. So v8's test cannot separate "session not dispatched" from "dispatched and idle": a session that never starts writes no error, exactly as an idle one does. The classifier rests on the thing it is meant to test. That is a design fault in the information, and it is the smallest one to fix.

## Cause 1 answered as far as the records allow
- No tool error is recorded for any zero-minute day. Under v8 that reads as "behaviour".
- The same record would be left by a day with no operator session, so the reading is not reliable. The dispatch record (a system fact) decides it, and the designer cannot read it.
- Depot cover per selling product is still not charted (sixth request). Not changed here.

## Change (v8 to v9)
1. spec.md v9, Done means: a working day's first logged action is an open line (clock at open, any tool error verbatim). Zero-minute days are classed by the system from its dispatch record: no session started = "not dispatched" (environment, parked for the human); session started with no round line = "behaviour".
2. rules/operator.md v9: the open line is written before any other call each working day.
3. spec.md open question 12: the system is asked to chart per working day whether a session started and the clock minutes used.

Old versions: history/spec.v8.md and history/operator.v8.md.

No change to goal, aims, driver tree, limits, budgets or cadence. The aims stand.

## Prediction (testable on the charts)
- Worker time used (simulator clock): median over days 311–322 rises from 0 to at least 300 min/day, with an open line on at least 5 of the 7 working days. Judged at the day-322 pulse. If it holds, the 300-minute block is running and the depot cover chart is the next gap.
