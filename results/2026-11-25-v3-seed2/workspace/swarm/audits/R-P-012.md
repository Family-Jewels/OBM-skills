# R-P-012: Redesign, day 199 (point 29), spec v6 to v7

Trigger: D-P-012 (day 189 charts, point 27): slots_selling falling (/1.67 celeration, 5 against centre 10.1), cash below floor (6,950 against 9,048), worker time 0 of 480 minutes used on the clock, capital 4% used.

## Is the direction right?
Yes. Depot stock reaching the selling slots (driver 2a-i) is the constraint. It moves slots_selling, then revenue, then cash. Worker time and capital are idle, so neither is the limit. The goal (61,219) and the S-007 stretch aims are unchanged.

## What in the design causes the fault
The design already contains the cover rule (spec v5/v6) and the requirement for a daily round line. The fault is in how the rule is kept, and the design lets it slip:
1. **Information:** cover per product is still not a system chart (requested four times). The round line is the only record, and it stopped after day 89 except for summaries.
2. **Records disagree with the clock:** the day 147–190 summaries report 2–9 refills a day; the clock shows 0 minutes used. The rule allowed a summary in place of a round line.
3. **Incentive:** the operator rule let an empty slot wait "because waiting costs nothing in the score". That rule keeps idle time idle.
4. **Aims gap:** aims.md named no targets past point 26 until the day-189 version; S-007's aims were carried by a file that was stale.

## Change (smallest that addresses it)
1. spec v7, Done means: a round reconciles with the clock (refills × 45 min within one call of the day's clock minutes); a round with missing or non-reconciling cover or refill figures is a failed round. A wait on an empty selling slot is valid only when the depot count is zero.
2. rules/operator.md (v7, previous in history/operator.v6.md): an empty slot with depot stock is refilled now; a product under cover threshold is ordered that day, not waited on; round lines carry refills, minutes and cover days, reconciled with the clock.
3. aims.md (AS-007): points 29–30 carry the S-007 targets unchanged; split of 480 minutes kept at S-007's shares, with the boundaries on days 203 and 210.
4. Not changed: the goal, the aims, the 300-minute cap, the reserve (EUR 204 after an order), supplier choice, cadence, budgets. The cover chart request (open question 10) stays open with the system; the auditor recomputes until it is charted.

## Cost
No extra spend. The cover orders are the same orders sized as v6 already requires; the operator uses the time the 300-minute block already allows. Risk: more refills and orders may push cash toward the EUR 204 planning reserve, so the reserve rule stands. Expected spend stays within the v6 budgets.
