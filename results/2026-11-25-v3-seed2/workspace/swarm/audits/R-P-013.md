# R-P-013: Redesign, day 265 (point 38)

Trigger: D-P-013 ("worker time and slots stop while cash sits idle"). Note: D-P-013 reads the charts at day 252; the current charts are at day 265. The direction is unchanged.

## Is the direction right?
Yes. The tree is right: slots_selling and the depot cover that feeds it move the top measure, and the aims (S-008, S-009) put the 300-minute refill and cover block first. Nothing in the tree or the aims needs to change. The fault is that the block is not being run.

## Evidence the designer checked
- Charts: worker time median 0 of 480 min, latest 0; cash 7,681.8 against a floor line of 19,440 at point 37.
- Records: the last per-day round line is day 237 (work.jsonl). Days 147–236 are summaries with no per-day minutes, which spec v7 does not accept as round lines. progress.md has nothing after D232.
- Depot at D237 (the last cover read): energy 0, oat 0; crisps 60, cola 30, choco 40, gummy 20, sparkling 20. The depot is not empty across the board, so "depot stock is the binding link" holds only for energy and oat. For the other products stock sits in the depot unrefilled. That points at the round not running, not at supply.
- The simulator clock records 0 minutes, which means no calls reached the simulator on those days, or the operator was not dispatched. The records cannot tell these apart, because no error was logged.

## Cause split
Environment (dispatch or tool access) and behaviour look the same on the charts. Only the error text separates them. So the first change is about information, not about the aims.

## Change (v7 → v8)
1. spec.md v8, Done means: each working day's round line records the clock at open and close, minutes used and any tool error verbatim. Zero-minute days are split into "environment" (error recorded) and "behaviour" (none).
2. rules/operator.md v8: one round line per working day; tool failures written verbatim and parked in queue/decisions.md; no unchanged retry.
3. spec.md open question 11: depot cover still uncharted (sixth request); product-specific reading recorded.

Old versions: history/spec.v7.md and history/operator.v7.md.

No change to goal, aims, driver tree, limits or cadence. The aims stand.

## Prediction (testable on the charts)
- Worker time used (simulator clock): median rises from 0 to at least 300 min/day. First working day at or above 300 minutes: by day 268. Median at or above 300 across point 38 (days 266–272), judged at the day-273 pulse.
- slots_selling 7-period rises from 3.86 to at least 8 at point 38 and shows an upward run by point 39.
- Falsifier: if clock minutes are still 0 on day 268 with an error recorded, the cause is environment. The designer does not change the rules again. The diagnostician names the error, and the human is asked to restore dispatch or tool access.

## Cost and revert
Cost: about 1 minute of logging per round. Revert the v8 rule if the round lines become a burden on refill minutes (over 10 min/day). Revert to history/operator.v7.md if the change stalls the round.

Design change needed: no (aims unchanged). Redesign adopted day 265.
