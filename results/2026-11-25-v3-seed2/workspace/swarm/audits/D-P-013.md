# D-P-013: Worker time and slots stop while cash sits idle (charts read at day 252, point 36)

Note on the trigger: `audits/P-013.md` is the day-98 pulse, not day 252. The latest pulse on disk is P-023 (day 238); no day-252 pulse file exists. This diagnosis uses `charts.md` at day 252 and the records through S-008 (day 224). If a day-252 pulse exists under another number, its file should be named so the designer can check the trigger.

**Problem (pinpointed).** From about day 224 (point 32), the operator records almost no work on the simulator clock: worker time median 0 of 480 min over the last 7 days, 4 of 7 days with none, latest 0. In the same window stocked slots fell to 0 at day end, slots_selling is wrong-way (/2.00, latest 0), units wrong-way (/1.55), revenue latest 0.0. Cash is 7,439 against a floor line of 18,009 and has not moved from its phase (/1.00 vs x1.08 needed). Cash is 3 points below floor, which brings the Strategist forward.

**Walk down.** Top: cash (flat, below floor) <- revenue (0) <- slots_selling (0, 2a) <- depot stock reaching slots (2a-i, the constraint) <- refill and cover block (300 min first claim) <- worker time (0 of 480).

**Evidence.**
- Resources: worker time 0 of 480 used, 100% idle; slots 0 of 72 stocked; capital 1% used (7,572 idle).
- aims.md (S-008) requires the refill and cover block daily as the first claim, with 300 min spent. The clock records none of it.
- Last per-day round line in work.jsonl: day 190 (P-023). progress.md has only a summary to D232 and nothing after. Days 233–252 are unrecorded.
- S-008 (day 224) already said worker time was 0 with 4 of 7 days unused and called it "a finding for the auditor." No fix on that finding appears in P-023.
- No Strategist record after S-008 (day 224). The 3-point cadence (day 245) is missing.
- Depot cover is still not charted (requested in D-P-008, D-P-011, D-P-012, S-008).
- A search of the swarm records for limits, credentials, outages, timeouts and usage found no environment failure recorded. This does not rule one out: an unreachable tool would also leave the clock at zero.

**What maintains it.**
- Idle time costs nothing and no rule converts it into work: unspent minutes are "logged, not moved".
- Refills need depot stock. The cover check that would trigger a purchase is not charted, so a near-empty depot gives no alarm to the operator, the pulse or the Strategist.
- Missing round lines and zero-minute days have no consequence (spec v4 calls them a failed round; nothing acts on it).

**Cause.** Not settled between two readings, and the records cannot separate them:
1. Environment (first to rule out): the operator's calls do not reach the simulator, or the operator is not dispatched on the daily cadence. Evidence needed: orchestrator dispatch log and run transcripts for days 225–252, any tool errors, and the simulator call log for those days.
2. Information and incentives (if the calls do run): the depot state is unmeasured, so the constraint is invisible, and zero-work days pass without consequence.

Resources are not the cause: minutes and cash are idle, so the limit is the operator's activity or the depot state, not capacity.

**What the design would need to change.**
1. Make a zero-minute day, and a day with no dated round line, an automatic pulse signal that names the failed step.
2. Chart depot cover per selling product (2a-i) now, with the 8-day guardrail.
3. Confirm the operator's daily dispatch and record any tool or environment error separately from agent behaviour.
4. Reconcile the R-P-012 date (charts: day 232; spec and aims: day 199).
5. Hold the Strategist to its cadence: S-009 is due and cash is below floor.

Measures to move: worker time used, slots_selling, stocked slots, units, then cash.

Diagnosis needed for the designer: yes.
