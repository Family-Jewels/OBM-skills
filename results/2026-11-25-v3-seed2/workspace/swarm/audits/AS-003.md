## Can the aims be met as the work is done now?
**No, not as written. Design change needed.**

1. **Depot cover is not holding, and the rule uses lead times that no longer fit.** The spec and operator rule trigger orders on fixed lead times (zuidas 5 days, bakker 12). Observed lead times in the log: zuidas 4, then 12, then 7; bakker 3–4, then 14; kanaal 6–7. At day 71, cover was zero for crisps, cola, choco and oat, and machines were empty on days 66–67 (0 units on day 67, 0 on day 68). The cover rule is in force; its trigger is wrong for the observed spread. Changing the lead-time assumption and the split across suppliers is a spec and rule change, so it waits for the redesign.
2. **Cover is not charted.** The redirect asks for a depot-cover chart. Charts are written by the system, and the spec lists it as open question 8. The aim cannot be checked from the charts until it exists. Requested, not done here.
3. **Refill minutes and the cap are not measured.** The spec fixes a refill at 45 min and caps refill time at 300 min/day. The log shows 8–9 refills on some days, which exceeds the cap at the fixed figure. AS-002's queued item (measure minutes per refill from work.jsonl) is still open. Until it is, the 21-slot aim cannot be shown to fit inside the cap.
4. **Revenue per slot.** Aim 2b is 9.0 from 4.63 now. This depends on demand per product (open question 3) as much as on price. Price rules are being met (day 72 round). No design change proposed here beyond the above.

Predicted outcome for the redesign (to be tested by the charts): every selling product holds ≥ 8 days' cover from observed lead times within two points; the count of days with zero cover on a selling product falls to zero; cash stays above 204 after cover orders. Revert if cash falls below 120 or cover orders push cash below 204.
