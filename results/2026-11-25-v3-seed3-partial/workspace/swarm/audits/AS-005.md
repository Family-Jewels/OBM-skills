# AS-005: aim update, day 111 (point 16), after S-005

**Input:** S-005 (day 105, point 15). Constraint: worker time, unspent. Stretch aims for point 16: depot cover 7–10 days on 5 of 7 days; slots_selling ≥ 20; revenue_per_slot ≥ 10.5; cash ≥ floor. Capacity split 45/35/10/10/0. Asks for depot cover to be charted.

**Written:** swarm/aims.md (AS-005). Old aims (AS-004) copied to swarm/history/aims.v5.md. Spec and rules not changed.

**Timing note:** S-005 aims are for point 16 (days 106–112). Applied from day 111 and carried to point 17 until the next redirect, as AS-004 did. Confirm this reading.

**Discrepancies to resolve (for the auditor). The aims use the charts figure in each case.**
- Worker time: S-005 gives a median of 25 min a day, 455 min idle, 3 of 7 days unused. charts.md (simulator clock) gives a median of 225 min used (47%), 255 min idle, 2 of the last 7 days unused. AS-004 flagged the same gap (25 vs 145 min); the 25-minute figure has come back. The constraint call stands on the charts figure, which still shows idle capacity, but the size differs.
- Cash: S-005 gives 4,789 latest and a floor of 3,676 × 1.08 = 3,970. charts.md gives 5,049.19 at day 112, floor line 3,966.59, aim line 13,817.49. progress.md gives a balance of €5,067 at d113. Cash is already above the floor line, so the top-measure aim is held, not at risk.
- Depot cover: S-005 asks for a chart. charts.md has none; instrumentation has been outstanding since spec v3 and was requested in R-P-003. The designer cannot add it (system-written). Aim 1 can only be judged from the operator's daily record in progress.md, not from charts.

**Can the aims be met as the work is done now?** No, for aims 1–3 as written.
- Stock, not worker time, decides whether slots hold stock. progress.md shows stock ran out on d97–d100 while orders were in transit, and refills ran on the days deliveries arrived (d102, d106, d112). Worker time was idle on those stock-out days. Charts: slots stocked at day end latest 4, median 7 of 72.
- The Strategist's own record (S-005) says the current rules cannot meet aims 2 and 3 without a delivered-receipt condition on refills. That condition is not in the spec or rules/operator.md. Adding it changes how the work is done, so it is a design change, not an aim.
- Aim 1 conflicts with the spec's order rule. Spec v3 sets the cover band at 7–10 days and orders when cover falls below lead time + 2 days. progress.md records that the lead time moved to 10 days (PO-1029, d93). With a 10-day lead time, the trigger is 12 days of cover, above the 10-day band top, so the band cannot be held as written. The band needs to be set from the measured lead time in the redesign.
- Aims 2 and 3 were missed in AS-004 and AS-003 for the same reason. Setting higher slot aims without changing refill timing repeats that miss.

**Redesign request (for the system to queue behind any fault diagnosis):**
1. A refill waits for a delivered receipt that confirms stock, against a same-day live supplier quote; a replacement supplier is named when one closes.
2. Cover band and order trigger set from the measured supplier lead time (currently 10 days, not 2–7).
3. Daily refill quota in clock minutes (not the 45-minute figure).
4. Depot cover charted (system-side instrumentation).

These do not lower any aim or the goal.

Design change needed: yes
