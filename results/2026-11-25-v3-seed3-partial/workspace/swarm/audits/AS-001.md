## Can the aims be met as the work is done now?

No.

- **Aim:** 48 of 72 slots selling by day 28. From 20 at day 24, that is +28 in four days, about 7 new selling slots a day. The recent rate is about 3 a day (10 to 20 over days 21 to 24).
- **Time:** each new selling slot needs one 45-min refill. Seven refills is 315 min. At today's read load (about 130 min), that leaves 35 min, before any price call, order or reserve. The day runs out.
- **Targeting:** the operator brief says to refill the highest-margin products in the slots with the most demand. It does not say to fill empty slots first. Refills into slots that already hold stock add no selling slot. The log shows refills to slots that may already hold stock (for example AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:B2, cola). The auditor should check this against inventory.
- **Depot:** the brief reads depot stock before each refill and skips to another product when short. It does not require stock to be bought ahead for the week, so the refills stall when the depot runs dry.

The aim can be met only if three things change: reads fall to 60 min a day or less, refills go to empty slots first, and depot stock is held ahead of the refill cycle. Each of these changes the operator brief and possibly the spec's resources, which this update does not change.
