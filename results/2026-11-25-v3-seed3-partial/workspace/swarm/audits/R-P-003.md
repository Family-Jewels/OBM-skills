# R-P-003: redesign, after D-P-003 (day 86, point 12)

**Trigger:** D-P-003 (day-77 data): slots_selling wrong way (/1.82), cover not instrumented, idle minutes and capital trigger nothing, the day-21 fixes not kept.

**Note on timing:** this redesign was invoked at day 86. When I went to write aims.md, it already held AS-004 (day 94, from S-004). I did not overwrite it. AS-004 stands; its aims are the Strategist's, and I did not lower any of them.

## 1. Direction: right or wrong?
Right. Driver tree is right: slots_selling and depot cover are the priority-1 drivers, and cash moves with them. The error is the constraint name. S-003 and AS-003 said worker time; the charts show worker time 12% in use with 420 min idle, and capital 3% in use. The gate is depot stock and order timing. S-004 and AS-004 now say the same. The spec did not say so.

## 2. Is the design causing it?
Yes, in three places:
- **Information:** depot cover is not charted. The rule exists in spec v3 but nothing measures it or flags it.
- **Incentive:** an order waits for the weekly quote cycle, and the refill count is not required. Idle minutes and stock-outs cost nothing and trigger nothing.
- **Naming:** the spec called worker time "the binding one", so the work was aimed at the wrong resource.

## 3. Change (smallest)
1. **spec v3 → v4** (`history/spec.v3.md`): depot cover is named the constraint, worker time is not binding. Added a per-working-day check (refill quota met or shortfall logged; cover recorded; order placed when the trigger is hit). Depot cover flagged as a request to the system to chart. Open questions 6–7 added.
2. **operator brief v3 → v4** (`history/operator.v3.md`): (a) cover is worked out and written every working day; (b) if cover is below lead time + 2, check_offers and order that same day, not on the weekly cycle; (c) refill quota: at least 2 refills a day while slots are below the aims.md slot aim and depot holds stock, up to 400 min; (d) a missed quota must name the depot shortfall.
3. **Not changed:** aims (AS-004 stands), goal, reserve, guardrails, capacity split (AS-004's split applies). The "refill waits for a delivered receipt" change from AS-004 stays pending; it is a separate change and I did not bundle it.
