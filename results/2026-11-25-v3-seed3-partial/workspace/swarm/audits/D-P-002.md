# D-P-002: diagnosis (day 42, point 6)

**Problem (pinpointed).** slots_selling fell from 20 of 72 (day 24) to 0 (day 42; 7-day average 9.3), beyond its lower limit. Revenue, units and revenue_per_slot are all on wrong-way celeration (/1.48, /1.47, /1.16). Over the last 7 days the clock shows a median of 60 of 480 worker minutes used, and 2 of 7 days unused. Capital in use is 41 of 2,501. Cash (2,580) is above the aim line, so the top measure hides the stall.

**Evidence.**
- `charts.md`: slots_selling beyond the lower limit; cash_gain_7 8 points below its centre; worker time 420 min idle; slots stocked median 3; capital 3% in use.
- `aims.md`: "As of day 24", aim ≥48 slots by day 28. No Strategist redirect since S-001. S-002 (due day 42) is not written.
- `log/progress.md`: day 22 a refill failed on empty depot stock (45 min lost); day 27 no refills because the depot was empty; PO-1010 ordered day 24 (lead time 4 days); PO-1015 and PO-1016 due day 42.
- `log/minutes.jsonl`: refills of 7–8 a day on days 21–30, each logged at a flat 45 min. Days 21–28 were back-filled for P-004, and days 33–40 are summaries with no per-day minutes.
- **Conflict:** the log shows 8 refills a day on days 29–40, but the clock reads a median of 60 min. Both cannot be true.

**What maintains it.**
- Weekly ordering with 4–7 day delivery. The depot empties at the start of each cycle, so refills stop. Reads (cheap, always possible) keep passing. Refilling only pays once stock arrives.
- A 10-unit refill sells out within a day (day 20). At 45 min per call, the worker's most valuable minutes buy the least sales per minute.
- Idle minutes and idle capital carry no consequence. Cash growth (1,384 → 2,580) rewards nothing in particular.

**Cause.**
- *Information:* aims are 18 days stale. The 45 min per refill is a fixed assumption (spec open question 1), not clock-measured. The weekly order rule does not match delivery lead times.
- *Resources:* after day 24 the binding resource is depot stock and its lead time, not worker time. Capital sits idle because ordering frequency, not funds, limits supply.
- *Incentives:* no consequence for unused time. The refill cap (7 a day) acts as a ceiling.
- *Records:* the clock and the log disagree for days 29–42. It is unverified whether the worker ran on days 36–42. This must be settled before any redesign.

**What the design would need to change** (not written here):
1. Issue the current aims now (designer, after S-002). Name slots holding stock as the aim, and set refill quantity to fill a slot.
2. Replace the work plan's weekly order with a schedule set by lead time, so stock lands before the refill gate closes.
3. Measure refill minutes per call from the clock, and add units stocked per worker-minute as a driver.
4. Add a driver check so settlement-driven cash cannot mask zero selling.

**Settle first.** Check `work.jsonl` and `runs.jsonl` for days 36–42 for errors or outages (environment failure is not performance). Compare the day-end clock with `minutes.jsonl` per day. Confirm each restock's result against `get_machine_inventory`.
