# Fidelity check F-E-002-2: E-002 moderate print marketing in AI-LOUNGE

Experiment: swarm/experiments/E-002.md
Design: Slice = AI-LOUNGE (both machines). Control = AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE. Change = one moderate print campaign per point via run_marketing(channel = print) at AI-LOUNGE only. Start = point 3.
Check date: day 13 of 91.
Prior check: swarm/audits/F-E-002-1.md (day 6, fail: snapshot lacked marketing). This re-check is run after that finding; I did not treat the earlier check as settling anything.

## What I compared

- Latest ground-truth snapshot: swarm/snapshots/00013.md (day 13), read in full.
- Snapshot before the experiment's Start plan: swarm/snapshots/00012.md (day 12), read in full, compared slot by slot with 00013.
- All twelve snapshots, 00002 to 00013, searched for any marketing content (terms: marketing, campaign, print). Zero matches. Snapshot 00013 is the latest present; no later snapshot exists.
- Other experiments: swarm/experiments/E-001.md (Slice AI-HOUSE:SNACK, Applied day 9) and swarm/experiments/E-003.md (Slice MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS, Applied not yet written). Neither touches AI-LOUNGE.
- Not used as evidence: the `Applied:` line in E-002.md, which is still blank, and any orchestrator notes.

What the snapshots record: slot table (product, price, quantity, unit cost, loaded day), storage batches and purchase orders. None of them records marketing state for any location: no campaign, channel, date, spend, multiplier or fatigue.

Day 12 to day 13 differences seen in the snapshots: AI-LOUNGE:SNACK:A3 quantity 4 to 3; AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE slots unchanged; no price changes at any location. Those are ordinary sales and core work on slice and control alike, and they are not evidence about marketing.

## Criteria

1. **Change on every unit of the slice (AI-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** The lever (run_marketing, channel print) appears in no snapshot, including 00013. Nothing in ground truth shows whether any print campaign was run at AI-LOUNGE, or how many.
2. **Change on no unit outside the slice (AI-HOUSE, MAIN-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** Same reason. No snapshot shows marketing state at the control locations, so it cannot be shown that none was run there.
3. **No other running experiment touches the slice: met.** E-001 touches AI-HOUSE:SNACK and E-003 touches MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS. Neither changes AI-LOUNGE. E-003 reads AI-LOUNGE sales only to choose its SKU.

## Mismatches

- None between the orchestrator's record and the snapshots, because the orchestrator's record of the change (`Applied:`) is blank and the snapshots carry no marketing data to contradict or confirm it.
- The only change visible at AI-LOUNGE in the snapshots is the ordinary sale of one mint-tin (quantity 4 to 3). It is not a marketing change.

## Finding

The snapshots do not cover the lever this experiment uses. The adapter's snapshot is missing marketing, so the change cannot be verified as made on the slice only, from ground truth. The same gap was found at day 6 (F-E-002-1) and has not been closed in the ten days since.

To settle it: the system's ground-truth snapshot needs a per-location marketing section (campaigns run, channel, dates, spend, multiplier, fatigue, from get_marketing_report) for day 2 through day 13. I would then re-run this check against it. Until then the experiment cannot start its clock.

Fidelity: fail
