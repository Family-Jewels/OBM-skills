# Redesign R-O-001 (day 49 of 91, point 7)

Trigger: opportunity review O-001 (day 42): cash below the floor line three points running; the constraint is stocked slots; 18 priced slots empty while depot holds their SKU.

## Is the direction right?

Yes. The top measure is cash, and cash moves with revenue = slots selling × revenue per slot. Slots are the constraint (charts: slots_selling 15 latest, 16 stocked at day end, of 72; capital 12% in use). The drivers chosen in v1 (slots first, then price and mix) still point at the constraint. The tree is right.

## Is the design causing the problem?

Partly. The design already said "restock an empty slot first" (operator rule 2) and check 2 requires no slot empty while depot holds its SKU. The brief does not rank placement above purchase, so the operator kept buying (cola and oat-latte bought day 41, still unplaced) while slots stood empty. Rule 6 leaves fills for tomorrow when minutes run short. The fault is in the brief's order of work, not in the goal or the measures.

## Change (one redesign)

Fix the cause, for all machines, not as an experiment (a fill uses stock already paid for; check 2 already requires it; a slice would leave control empty, which the spec forbids):
- Operator rule 2: place depot stock into empty priced slots before any other restock, purchase or swap.
- Rule 4: place such stock before any order.
- Rule 6: fills from depot come first in the day's plan.
- Spec v2: E-002 withdrawn (print cost EUR 8 exceeds the cap EUR 5.45; guardrail kept, not loosened); E-003 superseded.

Old versions: `swarm/history/spec.v1.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v1.md`.
