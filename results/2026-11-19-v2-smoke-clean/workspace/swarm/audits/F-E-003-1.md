## Criteria

1. **Change on every unit of the slice (MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS): not met.** In the day 7 snapshot, MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS has one stocked slot, A1, holding sparkling-water-500ml (price 1.5, quantity 9, unit cost 0.51, loaded day 6). The other nine drink slots (A2, A3, B1–B3, C1–C3, D1–D3) are empty. No slot on this machine holds the coffee SKU. The swap did not happen. The snapshot covers slot products, so this is verifiable, and the answer is no.
2. **Change on no unit outside the slice: met.** No coffee SKU appears in any drink slot on any machine in the day 7 snapshot (AI-HOUSE, AI-LOUNGE, and the other MAIN-LOUNGE slots). The unit-level check is met, but only because the change is absent everywhere.
3. **No other running experiment touches the slice: met.** E-001 touches AI-HOUSE:SNACK. E-002 changes marketing at AI-LOUNGE only and reads MAIN-LOUNGE only as a control. Neither changes MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS.

## Mismatches

- The change in the file is absent from ground truth: MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS shows no coffee SKU at day 7, and no slot on any machine holds one.
- The sparkling-water stock in MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS A1 (loaded day 6) is ordinary restocking. It is not a swap, since the drink slots were empty at day 3 and day 4. It is not a violation.
- The AI-LOUNGE drink slots are all empty at day 7, so the snapshot does not show which coffee SKU sold best there. That is a precondition of the change, recorded as a note; the verdict does not turn on it.
- The `Applied:` line in E-003.md is blank and was not used.

## Finding

The Change was not made as written. The experiment cannot be judged as run. Criteria 2 and 3 are met; criterion 1 is not.
