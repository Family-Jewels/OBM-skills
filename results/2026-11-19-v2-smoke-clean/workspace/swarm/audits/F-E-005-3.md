## Criterion 2: the change is on no unit outside the slice — **met, with a limit**

- The control machines (AI-HOUSE:DRINKS and MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS) keep the pre-Start SKU mix in every slot (sparkling, cola, energy, plus oat in A3 on both machines). Their post-Start placements match their pre-Start SKUs (for example AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A1 sparkling 58 then 69; MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:B2 energy 58 then 69 then 78).
- Oat-latte-can does appear in control A3 slots after Start (AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A3 loaded 64, 68 and 79; MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A3 loaded 64, 72 and 79). But oat was already in those same slots before Start (AI-HOUSE:DRINKS:A3 loaded 44; MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS:A3 loaded 49), so the snapshots do not show that the rule reached control. I found no mismatch here.

## Criterion 3: no other running experiment touches the slice — **met**

No other experiment was running in days 57–84 (see the windows above), and none touches AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS.

## Limits

- The day-56 snapshot is missing, so the strict "before Start" comparison could not be made. Day 61 was used instead.
- The snapshots do not cover marketing or pricing, so E-002's lever could not be checked directly; its window does not overlap E-005's, so it does not affect the result.

## Feedback

- Unmet criterion 1: the placements into A1, A2 and A3 after Start that were not oat-latte-can are the fix. Since day 81 the operator has placed oat only, which the day-84 snapshot supports. The window is not clean, so the designer must decide whether to run E-005 again as a new experiment with a recorded pre-Start snapshot, rather than amending this Change to match what was run.
- Done well: the orchestrator's day-81 note states the non-oat placements openly and ties the fix to the snapshots, and the slice's current stock is oat-latte-can throughout.

Fidelity: fail
