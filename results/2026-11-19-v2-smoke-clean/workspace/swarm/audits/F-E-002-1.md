# Fidelity check F-E-002-1: E-002 moderate print marketing in AI-LOUNGE

Experiment: swarm/experiments/E-002.md
Design: Slice = AI-LOUNGE (both machines). Control = AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE. Change = one moderate print campaign per point via run_marketing(channel = print) at AI-LOUNGE only. Start = point 3.
Check date: day 6 of 91.

## What I compared

- Before Start: swarm/snapshots/00002.md (day 2, ground truth).
- At Start: swarm/snapshots/00003.md (day 3).
- Now: swarm/snapshots/00006.md (day 6, ground truth).
- Also read swarm/snapshots/00004.md and 00005.md for continuity.
- Other experiments: swarm/experiments/E-001.md (Slice AI-HOUSE:SNACK, Start point 2) and swarm/experiments/E-003.md (Slice MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS, Start point 4).
- Searched swarm/ for any marketing or campaign record beyond the experiment file and spec. Only the spec, the operator rules and E-002 itself mention marketing. No ground-truth marketing log exists in the workspace.
- Not used as evidence: the orchestrator's `Applied:` line in E-002.md, which is blank, and swarm/board.jsonl.
- Snapshots after 00006 were not present when I looked (glob of swarm/snapshots/ stopped at 00006), so 00006 is the latest I could compare.

What the snapshots record: slot table (price, quantity, unit cost, loaded day), storage batches, and purchase orders. They record no marketing state of any kind: no campaigns, channel, count, dates, spend, multiplier or fatigue, for any location.

## Criteria

1. **Change on every unit of the slice (AI-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** The lever (run_marketing, print) does not appear in any snapshot, including 00006. Neither the day-2 baseline nor the day-6 state shows whether any print campaign ran at AI-LOUNGE. Since the change's only lever is marketing, the snapshots do not cover the change at all.
2. **Change on no unit outside the slice (AI-HOUSE, MAIN-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** Same reason. No marketing state exists in any snapshot for the control locations, so I cannot show that none was run there.
3. **No other running experiment touches the slice: met.** E-001 touches AI-HOUSE:SNACK and E-003 touches MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS. Neither touches AI-LOUNGE. E-003 reads AI-LOUNGE's sales only to pick its SKU; it does not change AI-LOUNGE.

## Mismatches

- No change in the snapshots can be attributed to marketing, so there is nothing to compare between 00002 and 00006 on this lever. The snapshots show that AI-LOUNGE slots are all empty at day 6 and that PO-1002 was delivered on day 5 to storage. These are ordinary core work that applies to slice and control alike and is not a violation. They are not evidence about marketing.
- The `Applied:` line in E-002.md is blank. It is the orchestrator's note and was not used.

## Finding

The snapshots do not cover the lever this experiment uses. The adapter's snapshot is missing marketing. That is the finding: the change cannot be verified as made, on the slice only, from ground truth.

To settle it: the system's ground-truth snapshot needs a per-location marketing section (campaigns run, channel, dates, spend, multiplier, fatigue, from get_marketing_report) at day 2 and day 6. I would then re-run this check against it.

Fidelity: fail
