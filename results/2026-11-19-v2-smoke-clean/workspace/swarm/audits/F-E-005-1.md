Not used as evidence: the `Applied:` line in E-005.md (still the blank template text), the orchestrator's notes, and swarm/board.jsonl.

- Pre-Start snapshot: swarm/snapshots/00056.md (day 56), the last snapshot before Start on day 57. (swarm/snapshots/00054.md is not present; it is not needed for this comparison.)

## Criteria

1. **Change on every unit of the slice: not met.** The Change applies to placements into new or emptied drink slots on AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS after Start. Two such placements happened: A2 (emptied, refilled with energy-drink-500ml on day 57) and A3 (emptied, refilled with cola-500ml on day 58). Neither was a coffee SKU. The mix lever is absent from the slice in ground truth.
2. **Change on no unit outside the slice: met.** No coffee SKU was placed in any slot outside AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS between day 56 and day 60. Control drink slots show only same-SKU restocking.
3. **No other running experiment touches the slice: met.** swarm/experiments/E-001.md (AI-HOUSE:SNACK) and E-003.md (MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS, superseded by E-005) do not touch AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS. E-002.md changes marketing at AI-LOUNGE, which is the slice's machine; but its fidelity checks F-E-002-1, -2 and -3 all failed, so its clock never started and it is not running. Its marketing lever is not in the snapshots, so this rests on the fidelity record, not on ground truth. (E-003 is also not running: F-E-003-3 failed and it was replaced.)

## Mismatch

- The experiment's Change is not in the ground truth. Placements into the slice's emptied drink slots after Start were energy-drink and cola, not the coffee SKU the Change requires. This is the only mismatch found.
- The `Applied:` line in E-005.md is still the blank template text, so there is no record of the change being made. The file and the ground truth agree that it was not made.

## Notes (not criteria)

- Start condition in E-005.md: slots_selling ≥ 25 by day 56. Counting slots with quantity > 0 in swarm/snapshots/00056.md gives 14 (AI-HOUSE 4, AI-LOUNGE 6, MAIN-LOUNGE 4). The experiment was not eligible to start on day 57 under its own condition. This is a pre-condition, recorded here, not a criterion.
- Guardrails, cost and minutes are measured by the system, not checked here.

## What would settle it

A snapshot after day 60 showing AI-LOUNGE:DRINKS placements into new or emptied slots holding the chosen coffee SKU (with the sold-most coffee SKU named in `Applied:`), the other AI-LOUNGE drink slots as they were, and no coffee placements on AI-HOUSE or MAIN-LOUNGE drinks. The designer would also need to decide whether the start condition still holds. Then re-run this check.

Fidelity: fail
