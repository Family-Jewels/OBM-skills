# Experiment fidelity check F-E-002-3: E-002 moderate print marketing in AI-LOUNGE

Experiment: swarm/experiments/E-002.md
Design: Slice = AI-LOUNGE (both machines). Control = AI-HOUSE and MAIN-LOUNGE (both machines each). Change = one moderate print campaign per point via run_marketing(channel = print) at AI-LOUNGE only, no other marketing anywhere. Start = point 3.
Check date: day 19 of 91.
Prior checks: F-E-002-1 (day 6, fail) and F-E-002-2 (day 13, fail). Both found the same gap. I did not treat either as settling anything.

## What I compared

- Latest ground-truth snapshot: swarm/snapshots/00019.md (day 19), read in full.
- Pre-Start snapshot: swarm/snapshots/00012.md (day 12), the same baseline F-E-002-2 used. I did not find an exact day for point 3 in the workspace, so I did not pick a different one. Compared slot by slot with 00019.
- Full search of every snapshot, 00002 to 00019, for marketing, campaign, print, multiplier and fatigue. Zero matches in any file.
- Snapshot dates: the highest-numbered snapshot present is 00019. I found no snapshot later than day 19, so nothing after day 19 was checked.
- Other experiments: swarm/experiments/E-001.md (slice AI-HOUSE:SNACK) and swarm/experiments/E-003.md (slice MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS). Neither changes AI-LOUNGE. E-003 reads AI-LOUNGE sales only to choose its SKU.
- Not used as evidence: the `Applied:` line in E-002.md (blank), the orchestrator's notes, and swarm/board.jsonl.

What the snapshots record: slot table (product, price, quantity, unit cost, loaded day), storage batches and purchase orders. None of them records marketing state for any location: no campaign, channel, date, spend, multiplier or fatigue.

Day 12 to day 19 differences seen in the snapshots: slot quantities and purchase orders (PO-1004 paid day 17, due day 20) at all locations. AI-LOUNGE:SNACK:A2 and A3 quantities moved as ordinary sales, and no prices changed at any location. These are core work on slice and control alike, and are not evidence about marketing.

## Criteria

1. **Change on every unit of the slice (AI-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** The lever (run_marketing, channel print) appears in no snapshot, including 00019. Nothing in ground truth shows whether any print campaign ran at AI-LOUNGE, or how many, on either machine.
2. **Change on no unit outside the slice (AI-HOUSE, MAIN-LOUNGE): not met, not verifiable.** Same reason. No snapshot shows marketing state at the control locations, so it cannot be shown that none ran there.
3. **No other running experiment touches the slice: met.** E-001 touches AI-HOUSE:SNACK and E-003 touches MAIN-LOUNGE:DRINKS. Neither changes AI-LOUNGE.

## Mismatches

- None between the orchestrator's record and the snapshots. The `Applied:` line is blank, and the snapshots carry no marketing data to confirm or contradict any campaign.
- No change visible in the snapshots is attributable to marketing.

## Finding

The snapshots do not cover the lever this experiment uses. The adapter's snapshot is missing marketing, so the change cannot be verified as made on the slice only, from ground truth. The gap has now been found three times (day 6, day 13, day 19). Because the change cannot be verified, the experiment's clock cannot start.

To settle it: the system's ground-truth snapshot needs a per-location marketing section (campaigns run, channel, dates, spend, multiplier, fatigue, from get_marketing_report) for day 2 through day 19. I would then re-run this check against it. A later snapshot showing the campaign on AI-LOUNGE only, and no marketing at AI-HOUSE or MAIN-LOUNGE, would be needed to pass.

Fidelity: fail
