# R-P-019: redesign, day 254 of 365 (after point 36; diagnosis D-P-019)

## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Slots selling and cash are the right drivers; output per minute is the constraint (worker time 91% used, 435 of 480 min). The tree is right. Cash is the top measure and is judged by the system (x1.06 against floor x1.08).

## 2. Is the design causing the fault?
Partly. Three things in the design do not match each other or cannot be checked:
- **Pricing block: 40 min in the rules, 90 min in the aims.** Operator rules 3 and 19 say 40 min; aims.md (point 34 and AS-012) says 90. The operator is told to cut pricing to 40 min when running over, so stocked slots can stay unpriced. Stocked-but-unpriced is not charted, so nobody can see it.
- **Order sizing is checked only as "an order was placed".** The D-P-019 hypothesis (orders late or small) is not supported by the daily lines I checked: days 250 and 253 record covers at or near the 18-day target, with the cash held by rule 16. So the order step is probably not the cause. A small or late order still counts as a pass, though, so the check needs the sizing written in.
- **Idle cash is read as a fault.** It is held by rule 16 because fill minutes, not cash, bind. Its check is right only while a product is under target.

The cause the logs point to is fill minutes (about 7 to 9 fills a day at 45 min each), with a restock cost that is still an assumption (45 min per slot, open questions 1 and 3). That is the restock step, not the order step. AS-012 has already queued that change behind the batch test.

Not changed, deliberately: the restock step. Its change waits on the batch test (rule 2, due by the end of point 37), per AS-012.

## 4. Prediction (testable on the charts)
- **Stocked-unpriced at close** falls to 0 on at least 6 of 7 closes by day 259 (end of point 37). This is the shift I expect most.
- **Last fill before 16:30 with stock in the depot** stops happening: the idle minutes median falls from 45 to under 15 by day 259.

If the test shows about 45 min per slot, it stays at 45–52 through day 266; that outcome is the trigger for the restock redesign (R-P-020), not a reason to push harder on the current step.
