# Redesign R-P-007 (day 94, point 14; after D-P-007)

**Trigger.** D-P-007: slots selling and depot stock fell because orders were sized to 5 days of fills, lead times rose to 10–13 days, and a late order still passed the order-step check.

**Direction check.** Right. The top measure is short of its aim line (cash 5,836 against the aim line 8,763, floor 3,389). The binding constraint is now stock, not worker time (charts: 89% in use, but idle minutes on days 89–90). Priority 1 (slots selling, depot cover, order step) stays. The tree is right in shape; the design fault is in the cover target and in the arrival check.

**Change (smallest that addresses the cause; one change, adopted now):**
1. **Cover target follows lead time.** Planning target = planning lead time + 5 days of fills, where planning lead time = longest of the last three receipts from the supplier ordered from, not less than 3 days. Today that is 15–18 days, not 5.
2. **An order counts only if it arrives before its product's cover runs out.** Placement alone no longer passes. Added as a check (spec Checks, "Each order").
3. **Order sizing:** each order brings cover to the target; still under 40% of cash and within the best quote.
4. **Logging:** the daily line adds planning target, cover-out day per product, slots empty for lack of stock, and each delivery against its cover-out day.

Spec v5 (`spec.md`); operator brief v5 (`rules/operator.md`, rules 14, 15, 17). Old versions: `swarm/history/spec.v4.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v4.md`.

**Not changed, on purpose:** the goal; aims.md (no aim lowered; the point-13 aims stay as they are); the time split; the supplier choice rule; budgets and model tier.
