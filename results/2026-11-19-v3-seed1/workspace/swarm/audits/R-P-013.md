# Redesign R-P-013 (day 179, point 26)

**Trigger.** Diagnosis D-P-013 (day 175): slots selling is wrong-way (/1.13), slots stocked is short (median 20 of 72, aim at least 26), and worker time and capital sit idle while stock is in the depot.

**Direction check.** The tree is right. Slots selling and depot cover lead the top measure, and the constraint is worker minutes, not cash or stock. Nothing in the goal or the aims changes. What is wrong is the environment: the 45 min per slot figure is an untested assumption, fills end early with no check, and the idle-cash rule reads "no order open" where the real limit is fill minutes.

**Data note.** Charts (day 179) show worker time median 430 of 480 used, 50 idle. D-P-013 used the 7-day figure of 410 (70 idle), from a different window. The charts are used.

**Changes (spec v8, rules/operator.md v8; old versions in history/spec.v7.md and history/operator.v7.md).**
1. Early stop: fills run to 16:30 while an empty slot has its product in the depot. Checked: a day ending before 16:30 with such a slot empty is a miss; two in a point means Diagnosis needed. (Operator rule 20; spec Checks.)
2. Restock batch test: once per point, try one restock call for several empty slots of one machine, only where the depot holds the product. Log minutes per call and slots per call. This tests the open 45-min-per-slot question before pushing on it. (Operator rule 2; spec driver tree row added, priority 1 test.)
3. Idle cash: order only products under the 18-day planning target; hold the cash otherwise and log the covers. Check reworded to match. (Operator rule 16; spec Checks.)
4. Open question 12: the system is asked to chart minutes per restock call, slots per call, and fills per day.

**Not changed.** The goal. Every aim in aims.md, including point 26's slots stocked ≥ 26 and slots selling ≥ 26. Those are not lowered here.

**Prediction (testable on the charts).**
- slots_selling celeration moves from /1.13 to flat or up by the point 27 pulse (day 189). Its 7-period average is at least 23 on day 189 (19.71 on day 179).
- Worker time median used rises from 430 to at least 455 min by day 189; idle at most 25 min.
- Batch test: by day 182, the operator logs minutes per call and slots per call. If one call fills several slots, the minutes per slot drop below 45.

**If the prediction fails.** If the batch test shows about 45 min per slot with no batching, the aim for point 26 cannot be met at this capacity. That goes to the Strategist via an aim update (AS record), with `Design change needed: yes`. I do not lower the aim.

**Revert.** At the day 189 pulse, if slots_selling's 7-period average is below 20 and minutes per slot are unchanged, revert operator rules 2 and 20 (keep rule 16). Cash below reserve at any close reverts rule 20 at once.

**Costs.**
- Later fills use the reserve for runs past 16:30 (8 min a day); if pricing reads are cut first, revenue per slot may dip.
- Holding idle cash at target keeps capital idle (about 80%). That is accepted: buying more stock adds nothing until fill minutes exist.
- Cash celeration (x1.04 against a floor of x1.08) may stay below the floor; this redesign does not fix that directly.

**Next.** No further redesign until the day 189 pulse. Spacing: at least 3 points after the last adopted change, unless a fault diagnosis says otherwise.
