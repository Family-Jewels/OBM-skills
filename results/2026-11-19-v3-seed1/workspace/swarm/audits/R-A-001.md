## 1. Is the direction right?

Yes. The top measure (cash, celeration x1.05, floor x1.08) is short because slots selling is flat (x1.05) and units per selling slot have fallen. The drivers the spec pushes (slots selling, depot cover, units, orders) are the right ones. The tree is not the fault.

## 2. Is the design causing it?

Yes, in one place: the time split. Pricing and reads were given 16 min a day (3%), and the guardrail "if reads or pricing run over, cut pricing, not a fill" removes pricing first. Rule 3 says a slot is priced before its next fill, but a fill has no price step ahead of it when the pricing block is 16 min. Stocked slots are not priced, so they do not sell. That is an environment fault: the rule ranks the step that turns stock into sales below the step that puts stock in slots.

Not the cause, on the evidence here: idle capital. Charts show 14,819 idle, but the depot is held at or above the planning target (D-P-013 found 170–330 units per product). Committing more cash would add stock the fills cannot reach. Order sizing is unchanged.

Not changed: aims. Point-28 aims are carried forward at the same values for point 30. Lowering them is not an option; the Strategist is asked to redirect at the point 30 review.
