# Redesign R-P-010 (day 121, point 17; after D-P-010)

**Trigger.** D-P-010: slots selling flat (/1.06, 7-period average 20.7 against aim 25); idle capital about 8,075; worker time at 96%; order step missed 10:00 on days 61, 62, 77, 105, 109–111; quote checks about 25 min and close reads about 50 min each day; no point-17 aim.

**Direction check.** Right. The drivers in priority 1 (slots selling, depot cover, orders arriving before cover runs out, orders placed by 10:00, idle capital) are the ones that move cash, and the tree's shape holds. The fault is in the environment: the brief lets quote checks run per order and close reads run uncapped, so the worker's time goes to reads while stock and orders wait.

**Change (one, the smallest that addresses the cause):**
1. **Quote check once a week per product** (rule 5): check_offers at the week's first order step; every order that week reuses the quote. The quote is valid for the week ("same week" in the Checks table), so nothing is lost on price.
2. **Close reads cut to about 10 min** (rule 14): get_status and get_machine_inventory only. The system measures cash and slots from the simulator itself, so the operator's close read added minutes but no chart data. Sales report once per point, or on a price change.
3. **Pricing reads cut first** (rule 19): when reads or checks run over their share, pricing goes first, never a fill, the order step or the reserve.
4. **Consequence for missed order steps** (spec Checks): two misses in one point make the next pulse say "Diagnosis needed: yes". Stock fills already stop for products below lead-time cover on a miss (rule 17); the pulse now escalates it.
5. **Quote-check measure** (spec Checks): quote-check minutes at most 5 a day, averaged over a point, from work.jsonl; an order on a stale quote counts as unchecked.

Spec v6 (`spec.md`); operator brief v6 (`rules/operator.md`, rules 5, 14, 17, 19). Old versions: `swarm/history/spec.v5.md`, `swarm/history/operator.v5.md`.

**Not changed, on purpose:** the goal; aims.md (no aim lowered, none added here); the time split (aims.md already allots orders 48 min and pricing 30 min; the change brings the brief into line with it); the supplier choice rule; the cover target and arrival check from R-P-007; budgets and model tier.

- Quote-check minutes fall from about 25 min on a check day to at most 5 a day averaged over days 122–126 (work.jsonl).
- Order step placed by 10:00 on at least 4 of the 5 working days 122–126 (progress.md order lines). Currently 5 misses in the last 20 days.

**Cost.** Stale-price risk within a week: low, because the quote is for the week. Less close-time logging: none that the charts use. No extra working capital beyond what the order step already commits.
