# Redesign R-P-028 (day 311, point 44; after diagnosis D-P-028)

Read: audits/D-P-028.md, swarm/charts.md, swarm/aims.md (point 44, day 303), swarm/spec.md (v10), rules/operator.md (v12), log/work.jsonl.

## Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is the top measure and slots selling is the driver that moves it. Stocked slots, depot cover and units per fill are the right drivers beneath it. The tree does not need to change.

## What is causing the gap (design, not effort)
1. **Information.** The stocked-but-unpriced count and minutes by activity are not charted. work.jsonl has 14 lines, all from days 7–19, so the time split cannot be checked against what was done. The designer has been working blind on the moving driver.
2. **Resources.** The pricing block is a fixed 90 min a day with no measure of its yield, and it is not tied to the slots that need a price.
3. **Process.** Pricing is done in a block before fills, not at the visit, so a slot can be stocked and left unpriced for a day or more.

## Change made (smallest that addresses 2 and 3)
- Rule 3 (operator v13): after each fill, price any stocked slot still unpriced in the same visit. Pricing minutes go only to unpriced stocked slots; unused pricing minutes go to fills. The cap stays at 90 min.
- Rule 21 (new): append each day's minutes by activity to work.jsonl. Rule 19 wording: pricing block up to 90 min, used only for unpriced stocked slots.
- Spec v11 checks: each fill visit leaves no stocked slot unpriced (direct); the stocked-unpriced count is logged at each close; work.jsonl has the day's minutes by activity.
- aims.md: the pricing claim is "up to 90 min, as needed"; fills get the rest. Stretch aims are unchanged. The floor figure now matches charts.md (33,812).
- Information (1): the system is asked again to chart the stocked-unpriced count and minutes by activity (spec open question 14). The operator logs both until it does. This does not change the system's charts.

Not changed: the goal, the stretch aims, the driver tree, the order and reserve guardrails, the lead-time planning target.

## Prediction (testable on the charts)
- Stocked-unpriced count at close: 0 on every close by day 318 (7 working days), in the operator's logged count and the system's chart once charted.
- Slots selling: its celeration moves from /1.05 (wrong way) to at least x1.00 by point 46 (day 325), and the 7-period average reaches ≥ 28 by day 325 (aim ≥ 30 by point 46 or later).
- Cash: celeration rises from x1.02 toward x1.06 by point 47 (day 332). Still below the floor line; that is expected, and the aim line is not reached in this redesign.
- Minutes: pricing minutes per day ≤ 30 on average, fill minutes per day rise by the same amount.

## Cost
About 3 min per fill for the price read and set on new stock, plus the work.jsonl write each day (a few minutes of record). Roughly one fill a day less, offset by slots no longer left unsold.
