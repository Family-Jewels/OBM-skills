# Diagnosis D-P-004 (day 63, point 9; from pulse P-004)

**Top measure is on track.** Cash 4,096.60 is above the floor (2,518.30) and the aim (4,532.21) is close. Celeration x1.11 against the x1.08 floor. The movement is not the problem; the drivers under it are.

**Problem (pinpointed).** Slots selling is flat at x1.04 (14 of 72 latest, own best 21; 32% of slots in use). Revenue and units are flat (x1.07, x1.08, provisional). The drivers below slots have not moved since point 7, and the aims in `aims.md` (written day 45 for days 42–49) have not been replaced for two points.

**Evidence.**
- charts.md: worker time 420 of 480 min median used (88%); 60 min idle. Slots stocked median 23 of 72. Capital in stock 220 of 4,196; capital idle 3,855 (8% used).
- progress.md, days 22–46: 7–9 fills a day, each 45 min. Days 33 and 26–27 had failed or empty-depot fills. Order step held to close on day 62 ("missed again"); on day 59 the order step was only partly met.
- progress.md quote log: each order checked against `check_offers`, but energy was bought at 1.29 from one supplier while the other quoted 0.80–1.14 (days 53–57). Unit cost has not come down toward the ≤1.00 aim.
- No depot-cover chart exists (R-P-003 item 9 still open), so the leading measure for slots selling cannot be seen by the system.

**What maintains it.** Each fill is the only path from stock to a selling slot, and it costs 45 min. Reads, prices and a quote check before every order take the rest of the day. Idle capital costs nothing when the order step is late, and nothing is charged for a late order-step or for an overpaid one-cent line.

**Cause.**
- *Resources:* worker time is the binding constraint (88% used). The time split in S-002 is not held: order step late, quote-check overhead, fills at 7 not 10.
- *Information:* `aims.md` is stale (day 45). The point-7–9 aims were never written, so the day-57–63 work was run without a current target. Slot counts are not reconciled (P-004 item). The depot is not charted.
- *Incentives:* the rule against idle cash above 240 (spec Checks) has no consequence on the day it is missed; orders placed late or on a higher-cost supplier pass without review.
- *Not knowledge or model tier:* the operator has the rules; the gap is the order and timing discipline and the aims.

**What the design would need to change.**
1. Designer writes the point-7–9 aims into `aims.md` now (slots selling, depot cover, units per fill, unit cost), and the Strategist runs its point-9 redirect (due now).
2. Put the order step in a fixed slot before 10:00 and make fills conditional on it; size orders to the 40% cap so idle capital (3,855) goes into stock. Measure: capital idle; orders per working day.
3. Add the depot-cover chart (R-P-003 item 9) so the leading measure is visible. Measure: depot cover days.
4. Set a supplier rule for the cost driver: buy from the lowest same-week quote per product, with the quote logged. Measure: unit_cost_paid_30d toward ≤1.00.

**Open.** Whether small order sizes come from supplier minimums (Zuidas EUR 75, Noordzee EUR 90) or from the operator's sizing is not settled by the records; the order-size data would settle it.
