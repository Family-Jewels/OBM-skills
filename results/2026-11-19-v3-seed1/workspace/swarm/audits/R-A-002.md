## 1. Is the direction right?
Yes. Cash is the top measure, slots selling and stock are the right drivers, and worker minutes are the constraint (95% used). The tree is right. The aims are not mine to change and are not lowered here.

## 2. Is the design causing the fault?
Partly, and the information gap is now closed by the operator's own logs:
- **The restock cost is measured, not assumed.** Every restock call on days 285 and 286 filled one slot and took 45 min (progress.md). The batch test (rule 2, due by point 37) was never logged as a result, so open questions 1 and 3 stayed open for four redesigns. The batch is not available: fill cost is 45 min per slot.
- **So output per minute cannot be raised by batching.** It can be raised by what each fill delivers. A 18-unit small row yields 18 units per 45 min; a 10-unit row or drink yields 10. The operator filled 10-unit slots first on days 285 and 286, while 18-unit slots (MAIN SNACK nuts, choco) waited. That is a rule gap, not an effort gap.
- **Stock count and fill count are linked, and the aims do not fit the minutes.** At about 4 units per selling slot-day (about 160 units a day on 40 slots), a 10-unit slot lasts about 2.5 days and an 18-unit slot about 4.5. At 9 to 10 fills a day (360 min of fills, 8 full fills at the aim's split), the sustainable stocked count is near 40 to 45. The point-40 aim of 60 stocked slots needs about 13 fills a day, or more minutes for fills. This is the Strategist's to address, not mine (see section 5).

## 3. Change made (smallest set)
1. **Rule 2 of the operator brief (v12; prior `history/operator.v11.md`):** when several empty slots are ready, fill the ones that take most units (18-unit rows before 10-unit rows and drinks) and whose product sells fastest. The batch test is closed: one slot per call at 45 min, do not try to batch.
2. **Spec (v10; prior `history/spec.v9.md`):** the restock row is measured and not pushed directly. Units per fill becomes priority 1, the lever on output per minute. Open questions 1 and 3 are closed (inferred from the logged slots and quantities).
3. **Not changed, deliberately:** the time split (aims stay with the Strategist's point-40 split), the pricing block (90 min), the order step, rule 20 (fills to 16:30). I did not find evidence that the pricing block is the cause, and changing it without the price-persistence fact would be a guess.

## 4. Prediction (testable on the charts)
