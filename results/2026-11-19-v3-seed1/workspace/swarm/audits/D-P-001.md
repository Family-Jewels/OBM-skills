**Evidence:** charts.md (slots_selling 0 on days 2–7, 7 on day 8; cash 1,236.3 on day 7); progress.md (two orders on day 1 totalling EUR 191.70, deliveries day 6–7, 8 drink fills on day 7); board.jsonl (T-001 to T-006 all open, attempts 0); no runs.jsonl or work.jsonl.

**What maintains it:** nothing prompts action between deliveries. The operator brief does not name board.jsonl, so the tasks are never dispatched, and missed daily logs have no consequence.

**Cause:**
- **Information:** the aim of 36 slots by day 7 was infeasible on the design's own numbers. 36 fills at 45 min is about 27 hours; with stock arriving day 6–7, at most about 20 slots were reachable. Supplier lead time (5–6 days) is absent from the spec.
- **Resources:** the brief omits board.jsonl. About 13% of cash was committed, and no supplier search (T-002) was run to widen the order.
- **Incentives:** skipped logs go unseen until a pulse.

**Corrections to P-001:** fills did start on day 7, so the "fills did not start" finding is wrong. The "65 of 480 min" figure conflicts with 8 fills (at least 360 min) and with the charts' resource median of 0. Settle it from the simulator clock before fixing.
