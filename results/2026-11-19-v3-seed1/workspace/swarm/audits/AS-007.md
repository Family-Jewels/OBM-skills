**Can the stock aim be met as the work is done? No, not on the evidence.**
- Each restock is logged as one call at 45 min per slot (log/work.jsonl, e.g. day 7: "restock x8, 45 min each"; AS-002). 408 min is about 9 restocks a day.
- Median stocked must rise by 12 over the point. That needs restocks to exceed slots emptied by sales by about 2 a day on average. Slots emptied per day is not charted, so this can be neither confirmed nor ruled out.
- The aim is only reachable if one restock call fills more than one slot, or minutes per call fall below 45. Neither is charted. The same gap has been named in S-002, S-003, S-004, S-005, S-006 and AS-004 to AS-006; it is still open after 150 days.
- Capacity moved from reads and reserve into fills cannot close it: it adds at most about one restock a day (AS-004).

**What the design needs (for the redesign, not done here):**
1. Chart slots restocked per fill, minutes per restock call, and slots emptied per day. Close spec open questions 1 and 3 from the first receipts.
2. Chart depot cover per product (spec question 9; asked since R-P-003).
3. If no call fills more than one slot, the stock aim needs a different way of working (for example, fewer, larger restocks per visit, or a different product mix in slots with fast sales), or the aim is set again by the Strategist. The designer does not lower it.
