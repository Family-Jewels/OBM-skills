**Cause (environment).**
- *Information:* the spec sets the order trigger at planning lead time + 5 days (15–18 days in practice), but the lead time per supplier is uncharted (open questions 9 and 11). The operator has no single number to act on, and P-011 and the day-76 log both use "under 5 days". The brief and spec do not state the number, so the trigger is easy to misread.
- *Incentive:* a missed order step or idle-cash day costs nothing until the pulse, and the pulse escalates only on two order misses.
- *Resource:* the worker has 70 min a day idle and about 10,200 of idle capital beside a short depot. The room is in the order step, not in fills.

**Change (one, the smallest that addresses the cause):**
1. **Explicit trigger** (spec driver tree, Checks; rule 17): until receipts per supplier are charted, planning lead time = 13 days for every supplier (the longest recent receipt, PO-1052), so the order trigger and planning target are **18 days of cover**. The operator must not use a 5-day trigger.
2. **Idle capital buys stock** (rule 16): when cash above 240 has no order open at the order step, buy for the product with the lowest cover, even if no product is under the trigger. Within the best quote, under 40% of cash, cash at or above 120.
3. **Consequence for idle cash** (spec Checks): two idle-cash misses in one point make the next pulse say Diagnosis needed: yes, the same as order-step misses.
